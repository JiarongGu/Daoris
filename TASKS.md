# Daoris (道衍) — open work

Open work only: a finished row moves to `docs/task-archive.md`, a release change goes to `CHANGELOG.md`, and parked rows stay
whole in `docs/backlog-parked.md` until one is moved back here. A row says what is open and why; its contract is its design or its
area document under `docs/areas/`. Each row's earlier full text is in git at `9f58e4f2a:TASKS.md`.

**Standing direction.**

- The order now (2026-11-05): the backlog is built whole in big chunks (`docs/2026-11-05-backlog-plan.md`, D240), then SCOPEVERIFY1's
  one verification and the install staged from it, then the owner's review of the code and the documents. Inside the scope builders run
  no tests and merges carry no gates (D180's note); Sonnet takes chunks that follow an exemplar, Opus the plans, reviews and
  exemplars (the dispatch skill). Nothing related stays parked (D240 point 1).
- The foundation, the structure and the agents' work come before optimization (OPTIMIZE1); a defect found on the way is fixed when
  found (2026-11-01).
- The steward carries the backlog with as little of the owner's input as the grants allow, and treats development speed, token
  cost and branches or processes left behind as defects Daoris must handle for its own development (2026-10-30, D193).
- Keeping knowledge current is a focus (D220): a fresh fact replaces the one it updates, and tests pin rules rather than counts.
- Development moves into the install's driven sessions wherever a row allows. Codex's short allowance serves brainstorming,
  cross-reads and tests of Daoris's Codex paths, not building; its owed reads are Opus reads while its spend cap holds (D240 point 9).
  The parent republishes the install when a batch is worth running, closing and restarting it rather than staging under running
  sessions (2026-10-27).
- Spend ordinary subscription allowance only: checkpoint at a limit and resume when it returns; no extra or reset credits, no
  API-billed fallback, no duplicate workers or repeated checks (2026-10-27). A proof that needs an account's model runs once, at the
  least that proves it, and its spend is reported (D240 point 7).
- A dependency a row names ("after X") completes before the row starts. Installed proofs are batched against one staged build.
  Owner-only work (downloads, account spending, installation, publication, decisions) keeps its grants and holds.
