## The questions

1. **The flow of a session's page.** Name its steps as the person meets them, and for each: what it shows, where the work stands,
   what comes next, and the one press it holds. Where does *Accept all* live, and how does the person reach it from the changes?
2. **A line and its parts.** What is a line's head, and which part takes the person's word (a chat line, a dispatched quest, a
   carry-on)? How does the page say so before the person speaks, and what happens to a word given to a part that is held?
3. **Related lines of one repository.** How does the page show lines whose work overlaps (INTEGRATE1l's paths), and who decides
   they become one: the person, the line's head, or the steward role?
4. **The one door that brings lines together.** What it asks of the head (merge the others into its tree before one *Accept*, or
   integrate them through INTEGRATE1's step), what it refuses, and what the person sees while it runs.
5. **What the person presses at all.** List every press on these pages after the design, and which of them the steward role could
   make for them once appointed (STEWARDROLE1), without a production or live go-ahead, a sign-in, an access widening, a push or a
   deletion of the person's data.
6. **The rows,** smallest first, each with its proof (stories, page tests, a driver test on a git fixture of three branches, the
   installed window).

## The form

At most 1,500 words. Answer each question with a choice, its reason, and what it rejects. Name the code and records you read, with
paths. End with the rows in order. Write without reading the other maker's proposal.
