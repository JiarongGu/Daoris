## D185 — The screens built since UX7 get one design: a setting's value beside its words, a waiting step that says its phase and time, a refusal where it was started, meetings and brainstorms in Sessions, and the parts they share (2026-10-20)

**Decision (UIDESIGN1), proposed: the owner reviews it, with another maker's read, before any row it files starts.** The
features built since UX7 (D176's brainstorms, D177's meetings, D180's heavy runs, D182's pushed state, D183's Ask Daoris
modes, D184's sessions' access, the fabric's work state under D173, and D171's This Daoris) were built from decisions with
no designed screen, while the fabric has a design of its own. The owner's report of 2026-10-20 asked for one of the same
standing, and two reports join it: Settings' content does not use the pane (UIFIT1a-c), and a sign-in panel says nothing
while it waits (CLAUDESIGNIN1b). `docs/2026-10-20-ui-ux-design.md` is the design: an audit of every screen built since UX7
(§1), the rules it shows are missing (§2), each newer surface (§3), the component catalogue (§4), the traps (§5) and the rows
(§9). Processes drawn as flowcharts are FLOWCHART1's (D186), referred to and not designed here.

1. **The audit's verdict.** The newer screens keep the language where an older screen showed them how (the held call, the
   running call, New conversation, the review and opinion gates); they break it where nothing did: values at the wide pane's
   far edge, forms capped at half of it and columns chosen by the window, cards with no rule between them, waits that say
   nothing, refusals as toasts, stories drawn at one width, and four features with no screen at all (brainstorms, meetings,
   the heavy-run slot, a session's access on its head).
2. **Seven rules, as amendments to the platform language and the frame model** (the design's §2):
   - **R1** a card's rows share one label track (12–22rem), so each value sits beside its words; a form takes its content's
     width up to the pane, laid out by its own width; cards are 16 px apart, set once by their container;
   - **R2** anything the person started and waits on is a list of its phases where it was started, with each phase's time, a
     bound only where one is known and *longer than usual* only where the usual was measured; the phases are the driver's;
   - **R3** a refusal is said where its action was started; a toast only where that place is gone or drew nothing;
   - **R4** one run's present is a phase list in its place, a process's life is FLOWCHART1's chart on its record;
   - **R5** every surface is reached from the frame: no new place, a door from *What needs you* where it waits on the
     person, its verb in the one command table, Ask Daoris's door or exemption, its terminal twin;
   - **R6** stories at the widths the frame gives (wide, half, narrow), with no decorator adding what the page lacks, and an
     organism holding hooks split so each state has a story;
   - **R7** no viewport variant inside a region, held by `tokens.test.ts`.
3. **The newer surfaces are placed** (the design's §3): meetings and brainstorms in Sessions, as a kind of the rail's rows
   grouped by state, started from a composer as New conversation is, read on a page whose packet is reviewed before
   *Send*; a sign-in's phases written by the driver and drawn in its panel; a session's access on its head; Ask Daoris's full
   access asked through the one `InlineConfirm`; the heavy-run slot on the session that runs or waits and on the repository's
   Details; the work state's milestone as a phase list; and an unjudged pushed slice drawn as loading, never empty.
4. **Shared parts** (the design's §4): `PhaseMark`, `Elapsed`, `PhaseList`, `SettingList` (the wide setting row),
   `FormGrid`, `SignInSteps`, `AccessFact`, `HeavySlotLine`, and the meeting's `MeetingComposer`, `PacketView`,
   `TranscriptRound`, `ProposalGroup` and `MeetingPage`, each with its stories named. `FullAccessConfirm` goes.
5. **Its rows follow D187**: each is one feature a person can see working, named by its outcome and built end to end by one
   builder across every lane it needs, never a host half and a page half; a feature too large for one builder is split only
   into parts that each work on their own (the meeting's four), and a part several features share (`PhaseList`,
   `SettingList`) is built by the first feature that shows it. The rules are written into the platform language by the
   feature that first applies each.
6. **Existing rows change rather than being filed again**: UIFIT1b becomes *Settings' rows and cards use the pane* and absorbs
   UIFIT1c's spacing, in every domain and Setup tab; CLAUDESIGNIN1b is one feature end to end, not split at the driver and page
   seam, and the design's §3.2 is its UX7 §4.5 note; CODEXSIGNIN2's refused panel, merged, becomes that panel's first failed
   phase; BRAINSTORM1d keeps its caller and gives its page to the MEETUX features.

**Rejected.**
- **A tenth place for meetings**, **meetings in the side bar**, **meetings in Quests**, and **a drawer to start one**: a
  place is a decision with its own counts (the UX6 design §8, D150); the side bar holds what is the same on every view
  (D118 §3c); a brainstorm's result is never work (D176); New conversation replaced the start drawer (D162).
- **A capped, centred settings column**, **two columns of cards**, and **values kept at the far edge with leader dots**:
  the first answers the wide pane by not using it, the second breaks the scan and the keyboard order, the third ornaments a
  gap the layout made.
- **A sign-in's phases read from the tool's printed words** (they change by version) and **a countdown on every wait** (an
  invented bound is a promise nobody keeps).
- **A heavy-run wait in *What needs you***: it waits on another session, not on the person.
- **Removing the value pickers' chevrons**: the platform's own select carries one; the arrow rule is about a region's menu.
