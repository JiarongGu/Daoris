## D336 — Only the owner's kinds wait on the owner: one number, one card, answered where it stands; the rest is kept off their desk (2026-11-20)

**Decision (UISH6, UIDS16, UISE2, UISE3, UISE9, UISE10, the window redesign `docs/2026-11-19-window-design.md` §5 and §9; amending
D196 §5 and D40's landing).**

1. **The owner's kinds** are Question, Go-ahead, Release, Sign-in, Money, Failed (after its retries and another agent's try) and
   Confirm it was you. One census in the driver, `AttentionOwnership`, classes every attention kind as the owner's or the agents'
   with its doctrine reason and fails a kind it does not class. An agents' kind is counted, amber, only while no appointed role in
   its workspace takes it, and says so; otherwise its role answers it, recorded as the role's.
2. **One number**: the owner's waits across every workspace, not deferred, in the waits pill, the taskbar and the window's title;
   the scope never filters it. Places wear marks, never counts.
3. **One card**: every wait, on Overview, in a session's dock or pinned on its object, is a `WaitCard` with the same parts (what
   it is in the asker's words, what yes does, what no does, where to look, how it is known, who asked on which model), one id
   wherever it shows, answered in one press or key. A field its asker did not supply reads "not said by its asker" and is counted.
4. **Overview answers in place**: *Waiting on you*, *Moving now* and *Since you left*, read without opening anything, as columns
   from 75rem of main width and stacked below. Its tiles, quest card and repository bars go.
5. *Not mine* hands a kind the owner may hand over to the agents, writing the setting that marked it the owner's; *Later* (an hour,
   this evening, tomorrow, when I'm back) replaces *Set aside*; waits with one cause merge into one card that holds them, ordered
   by a real deadline, then what it holds, then age.

**Why.** The purpose: answer what waits in one press with enough said beside it. Today *What needs you* holds 36 kinds, most of
them manager questions (the band's story: trust a folder, try again twice, allow a fetch, five reviews), each kind with its own
card, none saying what no does, and Overview only links to them. Doctrine rule 1 is the closed list; where the product makes its
own agents act against it, that is a defect.

**Rejected.**

- *Counting every attention row*: the number would stop meaning "yours".
- *Hiding agents' rows while the roles are built*: work held by an unanswered row would vanish; it is counted until a role takes
  it.
- *Set aside, and set all aside*: a set-aside wait hides work still held.
- *Overview as a summary that links elsewhere* (D196 §5): an answer takes two places.
