## D305 — The reporting line is not the authority (2026-11-16)

**Decision (from the reconciliation of an outside architecture briefing against D304).** Two relations are kept apart, never read
off each other: the reporting line (whom a role asks for help; D304 point 3) and the authority (the place's risk record, the owner's
marks and each role's grants: who may settle or spend). A level of the line whose holder is not granted the act is vacant, and help
passes up; the person holds a level only by an explicit mark or appointment, never by default. Accountability needs no graph while
Daoris serves one owner: every place's accountable party is the owner, and an outside identity is credited `outside: <identity>`.

**Why.** REPORTLINE1's first cut read an unticked grant as "the person's word", which kept the person the default holder D304 meant
to remove; and asking for help was being read as approving.

**Rejected:** three separate graphs (reporting, authority, accountability) now (no second person holds a role; the map's trigger is
the first who does); the owner role at the top of the line as the top of all authority.
