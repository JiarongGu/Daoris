## D201 — A terminal's standing confirmation: the person grants it once in the window, and while it is on a terminal outside every session Daoris runs may stop or finish a session and hold or resume a repository without the card, each use logged and said on what it acted on, revocable at once, on this machine alone (2026-10-23)

**Decision (TERMTRUST1, proposed).** **Status: proposed**, round 1, written by Claude Opus at `f506863e7`; Codex's read (D191) and, since
it changes a decided rule other designs rest on (D156 point 4), Fable's foundation read follow. The contract is
`docs/2026-10-23-terminal-trust-design.md`.

The owner set the direction on 2026-10-23: the steward, an agent the owner runs by hand, drives the owner's local install for
development and testing, and stopping or finishing sessions from a terminal should not need the owner to press Confirm in the window
each time. Read at `f506863e7`, every terminal press the pipe's census makes the person's waits for the window's card
(`InstancePipe.Accept`, RPC1c; D156 point 4), so each routine stop costs the owner a press. The steward set the shape: no agent
bypasses the card; the person grants a standing yes once, in the window; it names management presses only; it never covers words, go-
aheads, sign-ins, widenings, a landing's push or a widening setting; each use is logged and said; revocable at once; this machine
alone, never synced.

1. **The standing confirmation** (长期确认; the glossary's *trust* is a folder's, design §0.1). The person turns it on, with their own
   press, on Settings → This Daoris → *A terminal*. While it is on and Daoris runs, a terminal's press it names, in exactly its verb's
   form, from a process outside every session Daoris runs, runs without a card. Everything else asks as today.
2. **The presses it names** (design §2.1), each a route and a form whose every field the verb sends and nothing more: `stop`
   (`STOP_SESSION { id, said: true }`), `finish` (`RESOLVE_SESSION { id, state: "completed", said: true }`, no words of the person's;
   `said: true` because only the verb's path refuses a session not waiting on the person), `hold` (`SET_HOLD { repository, held: true }`)
   and `resume` (`SET_HOLD { repository, held: false }`, only a hold a terminal made under it). TERMTRUST1b adds `done`: `quest done`
   moved onto the pipe as `QUEST_DONE`, covered with no words, for a quest no session here still serves whose last session here was
   finished. A grant names its presses; a later build's press covers nothing until the person grants it.
3. **What it never names** (design §2.2): words as the person, the keeping stop, `decline`, any answer or yes, *Try again* and every
   release of the person's own stop or hold, landings, hand-offs, discards, deletes and clears, every other standing choice
   (`SET_DRIVABLE` among them), sign-ins and account acts, and itself.
4. **Who it covers** (design §3). The pipe names its client's process; every agent Daoris starts runs in a job that allows no
   breakaway (`ProcessJob`, joined by `SessionProcesses.Track` from `Driver.HoldAsync` and the conversations; a partner's piece and a
   heavy run's gates in named jobs). A press whose process is in any of them asks; one the judge cannot place (an agent with no job, a
   session another process on this home runs, a client gone) asks. Being in no job never makes a caller the person (D156 §2.4): the
   person's grant covers the press, and the job only takes the cover from a session. An agent the person runs by hand outside Daoris
   rides it, and the grant's ask says so.
5. **Made and revoked only in the window.** `SET_STANDING_CONFIRMATION { presses }` is `Window` in the census and refuses the
   terminal's door in its handler; no verb, Ask Daoris door or `settings_*` grant reaches it; no card offers it. Turning it on asks
   once, inline, each press ticked; turning it off asks nothing, clears the instance's memory before the write, and takes the next
   press. A grant confirms no press already waiting.
6. **Where it lives** (design §4.2): `data/standing-confirmation.json`, a home entry of its own (three twins), written only by that
   route and by a covered hold or resume (its `holds`, the provenance `resume` reads), read at the instance's start and kept in memory,
   bound to this machine and account by a hash, off where absent, unreadable, newer or another machine's. No sync reads it. A process of
   the account can still write it while Daoris is down (D156 point 7); PERSONDOOR2 moves that reach.
7. **Each use said** (design §5-§7): `person.confirmation` `state: standing` before it runs, and `person.standing` for a grant, a revoke
   and what a start found, codes only; the terminal told `STANDING` and a line on its error stream; the session's record gains the coded
   part `standing.terminal` after its move's own note; a hold made under it is shown as a terminal's; *Notifications* heads its presses'
   group with a line, its count since the start and **Turn off**. A press the grant names that asks anyway says why, to the terminal
   and in the `asked` line.

**Why.** A card is worth a press only where the person decides something. A stop, a finish of a session already waiting on them, and a
hold decide nothing the person did not decide when they asked the steward to work: each only ends or holds work, nothing is lost, and
each is undone by the person's next press but the finish, whose quest then sits for them. Pressing Confirm on each teaches the person to
press it unread, which is the failure D37 and D196 name. The person's one grant keeps the act theirs (D156 point 5 counts a confirmed
grant), the job check keeps it from every session Daoris runs, and saying each use keeps it visible. The account's reach already does
each of these without a grant (the request folder, the home's files, the ungated service doors), so the standing confirmation adds no act
to it.

**Rejected** (design §10): a verb, an Ask Daoris door or a card that grants it; *don't ask again* on a press's card; covering every
person's press or a class of them by a word; covering a caller by who it is; a grant held in memory only (every update's restart would
bring the cards back); a key in `driver.json`, beside writers a terminal reaches; a sealed record; watching the record for an outside
write; an expiry; a notice per use; `decline`, the keeping stop, a work's pause and resume, *Try again* and `SET_DRIVABLE`; and closing a
quest as done by confirming the service's own confirmation, which names no process.

**Amends,** once accepted: **D156 point 4** and its RPC1c note (a terminal's press of the person's waits for the window, unless the
standing confirmation names it in its form and the terminal is outside every session Daoris runs); **D156 §5.1** (one coded note part on
an act taken under it, on that record alone); **the terminal RPC design §4.3** (the key presented on a covered press too) and **§7.1**
for `quest done` (TERMTRUST1b); **the This Daoris design §3.7**; **D196's view** (the presses' group's line); **D172's layout table and
D195's inventory** (a `data/` entry, kept); **the machine log design §4**. **These stand:** D37 and D52, D72 and D74, D58 and D126 §3.3-3.4,
D133, D135, D154 point 8, D163, D156 points 1 to 3 and 5 to 7, D196 and CONFIRMSEEN1 for every press that asks.

**The rows** are the design's §9: **TERMTRUST1a** (stop, finish, hold and resume; the judge, the job check, the grant and its record,
the wire's two words, the note part and the hold's provenance, the log, This Daoris's row, the line in *Notifications*; the row to
build) and **TERMTRUST1b** (closing a quest as done over the pipe under it; after 1a).

**What the checks do not cover.** Documents only; nothing is built or run. Every statement about today was read from the code at
`f506863e7`. That `IsProcessInJob` answers for a pipe client in a session's job, that every agent spawn is tracked in time, and that no
harness starts a child outside its job have not been measured here; the design asks only when it cannot tell. `verify` checks this
entry's place and the design's links, and none of their words.
