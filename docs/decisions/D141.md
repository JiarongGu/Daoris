## D141 — A page is one column, and its pane is the measure: no block keeps a measure of its own, and a cap, if one is ever set, is the column's, once, in em (2026-10-03)

**Decision (LAYOUT11, amending D41 §4 and the platform language §2 and §4).** The owner, 2026-10-03: *"some page content
still not auto sizing with the outer like quest body and plugin detail"*. Seen on the install at 1600 × 900, the right
dock hidden, in 中文: a quest's title ran the pane while its body was a 456 px column (`max-w-prose`, Tailwind's 65ch, on
the body's paragraph) with about 750 px empty beside it; a plugin's detail wrapped its description and its data folder's
note at that column and its source line at the pane's edge, two edges on one page (PLUGUI2 has since uncapped that page
alone, D140 §2); and 65ch, counted in a Latin digit, held about 32 glyphs of 中文. Read from the code at `66861120`:
26 web sources set `max-w-prose` at 55 sites, a quest's and an ask's pages capped nine more blocks at `max-w-3xl`
(48rem) beside blocks that ran the pane, and the setup card's sentence stood at `max-w-[28ch]`.

1. **A page is one column, and the column is its pane.** Every block of a page takes the column's whole width: its
   header's line, a quest's body, an ask's words, a note, a record, a refusal, the console's own explanation, a box a
   move asks in, a list of rows with their acts. No block keeps a measure of its own, so a page wraps at one edge in
   every language.
2. **The column is `ViewMain`, and it caps nothing.** The main area is the page column on every view, Sessions' centre
   included, and it is the one place a line's length is set. It sets none: the person sizes the pane with the frame's
   own controls (the list's edge, its closing, the side bar), and the page follows.
3. **If a cap is ever set, it is the column's, once, in `em`.** One value on `ViewMain`, never on a paragraph. `em` is a
   CJK glyph's advance and about two Latin letters, so its number says what a 中文 line holds; `ch` counts a Latin digit,
   which is how 65 came to hold 32 glyphs.
4. **What keeps a size of its own is no page's block**: a form sized to what it holds (a short field, a settings grid's
   48rem), an overlay (a tip, a toast, a menu, a popover, a drawer), an empty state's notice with its narrow body, a
   drawing at one unit a pixel, a code block that scrolls inside its box, and a control in the strip.
5. **`Prose` is a muted paragraph and no longer a measure, and `PageHead` loses `measure`**: a page's line takes the
   column's width on every page, which D140 §2 asked for a plugin's alone.

**Why.** The owner asked for the pane twice: UX5 U59 took the 72rem cap off the content column (D76), and this takes the
65ch off the paragraphs inside it. The 190-character line UX5's first pass measured (2026-09-22) was in a column nothing
stood beside, before every record page had its list pane (D118) and every view the side bar (DOCK1a). A measure per
paragraph answered it by giving each page two edges, and at a pane the person had widened, a column a third of it.

**The cost, said so that a look reads it as known.** At 1600 px with a list open and the side bar closed a page is about
1,200 px: an English line of 13 px type runs near the 190 characters UX5 measured, past the 45 to 75 a reading measure
holds, and a 中文 line about 90 glyphs. Narrowing it is the person's, with the side bar or the list's edge. If the window
shows that is wrong, the answer is point 3, never a paragraph's measure.

**Rejected.**
- **Keeping 65ch for the console's own explanation** (the backlog row's first framing, *D41 §3's measure kept for prose
  only*): a page mixes explanation and content, so two edges stay on it, and 65ch is what held 32 glyphs.
- **A wide cap on the column now** (about 100em): the same defect on a wider screen, which D76 (U59) rejected. At 1600 px
  with the list closed, or at 1920 with it open, it leaves the empty beside the page the owner has named twice.
- **A measure per language** (wider under `:lang(zh)`): two numbers for one edge, and the edge moves when the language
  does.
- **`ch` for any measure**: it counts a Latin digit.
- **A token read by every block** (`--measure-page: none`, as `*:max-w-…` on the column's children): a class that does
  nothing today, and a width on every child would override a form's own size the day it carried a value. The column is
  the place, and the source scan names it when it has one.

**This amends:** D41 §4 and the platform language §4 (*prose has a measure of its own* becomes *a page wraps at one edge,
its pane's*), §2 (*prose at 65ch* inside the column) and three sentences of §5 (the note's flex-item trap, the adoption
steps and a blocked start's sentence *at a reading measure*); D76's U59 sentence *prose at 65ch* (its rejection of a wider
cap stands, and point 2 rests on it); D140 §2's *LAYOUT11 sets the measure*, settled here as the pane, set in `ViewMain`.
The type scale, a form's size and D118's frame are unchanged.

**What the checks do not cover.** jsdom lays nothing out, so the checks hold the classes and never a measured width: the
look is the parent's, at 1600 px with the dock hidden, 1280, 888 and 680, in both themes and both languages. The cost
above is a judgement the look confirms or overturns. The setup card's head may wrap differently now that its intro and
its stand-in sentence keep no measure; a row whose act sat at 48rem's edge (an ask's proposals, a quest's conflicts)
puts its act at the pane's, as a settings row does; neither was seen.

**LAYOUT11, built 2026-10-03: the sweep and its holds.** `Prose` and `SettingRow`'s hint lost `max-w-prose`, `PageHead`
its `measure`, and every page its own caps: the quest's body, note, session note and five 48rem blocks (the pause, the
decline, the trust ask, the conflicts, the chain); the ask's words, close, intake note, proposal and pause; the go-ahead
list, the abandon box and what went and stayed; the finding's sentence, the repository's undeclared line, adoption steps
and standing answer; the code map's refusal, Overview's hint, the start form, the conversation's empty sentence; every
settings domain's notes and refusals, `PluginUpdatePlan`'s refusal on the plugin page among them; the setup card's
sentence's 28ch. Held by `tokens.test.ts` (no source names `max-w-prose` or a width in `ch` or `em`, and no product source
a named width but the strip's command center), `pageMeasure.test.tsx` (every story of the quest, ask, plugin, offer,
repository, entry, finding, session head and settings pages draws no capped block, a story's frame, a form's own size and

plugin and offer pages' suites read too.
