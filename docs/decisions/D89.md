## D89 — Ask Daoris is a session that proposes, on an agent of its own choosing, and every change is confirmed (2026-09-29)

**Decision (HELP1, the owner's calls on `docs/2026-09-29-ask-daoris-design.md` §8).** Ask Daoris is a
conversation with a harness session in a room of its own (`<home>/help/`), opened from the app strip,
the palette and F1 into the right dock. It reads, and it proposes. A setting is proposed through the
same route the screen uses, and an ask through the ask door. **It runs on an agent of its own**, named
under Settings → *Daoris's own AI* as a third job, off until named, as the intake is. **Every change is
confirmed**: its card says what it changes and the terminal command that does the same, and nothing
applies until the person presses Apply. With no agent named, it offers starters from what the machine
lacks, each a door to the screen that fixes it (D24's no-model tier).

**Why.** The owner, setting the first real workspace's landing rule: *"we will need some chat agent to
support configure for workspace"*. Proposing through the screen's own routes keeps what it can do
exactly what the person can do (D50), and PERM2 already settled that an agent proposes and the person
applies (D74).

**Rejected.**
- **Following the intake's agent.** Answering asks and helping a person are two jobs that may want
  two agents, and changing one must not quietly move the other (the intake's own reasoning, INT4b).
- **Remembering a confirmation per kind of change.** Not in this build: every change is the person's
  press.
- **Running `daoris` commands from the room.** The install does not carry the CLI, and a helper that
  ran commands would be a second door with its own approval problem.

**As built (HELP1a, 2026-09-29): its session is the one exception to D81.** Every other session runs
in its harness's own `auto` mode; Ask Daoris's asks the agent for its own asking mode (`default`)
where the agent offers it. The first real conversation ran shell commands under `auto`, reading a
checkout to research its answer, which showed the room's allow-list is no gate in a mode that judges
its own actions — and a helper able to run a command could run `daoris driver …`, the change nobody
confirmed that this decision forbids. In `default` every tool off the allow-list asks, and over the
protocol door every ask is refused by construction (D52).

*Amended by D107 (READ1, 2026-09-30): what it is handed at spawn also reads each checkout reading across
allows, by file and by two git commands on exact prefixes, and its room names those checkouts' paths. It
still has no shell, and it still runs in `default`.*

*Amended by D158 (ASKHIST1, 2026-10-09): a press after a restart still opens the next conversation, and the person
may instead go on in an ended one, from the panel's Go on in it or its history; the help place keeps its harness
conversation's id for that.*

*Note (HELPSTART1, SENDLOST1, 2026-10-15): the press answers once the conversation is placed.* On a loaded machine the room's
session look took 24.9 s (`help.room.look`, bound 500 ms), so `START_HELP` answered past the page's 30 s bridge bound and the
person's first words vanished, though the driver went on to open conversation `2b751dcf`.

`START_HELP` waits only for the record: the agent resolved, the account chosen and the record opened in the room's folder
(`ChatRunner.PlaceHelpAsync`), answered `{sessionId, starting: true}`. The room is read through the loop's `DescribeHelp` and
written, and the agent spawned, off the request (`RunHelpAsync`), its end told as any conversation's (`SESSION_ENDED`). A second
press while it starts is handed the same conversation. `HELP_START_FROM` still reads the room on its request.

Words said to it meanwhile wait in the shell (`HelpStarting`), answered by `SESSION_QUEUE` as its queue, `opening`, and go to its
door in order once it opens. Where it could not start, its record says why (`failed`, *Ask Daoris's room could not be written — …*
or the spawn's error) and keeps them as the person's.

The room reads the last whole Sessions look this shell took where it is under 30 s old (`SessionsLooked`,
`HelpRoomLookLog.Recent`), its log line saying `kept`. This reverses SESSUX1h3c's *no keeping* (D161), whose premise was a look a
tenth of a start: the room is a reading of a moment either way, and every proposal is judged against the sessions as they are
when the person applies it. The facts the room says are unchanged.

The page keeps words whose send it stopped waiting on (SENDLOST1): shown at once as sending, kept on a TIMEOUT with *the machine
has not answered yet; it may still take them* and nothing put back in the box to send twice, settled by the conversation's record
or queue or the list's next read, and given back, said so, only where their conversation ended without them.

Proof: `DriverModuleHelpStartTests`, `HelpStartingTests`, `HelpChatTests`' two placed tests, `HelpRoomLookLogTests`, and
`AskDaoris.test.tsx`'s HELPSTART1 test and three SENDLOST1 tests.

## Note (2026-10-16, HELPSTART1b: starting from an earlier conversation answers at once too)

`HELP_START_FROM` is placed and run as `START_HELP` is: it answers once the new conversation is placed and the earlier one's words
are kept for it (`ChatHelp.PlaceHelpFromAsync`: the running conversation set aside, the record opened, the transcript kept), the
press holding `HelpStarting.Placing` meanwhile. The room is read through the loop's `DescribeHelp` and the agent spawned after, off
the request (`RunPlacedAsync`), so the words said meanwhile are held as the queue and a failure is said in the record. The answer
keeps its shape (`SessionId`, `Message`, `From`). The set-aside's wait for a stopped conversation (10 s at most) is still on the
request. Proof: `DriverModuleHelpStartTests`' starting-from test, which holds the room's reading and still sees the answer.

## Note (2026-10-19, HELPSTUCK1: the room's reading is waited for only so long, and a start past it is said)

On the owner's loaded machine (test runs beside, each git taking seconds, the loop's own looks at 15.6 s and 50.3 s) a message
to Ask Daoris placed conversation `dc46ef27`, which sat at `queued` for minutes: its record held the opening note alone, no
agent started, and the person's words stayed held for it. `RunHelpAsync` awaited the room's reading of the machine
(`HelpRoutes.HelpMachineAsync` through the loop's `DescribeHelp`) with no bound and the press's `CancellationToken.None`, and
that reading walks git across every repository and tree in turn: the lines, the session look's trees, each workspace's
history, the work inventory. It waits on neither the loop's look nor the planner: it reads the last look's verdicts, and plans
afresh only where the loop never looked. Nothing said why while it waited.

The reading is now waited for 30 s (`ChatHelp.ReadAsync`, `DefaultReadBound`). Past it, the room as last written serves, as a
conversation going on already used it where the reading failed (ASKHIST1), reading no checkout outside it, and the conversation
is told first by code (`help.room-as-written`, its `seconds`). The reading left behind goes on, until a 10-minute backstop, and
writes the room for the next conversation when it answers. Where no room was ever written nothing can serve, and the start ends
`failed` saying so (`help.room-unread`), its held words kept as the person's as before. A conversation going on in the room
(`HelpRoomNowAsync`) takes the same bound and says the same line. Proof: `HelpStartReadTests`, whose reading never answers.

Not taken here, each a row of its own: bounding each section of the reading, so one slow section is omitted and said rather
than the whole room left as last written (the modules' `HelpMachineAsync`); the roster's walk lock held across account
questions, which made the placing itself take some 32 s (LOOKWAIT); and the four process-start places git and a conversation's
agent share (`ProcessStarts`).
