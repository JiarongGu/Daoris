## D280 — One conversation under every chat, driven session and Ask Daoris: it outlives its processes and its agent, *Stop* ends the reply, the agent and account are attributes changed between turns, and Ask Daoris's reach is its access mode alone (2026-11-12)

**Decision (CONVO1, proposed).** The owner asked, on 2026-11-12, for the foundation of chat and session to work like a regular LLM
chat app, carrying on across account and agent switches, shared by sessions and Ask Daoris, with Ask Daoris a regular assistant for
all of Daoris. The same day's use showed the gap. A word could not reach a running turn. A stop ended a conversation for good and
lost the word waiting behind it. A new conversation's first word went into the running one. One conversation's turn held another's
words. And the room told Ask Daoris both that it presses as the person and that seven presses are never its own. Daoris already keeps
everything a conversation is; the record's end, the session's stop and Ask Daoris's per-workspace slot made it many things. The
contract is `docs/2026-11-12-conversation-foundation-design.md`. Its rows build after the owner's yes and the independent read it
asks for.

1. **The conversation is the line.** Its id is the line's key (`LineKeys.Of`). Its parts are harness conversations, the records; its
   runs are processes; its messages are its parts' events read as one, of four kinds (words with their sender, answer, tool, note).
   It outlives every run and every part. Its state is read, never stored: replying, ready, waiting, archived. A part's end is never
   the conversation's end; only a delete removes one. What no record holds (name, pin, archive, home, focus, a branch's origin, the
   person's pick of agent, account, model and effort) is one table keyed by the conversation's id.
2. **A regular assistant's operations.** *Send*; *Send now*, the person's own (D188's note); *Stop*, which ends the reply only,
   ending the run's process tree past a 10 s bound and keeping what waits; the next word continues every end. *Retry* says the last
   words again. A waiting word is edited in place; a sent word is never rewritten, and an earlier turn's edit is a branch: a new
   conversation handed Daoris's brief cut at that turn. Attachments are the conversation's. A new conversation is always new, and
   each conversation answers on its own.
3. **Runs are warm by a bound, never on a clock.** A ready conversation's run stays up within a count of warm runs, the least recently
   spoken released first, and never while something it started still runs. A release is no end: the next word resumes by kept id.
4. **The agent, account, model, effort, access and focus are attributes.** They change between turns, by the person or by a limit.
   The conversation goes on the first way that holds: the same part; its harness conversation carried to another account of the same
   agent (whole, compacted, else briefed, D206's note); or a new part handed Daoris's brief (D189). Each way is said with its case and
   figures, and the brief says what it left out. Ask Daoris takes every way on. The switch is measured once on the installed doors
   (resume, carry, brief both ways, a ten-fact recall scored by exact string) before any bound moves.
5. **A driven session is a conversation a workflow speaks in.** Its start prompt is its first words, its steps' words are messages
   from their senders, and its review and landing follow a turn's end. Once the switch is measured, every door's driven run is the one
   run every conversation uses, so *Send now*, *Stop* and the moves are one path.
6. **Ask Daoris is a conversation in its room with Daoris's tools and the person's delegate; its reach is its access mode alone.**
   Full access acts as the person. Restricted mode asks on the card, a family answered *Always* being the grant. Five fences hold in
   both modes: the person's deny rules and the floor; its own mode and grants; a secret or a sign-in; staging, installing or restarting
   Daoris; and another repository's files. The room's special rules are kept, folded into this rule, or removed, listed in the
   design's §5.3; the *yours alone* rows and *Done at a terminal* go.

**Rejected.**
- *A store of the conversation's messages*: two records of one conversation (D228); D189 point 1 stands.
- *Daoris holding the history and calling models statelessly, as a chat app's server does*: Daoris runs no model loop, harnesses
  own their context and tools, and D253 point 7 forbids narrowing an agent to a tool.
- *Translating one harness's conversation into another's format at an agent switch*: Daoris never writes an agent's conversation in
  a format it does not own; the brief is the portable context.
- *One process for a conversation's life*, and *idle runs released on a clock*: a conversation outlives its processes, and nothing
  is ended on a clock.
- *A Stop that ends the conversation, with a Finish beside it*: the owner lost a conversation and a word to that meaning.
- *Rewriting or truncating a sent word, as apps branch in place*: the record is the line's account, and the tree and presses have
  moved on.
- *A harness's own fork for every branch*: no door's fork has been read; a door that declares one, measured, may serve later.
- *Ask Daoris's reach as mode × grants × per-verb exceptions*: that is the mess; one rule, its standing yes on the card, five fences.
- *Ask Daoris never pressing a run's step in restricted mode* (D277 point 5's restricted half): the card is the person's own yes,
  and a press the person must make elsewhere for work they asked for is the friction D277 began from. Through words stay the way to
  authorise ahead, in both modes.
- *A brief of the workspace's last conversation handed to every new one*: a new conversation is new; the room names the recent
  ones, and Ask Daoris reads the one the person points to.
