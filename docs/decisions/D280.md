## D280 — One conversation under every chat, driven session and Ask Daoris: it outlives its processes and its agent, *Stop* ends the reply, the agent and account are attributes changed between turns, and Ask Daoris's reach is its access mode alone (2026-11-12)

**Decision (CONVO1, decided 2026-11-12).** Settled after Codex's independent read (D191), each of its fourteen findings checked
against the code and treated in the design's §10: all taken, and the one way it proposed that is not (refusing a harness that cannot
hold a fence) answered. The owner asked, on 2026-11-12, for the foundation of chat and session to work like a regular LLM chat app,
carrying on across account and agent switches, shared by sessions and Ask Daoris, with Ask Daoris a regular assistant for all of
Daoris. The same day's use showed the gap. A word could not reach a running turn. A stop ended a conversation for good and lost the
word waiting behind it. A new conversation's first word went into the running one. One conversation's turn held another's words. A
start sat silent for minutes when its tool server was down. And the room told Ask Daoris both that it presses as the person and that
seven presses are never its own. Daoris already keeps everything a conversation is; the record's end, the session's stop and Ask
Daoris's per-workspace slot made it many things. The contract is `docs/2026-11-12-conversation-foundation-design.md`, built in its
§7's order.

1. **The conversation is the line, with an id of its own.** The id is set when it opens (its first part's, a driven line's quest's)
   and written on each part's record, so one read gives its parts to sending, reading, the brief and a workflow; no walk recomputes
   it. Its parts are harness conversations, the records; its runs are processes; its messages are its parts' events read as one, of
   four kinds (words with their sender, answer, tool, note). It outlives every run and every part. A workflow's run is its work, not
   its conversation. Its state is read from the intent kept (a stop's hold, a switch pending, a card, a move, a limit, a workflow's
   hold), never stored as a state: replying, opening, ready, waiting, archived; archive never hides what needs the person. A part's
   end is never the conversation's end; a delete, or a clear of finished history, removes it whole. What no record holds (name, pin,
   archive, home, focus, a branch's origin, the person's pick of agent, account, model and effort, a switch pending) is one table
   keyed by the conversation's id.
2. **A regular assistant's operations.** *Send*, which keeps the word, with its files, sender, level and request key, before it is
   acknowledged, a harness or not; *Send now*, the person's own (D188's note); *Stop*, which ends the reply only, ending the run's
   process tree past a 10 s bound taken for that run and turn, and holds what waits until the person sends; the next word continues
   every end. An opening is bounded, phase by phase, and stoppable; a server that does not come up is said with the words kept.
   *Retry* says the last words again. A waiting word is edited in place; a sent word is never rewritten, and an earlier turn's edit
   is a branch: a new conversation handed Daoris's brief cut at a durable boundary (a part and an event position). Attachments are
   the conversation's. A new conversation is always new, and each conversation answers on its own.
3. **Runs are warm by a bound, never on a clock.** A ready conversation's run stays up within a count of warm runs, the least recently
   spoken released first, and never while something it started still runs; held runs are listed. A release is no end: the next word
   resumes by kept id.
4. **The agent, account, model, effort, access and focus are attributes.** A change is a revision applied before the next words are
   taken, by the person or by a limit. The conversation goes on the first way that holds: the same part; its harness conversation's
   files carried to another account of the same agent where the provider declares the carry with evidence for its version, the copy
   staged and never overwriting (whole, carried past the bound where the harness compacts, else briefed, D206's note); or a new part
   handed Daoris's brief (D189). Each way is said as it ran, and the brief names what it left out. Ask Daoris takes every way on. The
   switch is measured on the installed doors before it is built and again once it is, by exact strings and fixed continuation tasks,
   before any bound moves.
5. **A driven session is a conversation a workflow speaks in.** Its start prompt is its first words, its steps' words are messages
   from their senders, and its review and landing follow a turn's end. A stopped reply, a workflow's pause, finished work and a closed
   quest stay four things: no landing or done follows a stopped turn, and none runs twice. Every door that holds a conversation runs
   a driven session on the one run every conversation uses, so *Send now*, *Stop* and the moves are one path; a door that holds none
   runs a turn per run.
6. **Ask Daoris is a conversation in its room with Daoris's tools and the person's delegate; its access mode alone sets who says
   yes.** Full access acts as the person. Restricted mode asks on the card; *Always* grants one named family, shown before the yes,
   never a role. Five fences hold in both modes: the person's deny rules and the floor; its own mode and grants; a secret or a
   sign-in; staging, installing or restarting Daoris; and another repository's files. Each is held by its owner on each path (the
   routes, the harness's rules), and where none holds it the room's instruction and the second check do, said for the harness that
   runs. It may ask a read-only helper about a repository. The room's special rules are kept, folded, changed or removed, listed in
   the design's §5.3; the *yours alone* rows and *Done at a terminal* go.

**Rejected.**
- *A store of the conversation's messages*: two records of one conversation (D228); D189 point 1 stands.
- *Daoris holding the history and calling models statelessly, as a chat app's server does*: Daoris runs no model loop, harnesses
  own their context and tools, and D253 point 7 forbids narrowing an agent to a tool.
- *Translating one harness's conversation into another's format at an agent switch*: Daoris never writes an agent's conversation in
  a format it does not own; the brief is the portable context.
- *One process for a conversation's life*, and *idle runs released on a clock*: a conversation outlives its processes, and nothing
  is ended on a clock.
- *A Stop that ends the conversation, with a Finish beside it*: the owner lost a conversation and a word to that meaning.
- *A Stop whose waiting words start the next turn at once*: the assistant would answer again the moment the person stopped it;
  *Send now* is the press that stops and goes on.
- *Rewriting or truncating a sent word, as apps branch in place*: the record is the line's account, and the tree and presses have
  moved on.
- *A harness's own fork for every branch*: no door's fork has been read; a door that declares one, measured, may serve later.
- *Ask Daoris's reach as mode × grants × per-verb exceptions*: that is the mess; one rule, its standing yes on the card for a named
  family, five fences.
- *Ask Daoris never pressing a run's step in restricted mode* (D277 point 5's restricted half): the card is the person's own yes,
  and a press the person must make elsewhere for work they asked for is the friction D277 began from. Through words stay the way to
  authorise ahead, in both modes.
- *Refusing full access on a harness that cannot hold a fence on its own tools*: a restriction is the person's to choose (D273); the
  window says which fences only the instruction and the second check hold there.
- *A brief of the workspace's last conversation handed to every new one*: a new conversation is new; the room names the recent
  ones, and Ask Daoris reads the one the person points to.
