## D289 — The person's review is read as a run's Outputs, a side-bar view of typed outputs pinned to a revision, and accepted on one Accept card in the session's conversation, bound to the run, node, attempt and revision it draws (2026-11-13)

**Decision (extends D273).** Approving a real change on an installed build, the owner found no code change view: the review page drew
a branch's files as path-and-hash rows opened one at a time, with no commit list and no hunks, below a page of handback text, and its
base was read against the checkout's local line, so a remote's merged pull requests counted as the run's work. The owner asked for
approval to be designed as other LLM desktop apps do it, with code changes as one kind of output. The steward decided from two
independent proposals (Codex and Fable).

1. **Outputs (产出).** A side-bar view replaces the Review view. An output is a typed artifact with a stable id across attempts and a
   digest of the version captured; its type is declared by what made it (a part's handback, the changes a kind read, a system's plan),
   never inferred from a path; its display comes from a small vocabulary (change set, document, resource plan, evidence, structured
   record) with a raw fallback. D273's review record is the manifest: the outputs are its reading. The view follows the session to its
   run, pins the attempt first answered, picks any revision, and holds two groups: *Under review · rN* and *After the Accept*. A newer
   revision is a banner naming the outputs that moved, a moved tip and a corrected handback among them.
2. **The change set** is the commits, newest first (one narrows the files), then every file's hunks in one scroll, unified or side by
   side, each marked *Viewed* by its patch; a viewed file and one past the bound stay folded, and uncommitted work is named apart. Its
   base is the commit the run's chain grew from the line, frozen with the tip on the record.
3. **The Accept card** (`ReviewCard`), inline in the session's conversation after the turns that did the work, states what is
   accepted, the revision, the outputs with their viewed marks and what follows, and takes *Accept* in one press, *Send back…* with
   words (the glossary's word for "request changes"), *Stop…* asked once, and *Open outputs*. It binds run, node, attempt and
   revision; a newer revision supersedes it and appends a new card, and a stale press is said `moved`, never retargeted. The
   supervisor's held decision is the card's held state.
4. **One way (D228).** The Review view, `DecisionBar`, the `review/<run>` opener and the `work.runReview.*` words went in the same
   chunk; the view is `outputs` and its opener `outputs/<run>`.

**Rejected.** Keeping the Review view beside the card (two ways to one word); one card for a whole ask (revisions differ per run and
acceptance is not atomic); a card that follows the newest revision (it would accept what was not drawn); an Accept in the head (D273:
it drew no revision; the card draws it); a tab per output kind (kinds grow with plugins); kinds inferred from a path (a guess reads as
fact); plugin-supplied markup or live plugin pages as evidence (they escape the design language and move under the reader);
summary-only approval (it cannot show the exact artifact); a joint Accept as release coordination (acceptance supplies consent,
workflow dependencies enforce order, D288).

**Later parts.** The card in Ask Daoris and as *What needs you*'s row body, with the ask scope and a group press (one word per run);
live outputs after the Accept (push, pull request, merge, release) and the release-target hold card (D288); migration and evidence
displays; plugin presentation metadata with a read-only resolver; *new since you last looked* per reader.
