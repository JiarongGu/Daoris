## D208 — The owner appoints roles that act on their behalf, each act recorded as the role's and overruled by them; the supervisor is the first, answering a session's recorded question per repository, held by the steward from a terminal until its actor runs; and the question card reads each choice whole (2026-10-24)

**Decision (SUPERVISOR1, QUESTIONUI1), proposed.** **Status: proposed**, round 4, written by Claude Opus at `478e6010b`, revised at
`fb34cf7db` after Codex's read (D191 point 1; 18 findings, 14 blocking) and an independent Opus reader's foundation read (D191's

verbatim in `docs/2026-10-24-supervisor-reads.md`, and each finding was checked against the code on main and taken, answered or deferred in
the design's §13. It changes D156's person's door, D201's judge and record, the pipe census's classes, the session record's authorship and
the go-on's move to working. The contract is `docs/2026-10-24-supervisor-design.md`. The direction (the owner, 2026-10-24, through the
quest that asked for this design): a session's question needs a better option selection, and a supervisor decides among the options and
replies as part of the workflow, continuing the co-work and company frame; until it is built the steward plays the role. The frame is
D193's: the person is the owner and gives roles; a role acts on the owner's behalf within its scope, by its holder's own decision; the one
grant in the window is the appointment, not a confirmation of each act; each act is recorded as the role's, never as the owner's words, so

the appointment lasts until Daoris restarts, so after a restart or an update the owner presses *Appoint again* once, and an appointment
found at a start stays dormant until then (keeping it across restarts is a widening only the owner may choose later); and the role is held
by a dedicated session that does only the role, answering questions and nothing else, building, testing, merging and dispatching nothing
and running no branch's code or hooks, so the steward's working session never holds the key, and the verb checks that the pipe's server
is the install's own executable before the key crosses it. QUESTIONUI1a is being built on its own branch from the design's §8, with
Codex's round-2 findings 14 and 15 settled in its brief.

What was read (design §0). A session's question is recorded by `question_ask` and answered by a press through `SESSION_INPUT`, kept as
the person's words and handed as `chose: <label>`. A word is the person's by having no author (`SaidWord.Persons`), and `SaidFrom` names
only Daoris and a passed-on agent, a vocabulary the ungated service door takes from any caller's body; the say door keeps one word per
producer key and answers a later say with that key as a retry without comparing what it carries. More than a dozen carriers and readers
mark a word only by `Persons` or `Daoris`, among them the working note and the planner's go-on sentence, which say *your answer* for any
park. A parked record with any author's waiting words is eligible to go on, subject to the look's holds, and the move to working names no
words. A terminal's answer is `sessions say`, which the census makes the person's, so it waits for the window's card and, confirmed, is the
owner's words; with no Daoris running it acts alone. D201's judge covers four management presses that only narrow, never words, asks only
for `Person` routes, writes its record outside its lock, and is not offered yet; its caller look places a caller outside the agents' jobs
and names no holder. The bench holds O and R questions as the person's. The card draws each choice as a bare button with its
recommendation outside its accessible name, and neither `ParkAnswer` nor `AwaitingPerson` has stories of its own.

1. **Roles** (design §2). The owner appoints a role in the window with their own press, per scope, off by default; no verb, Ask Daoris
   door, card or grant appoints one. A role's acts are named routes in exact forms; its holder decides each; an act it covers runs with
   no card and one it does not is refused, never turned into a card. Each act carries the role's mark, never the owner's absence of an
   author, is said where the owner reads it, and is overruled by the owner's press while it has not taken effect. No role decides what
   is the owner's (D191 point 3, D193 point 1, D135 points 2 and 7). Revoked at once; this machine alone.
2. **The holder** (§2.2): `terminal`, a dedicated terminal session the owner gives the appointment's **holder key** to, which does only
   the role (the steward's, by the owner's choice; never the steward's working session, which builds, tests, merges and dispatches): a
   key drawn at the appointment, shown once in the window, kept by Daoris only as its hash and **in memory alone**, read by the verb from
   the terminal's environment alone and sent only to a pipe served by the install's own `Daoris.exe`, and checked after D201's caller look
   in a census class `Role` judged by the judge's own method; a caller with no key is refused, never asked. Daoris removes the key from
   its own environment at each executable's entry and strips it from every start it builds. The appointment lasts until Daoris restarts.
   The account's reach (another process's memory) is the said limit. Or
   `actor`, Daoris's own actor for the role (D205), its answer read from its own run, never a connector door. In the first rows a holder
   answers **one choice the session offered**, never words (SUPERVISOR1f, after its own read).
3. **Where an appointment lives** (§2.3): `appointments` in D201's record, `data/standing-confirmation.json`, schema 1, additive, each with
   a `generation` every act carries and admission, release and execution compare, and never the key nor its hash; every write of the
   record, presses and appointments, one mutation step under the judge's lock, which is never held across an await, a revoke sweeping its
   decisions only after its step; written only by `SET_APPOINTMENT`, `Window` in the census; one found at a start dormant until *Appoint
   again*, and said so; offered on its own flag, only after D201's offering, the bounds (1b) and the activation proof on a candidate build.
   The presses and the appointments are separate ticks; the role is never a press.
4. **The supervisor** (主管, §3): the first role, per repository (a workspace later, the repository's own appointment winning). It answers a
   parked session's recorded question with one offered choice and one to five reasons, each naming its source (D135 point 4), through
   `SUPERVISOR_ANSWER`; or leaves it to the owner with a bound and its reasons (`SUPERVISOR_DEFER`). One decision per question, a retry
   idempotent, a question overruled, stale or not handed the owner's. It is handed the question and its choices, the session's evidence,
   the go-aheads on its ask with their answers, the work order with the owner's quoted words and the standing answers, and the records.
5. **The decision before the word** (§3.4, §3.6): each live decision held **whole in this instance's memory**, every check reading it
   there; its files written for the session's history and never read back in the run that wrote them, a file that differs moving it to
   `stale`; at a start nothing is live and every waiting supervisor's word is taken back. Held for the overrule window with nothing on the
   record, then released in **one conditional step** at the service's lock, which composes the word's text from the chosen label itself
   and refuses where the record, the owner's words, the question, the label or the go-aheads no longer allow it, or another word already
   claims the decision. The release owns its word by a receipt only memory holds and records the word's id. Its states and their only
   moves are one lifecycle: held, released, admitted, went on; overruled, superseded or stale before admission; not handed or interrupted
   after it.
6. **Its bounds** (§4): never a go-ahead (refused while one on the ask is open or unread), a sign-in, an access widening, spending, a
   deletion of the owner's data, a question a repository document says to ask the person (D135 point 7), the owner's own preference, a
   question the records do not answer, an answer that contradicts a decision, or a question that tries to direct it. Daoris holds the
   structural ones and an agent's `owner` mark; the holder defers the rest; the owner overrules. What the holder reads is the asking agent's
   claims, never instructions: printed fenced and labelled, with adversarial cases on the bench. The session is told every time that the
   choice tells it which offered answer to follow and grants nothing, the chosen answer's own words included. One answers-alone count,
   D188's, holds helpers', opinions' and the supervisor's answers alike, by both its predicates.
7. **Recorded and said as its** (§6): a word `From: supervisor`, kept only through the conditional step, never `Persons`; **authored**
   only where its id is the one the release recorded, **handable** only while its decision is released or admitted under the go-on's
   ticket, and handed as the paragraph composed from the decision in memory; any other word claiming the supervisor quarantined, excluded
   from every handed list, never a turn, taken back; history after a restart read from the files as history, granting nothing. Every
   carrier and reader of a word's author given its case under a sweep test that finds them by search, and the working note and the go-on
   sentence chosen by whether the owner answered, never saying *you* where they did not. The say door writes no note line for it; the
   supervisor's line is the driver's, for an attributed word. Shown on the session under *From the supervisor, on your behalf*, as a notice
   in *Notifications*, and on the question card; a held or released decision is not a wait on the owner.
8. **The overrule** (§6.3): the decision is held five minutes, the record held whatever else waits; the owner's own words supersede it,
   after release through one ledger operation that every door keeping the owner's words uses, in the same step. **Overrule**, a `Person`
   route, drops it while held and takes the word back while released, until the move to working admits it: that move runs under the
   decision's lock, rechecks the generation, names the supervisor's words it admits and requires the record still parked, and the ledger
   refuses it otherwise; a released decision on a record no longer parked is stale. The admitted word is handed only under a single-use
   ticket carried into the prompt, and goes on once the run's start accepts it; any failure before then is `not-handed`, the park restored
   with its other words. Once the record runs, the owner's correction is their own words.
9. **Its place** (§7): the owner's delegate for questions asked of the owner, not a co-worker, not a lead's helper and not a manager of
   sessions; Ask Daoris may explain it and never appoints or overrules it. Its cost, held by the actor: one turn per question it looks at,
   one attempt per question, only in appointed repositories, measured on the question bench before the actor is offered, with no wrong
   answer of any class and a verdict on every case.
10. **The question card** (QUESTIONUI1, §8): one molecule in the box and the head's card; each choice a radio row that reads whole (label,
    what the agent does on it, the recommendation and its why), its accessible name the label and *recommended* by `aria-labelledby`;
    nothing preselected, and *Clear choice*; no second field: the box's own draft and *Send*, and the head's card's own text area and
    *Send answer*, carry the frame's one selection, tied to the question's id; the go-aheads first for every answer; keyboard and screen
    reader by native radios; the supervisor's pick, reasons and overrule with `Remaining` where it answered; en and zh, both themes, the
    three widths, stories of its own.

**Why.** The owner's attention is spent where only they decide (D37, D193 point 1), and of the 46 items that reached the owner in D135's
evidence, 21 did not need them: the ticket, the code, a look, the repository's documents or Daoris's own mechanics settled them
(`docs/2026-10-03-knowledge-use-evidence.md`, finding 2). The steward already settles them by hand, recorded either as the owner's words or
as a card the owner must press. A role recorded as itself keeps the owner's words theirs, the decision traceable to who made it and why,
and the owner's overrule real. Building it on D201 reuses a judge, a record and a caller look already built and reviewed. The holder key
makes the appointment, not mere absence from Daoris's jobs, what admits a holder, because its hash lives only in memory, Daoris strips the
key from every environment it gives, the key goes only to the install's own pipe, and the session that holds it runs nothing but the role.
Holding the live decision in memory, composing the session's word from it, owning the word by a receipt and handing it only under an
admission's ticket keep what the owner was shown and what the session reads the same.

**Rejected** (design §12): the supervisor's answer as the owner's words; a card per answer; a machine-wide press with no repository; no
overrule window, or one of 0; keeping the word on the record during the hold; an overrule after the session acted; a model judging whether
a question is the owner's; preselecting the recommendation; a second answer field in the card; a separate roles file now; resting the
role's authority on the act's shape alone; handing an unattributed word with a prefix; the release adopting a word found under its key, or
the driver handing a word's stored text; one envelope replaced by the next question; a note line written by the say door for a
supervisor's word; a bench floor of one right answer; an allow-list of the holder's process; a separate count for the supervisor; words in
the first row; the supervisor answering go-aheads; carrying its answer into a new session; reading a live decision back from its files;
recovering the release's word by an identical retry; `went-on` at the move to working; putting a park back by a move that clears its
words; holding the judge's lock across the say, or a revoke taking decision locks inside its step; supersession in one door alone; the
steward's working session holding the key. Round 2's rejection of a holder-bound credential is withdrawn. Keeping the key's hash across
restarts is not taken: a widening only the owner may choose later.
