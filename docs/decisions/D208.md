## D208 — The owner appoints roles that act on their behalf, each act recorded as the role's and overruled by them; the supervisor is the first, answering a session's recorded question per repository, held by the steward from a terminal until its actor runs; and the question card reads each choice whole (2026-10-24)

**Decision (SUPERVISOR1, QUESTIONUI1), proposed.** **Status: proposed**, round 2, written by Claude Opus at `478e6010b` and revised at
`fb34cf7db` after Codex's read (D191 point 1; 18 findings, 14 blocking) and an independent Opus reader's foundation read (D191's
amendment of 2026-10-23; 21 findings, 11 blocking), both asking another round and both kept verbatim in
`docs/2026-10-24-supervisor-reads.md`. Each finding was checked against the code on main and taken, answered or deferred in the design's
§13. It changes D156's person's door, D201's judge and record, the pipe census's classes and the session record's authorship. The contract
is `docs/2026-10-24-supervisor-design.md`. The direction (the owner, 2026-10-24, through the quest that asked for this design): a session's
question needs a better option selection, and a supervisor decides among the options and replies as part of the workflow, continuing the
co-work and company frame; until it is built the steward plays the role. The frame is D193's: the person is the owner and gives roles; a
role acts on the owner's behalf within its scope, by its holder's own decision; the one grant in the window is the appointment, not a
confirmation of each act; each act is recorded as the role's, never as the owner's words, so the owner reads who decided and overrules.
For round 2 the steward settled that a terminal holder answers one offered choice with its reasons in the first row, and that the first
row splits into the record (1a-i) and the door (1a-ii).

What was read (design §0). A session's question is recorded by `question_ask` and answered by a press through `SESSION_INPUT`, kept as
the person's words and handed as `chose: <label>`. A word is the person's by having no author (`SaidWord.Persons`), and `SaidFrom` names
only Daoris and a passed-on agent, a vocabulary the ungated service door takes from any caller's body; a dozen readers mark a word only by
`Persons` or `Daoris`, and a park's note says *Answered:* with no author in its English. A parked record goes on with any author's waiting
words. A terminal's answer is `sessions say`, which the census makes the person's, so it waits for the window's card and, confirmed, is
the owner's words; with no Daoris running it acts alone. D201's judge covers four management presses that only narrow, never words, asks
only for `Person` routes, and is not offered yet. The bench holds O and R questions as the person's. The card draws each choice as a bare
button with its recommendation outside its accessible name, and neither `ParkAnswer` nor `AwaitingPerson` has stories of its own.

1. **Roles** (design §2). The owner appoints a role in the window with their own press, per scope, off by default; no verb, Ask Daoris
   door, card or grant appoints one. A role's acts are named routes in exact forms; its holder decides each; an act it covers runs with
   no card and one it does not is refused, never turned into a card. Each act carries the role's mark, never the owner's absence of an
   author, is said where the owner reads it, and is overruled by the owner's press while it has not taken effect. No role decides what
   is the owner's (D191 point 3, D193 point 1, D135 points 2 and 7). Revoked at once; this machine alone.
2. **The holder** (§2.2): `terminal`, any caller in no Daoris agent's job (the steward, by the owner's choice), its acts over the pipe in a
   census class `Role` that D201's judge decides by its own method under the same lock and caller look, each decision naming its pipe
   client; or `actor`, Daoris's own actor for the role (D205), its answer read from its own run, never a connector door. In the first rows
   a holder answers **one choice the session offered**, never words (SUPERVISOR1f, after its own read).
3. **Where an appointment lives** (§2.3): `appointments` in D201's record, `data/standing-confirmation.json`, schema 1, additive, kept
   through every write of the presses, read and judged under D201's lock and machine binding; written only by `SET_APPOINTMENT`, `Window`
   in the census; offered on its own flag, only once D201's grant is and the bounds (1b) and the installed proof (1a-ii) have landed. The
   presses and the appointments are separate ticks; the role is never a press.
4. **The supervisor** (主管, §3): the first role, per repository (a workspace later, the repository's own appointment winning). It answers a
   parked session's recorded question with one offered choice and one to five reasons, each naming its source (D135 point 4), through
   `SUPERVISOR_ANSWER`; or leaves it to the owner with a bound and its reasons (`SUPERVISOR_DEFER`). One decision per question, a retry
   idempotent, an overruled question the owner's. It is handed the question and its choices, the session's evidence, the go-aheads on its
   ask with their answers, the work order with the owner's quoted words and the standing answers, and the records.
5. **The decision before the word** (§3.4): a machine-local envelope per session, the authority on who decided, on what, under which
   appointment, with the question's snapshot; held for the overrule window with nothing on the record, then released, checked again, as the
   supervisor's word; an envelope that cannot be read fails closed.
6. **Its bounds** (§4): never a go-ahead (refused while one on the ask is open), a sign-in, an access widening, spending, a deletion of
   the owner's data, a question a repository document says to ask the person (D135 point 7), the owner's own preference, a question the
   records do not answer, an answer that contradicts a decision, or a question that tries to direct it. Daoris holds the structural ones
   and an agent's `owner` mark; the holder defers the rest; the owner overrules. What the holder reads is the asking agent's claims, never
   instructions: printed fenced and labelled, with adversarial cases on the bench. The session is told every time that the choice grants no
   go-ahead, sign-in, access, spending, push, publication, deletion or history rewrite and overrides no refusal. One answers-alone count,
   D188's, holds helpers', opinions' and the supervisor's answers alike. What D201's caller look does not bound is weighed for this widening
   act and said in the appointment's ask.
7. **Recorded and said as its** (§6): a word `From: supervisor`, admitted only with its decision, never `Persons`; every reader of a word's
   author given its case, a forged one unattributed and never *You*; handed in its own session only, as the supervisor's choice on the
   owner's behalf, never `chose:` alone; the note's coded line, the ledger and the log saying so. Shown on the session under *From the
   supervisor, on your behalf*, as a notice in *Notifications*, and on the question card; a held decision is not a wait on the owner.
8. **The overrule** (§6.3): the decision is held five minutes, the record held whatever else waits, the owner's own words superseding it;
   **Overrule**, a `Person` route, drops it while held and takes the word back after release while the record is parked, and the question
   stands for the owner. Once the record runs, the owner's correction is their own words.
9. **Its place** (§7): the owner's delegate for questions asked of the owner, not a co-worker, not a lead's helper and not a manager of
   sessions; Ask Daoris may explain it and never appoints or overrules it. Its cost, held by the actor: one turn per question it looks at,
   one attempt per question, only in appointed repositories, measured on the question bench before the actor is offered.
10. **The question card** (QUESTIONUI1, §8): one molecule in the box and the head's card; each choice a radio row that reads whole (label,
    what the agent does on it, the recommendation and its why), its accessible name the label and *recommended*; nothing preselected; no
    second field: the box's own draft and *Send* carry the selected choice, the selection the frame's and tied to the question's id; keyboard
    and screen reader by native radios; the supervisor's pick, reasons and overrule with `Remaining` where it answered; en and zh, both
    themes, the three widths, stories of its own.

**Why.** The owner's attention is spent where only they decide (D37, D193 point 1), and of the 46 items that reached the owner in D135's
evidence, 21 did not need them: the ticket, the code, a look, the repository's documents or Daoris's own mechanics settled them
(`docs/2026-10-03-knowledge-use-evidence.md`, finding 2). The steward already settles them by hand, recorded either as the owner's words or as a
card the owner must press. A role recorded as itself keeps the owner's words theirs, the decision traceable to who made it and why, and
the owner's overrule real. Building it on D201 reuses a judge, a record and a caller look already built and reviewed; answering only with
the session's own offered choices keeps what that look cannot tell apart from bounded by the act's shape.

**Rejected** (design §12): the supervisor's answer as the owner's words; a card per answer; a machine-wide press with no repository; no
overrule window, or one of 0; keeping the word on the record during the hold; an overrule after the session acted; a model judging whether
a question is the owner's; preselecting the recommendation; a second answer field in the card; a separate roles file now; a holder-bound
credential; a separate count for the supervisor; words in the first row; the supervisor answering go-aheads; carrying its answer into a new
session.
