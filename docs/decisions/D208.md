## D208 — The owner appoints roles that act on their behalf, each act recorded as the role's and overruled by them; the supervisor is the first, answering a session's recorded question per repository, held by the steward from a terminal until its actor runs; and the question card reads each choice whole (2026-10-24)

**Decision (SUPERVISOR1, QUESTIONUI1), proposed.** **Status: proposed**, round 1, written by Claude Opus at `478e6010b`, for Codex's read
(D191 point 1) and an independent Opus reader's foundation read (D191's amendment of 2026-10-23), since it changes D156's person's door,
D201's judge and record, the pipe census's classes and the session record's authorship. The contract is
`docs/2026-10-24-supervisor-design.md`. The direction (the owner, 2026-10-24, through the quest that asked for this design): a session's
question needs a better option selection, and a supervisor decides among the options and replies as part of the workflow, continuing the
co-work and company frame; until it is built the steward plays the role. The frame is D193's: the person is the owner and gives roles; a
role acts on the owner's behalf within its scope, by its holder's own decision; the one grant in the window is the appointment, not a
confirmation of each act; each act is recorded as the role's, never as the owner's words, so the owner reads who decided and overrules.

What was read (design §0). A session's question is recorded by `question_ask` and answered by a press through `SESSION_INPUT`, kept as
the person's words and handed as `chose: <label>`. A word is the person's by having no author (`SaidWord.Persons`), and `SaidFrom` names
only Daoris and a passed-on agent. A terminal's answer is `sessions say`, which the census makes the person's, so it waits for the
window's card and, confirmed, is the owner's words. D201's judge covers four management presses, never words, and is not offered yet. The
card draws each choice as a bare button with its recommendation outside its accessible name, and neither `ParkAnswer` nor
`AwaitingPerson` has stories of its own.

1. **Roles** (design §2). The owner appoints a role in the window with their own press, per scope, off by default; no verb, Ask Daoris
   door, card or grant appoints one. A role's acts are named routes in exact forms; its holder decides each; an act it covers runs with
   no card and one it does not is refused, never turned into a card. Each act carries the role's mark, never the owner's absence of an
   author, is said where the owner reads it, and is overruled by the owner's press while it has not taken effect. No role decides what
   is the owner's (D191 point 3, D193 point 1). Revoked at once; this machine alone.
2. **The holder** (§2.2): `terminal`, an agent the owner runs by hand outside Daoris (the steward), its acts over the pipe through D201's
   caller look; or `actor`, Daoris's own actor for the role (D205), its acts recorded by Daoris's own code. Named on each act's record.
3. **Where an appointment lives** (§2.3): `appointments` in D201's record, `data/standing-confirmation.json`, schema 1, additive, read
   and judged under D201's lock and machine binding; written only by `SET_APPOINTMENT`, `Window` in the census; offered only once D201's
   grant is (`OfferedInThisBuild`). The presses and the appointments are separate ticks.
4. **The supervisor** (主管, §3): the first role, per repository (a workspace later). It answers a parked session's recorded question with
   one offered choice, words, or both, and one to five reasons, each naming its source (D135 point 4), through `SUPERVISOR_ANSWER`, a
   new census class `Role`; or leaves it to the owner with a bound and its reasons (`SUPERVISOR_DEFER`). It is handed the question and
   its choices, the session's evidence, the work order with the owner's quoted words and the standing answers, and the records.
5. **Its bounds** (§4): never a go-ahead (refused while one on the ask is open), a sign-in, an access widening, spending, a deletion of
   the owner's data, the owner's own preference, a question the records do not answer, or an answer that contradicts a decision. Daoris
   holds the structural ones and an agent's `owner` mark on the question; the holder defers the rest; the owner overrules. Such a
   question waits for the owner as today, with the supervisor's why where it looked. A line goes on by the supervisor's answers alone at
   most three times.
6. **Recorded and said as its** (§6): a word `From: supervisor`, never `Persons`; its event carrying the choice, the holder and the
   reasons; handed to the session as the supervisor's answer on the owner's behalf, never `chose:` alone; carried only in its own
   session; the ledger's note and the log saying so. Shown on the session under *From the supervisor, on your behalf*, as a notice in
   *Notifications*, and on the question card.
7. **The overrule** (§6.3): the answer is held five minutes before the park goes on; **Overrule**, a `Person` route, takes it back through
   the service's take-back while the record is parked, and the question stands for the owner. Once the record runs, the owner's
   correction is their own words.
8. **Its place** (§7): the owner's delegate for questions asked of the owner, not a co-worker, not a lead's helper and not a manager of
   sessions; Ask Daoris may explain it and never appoints or overrules it. Its cost, held by the actor: one turn per answered question,
   only in appointed repositories, measured on the question bench before the actor is offered.
9. **The question card** (QUESTIONUI1, §8): one molecule in the box and the head's card; each choice a radio row that reads whole (label,
   what the agent does on it, the recommendation and its why); nothing preselected; one *Send* with words added if wanted; keyboard and
   screen reader by native radios; the supervisor's pick, reasons and overrule shown where it answered; en and zh, both themes, the three
   widths, stories of its own.

**Why.** The owner's attention is spent where only they decide (D37, D193 point 1), and of the 46 items that reached the owner in D135's
evidence, 21 did not need them: the ticket, the code, a look, the repository's documents or Daoris's own mechanics settled them
(`docs/2026-10-03-knowledge-use-evidence.md`, finding 2). The steward already settles them by hand, recorded either as the owner's words or as a
card the owner must press. A role recorded as itself keeps the owner's words theirs, the decision traceable to who made it and why, and
the owner's overrule real. Building it on D201 reuses a judge, a record and a caller look already built and reviewed.

**Rejected** (design §12): the supervisor's answer as the owner's words; a card per answer; a machine-wide press with no repository; no
overrule window; an overrule after the session acted; a model judging whether a question is the owner's; preselecting the
recommendation; a separate roles file now; the supervisor answering go-aheads; carrying its answer into a new session.
