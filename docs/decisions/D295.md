## D295 — A declared ceiling may fail; the brief's does, and the region is held in words (2026-11-15)

**Decision (BRIEF1).** Three rules for the sizes `daoris check` measures (area `doctrine-and-cli.md`, rule 21):

1. **A ceiling declared `"fails": true` fails `check`;** one without it reports, as before. This repository declares the
   brief's (`documents.brief`: 1,300 words and 8,600 bytes) and the region's (`coreBudget`: 780 words) failing.
2. **The brief's ceiling measures the whole root instruction file,** its doctrine region included, in words and in bytes,
   because every session pays for both together.
3. **The region's ceiling is in words (`coreBudget.words`), and `coreBudgetBytes` is refused** with the line that replaces
   it: one number per thing, and a retired ceiling silently ignored would be one nobody knows has stopped applying.

**Rejected:** D54's "a fact gates, a judgement reports" for every size. Under it, 12 of 13 area documents and the backlog
stood over their ceilings and nothing pulled them back. What stays of D54 is that a size is a judgement: a repository
chooses which ceilings to hold, and a document already over one is held where it stands (it may shrink and may not
grow), which is a fact. Also rejected: a separate existence check for the brief (`tools/doc-current.mjs` already resolves
the brief's paths and names, and now fails on them) and keeping `coreBudgetBytes` beside a words ceiling.
