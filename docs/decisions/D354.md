## D354 — One risk policy and one judge decide every hold: an act's class at its place says pass, a role's yes or the owner's, and only a change to the policy itself is fixed at the owner (2026-11-20)

**Decision (RISKGATE1; the owner's direction of 2026-11-20, designed through the brainstorm `riskgate-1120`, Fable and Sol; amending
D284 point 6's approver, D305's authority record, D312's and D318's refusal of an agent at a step a press settles, D350's acceptance
and GATEACCEPT2's acceptance by access mode (D353), and roles rule 49a's levels).**

1. **Only risk is managed.** Every hold, approval, acceptance and *waits on you* in Daoris, at every node and for every actor (the
   person, an agent, a plugin's actor), is one question to one judge: does this act, at this place, need a yes, and whose? Nothing else
   makes work wait on a yes: not a node's kind, an access pause or a door that is person-only by construction. Where the policy
   declares no risk there is no gate, and where nothing is configured nothing is restricted. Work still waits on what it needs (a
   check's verdict, evidence not yet in, a provider's own approval): those are dependencies, never gates, and a pass lifts only the
   authorization. A sign-in needs a person in a browser; that is a physical act no setting grants or withholds, not a policy gate.

2. **The shape: a closed census of act kinds, each with one class at a place.** A kind is named by the door or tool the act comes
   through (a node's kind, a plugin command's declared kind, the git door, a settings door), never by an actor's words. The classes are
   the owner's three levels: `none` (no approval: the act passes, recorded), `role` (not high: a role answers, an agent by default, the
   row's `by` or else the place's supervisor role, never the act's author) and `owner` (high: the owner's own approval). The seed:

   | Act kind | Covers | Seed class |
   |---|---|---|
   | `work.done` | a step's done, an unchecked done (`pass` at `quest.holds`), a quest's departure from its brief, an evidence hold | `none` |
   | `work.accept` | a review's Accept of a work (`accept`) | `none` |
   | `task.approve` | an `approval` step's approve or reject | `none` |
   | `work.recover` | a retry, done outside, a side chosen, a vacancy filled, a held or left attempt (a check red past its rounds, which with no holder reaches the owner's final exit, point 4), a skip of `content.holds` or `work.requests` | `none` |
   | `line.land` | a merge to the repository's line | `none` |
   | `remote.push` | a push to a remote the repository declares | `none` |
   | `release.start` | a release stage; a row may name one destination (`release.start/production`) | `none` |
   | `publish.outside` | publishing beyond what the repository declares | `none` |
   | `spend.money` | a cap raised, a top-up, a budget set | `none` |
   | `direction.set` | a target or a direction set or changed | `none` |
   | `check.narrow` | a check's reach narrowed: paths or test files dropped (D355) | `none`, said in one line |
   | `reach.widen` | an act outside the harness's reach: a held permission, a prompt | `role` under restricted access (the place's approver, D284), `none` under full |
   | `command.change` | what a repository's checks or a tree run (a declaration unit's digest, D355) | `owner` under restricted access, `none` under full |
   | `agent.version` | a move of an agent's managed version: `daoris agent pin` and `update`, the machine's or a workspace's (AGENTPINDOOR1) | `none` |
   | `history.rewrite` | a rewrite of published history | `owner` |
   | `data.delete` | deleting the owner's data | `owner` |
   | `policy.change` | this policy's rows, a cast or role that grants an act, an access mode, a grant of what agents may do unasked | `owner`, fixed |

   The owner may change any seed but `policy.change`. The list the doctrine names as the owner's (direction, money, sign-ins and
   credentials, marked releases, publishing outward, history rewrites, deleting their data) is a set of defaults, not a fixed list:
   accounts, their choice and switching, and budgets are Daoris's to run; a release is the owner's only where a place's row says so.
   The doctrine's rules 1 and 3 are restated to read the policy by RISKGATE1j; until then they stand as written.

3. **One record, resolved narrowest first.** The policy is `risk` in the home's `driver.json`, replacing `placeRisk`: rows keyed by
   place (`machine`, `workspace/<name>`, `repository/<name>`), each `kind[/qualifier]` to a class, with an optional `by` role and an
   optional access mode. For an act the narrowest place with a row for its kind decides (repository, workspace, machine, then the seed);
   at one place a qualified row stands over its kind's, and a row keyed by a mode applies under that mode. A repository keeps no tracked
   copy: a raise it wants is a proposed `policy.change`. An absent record reads as the seed; an unreadable one reads every act as
   `owner` and says its fault once, since a broken governing record is the owner's to mend; a class a build does not know reads `owner`,
   so an older build never loosens a newer record. The census is closed at build time: a door naming a kind outside it fails the census
   test, so no unknown kind reaches the judge. Configuration is carried and code is not: a place's `high` reads as every kind at
   `owner` there, `low` and `medium` as no rows, a mark (`release.production`) as its destination's row at `owner`, a `line` mark stays
   the cast's; a hold that is person-only only in code becomes no row, it goes. The older reader of `placeRisk` ends when no home on a
   supported update path holds one (`compatible-builds`).

4. **One evaluation.** The judge is model-free and pure: act, actor, place and subject give `pass`, `gate` on a role, or `owner`, with
   the rule that decided (the place's row or the seed, at its revision) and a reason. It is asked where the hold would open and again
   where it would release, so a policy changed between them is read again (a raise opens a gate, a lowering releases one, both
   recorded). The service's holds (`quest.holds`, a departure) take the driver's verdict as a record; the service has no judge of its
   own. A gate names a role, never a person: the place's cast and the reporting line fill it, a leave climbs, and the person holds it
   only where they cast themselves or set a `line` mark. A gate with no holder but the author climbs the line. Nothing waits on the owner
   unless the policy classes the act `owner`, and the owner holds no role. Where every holder has failed (today the step's role holder and
   the vacancy path), the owner is the **final exit**: Daoris sends one notice carrying the whole story (what it is, who held it and for
   how long, why each could not, and the one answer the owner gives), and the item then waits in *What needs you* until the owner looks.
   There is no give-up in place of the notice, no silent give-up after it, and no second notice.

5. **No worker approves its own work.** Where a gate exists, the door that takes the yes (`SettleDoor.Admit`, the decision door) reads
   the answer's author from its channel, proved by its session key, never from its words, and refuses an author in the subject's maker
   lineage (its working sessions, their continuations, the helpers they started) as `own-work`, in every class, place and mode. An
   answer from the author's family is recorded as not independent and stands. Where no gate exists nothing is approved: the record says
   the act passed with no risk declared, attributed to the policy, and a worker's done stays a report. The two agent reviews before
   landing are workflow work, not holds, and stay as they are. D312 refused an agent at a step a press settles because no agent's
   channel spoke a press; D318's `step_settle` gives the agent a proved channel, and `own-work` is the safety the person-only rule
   stood in for.

6. **The governance root is the one fixed rule.** A `policy.change` (a row of this policy, a cast or role that grants an act, an
   access mode, a grant of what agents may do unasked: whatever decides who holds power) takes effect only on the owner's own approval,
   given in the owner's own channel: a press in the window (`PipeOwnerOnly`), the CLI under the person's key, or Daoris's assistant,
   which proposes the change (before and after, and the waits it opens or releases) and applies it when the owner confirms it in the
   assistant's conversation by their own turn, bound to that proposal's id and digest. An agent's words, a quote an agent relays, a
   commit or a file saying "approved" never count, and a keyless process is refused.

7. **Every verdict is recorded.** Each verdict is one `risk.judged` line on its run (time, act and qualifier, subject and its pin,
   actor, place, verdict, rule and revision, the answer's author once answered), a pass as fully as a gate, written per hold and
   release, never per tool call. Each policy change is appended to the home's change journal (who, door, before, after, the confirming
   turn). Measured from these: the owner's asks by kind (every one among the kinds classed `owner`), gates on acts nothing configured
   (none), self-approvals (none), time on gates, vacant gates, and regretted passes (a reverted landing, a red line, by kind), each
   regretted kind becoming one raise the assistant proposes.

8. **Shown and changed where risk already lives.** The place's *Risk and your marks* row becomes the act table: per kind its class
   there, where its rule comes from, who answers by the cast, and seven days of passes and gates, `policy.change` shown locked, and the
   waits a change would open or release previewed before it is saved. `daoris settings risk` prints the resolved table, `set <place>
   <kind>[/<qualifier>] none|role|owner [--by <role>]` writes a row at the revision read, and `why <run> [<step>]` runs the same judge
   dry. Each wait says its rule. Labels translate through both catalogues; kind ids stay as written. A set an agent sends is a proposal
   (point 6).

9. **The access mode is reach.** Restricted or full says what a harness can touch; whether an act needs a yes is the judge's. The mode
   enters the policy only through rows keyed by it, the seed's `reach.widen` and `command.change`, which the owner changes like any
   other row. GATEACCEPT2's acceptance by mode becomes the seed `command.change`: `none` under full access, `owner` under restricted.

10. **First in shadow, then one hold per change.** The judge and its record land first in shadow: the verdict it would give is
    journaled beside every hold today's gates open and release, deciding nothing, and its disagreements over days of real runs are
    counted. Then each change moves one hold, at every door it has (window, CLI, MCP, the service), to the judge, and deletes its
    hard-coded holder in the same change; a census lists every hold and fails where one names a holder outside the judge, its allowance
    falling by one per change. The `own-work` refusal lands with the first move, before any seed takes effect. The order, by how often
    each waits for nothing: the unchecked done; a departure and the evidence holds; a review's Accept; a task's approval; a
    declaration's acceptance; go-aheads, parks and held permissions (`AccessRules`' pauses, `GoAheadAuthority`'s refusals, the spend
    census mapped to kinds); the recovery waits and skips. HOLDSACTOR1's step-role holder of the unchecked done, HOLDSACTOR2's agent
    holders of the Accept, the approval and the departure, and GATEACCEPT1's and GATEACCEPT2's acceptance by field and by mode are
    interim forms this migration replaces. A wait a move released, and work that went on under a pass, stay as they are; an old
    approval is no authority for a changed target.

**Why.** Four things each decided a hold and none knew the others: the node's kind (an Accept or an approval person-only), the access
pauses, the place's level and marks, and the spend census. Several holds stood on the person by construction, so runs waited on presses
for acts that carried no risk, and changing any of them needed code. One record classed by act and read by one judge gives one answer to
"why did this wait" and "why did this not". The one thing every setting must leave out of reach is the setting itself: an actor that
could change who approves could approve anything, so `policy.change` alone is fixed, and its door takes the owner's word in the owner's
channel. A free default is safe only with the identity refusal and the verdict record in place, which is why both land before a seed
takes effect.

**Rejected.**

- *Per-node hard-coded gates* (today's): each node kind, door and pause with its own holder keeps waits nobody configured, cannot be
  changed without code, and gives four answers to one question.
- *The owner's list as fixed rows* (Fable's eight owner kinds that cannot change, the synthesis's seven): it was this owner's default,
  not a law; it would keep waits on releases, money and accounts that Daoris runs, and the policy would no longer be the one setting.
- *The access mode as the approval switch* (GATEACCEPT2's form, and the mode granting standing authority): a home switched to full
  would lose every gate at once and a restricted one would wait on acts that carry no risk; reach and authority are read apart.
- *Owner rows that cannot be lowered, a place widening only by declaring* (Fable): a locked row is a hard-coded permission again, and
  widening by declaration beside a row is two ways to say one thing.
- *Levels low, medium and high* (the place-wide level of `placeRisk`, or a graded scale per act): a place's level cannot say which act,
  and `medium` names no holder; three classes say exactly who answers: nobody, a role, the owner.
- *A tracked risk record in the repository* (Fable's open choice): a second source a branch could write; a grant of power is the
  owner's to give through their own door.
- *A vacant gate falling to the owner at once*: it asks the owner a thing that is not theirs while a holder may still take it; only a
  vacancy past its bound reaches the owner, as the final exit.
- *Every hold moved in one cut-over*: the waits released at once could not be taken back, and no step could be reverted alone.
