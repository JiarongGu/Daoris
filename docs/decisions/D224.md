## D224 — Every graph Daoris draws stands on one canvas: a viewport over a measured or positioned world, never moved by hand (2026-11-02)

**Decision (CANVAS1, the owner's direction of 2026-11-02; co-designed under D191, accepted by the steward 2026-11-02).** Per
`docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md`. One canvas platform carries the workflow (its chart, editor, run and a proposal's diff), the
agents' structure and the display-only maps: a viewport with pan and zoom (plain wheel pans only while the world overflows, Ctrl+wheel
and pinch zoom about the pointer, drag pans, Shift+1/2/0 fit, reveal and actual size), layers whose overlays never change layout,
selection, find, a minimap only past twice the viewport, and a list or rail below a 36rem floor. A placed world is HTML laid out by
CSS and measured (the workflow, the structure); a positioned world has coordinates computed ahead (the maps), culled and drawn by
tiers at size. No feature moves nodes or saves positions. The structure is drawn as bands, one per place; the reference map keeps its
layered layout in workspace blocks; the code map is clustered by folder. The engine is chosen by measurement in the first row:
extending Daoris's measured layout is the default, and React Flow is taken only if it passes every blocking contract the extension
fails; one engine serves every feature.

**Why.** The workflow editor, the structure and the maps each need to be read and worked at sizes a fixed-size flow cannot show, and
the owner wants one proper canvas under all of them. Both sealed proposals put the measured comparison first; they differed only on
its likely outcome.

**Rejected.** Two engines chosen per feature. Free placement and saved coordinates (a workflow and a structure are governed records,
the maps a display). Cytoscape's canvas-drawn nodes for anything a person reads or edits, elkjs on size and licence, dagre on D186's
table, d3-zoom's listeners beside the window's zoom. sigma.js stays the one named exit, for the code map only, on a measured failure
and a verified GPU in the installed window.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (CANVAS1a, built): the extension locked, and the workflow's chart and editor in its viewport.** Both candidates were
measured on the same scenes in Chromium (1600 × 1000, the dev server): the extension as built (`measured/canvas.spec.ts`), and
`@xyflow/react` 12.12.0 adapted whole in gitignored scratch, never imported by `src/` (`_fixtures/canvas-spike/`, removed after): Daoris's
placement measured in a hidden pass and handed over as coordinates never stored, the chart's own nodes, Daoris's routes, labels and ＋ in
a viewport portal, React Flow's mutations, node focus and keys off, and the walk, pick mode, reveal and port drag the adapter's. Each
bundle target is its candidate's own; every other contract is scored alike. React Flow fails three contracts the extension passes and
passes none it fails, so the extension stays and serves every feature. A frame's figure is the p95 interval between frames of a
scripted pan and zoom: under one and a half display intervals is 95 frames in 100 made in time, and headless vsync jitter puts a
frame made in time at 17-19 ms.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (CANVAS1b, built): selection, marks and the connection line.** *Selection.* A press on a step selects it, in the
editor as before and now on a place's chart, the tab's and a run's too, as an outline (one, released by the ground and by Ctrl on it).
In the editor Ctrl toggles and Shift adds a step to the selection, and a Shift+drag box on the ground chooses what it touches in reading
order; the first is the primary, the inspector's, and **Copy alone takes them all** (the draft keeps `chosen` beside `selected` and drops
it when the primary moves). A ground press that does not travel is the feature's: nothing in the editor, the release in a chart read,
and in pick mode **open ground to choose**, which is new (before it was only the release of a dragged line). *Marks.* A node carries
`marks` (`FlowMark`: a word and one of refused, new, remove, changed); the canvas places a chip for each (`canvas/marks.ts`): above the
node's right end, else its left, else below, else straddling its top edge like a tag, the first place that clears every node, divider,
label, ＋, chip placed before it and point of ink, and not shown where none does (its words stay in the node's description and the head's
*To put right*). In the rail a chip stands under its step. A refused step wears declined's outline as an inset ring over a one-pixel
border, as the current step does; **no state changes a border's width any more** (`NodeFrame`, `design/Flow.tsx`), and the sentence that
was a line inside the step is in its description alone. A refused way's sentence was appended to its source's foot, which moved the ranks
below (Review grew from 93 to 108 px); it is now `FlowEdge.note`, said in the source's description and drawn nowhere. Measured:
every node's box equals with and without marks (`canvas/Plain12` against `canvas/Marked`) and with and without the driver's refusals
(`judgement/CouldNotBeJudged` against `judgement/Refused`); every chip shown clears every node, label and ＋ at 80 and 39rem, English and
中文. *The connection line* is the canvas's (`canvas/useConnection.tsx`), not the chart's: a press on a port begins the way as before, a
drag past 3 px draws a rubber line from inside the port's box (the box's middle, a quarter up from its foot: at 80, 100, 150 and 200% the
line starts inside it, ends at the pointer, and the port stands where it was pressed) and outlines the step under the pointer where
`pick.can` allows; the view pans at 32 px from the viewport's edge and stops when the pointer does; Escape (heard before the chart's own
keys), a cancelled pointer and a lost capture give the way up once, and a lost capture after a release gives nothing. The view is the
person's while a port is pressed: the focus pick mode moves is followed when the press ends, if it was no drag (before, the first press
panned the chart 292 px under the pointer). Pressable edges take `onEdge` and a 14 px invisible stroke under the nodes (the workflow's
edges stay inert; the maps use it). The editor's inspector stands beside the chart where the region holds 64rem **of the root's size**,
not 1,024 px, so at the window's 200% the chart keeps its room and the inspector opens as a drawer. The figures are
`measured/canvas.spec.ts`'s new tests (marks, chips and the line at four scales).

**Note, 2026-11-02 (CANVAS1c, built): the structure as bands.** `agents/roles/structureBands.ts` lays the diagram out as one band per
place in tree order (depth 0 this machine, 1 a workspace, 2 a repository that sets something of its own), the roles and appointments
placed there in it, and the agents once each beside them ordered by the mean band of the roles leaning on them; a role placed in two
places is drawn in both under one identity (the first occurrence keyed by the node, a later one `<node>@<band>`), an appointment per
place it applies to, and an offered role nobody holds is named in the first band. Only staffed edges are drawn, from each occurrence;
*placed* and *appointed* are what the band says and stay in each node's description and the list. Past 24 nodes each workspace folds to
one band, with one edge from it to each agent, counted (`groupFolds`, unchanged); opening one reads nothing more. `StructureCanvas`
(with `StructureNode` and `structureWords.ts`) draws it in the canvas from 36rem of the region (the label beside the roles from 40rem:
12 + 12 + 4 + 12), routes the edges with `routeInk` over a two-rank flow built for them, and is the list below 36rem and wherever nothing
measures; `RoleDiagram`, `diagram.ts` and their test are gone, and `DiagramSection`'s API is kept. **`routeInk` holds for bands**,
measured over every story at 80, 39 and 25rem in both languages and themes: no staffed edge through any card or label, none over another
label, and the fixture's ten choices cross six times (the table in `measured/roles.spec.ts`); the folded bands cross none, and a hundred
places and a hundred roles drawn whole (124 nodes, 61 paths) cross none and collide with none. At 39rem the region holds the floor, so the
structure is drawn there with the label above its roles and the detail below, where the columns were the list. Reading order is now each
band's label then its roles, then the agents. Left to later rows or the steward: the release of the selection by the ground (the page's
`onSelect` takes an id), a minimap, and the window's own checks (row g).

**Note, 2026-11-02 (CANVAS1d, built): large graphs.** `design/canvas/PositionedCanvas` takes a scene (nodes with a title and an optional
group, edges with an optional kind) and draws it in the canvas; no feature uses it yet (rows e and f). *Layout* (`layoutScene.ts`) is
pure: each group is layered alone (ranks by longest path with back edges left out, two barycentre sweeps, a rank wider than 12 wrapping),
groups are packed in shelves at sizes their counts predict, and the edges between groups are summed by direction and kind with every
member's id. `layoutClient.ts` runs it inline below 8,000 nodes plus edges and in a module worker above (about 50 ms here), every
request under a revision; an answer for an older one is dropped, a cancel ends the worker, a worker that cannot run lays out inline, and
the drawing keeps the scene it was laid out for until the next layout arrives. *Cull and tiers*: `spatialGrid.ts` buckets boxes by 256 px
cells; past 400 nodes only the viewport grown by half on each side is mounted, plus the focused, the selected and the find's match
(`aria-setsize`, `aria-posinset` on every item mounted, a key walking to the nearest node on its side in the grid whether mounted or
not, Home and End to the ends). The tier follows `text-meta`'s effective size, 11 px × root/16 × k: 9 px full, 4 px chip, below it a dot
on a canvas2D layer; the budget drops it when more than 250 nodes (full) or 1,500 (chip), or 2,000 paths, are in view, so a hundred
nodes fully drawn is a hundred, and at the zoom floor of 10% a whole world is dots. A chip's word is counter-scaled and shown only where
its width clears the box. A grouped world past 1,000 nodes is an overview, a card per group and the lines between groups counted (the
heaviest 2,000 drawn, the heaviest 150 with their count, each direction and kind in a slot of its own), one group opened at a time
(*Fold again* closes it); its nodes then draw at their places and the other groups stay cards. *Find* (from 40 nodes) searches every
node's title, says *n of m*, lists the matches by name, steps on Enter and Shift+Enter with the focus in the field, opens the group,
selects and shows the node at a readable scale, ↓ hands the focus to the match, Escape clears and puts the view back. *The minimap*
stands past twice the viewport either way (the world at 100% against the viewport, so a zoom does not make it come and go), from the
36rem floor, switched in the sizing menu and remembered (`daoris.canvas.minimap`); its rectangle is the view and a press moves it.
*The viewer's place* is `daoris.canvas.<feature>.<key>` (`viewMemory.ts`, in a try/catch): the same graph identity (a hash of the ids
and the caller's `version`, such as a fed commit) gives the view back exactly, a changed one the surviving anchor's screen place, else
its group's, else nothing, and `onGone` says the selection is no longer there. Below the floor, or on *Show as list*, the graph is
`CanvasList`, grouped, a group opening on a press and only an open group's items drawn.

*Measured* (`measured/large.spec.ts`, Chromium, 1600 × 1000, a busy developer machine, so the figures vary by a frame between runs; the
last full run): 100 nodes first usable 298 ms, 100 in the DOM, p95 frame 21.0 ms; 1,000 nodes 319 ms, 130 in the DOM, p95 35.6 ms (one
of three runs; the others 31 and 36); 5,000 in fifty groups 293 ms, 50 cards, p95 21.4 ms; 10,000 nodes and 50,000 edges in a hundred
groups 378 ms, 100 cards, p95 22.4 ms (the first fixture, whose cross-group edges went to random groups, made 7,000 lines and p95 45 ms
with 60 ms frames; modules lean on near ones, so the fixture does and the lines are capped regardless). No long frame at 5,000 and
10,000; the 1,000 case, culled with chips, is the one over the 33 ms line a run in three and is the next thing to tune (the mounted
chips rebuild on each snapped step). `layoutScene` lays 10,000 nodes and 50,000 edges out in under 300 ms in Node. The tiers match the
rule at window zoom 50, 100 and 200%; the minimap's rectangle equals the view within 2 world units before and after a press; a key
(End) reaches node 1,000 of 1,000 with every scroll at 0; find reaches a culled node and keeps the focus in the field; twenty opens
and closes of the 5,000 world leave the element count and the worker count where they began (a worker was made each time) and at rest
no animation frame is asked for in a second. Left to later rows: the member list of an aggregated line (the data is in the layout), the
bundle size of the added files, a node's own component beyond the default card (rows e and f bring their own `renderNode`), and the
installed window's wheel and pinch cadence (row g).

**Note, 2026-11-03 (CANVAS1e, built): a positioned world the feature supplies.** `SuppliedCanvas` takes `world` (`frame`, `boxes`,
`order`, `lines` with their two ends, `statics`, `groups`), `renderNode(id, {tier, selected, dim})` and `renderEdges({tier, selected,
hovered, line(id)})`, `zoom` (a range or `'off'`, fitted), `opening` and `selected`/`onSelect`; every card and line is a stop (→ line →
card, ← back, ↑↓ cycle). The map is its first use; `ONEWAY1aa` and the code map take it next.
