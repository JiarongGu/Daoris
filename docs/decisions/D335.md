## D335 — Notifications retire as a view; each of its parts has a home, and the computer announces only counted waits (2026-11-20)

**Decision (UISH12, the window redesign `docs/2026-11-19-window-design.md` §5.6 and §5.7; superseding D196).**

1. The *Notifications* view and the bell that opens it go. *What needs you* becomes Overview's *Waiting on you* (the owner's kinds)
   and *Kept off your desk* (the agents'); a terminal's press becomes a *Confirm it was you* wait (D156's door unchanged: no answer
   without the person key); *Messages* become unread marks where they belong and a *Since you left* line (the reads kept);
   *Done and failed* becomes Failed waits and *Since you left* lines; the steward's, Ask Daoris's and the terminal's lines become
   *Since you left* lines with their acts; *Set aside* becomes *Later*.
2. The computer is told only of counted waits that hold work, batched and held in the owner's quiet hours; a notification opens
   the wait on Overview in the main window that holds its scope, and never answers. *Notifications* remains only as the Settings
   domain of these announcements.
3. `daoris notices` becomes `daoris waits` (`--all`), its JSON the same rows.

**Why.** The owner, 2026-11-19: much of the view is obsolete, and most of its rows are not the owner's to act on. A wait shown in
the view and on Overview is one job in two places; the view's other groups each belong where their object is.

**Rejected.**

- *Keeping the view for messages and outcomes only*: a second inbox beside Overview's *Since you left*, read by no one.
- *Announcing every notice*: noise the owner would silence, losing the waits with it.
