## D368 — A session's state is a fold of its witnessed facts, every command answers in every state, and stillness is shown and escalated (2026-11-28)

**Decision (SESSIONLIFE2, under D293, D323 and D231; the owner's direction: a session shown in progress for two days
is a defect, hours without movement must be visible, and a structure whose defects keep returning is rebuilt rather
than patched while the product is unreleased).** Evidence on the install (0.0.39): session `dbe9415a` showed
*working*, reason `awaiting-step`, for 1 day 23 hours with no run, no work and no quest behind it, and no command
could end it: `session stop` answered that nothing on this machine runs it, `session finish` that it does not wait on
the person, `session archive` that it is still running and must be stopped first. A census of main at `f98dca5a6d`
found ten stored states; 67 sites in 32 files that write a session's state; the ended and active sets spelled in
seven places; three classifiers for a stop; the shown state derived twice (the driver and the web); four put-backs
that restore an earlier state verbatim; every ender finding a session's run only through its note, so a wait whose
note names no run has no ender and no verb; and no test that walks a state × command table. The fix log holds 39
entries and the archive about 120 rows on a session's end or state. The fresh-design brainstorm `sessionlife-1128`
(Fable 5.2 and DeepSeek, each from a purpose brief alone, no repository) reached the same shape in all eight parts;
its condition for evolving instead ("the status is written in three or fewer places") fails at 67, so the lifecycle
core is rebuilt:

1. **One append-only journal of witnessed facts per session.** Each fact is written only by the part that witnessed
   it, stamped with the application's generation, the step and its attempt, and the time. State, holder, since-when
   and time buckets are one pure fold of (journal, clock, live leases), computed on read; no status is stored. The
   service keeps the journals and serves the fold.
2. **Four states.** *Working* (a live lease), *Waiting* (a named holder: a check slot, memory, an account, a
   reviewer, a landing, a step of its run), *Needs you* (an open question for a person or the steward), *Ended*
   (done, declined, failed or stopped, with its reason). Archived is a shelf; the list's groups are filters. Each
   tick audits one invariant: a session not ended has exactly one of a live lease, a named holder, an open question;
   a breach is *Needs you* with its facts, never *Working*. Failed is an outcome of Ended, reopened at its step by
   resume, say or hand-over.
3. **A total command table.** Stop, pause, resume, say, hand-over, finish, decline, archive and delete each answer
   *Does*, *Will* (naming the trigger) or *Already* (naming the fact) in every state; none points to another
   command. Commands are journalled intents folded by the same reducer; the table is printed from the code and
   walked by the tests. Finish never waives the checks or the second opinion (the two reviews before landing stand).
   Delete acts only where nothing is live; on a live session it answers what happens first, and a deleted session's
   facts are purged after a retention window.
4. **Stillness.** Two clocks per session, its last sign of life and its last step advance, against bounds per step
   kind read from the step times measured on this machine (the kind's 90th percentile times 1.5), with stated
   defaults where nothing is measured; only real progress moves the advance clock. From the first bound a still
   session shows since when, which step and what holds it on its row. A step with no outside effect yet (a turn
   before any push, a check, an opinion) is cut and run again once by Daoris; at the second bound the session is
   *Needs you*, to the steward first where one is cast, with the facts. Over time is twice the repository's median
   time to land.
5. **Liveness is a lease of one run of the application.** Each start voids every earlier lease, and a reconciler
   settles each open step within a minute: a turn goes on from its conversation, a check runs again, a landing is
   verified at its target (its intent journalled before it acts), or the session ends with its reason. A process
   that survived a restart is never adopted.
6. **The machine room alone starts steps** and names what holds each one: an account switched off or at its limit
   is a holder like a check slot or memory; its turns move to another enabled account or wait visibly; none enabled
   is *Needs you*.
7. **Ends are observed at the landing target,** whoever landed: a session whose work is on its target (by commit,
   by content, or by an explicit take-over link to the session that landed it) ends done by that landing. An ended
   session is archived after seven days, its unlanded changes saved as a patch before its tree is freed.
8. **Measures and tests come from the same fold.** Each second of a session falls in one time bucket (working, each
   kind of wait, checks, fixing failed checks, the reviewer, landing, on a person, still, application down), and the
   buckets sum to its wall time. One table of (facts, event or command, expected state, holder, answer) is walked by
   the tests, with crash injection after appends.

**The trial comes before the switch.** Part a runs the fold in shadow beside the stored status. Kept if: after 20
kills of the application at random moments the fold never shows a session *Working* without a live lease and every
open step is settled within a minute of the start; a week of ticks each holds the invariant or surfaces its breach;
every disagreement examined is the stored status being wrong; a list of 50 sessions folds in under 100 ms; each
session's buckets sum to its wall time. Retired if: the fold shows *Working* where nothing runs; more than one
session in ten needs a fact no part can witness; appends add more than 1% to step time; or the fact vocabulary
changes incompatibly more than twice in two weeks.

**Rejected:** a patch for `dbe9415a`'s shape (SESSIONSTILL1, held and folded here), since each earlier patch covered
one shape and left the class; evolving the stored status, written in 67 places where the route needed three or
fewer; a durable-workflow engine, a heavy dependency for a memory-limited desktop that supplies none of the holders,
the stillness bounds or the landing watch; adopting a surviving process, which needs proof it is ours for little
saved work; a delete that cuts a live session at one confirmation, since deleting data is the owner's act and must
stay recoverable.
