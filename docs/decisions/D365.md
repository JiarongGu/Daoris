## D365 — Priority is one of four classes on a quest, one comparator orders every queue, nothing in flight is cut, and a waiting item says its place (2026-11-26)

**Decision (PRIORITY1, under D349, D354 and D362; the owner's direction: jumping the queue and priority are needed, as
a company decides what goes first).** On 2026-11-26 the fix for a defect that slowed every session waited 4th of 4 for a
heavy-run slot behind ordinary rows, a session whose check had passed waited over five hours to be resumed, and the
steward's only way to reorder was pausing other quests, which cuts their checks. Brainstorm `priority-1126` asked Fable
5.2 and DeepSeek; both answered one model and differed on four points, chosen below.

1. **Four named classes on a quest:** urgent, high, normal, low, defaulting from its ask, normal where none is set. A
   class is a word a wait line can say, never a number to inflate.
2. **One comparator for every queue:** effective class, then stage (a landing, a run back at its checks, a resume, first
   checks, a new start), then first arrival, read at each look and never copied into a run. Starts and resumes, heavy-run
   admission, the landing line and second opinions sort their waiting items by it; each queue keeps only its own
   eligibility. A run back at its checks keeps its first arrival. Account ranking is not ordered by it; the actor's weight
   still picks the actor.
3. **Nothing in flight is cut.** Priority takes the next freed slot, start or line turn: a heavy run finishes, a turn
   ends, a landing candidate is passed over only before its gates begin. The lever to move other work aside is *hold
   waiting*: a quest's queued items stand aside while its running work finishes and keeps its result; lifting it returns
   them at their first arrival. Admission stays measured (D362): no slot is reserved for urgent work.
4. **Aging by promotion:** an item waiting in one queue past its class's bound (the workspace's; 2 h for normal and 8 h
   for low unless set) is ordered one class up and keeps its first arrival; nothing ages into urgent, and the wait line
   says "aged from normal".
5. **Who sets it:** any actor may set a class on an ask or a quest with a why, each change a recorded event, unless the
   workspace's risk policy classes the act `priority.set` (D354: with none, nothing waits). Urgent carries a why and an
   end, and a workspace caps how many quests hold it at once. Daoris raises nothing by judgement: only the stage rank and
   inheritance from quests recorded as waiting on this one, each shown as a reason.
6. **Every waiting item says its place:** its queue, why it cannot go now, its place and each item ahead with its class
   and reason (set by whom, stage, inherited, aged). The rows carry the sort inputs and the reader composes the place with
   the same comparator; the window and `daoris queue` read one reader, and `daoris priority <ask|quest> <class> --why`
   and one press set it.
7. **Framework and configuration** (D349): Daoris fixes the class names and order, the comparator, the stage ranks,
   kept arrival, the wait line and the no-cut rule, since heavy-run slots are the machine's and shared by its workspaces;
   a workspace sets defaults per ask kind or repository, aging bounds, the urgent cap and life, and the risk class of
   `priority.set`.

**Rejected.** A numeric rank or a credit that grows with the clock (it inflates, and a place that moves at each look
cannot be explained); per-queue priorities or weights (one priority must read the same in every queue); a reserved
emergency slot (it idles capacity outside measured admission); cutting or parking running work at checkpoints, and
cutting a re-runnable check (nothing shows a cut leaves nothing behind; leave it until a case asks); a risk-policy floor
or a Daoris raise by "unblocks others" or "near done" (nothing records either yet, and a raise with no cap and no end puts
work ahead of what the person ordered); pausing quests as the reorder tool; urgent as the person's act by default (the
steward needed it on the day, and D354 makes an act the person's only where the policy says so).

**First slice.** The urgent class on a quest, set by `daoris priority` with a why and an end, read by heavy-run admission's
waiting order alone, with kept arrival on a rejoin and the wait line for that queue: the fix of 2026-11-26 would have had
the next freed slot. Then the comparator in starts and resumes (whose stage rank also ends the five-hour resume wait),
the landing line and opinions, one queue at a time, each its own revert. Rows `PRIORITY1a` to `PRIORITY1d`.
