## D165 — Accounts switch on their own, by a curve the person may set, on each account's real use (2026-10-12)

**Decision (the owner's direction).** The owner, 2026-10-12, after a conversation opened on an account at 99% of its week
while another stood at 60% marked near, and the low-allowance offer asked them to move it by hand: *"usage should be auto
switch by some config (we can have a smart switch for default and custom curve by user if they really want to have the
control) instead what we currently have as continue with other account? which is kind confusing and we currently do have
an issue with the real usage check"*. The design, its facts and its rows are `docs/2026-10-12-account-switch-design.md`.

1. **Daoris reads each account's real use, at no model spend.** It asks the agent under that account's home for its plan's
   windows (Claude Code's `get_usage` control request, Codex's app-server read), at a start over a list, at a running
   session's turn's end where a switch is near, and on the Accounts screen; each reading records its source and age. The
   read includes the person's own use, which a session's word never did. Measured first (USAGEREAD1).
2. **Near is the curve.** Per window, the most of it an account should have used by each point of its time. `smart` (the
   default): the five-hour window flat at 90%, the week its pace plus 20 points and never above 95%. `curve`: the person's
   own points. `off`: no switch while a session runs. Claude Code's own warning, which measures pace by thresholds of its
   own, is kept and shown and is never near alone.
3. **A running session moves on its own at its turn's end** to an account of the same agent with room (ten points under its
   curve on every window), never mid-turn, and not again within thirty minutes unless refused: a quest's session by the
   carry-on, a conversation by the hand-over with its agent unchanged. The move is said on both sessions, why and where.
4. **The offer is the fallback**: where no account of the agent has room, the `handOver` rule (D163) decides another
   agent's account, and under `ask` the offer appears.

**Rejected:** switching mid-turn; a fixed percentage with no time in it; the agent's warning as near; Daoris reading the
usage endpoint with the account's credentials itself; a start made to ask, which spends allowance.

**Amends:** D125 §1.4 and D130 §5.3 (a read that spends nothing is made); D130 §6, §16.3 steps 2 and 5 and §16.6 (`early`
and `near` become `switch` and `curve`, read on); D163's *automatic hand-overs by default* rejection, for another account
of the same agent; TOOL6d, superseded.
