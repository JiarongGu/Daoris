## D283 — A changed declaration is accepted by the repository's access mode: as it lands in full access, by one press in restricted mode, said as checks added, removed and changed (2026-11-12)

**Decision (GATESACCEPT1).** The Daoris repository's `daoris.gates.json` changed on its own line (a `comments` check and a
`driver-process-git-pinned` suite added, a path added to the tools check) and the window asked the person to accept it. They found
it "really bad", the message and the state confusing: the one file was accepted twice (for landed verification and for integration),
each with its own record, tick, standing and withdrawal, then one confirmation applied the ticked ones; every standing named a blob
id, "the declaration's `integration` object" and "accepted as none", and none said which checks changed; after the press the row
stayed in *What needs you* and in `daoris notices` until the next look; the terminal's `notices` listed six waits for the person's
word while the window's Notifications listed three; and `daoris integrate` had no verb to accept a declaration. The rule:

1. **Accepted by mode.** The mode is the repository's resolved access (`AccessRules.Resolve(...).Full`, the place D277 reads for a
   press Ask Daoris may take). In full access a repository's line is the person's own, what lands there passed its landing, so the
   declaration the line commits is accepted as it lands: no row, no press, and the look says it in one sentence in the activity
   ("engine's checks changed on main: added `comments`; they run from now on."). In restricted mode a changed declaration waits
   for the person. The rejected alternative is every change waiting for a press in every mode, which asked the person to confirm what
   their own line committed under full access. The accepted blob records stay as they were (`integrationGates`, the landed route's
   `gates`), so a change of mode needs no migration.
2. **One acceptance, one press.** Where a press is still asked, one row and one press accept the line's declaration for every purpose
   it serves (`DeclarationAcceptance`: the integration's blob and, where the landed route verifies, its gates), with one withdrawal.
   The per-purpose ticks, the confirm-all step and the landed route's own *Accept the gates* press are removed (D228).
3. **The change in plain words.** The row and the page say the change as checks added, removed and changed, by name with each one's
   command, read from the JSON of the accepted blob and the line's (`DeclarationChange`); no blob id appears in the person's words,
   only in the record and in `--json`.
4. **State ends at the press.** What is owed is judged from the look's reads against the choices as they stand each time it is
   asked, so an acceptance leaves *What needs you*, the window's panel and `notices` at once, with no look and no git.
5. **One list.** The rows of *What needs you* are not filtered by the window's workspace scope: a wait is the person's wherever it is,
   so the window's Notifications group and the terminal's `notices` read the same rows. The scope had hidden the departures and the
   declaration of another workspace from the window while the terminal, which has no scope, listed them.
6. **A terminal door.** `daoris integrate <repository> --accept|--withdraw [--plan]` presses `INTEGRATION_GATES` through the running
   Daoris as a terminal press, says the change in the same words first, and with `--plan` writes nothing.
