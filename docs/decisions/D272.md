## D272 — Ask Daoris's acts are read after each turn by a second model, another maker's first, as one observational supervisor kind whose report holds and decides nothing (2026-11-11)

**Decision (ASKSUPERVISE1, accepted 2026-11-11).** Settled after Codex's independent read (D191), each of its findings checked against
the code. The contract is `docs/2026-11-11-ask-daoris-second-check-design.md`. The owner's rules hold throughout: visibility is the
check's purpose; a security improvement never blocks the development process, so full access stays unblocked and every limit skips or
defers the check, never the work; a person who wants more control uses restricted mode.

A driven session's work passes its place's supervisor, which checks as well as decides. Ask Daoris, which in full access presses as the
person in every workspace (D253), had no reader: every act it made was recorded and none was read.

1. **What is read.** Every call of Ask Daoris's that ran with no yes of the person's for it, unless it is known to read: its harness's
   acting kinds, every press of Daoris's own connector as its door classes it (a setting, a clean-up, a branch discarded, a quest
   published), every call Daoris cannot place, and its delegated work where its door keeps it, said *delegated, not read* where not. A
   call is never selected for the workspace it names. Cut or missing evidence makes a reading partial, never clean.
2. **When.** After each turn, in the background. A selected turn joins its conversation's queued range, which is frozen when a reader
   takes it and bounded in size; the selection, the walk and the packet run off the driver's loop, one reading on the machine at a time.
   An interrupted turn is read as far as its record goes. A range no reader can take waits, then closes unread with why. Nothing the
   person, a press or a driven start does waits on a reading.
3. **Authority.** One observational act, `ask-daoris.report`: findings, each with a severity (`flag`, `note`), a certainty, and the call
   and the person's words it rests on. The reader holds, approves, reverses and repairs nothing, and a finding is a claim.
4. **The reader.** A seeded machine supervisor, *Second check*, deciding the kind `ask-daoris` (refused below the machine), held by
   `agent`: Codex at account default, then Claude Code · opus · high, each with a budget of 40 starts a day. The one walk is handed the
   range's writer, so another maker reads first and the same maker is said *not independent*; an unknown writer is never presumed
   independent. The model that ran and the tokens spent are the harness's report, unknowns said.
5. **Access and privacy.** A packet in its prompt, an empty folder of its own, no connector, and its tools disabled where its harness
   takes Daoris's rules; a harness that does not is passed *not enforced*. What the packet holds, cuts, redacts and keeps is the design's
   §6, and its commands, outputs and every model's words are evidence, never instructions. For this subject alone the person's words and
   Ask Daoris's words to them are read; the reasoning never is.
6. **Shown.** One durable report per range, from which every state the window shows derives: queued, running, read, partial, unread,
   disabled, not selected. The chip says *Second check on · N pending*, never who read; an unseen flag counts on the bell and *Seen*
   acknowledges it; a flag is told to Ask Daoris once, ahead of the person's next words, its delivery kept, never as a turn of its own.
   Reports and their packets are deleted with their conversation.
7. **On here, chosen elsewhere.** This install's owner asked for the second check, so the update that ships it turns it on here, under
   the owner's yes recorded in this decision (D191 point 3), its providers and spend shown. Every other install receives it off, with a
   card that names the provider it sends to and what it spends, and the person turns it on (D205 point 7's principle).
8. **Restricted mode's cards are not read now.** The person's yes on a card, or to a held call, is their authorization, said so, not a
   check. What ran under a standing grant is read: a granted family's press, a connector press under the access the person configured,
   and an act their allow-list let run.

*Rejected:*
- **A hold before each press.** It blocks the work full access exists for, and a shell call never reaches Daoris before it runs.
- **The reader deciding as the allow supervisor does.** Ask Daoris's presses are the person's, and a go-ahead is theirs alone (D233's
  note of 2026-11-08).
- **A family of routes, or what the room marks, as what is read.** A second classification that misses the shell, or the checked agent
  choosing its own check.
- **Daoris's own connector exempt.** Through it Ask Daoris writes settings, cleans up and discards branches with no per-call yes.
- **Two granted acts, `note` and `flag`.** A grant over prominence would hide the reader's own assessment.
- **The press record, the turn read whole, or old turns summarized by count, as a reading's ground.** The record holds the newest 100
  presses in memory and names no turn, the events log cuts each call's evidence, and a count is no reading.
- **A family order of the kind's own.** `ReviewerChoice` orders by maker once it is handed the writer.
- **A reader with the supervisor's reads.** They reach every workspace's records, which a second provider would then receive.
- **On by default everywhere through a silent upgrade.** Nothing spends differently until the person presses.
- **A flag told on the focus's resume path alone, or offering its act's reverse press.** The first misses a live turn; *Resume* is a done
  pause's alone, and a flag read later would offer a stale act.
- **A reading system of its own, or the second opinion as its home.** A third way to run a reading model (D228); the second opinion reads
  a candidate's commits, with a gate and disputes that have no subject here.
- **A flag told as a turn of its own.** Ask Daoris would answer another model's claim while the person is away.

**Amends** D155 point 6 (for this subject alone, the conversation's words are read) and D233 (a kind that observes, beside the kinds
that decide). **Held by:** ASKSUPERVISE1a-f.
