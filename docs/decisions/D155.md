## D155 — A second agent, from another maker, reads a session's work before it lands: named by the person, read-only in a copy of its own, its findings answered by the working session as its next turn and shown to the person beside *reviewed*, and settled before D154's look (2026-10-08)

**Decision (XAGENT1).** The owner, 2026-10-08: *"you can always use Codex to assist the development"*, and *"this is
also the logic that applies to Daoris, since we have a multi-agent system in Daoris"*. This repository is developed
with Claude Code, and Codex reads merges and designs as a read-only second opinion. Its findings are checked against the
source before they are routed (`docs/2026-10-07-second-opinion-review.md`). Daoris drives sessions on several harnesses
and accounts, but read at `25d7f716` it never sets one to check or help another. A machine's driven work runs on one
adapter (`Driver.cs:928` into `HarnessRoster.SelectAsync`), whose walk chooses an account within that adapter's owner
(AGT7). The contract is `docs/2026-10-08-second-agent-design.md`. A read-only second opinion from Codex was taken on
this question, and its §11 adopts, amends or rejects each of its points.

1. **A second opinion is one agent's reading of another session's work**: what it read, its findings, and its limits,
   bound to a candidate, a base and a tip commit. It is not called *review*. That word holds the review pane and D154's
   *reviewed*, which stays the person's alone.
2. **Four occasions** (design §2.1):
   - `landing`, on once the setting is on: a chain's work in one repository, once its last step there is done and not
     held, before D154's look and before the work is offered to land;
   - `steps`, off by default: a step's work before the chain's next step starts, which is how a design is read before
     it is built;
   - failure: offered on a quest its strikes or a gate's evidence hold, and never started by itself;
   - the person's ask, at any time.
3. **A setting of its own in `driver.json`**: `opinions` (a repository's rule, or `false`) and `workspaceOpinions`. A
   rule has `reviewers` (adapters in the person's order, named and never guessed), `on`, `required`, `verify`,
   `minutes` and `recheck`, and a repository's rule replaces its workspace's whole. The task level (the chain, the ask)
   chooses among what is declared, and the person's *Go on anyway* at the gate is the top level. The intake sets the task
   level only on the person's quoted words, either way, and otherwise proposes, as D154 §1.5 does. The doors are `daoris
   driver opinion …`, the Setup rows beside *Review before landing*, the task level's choice, and Ask Daoris's `setting`
   card. Nothing set anywhere is today's behaviour.
4. **Another maker's agent first, then an account.** A family is an owner (AGT7) and a maker, so Claude Code by either
   door is one family, and another account of the same agent is the same family. The working session's families are
   every family that wrote commits in the candidate. A new walk over the rule's `reviewers` tries every entry outside
   those families first, and calls the account walk unchanged for each: the workspace's scope, cool-offs, sign-ins,
   pins, and a slot under the cap. An entry of the same family is taken only when the person listed it and no other can
   run, labelled *the same agent, fresh*. Otherwise the opinion is *unavailable*, with its code and a press for the
   same agent. A maker not declared is never taken as independent: D64's manifest gains `product` and `maker`.
   Independence is by maker; Daoris names no model (D24), and says so.
5. **Read-only beyond the prompt.** The reviewer works in a clone of the repository at the candidate, made by Daoris,
   with no remote, removed afterwards, and never the working session's tree. Nothing in the clone is ever read back.
   The isolation is the floor, because a harness's mode name cannot carry it: `codex-acp`'s `read-only` runs a
   workspace-write sandbox, as read from its bundle, and the repository's own doctrine tells an agent to write as it
   goes. On top of it, the adapter's posture, in its own words and only where measured (D53): for Claude Code, Daoris's
   new `opinion` default denies the edit tools and withholds `commit`. Its connector offers reading and `opinion_give`,
   and nothing that closes, publishes, permits or asks. It takes one turn and no words, within `minutes`. It cannot ask
   for a reviewer. With `verify`, it builds and runs only what the repository declares safe, in its clone.
6. **What it reads**: the candidate, the requirements with the person's quoted words, the working session's closing
   note given as its claim, the evidence Daoris read, and the repository's own rules. It does not read the working
   session's conversation: it starts a fresh one every pass.
7. **Findings go to the working session once, as its next turn** (D137), marked as another agent's claims. `said` gains
   `by`, absent being the person's. The findings are never kept on the ask as the person's words, and they arrive at a
   turn's end. The working session answers each with `opinion_answer`: `fixed` with a commit, which Daoris checks is one
   the turn made; `rejected` with evidence; or `unresolved`. Anything unanswered reads as unresolved. Where the turn
   added commits, one focused recheck reads them, and its findings go to the person, never back by themselves. Where
   the session cannot resume, the findings go to the person. For failure, the help is *Try again* with a diagnosis.
8. **Claims and facts.** A defect is established only by a check Daoris reads itself, a declared gate's verdict.
   Agreement between agents establishes nothing. A `must` finding left rejected or unresolved after the recheck is
   **disputed**, and a dispute is the person's. The press that comes next anyway answers it, with the disputes shown
   in it: *Accept…*, or D154's *Reviewed*. Where no press is coming, under *Accept automatically* with no look, the
   look holds with *Go on anyway…* and *Send back…*, and never lands over a dispute. A pass with no findings says what
   it read, and never *no issues*.
9. **One gate, in a fixed order**: the quest's own holds (D133, D144), then the second opinion, then the person's look
   (D154), then the landing. D154's set-up step sits while an agent is still at work on the opinion (reading,
   answering, reading again), not while a dispute waits for the person, who is about to look. No agent settles either
   gate: an opinion never gives *reviewed*. The disputes, and the commits a set-up holds that the opinion did not read,
   are shown beside *Reviewed*, and that press is the person's answer to them.
10. **The gate.** Wherever the level says `landing`, *Accept…*, `trees land` and an advance wait, and *Accept
    automatically* keeps a due session due as `opinion`. Under the setting, a chain is read once per repository, at its
    last step there: earlier steps' dones wait instead of landing one by one. It is settled when an opinion covers the
    tip (`merge-base --is-ancestor`), the working session has answered, and no dispute is open or the person answered
    it. The cap is one pass and one recheck per landing. After it, commits nobody read ride beside the person's press,
    or hold the automatic look.
    `required` governs only absence: with it, no opinion holds with *Try again*, *Ask the same agent, fresh*, *I looked
    myself…* and *Go on anyway…*; without it, the line rides beside *Accept…*. An opinion that was given always counts.
    *Go on anyway…* settles the opinion's part and lands nothing itself: the gate's next item follows.
11. **What is kept, and where.** The opinion is kept by the local host beside the session records and answered only on
    this machine (D47 §4), never as quest operations, which travel. The reviewer's record is kind `Chat` with an
    `opinion` field and serves no quest, the intake's precedent. Its usage counts toward its account, and absent is
    never zero (D57). The landing record gains `opinion`, the log gains `opinion.asked`, `.given`, `.answered`, `.held`
    and `.unavailable`, and clearing a closed quest's work clears its opinions (D153).
12. **The no-model tier** builds the packet, chooses the reviewer by table, reads coverage and fix commits from git,
    routes by fixed words, holds the gate on facts and presses, and accepts *I looked myself…* as an opinion of tier
    *person*. Every opinion says its tier, and *no agent could read this* when none ran.

**Why.** What a second agent adds is a reading by a reader that did not write the work. A fresh conversation of the same
agent, or another account, is the same reader. Taken by a person, the practice already works here: the second agent
reads and never writes, the owner of the work checks each finding against the source, and the record says which were
checked. The design keeps each of those properties in a mechanism, not a prompt. The reader is named, and is another
maker's. Its copy cannot reach the work. Its findings reach the session that owns the work, as claims to check. The
answers sit beside the findings for the person, and only a check Daoris reads is called a fact. It runs before the
person's look, so the person looks once at work that already answered another agent. Each piece reuses what exists:
the account walk, D51's trees, D137's reopen, D154's gate and its quoted task level, D145's look, and the intake's
precedent for a record that serves no quest. The new parts are the walk over agents, the clone, the two tools, and the
order of the gate.

**Rejected.**
- **Daoris choosing a reviewer among whatever is installed.** It would spend an account the person never named (D23).
- **The reviewer as a quest, or a chain step.** It does no work in the repository, must be a particular other agent,
  and moves nothing; a quest is taken by whatever the machine drives with, moves its chain and travels.
- **Quest operations as the record.** They travel and replay everywhere (D68), and an opinion's candidate is this
  machine's commits and its reviewer an account.
- **The working session's tree, or a worktree**, as the reviewer's: the work itself, or the person's refs.
- **A harness's read-only mode as the guarantee**, since one such mode runs a workspace-write sandbox.
- **Handing the reviewer the worker's conversation**, which anchors it on the worker's reasoning.
- **Taking the reviewer's edits back as a patch.** The work is the owning session's (D45); a proposal travels as text.
- **Agents talking until they agree**, which is a loop with nobody in it (COWORK1's ground), and **an automatic loop
  until findings clear**.
- **Findings kept as the person's words**, which DRIFT1 would turn into requirements.
- **An opinion after the look**, which costs a second look per fix; **instead of the look**, since D154's case was
  checks that were all agents'; and **one that gives or lifts *reviewed***.
- **Holding the build's done** (D154's reason), **failure help started by itself**, and **on by default**.
- **Another account, or a fresh conversation of the same agent, counted as independent.**
- **A model deciding which findings are right** (D24, D54).
- **A turn limit.** A pass is one turn by construction (INT4i), so only wall-clock is a setting.

**This amends:**
- **D145 §3**: under `landing`, a chain's step waits for its last step in that repository, and the look's code
  `opinion` keeps it due.
- **D87 and D145**: a landing waits for a settled opinion where the level says one.
- **D154**: its set-up step sits while an agent is still at work on the opinion, the gate's order puts the opinion
  before the look, and the disputes and the commits since are shown beside *Reviewed*, which answers them.
- **D137**: `said` gains `by`. Another agent's words reach a session at a turn's end, never kept on the ask as the
  person's, and the say door refuses words to a reviewer's record as `opinion`.
- **D65 §1b**: the intake's room names each repository's reviewers, and the intake sets the choice only on a quote.
- **D64**: a plugin's harness may declare `product` and `maker`.
- **D72**: a default, `opinion`, denies the edit tools to an opinion's session, and `commit` is withheld from it.
- **D110**: the `setting` kind takes `opinion`, and the asks and judgements are exempt as design §9 says.
- **D94, D102 and D143**: the log's lines, and the landing record's and the trace's `opinion`.
- **D153**: opinions clear with the work they read. **D130**: a reviewer's start counts and takes a slot.
