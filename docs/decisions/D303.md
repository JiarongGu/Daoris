## D303 — A performed step's progress is its own answer kind, naming the actor it waits on (2026-11-16)

**Decision (RELEASEWAIT1).** While a performed attempt is out, its plugin may send `work/step` with the state `progress`, bound to the
attempt's invocation under an id of its own: it may name the `actor` whose act the step waits on (`approver`, `deployer`), with its
words, page and place as evidence. Daoris keeps the newest on the attempt, the next replacing it; it settles nothing and takes no
receipt. Where it names an actor the run's step reads `waiting` on that actor by the holder `StepHolders` gives, and an actor the
person holds is a row in *What needs you* and places the run's session in Sessions' Needs you, by one reading (`StepWaiting`). A watch
tells its first look and each change after (area `workflows-review-landing.md`, rules 18 and 55; area `plugins.md`, Azure DevOps).

**Why.** On 2026-11-16 a release's production stage waited on an approval in Azure DevOps and Daoris showed nothing, so the owner did
not know the release waited on them. A step names its actor, never who fills it (D304), so the wait is said the same for any holder.

**Rejected:** carrying the wait on the `work/reconcile` answer's `running` (it is asked only of an earlier process's attempt, so a wait
would surface only after a restart, and an answer about whether an attempt still runs would carry a second meaning); a failure or an
`unknown` outcome for a wait (each settles the attempt and holds the step, though nothing failed and the stage goes on by itself);
naming the holder in the frame (the place chooses who fills an actor, never the plugin).

**Left:** every actor is held by the person until a role appoints one (D304's CASTING1); an approval Daoris can make itself with one
press is a channel still to build.
