## D331 — The catalogue shows any story in either theme, language and width from its toolbar, and tagged stories keep tracked picture baselines (2026-11-20)

**Decision (UIDS3, UIDS5, the window redesign `docs/2026-11-19-window-design.md` §16).**

1. **Globals, not variants.** Storybook's toolbar carries `theme` (light, dark), `locale` (English, 中文) and `width` (1600, 1280,
   1024, 900, 720), applied by `withLocale`, `withTheme` and `withRegion` (a story drawn at its region's width in a window of that
   width, from `design/regions.ts`). The variant decorators and the 1,482 variant exports they produced go, by a one-off codemod;
   a story whose content is its variant (Han data) is kept and named by its state. A check fails a story that pins a global or
   frames itself.
2. **The whole window and every screen have stories** over fixtures (`Window/*`, `screenStory()`), and the token pages are
   generated from `tokens.css` and held equal to the test's reading.
3. **Tracked baselines.** Stories tagged `visual` are shot on the diagonal pair (light·en·1600, dark·zh·900) and `visual-full` at
   both themes, both languages and three widths, by the Playwright harness the measured specs use, on Windows with its system
   faces (a missing face stops the run by name), software raster, a frozen clock and reduced motion, at a tolerance measured from
   three runs. Baselines live in git under `src/Daoris.Web/visual/baselines/`, within a size budget, and change only in the commit
   that changes the look, naming the stories moved. The gate `web-visual` is a browser spec the merge's fast plan defers to the run
   on main; a builder runs the stories its change reached before hand-back.

**Why.** No story draws the whole window, eight screens have none, the theme helper is copied into 117 files, and nothing checks a
picture: a change can move the window unseen. A toolbar shows every axis of every story without a story per axis, and fewer jsdom
renders shorten `web-unit`.

**Rejected.**

- *A hosted picture service*: it needs a remote, and local operation needs none.
- *Untracked renders compared with main*: nothing to review in the commit, and two ways to keep a baseline.
- *Every story at every combination*: about 16,000 images, more diffs than anyone reads.
- *MDX documentation pages*: a second authoring format the story roster would not render.
