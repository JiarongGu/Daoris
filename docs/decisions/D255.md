## D255 — A done of an ask's quest that carries no requirement waits unchecked for the person; it is never given the ask's words as a requirement (2026-11-10)

**Decision.** A quest an ask published may carry no requirement of the person's: a receiver named with no draft, or an intake that wrote
none. Its done was then checked against nothing, and the run's `quest.holds` passed on nothing, so unfinished work reached the person's
Accept as if checked. The rule:

1. **Unchecked, never checked.** A run's `quest.holds` reading a done whose quest an ask published and that carries no requirement does
   not reach: it waits, said as *unchecked*, naming the quest whose done note (its handback) the person reads. The person's pass of the
   step with their words is the way on, as for every hold, and a version with the hold's `yes` off lets it go, since `yes` is the
   person's word on a done.
2. **Only the person's quests.** A quest one repository asks of another is asked on no ask and can carry no requirement at all; its
   publisher reads its done at its own `work.requests`. Holding those would ask the person to check every quest between repositories,
   so the rule stops at a quest an ask published.
3. **The publish says it.** An ask's publish that carries no requirement says in its answer that its done is checked against nothing
   and waits, unchecked, for the person's read; the terminal's `daoris ask publish` carries requirements with `--require`.
4. **The quest's own hold is unchanged.** The exchange's hold (departure, evidence, review) stays what it was, so a chain, an ask's
   done and the quests waiting on one read as before; the wait is the run's step.

*Rejected:* giving such a quest its ask's words as one requirement at publish. A requirement's check is only as good as its writer: the
whole ask quoted with a check nobody wrote is answered `met` by the agent that did the work, so the step would pass on the agent's own
word and read as checked, which is the defect again; a long ask is past a requirement's bound, and a quest between repositories has no
ask to quote. *Rejected too:* making *unchecked* a hold of the quest itself, which would change the meaning of every done already
recorded, hold chains and asks' done on the person, and add a second hold beside the step that judges it.

**Held by:** APPROVE1 (b).
