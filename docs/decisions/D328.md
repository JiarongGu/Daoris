## D328 — An authentication refusal outranks the agent's status for its hour, and a step refused for its sign-in moves once (2026-11-19)

**Decision (ACCOUNTREVOKED1, amending D125's TOOL6h).** A start or a turn its agent refused for its account's sign-in reads that account
signed out from the refusal, kept in `reads.json` as any reading is, and the walk passes it until a sign-in marks it (`daoris agent
login`, the card's *Sign in*) or the hour after the refusal is over (`SignedOutAskedAgain`), whatever the account last read: no status
question is asked of it before then. TOOL6h's one fresh reading owed to a refused account last read signed in is removed. A plugin step
performed by an agent takes the driven start's move (`ClaimedStart`): a session its agent refused for its account's sign-in goes on once
under the walk's next account, by Daoris itself (`StepAgents`), and a walk that gives the refused account again runs nothing. Where no
account could take the step for its sign-in, the step fails `sign-in` (`StepAnswer.SignIn`), its message naming the accounts refused and
those not signed in, and the sign-in to make (`StepSignIns`); every other unanswered end stays `no-answer`.

**Why.** On 2026-11-19 a work workspace run's plugin `pull-request` step started on an account last read signed in whose sign-in its
server had revoked; the agent failed "OAuth token revoked", the door said *Authentication required*, the account was marked signed out,
and the step read only `no-answer`, held for a person's *Try again*, while the other account was signed out too. Claude Code's status
reads its own store, which a revocation at the server leaves in place, so TOOL6h's owed question can read signed in again and spend the
next start, a step's one move among them, on the same refusal (D125 left this unmeasured).

**Rejected.**

- *Keep TOOL6h's owed question and honour its yes*: it saves a person who signed in at the agent's own terminal, outside Daoris's doors,
  at most the hour, and costs a step its one move on the account that just refused it.
- *Keep a separate `refused` time beside the reading, with its own lifetime*: a second hold beside the signed-out reading, which already
  holds the account for the same hour and is cleared by the same sign-in mark.
- *Pass the refused account to the move's walk as a model refusal (`ReviewerPassed`)*: the walk would then say, with every other account
  signed out, that the account does not offer the model, which is not why.
- *Retry the step until an account answers*: once is the driven start's bound; a second refusal is the person's sign-in to make.
