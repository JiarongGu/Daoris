## D363 — A staged build waits for the turns that run, never for the sessions that live (2026-11-26)

**Decision (SWAPWHENBUSY1, amending D327's wait; the owner's direction: keep Daoris running, publish when fixes land).** The idle a
staged build installs at is no turn running: no driven session with a turn in flight, no conversation's turn in flight and no run's
plugin step in flight. A driven session whose turn has ended and which waits on its own background work (a check it started) runs no
turn: its door says so (`AcpSession.WaitsOnOwnWork`, the watcher's state C), and the loop's work does not count it
(`DriverWatch.RunsInTurn`, read by `DriverLoop.Work`). The drain is otherwise D327's: new work is held for the machine's `drain`
setting at most, then the state is `deferred` and the build installs at the first look that reads no turn running. The install's close
is *Update now*'s close: each driven session still alive is recorded stopped for the update (`update.closed`) and carried on at the
next start, in its own conversation.

**Why.** On 2026-11-25 the steward installed staged builds with `update --now` because the drain waited for hours: sessions that
had ended their turn and waited on checks a long time still counted as running work, and a driven Daoris, with sessions always at
some stage of a next turn, never reads "nothing runs". Each `--now` cut checks and sessions mid-turn, the one cost the drain exists to
avoid. A session between turns is cut at no turn's expense: what it lost is a wait on a check, which its carry-on resumes.

**Rejected.**

- *Wait for nothing to run, as D327 did, and rely on the person's `update --now`*: the person's word cuts mid-turn, and a build staged
  while any session lives waits for ever; the evidence of 2026-11-25 is that the steward said it each time.
- *Past the bound, cut at once, mid-turn*: the cut is the harm. The install still waits for each turn to end, and the bound is the
  hold on new work, not a deadline on a turn.
- *Hold the sessions' own next turns during the drain to force a boundary*: a woken turn is the harness's own, and a go-on's hold
  would stall the quest's work for as long as the drain, which D327 rejected for new starts.
