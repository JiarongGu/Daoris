## D338 — Get started is a path the owner finishes alone; Daoris takes the other setup choices once and says what it chose (2026-11-20)

**Decision (UIST3, UIST4, UIST8, the window redesign `docs/2026-11-19-window-design.md` §14; amending D97).**

1. Get started holds only the owner's steps, in place: **sign in an agent** (Accounts' own sign-in, per maker) and **add
   repositories** (choose folders or an address, each read, a workspace proposed and renamable); then, optional, the code hosts
   the repositories' remotes name, signed in on the spot, and connectors offered. One step is open at a time; a done step folds
   to its summary with *Change*. The window opens on it while a required step is open; the finish opens New ask; *Later* replaces
   *Don't open at start*.
2. **Daoris takes the rest once** (a unit of its own in the driver's setup, registered at start): at the first account signed in
   on a home with no helper or role choice, that agent becomes Ask Daoris's and the intake's and the roles' suggested choices are
   applied; a repository registered with no setup gets its suggested plan (its own declaration's landing, else *merge after you
   accept* on a local branch; never a push or a pull request unless the repository declares it). Each is kept as Daoris's
   decision with its reason, listed under *Daoris decided* with a door to change it; an owner's own choice is never overwritten.
3. The guide's steps *Ask Daoris's agent*, *Set a place up* and *What agents may do* leave it.

**Why.** Today Get started orders five screens and sends the owner to set what Daoris could choose, against "owner, not manager";
the steward set up the install by hand because no path finished alone. Two steps are the owner's: a sign-in and the choice of
their own repositories.

**Rejected.**

- *A guide of links to every setup screen* (D97): the owner does the agents' choosing.
- *Choosing silently*: a choice the owner cannot see cannot be changed; each is listed with its reason.
- *Asking the owner to confirm each default*: a question that is not theirs.
