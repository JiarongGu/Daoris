## D318 — A step cast to the role's agent is the run's working agent's to take, settled through its one tool (2026-11-18)

**Decision (ACTORFLOW1a, amending D304 points 2, 3 and 5, and D312's agent holder).**

1. A step cast to the role's agent is the run's working agent's to take. When such a step waits on its holder's word (a `task`'s Done or a
   decision's answer, `AgentSettles.Tool`) and an agent holds it, the run's newest working session of this machine is handed it as its next
   turn through the door `session say` uses (`StepHandOffs`, after the pass keeps the attempt's tenure): a session awaiting the run's step
   wakes with it, one that ended goes on with it. The words say what the step is, its outcomes, the evidence it names and the tool that settles
   it, once per attempt and epoch (`step-hand:<run>:<node>:<attempt>:<epoch>`, the say door answering a repeat with the word kept).
2. The session settles it through one tool, `step_settle` (run, node, outcome, words, evidence, epoch), a file under the home's sessions
   folder the driver takes at its next look (`StepSettleAsks`), through the run's node door at the settle door (`SettleDoor.Admit`) as the
   agent its start ran (`SettleBy.OfChannel` for a session's channel), never the person. It is refused, and nothing kept, where the step does
   not wait on a task its holder settles, where its outcome is not one the step declares, where an evidence name the step's `evidence` option
   lists is missing (`TaskEvidence`, the config change's `version` and `capture`), and by the door where the agent does not hold the step or
   its epoch moved; what it came to is the state file's, and a refusal is said to the session as its next turn.
3. A decision its agent holds is settled by the agent's answer (`NodeWord.Decider`, "decided by agent:<name>"), no proposal held for the
   owner's confirmation; where the place marks the step the owner role holds it, and the door refuses the agent.
4. Where the agent cannot settle it (no access, a refusal, a yes it needs), its request goes up its reporting line: a question, or a go-ahead
   for an act that is the person's to allow (D304 point 3), and the step waits on.
5. One way for the person: the step's row in *What needs you* reads "waiting on `<role>`: `<agent>`" with no press of the step's own, its one
   press *Take*; a take ends the agent's epoch, so a settle it still sends is refused `ended-epoch`.

**Why.** The work workspace's release path cast the release manager, the config changer and operations to the role's agent, and those steps
are `task` steps settled by a press; with no agent channel (D312's amendment: "a wait an agent holds that only a press settles stays the
person's row with *Take*") every step after the release fell back to the owner. The owner's direction (ACTORFLOW1) is that an act is the
actor's tool under its grant, journaled and settled by its receipt, and that the run's agent carries the work past landing.

**Rejected:** a separate session per step's role (it starts cold without the work's context, and spends a second agent where the working one
holds the line); the owner's *Take* as the agent's channel (the person would press for every agent-held step, which is the failure this
removes); a press from the session's terminal as the channel (a terminal press is the person's door, confirmed on a card, and the tool names
the step's evidence where a verb would not); a confirmation asked of the owner for every agent's decision (it puts the owner back on a step
the place cast to the agent; a marked place already keeps the step the owner role's).

**Left:** the release's acts (target, create, start, watch) as the agent's own tools under its grant rather than plugin steps (ACTORFLOW1b);
an approval and an Accept an agent holds; the shipped `pull-request-release` preset declaring the change's evidence, which changes its digest;
a hand-off read back after a restart from the say door's key alone.

**Amended 2026-11-19 (D325).** A step whose `receipts` option maps its evidence is settled by its acts' receipts, never through
`step_settle`, and its hand-off names the repository's delivery knowledge entry it follows.
