## D343 — Money is shown, against a weekly budget the owner sets (2026-11-20)

**Decision (UIRP28, UIWF14, the window redesign `docs/2026-11-19-window-design.md` §13.6; the owner's direction of 2026-11-19;
reversing D179's rejection of a price or money in any form and the glossary's *usage* as "measured and never priced").**

1. Spend is shown in money per agent, account, model and session: **measured** where the maker states money (a prepaid balance
   and its spend, a key's spend), elsewhere **about**: the usage ledger's turns times a dated, sourced price table per model and
   token kind (D179 point 3's table gains prices), with the plan's share of its windows beside it, since a subscription's
   accounting is not its maker's API price list. A missing reading is *unknown*, never 0.
2. The owner sets one **weekly budget** on Company › Accounts. As the week's spend nears it a **Money** wait arises: *Yes* raises
   the budget to a sum the card proposes; *No* holds new starts until the reset while running work goes on; the card says how
   much of the spend is estimated.
3. The same figures appear in the status strip (the week against the budget), Overview's *Used* line, a session's *Models, tokens
   and cost* and an ask's cost so far. The weekly budget is the one budget; an ask shows its cost and holds none of its own unless
   the owner asks for one.

**Why.** The owner: money is shown, with a weekly budget they set, and a wait when spend nears it. Money and budgets are the
owner's, so the window must say what the company costs in the owner's terms, and a budget is how the owner says how much may be
spent while they are away.

**Rejected.**

- *Money with no budget*: the owner's answer; spend would be seen only after it happened.
- *Tokens only*: the owner's answer; tokens are not the owner's unit.
- *A budget per ask beside the weekly one*: two budgets for one spend; offered to the owner as a question rather than built.
