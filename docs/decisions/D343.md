## D343 — Budgets follow how an account is paid, and money is shown only where it is real (2026-11-20)

**Decision (UIRP28, UISE7, UISE19, UIWF14, the window redesign `docs/2026-11-19-window-design.md` §13.6; the owner's direction
of 2026-11-20; amending D179's rejection of a price or money in any form and the glossary's *usage* as "measured and never
priced").**

1. **A subscription account carries no money budget.** Its use is shown against its plan's own windows (the session and weekly
   limits the accounts already report), as a share and a reset. Its price is shown only if the owner enters it, as the plan's
   price and period, never spread over the work as an estimate. Every account today is a subscription.
2. **An account paid per use** (an API key) carries a **budget** the owner sets on Company › Accounts, by the week or by the
   month, the owner's choice of period. Its spend is shown as measured (the key's spend, a prepaid balance). As the period's spend
   nears the budget a **Money** wait arises on that account: *Yes* raises the budget to a sum the card proposes; *No* holds new
   starts on that account until the period resets, while running work goes on and other accounts take what they can. A prepaid
   balance running out raises the same kind.
3. **Money is shown where it is real**: a per-use account's spend, and a subscription's price where the owner entered it. A
   missing reading is *unknown*, never 0.
4. **An ask carries no budget.** It shows its cost so far: its sessions' share of each subscription's windows with their tokens by
   model, and money for the work that ran on per-use accounts. A session's *Models and use* and Overview's *Used* read the same.
5. The status strip shows spend only while a per-use account has a budget: the account nearest its budget, with its period.

**Why.** The owner: budgets follow how an account is paid. A subscription's limit is its plan's windows, which the accounts
already report; a money budget over it would cap nothing the owner pays for. An account paid per use spends real money as work
runs, and the owner sets how much in the period they choose.

**Rejected.**

- *One weekly money budget over subscriptions*: the owner's answer; a subscription's spend is its plan's price, not its work.
- *A budget per ask*: the owner's answer; an ask shows its cost, and the accounts carry the budgets.
- *Tokens only*: the owner's answer; per-use spend and an entered price are money, and the windows' share is what a plan limits.
- *Money estimated from a price table for subscription work*: not what the owner pays (D179: a maker's subscription accounting is
  not its API price list), and money is shown only where it is real.
