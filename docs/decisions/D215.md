## D215 — A repository's integration is a fabric workflow Daoris runs (proposed) (2026-10-28)

**Decision (INTEGRATE1, proposed 2026-10-28).** **Status: proposed**, round 1 under D191, written by Claude Opus 5.5 from main at `7ebeba1ba`;
Claude Fable's and Codex's reads are owed, each without the other's, and the steward settles it. It changes a foundation (how work reaches a
line, who may move one and when, a protocol between Daoris and a repository's runner, and rules D87, D145, D192, D207 and D209 rest on), so by
D191's note of 2026-10-24 no part of it is accepted on one maker's read. Nothing is built. FOUNDATION1 (D212) stays the building priority; the
rows that run beside it are marked in the design's §11. The contract is `docs/2026-10-28-integration-design.md`.

The owner directed on 2026-10-28 that a repository's merging not depend on a queue the parent runs by hand: Daoris gets a proper merge step and
completes it, the deeper git-workflow logic configured in the fabric, a merge task becoming a landing task that includes rebasing when the line
moved. The same day the owner directed that the foundation move away from unmanageable scripting. Read at `7ebeba1ba`: this repository's work
lands as branches the install never merges, and the parent integrates them with `tools/merge-branch.mjs` (6,837 lines), a detached runner its
shell starts, which batches, gates, bisects and fast-forwards; every other repository's merge rule merges one press at a time into the checkout,
with no gates and no batch. In the queue's records of 2026-10-26 to 28, 9 of 29 candidates were sent back only on failures its judge could not
reproduce or place, against 3 with a member pinned, and the candidate after the day's last promotion came back red five hours later; the
steward dropped, requeued and resolved by hand, ended runners a drop left running, and found the install's home variable in the gates'
environment.

1. **The principle** (design §3). The procedures the development's day depends on are product: fabric workflows with steps, events and tests,
   shown on the page and operable by the appointed steward. A script stays only as a check or generator that reads the repository, a harness
   that proves the product from outside it, or an instrument a person starts. The design's §7 places each of the day's scripts and procedures.
2. **Two homes for the declaration** (§4.1). The repository's facts in its `daoris.gates.json`, key `integration` (`by`, `gates`, `runner`,
   `scratch`, `merge`, `message`, `env.pass`), run only as the owner accepted it by its blob; the install's policy on the landing rule, key
   `integrate` (`request`, `commit`, `batch`, `order`, `gates`, `unconfirmed`, `conflicts`, `line`), inherited whole with the rule (D164).
3. **The shapes** (§4.2): a branch for review and a pull request through a plugin as today; merge, rebase-and-merge and squash through
   integration. From its row g every merge rule integrates, one with no `integrate` read as today's press: owner-requested, ungated, one at a
   time, today's message. The line moves only forward to a gated commit; integration never pushes or rewrites the line or a pushed ref.
4. **One integration run per line** (§5.1, §5.2), its steps fixed and each a fabric event: request, form, replay, resolve, conflict, gates,
   judge, promote, settle, tidy, close. A unit waits on it through an `integrate` node between `handed` and `line`, reached when a promoted
   candidate holds its frozen tip or it is found contained (`work.integrated`). It runs in Daoris's own integration tree; a candidate's
   processes run in one job closed by a drop, a stop or its end before its admission is let go; its state is Workplace's store family, recovered
   by ancestry; the line's committed `integration.by` names its one integrator.
5. **Mechanical first** (§5.2 steps 3 and 4). A member the line or an earlier member already holds is contained, never set aside. Generated
   folders, union records with their joins, the backlog, the router and the repository's declared row tables and line rules settle by rule; only a
   conflict of meaning goes to the member's session through the rebase words, or to a landing agent where declared, or holds.
6. **The gates** (§4.3, §5.5, §5.9). The declared gates, through the repository's runner by a three-verb contract (`plan`, `run`, `probe`)
   shared with D192's `check`, or each gate whole; every heavy gate in the machine's one admission (HEAVYQUEUE1); an environment Daoris
   composes, every `DAORIS_` variable, git's repository-locating variables and the merge tool's switches removed, so no person's or steward's
   shell reaches a gate.
7. **A failure is confirmed before it is blamed** (§4.5, §5.3). The failing run is repeated once on the candidate's tip as composed, holding the
   whole admission. Recurring, it is judged as D207 judges it; not recurring, the gate's verdict is that pass, the first failure a sighting, and
   the rule's `unconfirmed` decides the candidate by whether any member reaches the unit: `land`, `split` or `hold`. The default `unreached: land,
   reached: split` takes effect only once the design's row b replays the recorded runs and the steward reads it; until then both are `split`,
   today's rule. Three sightings of one unit in a week are shown with the fix the unit needs.
8. **After landing** (§5.7). A later commit is contained, superseded (said with the line's commit, kept on a recovery ref, offered to its session
   or the steward) or new (a new request); never waiting work. A unit's note is written from its record, *Accept* said only where a press is due.
   A tree goes by its rule with the repository's declared scratch disposable, never forced.
9. **Who acts** (§8). Daoris runs every step under the accepted declaration. The steward gains integration acts (request, drop, requeue,
   recheck, decide, order), each recorded as its own, none for a repository whose landing pushes. The owner accepts each declaration's blob, sets
   `request: done` and `unconfirmed: land`, and keeps pushes, publication, history rewrites and forced removals. `request: done` requires
   declared gates.
10. **Same-file rows** (§9): a quest's declared paths are compared at its start with every open quest's, the overlap said on both and, where the
    rule says `overlap: wait`, the later start held on the earlier's `integrate` node; two running units that change one path are each told.
11. **The tool** (§6). Daoris takes the queue, the candidate, the resolvers, the judge, the findings, the run and its events into Workplace,
    Review, Workflows, Events, Settings and Roles; the gate plan, the censuses, the CI and the stage's evidence stay this repository's, as its
    declared runner and tools. The queue's verbs retire when the line's declaration says `by: daoris`, the move made through a shadow day.

**Why.** Every repair the steward made by hand on 2026-10-28 was a part of integration that lived in a script's process or the parent's shell:
cancellation, the environment, a decision on a held candidate, two mechanical resolutions. Built as product, each becomes a step the page shows
and a role may operate, and one implementation replaces two that disagreed about gates, batches and where the merge is made. The repository's
facts belong with its files and its trust choices with the install, so neither can lower the other. A failure that does not reproduce cost most
of the day; repeating the failing run as composed and with nothing heavy beside it tells load from a member's defect better than a class run alone,
which hides a test polluted by another, while a real break reproduces and is bisected whatever the census says, and the install still waits for
the background run's whole set. Keeping the gate plan the repository's keeps one repository's census out of the product.

**Rejected** (design §10): keeping the tool as the integrator, fixed in place; Daoris carrying this repository's gate plan; a pipeline marked
`integration`; a node per step on each unit; promoting by a merge in the checkout; landing every unconfirmed failure whatever its reach;
re-running a red candidate until it passes; confirming by the class alone; a wider window; Daoris resolving a conflict of meaning, or a
strategy option; `request: done` with no gates; `autoAccept` as a merge rule's switch; the whole declaration in `driver.json`, or the whole in
the repository; two integrators during the move; reverting a promoted candidate the background finds red; moving the rehearsals into the product;
detecting same-file rows by a model's reading.

**This would amend**, each by a dated note written by the row that makes the change and never before:
- **D87**: the merge form lands through integration (row g); point 5's refusal of a conflict stays, a conflict first settled where its paths are
  declared mechanical (row c).
- **D145 point 1**: a merge rule carrying `integrate` may request at done, only with declared gates (rows d and g).
- **D207 point 3**: *a passing re-run never making a candidate green* gives way, for the confirm run alone, to the rule's `unconfirmed` (row b);
  its rejection of re-forming a candidate until a run passes stands.
- **D192**: the runner contract gains `plan` and `probe` beside `run` (row j); the lowering table gains `request: done` and `unconfirmed: land`
  (row d).
- **D209 point 3**: integration into a local line under an accepted declaration leaves *landings*, which keep meaning a push or publication and
  every acceptance of what is delivered; the steward's integration acts join its table (row i).
- **D115** and **D180**: the parent's merge tool stops being this repository's integrator at the flip (row k), its plan staying the declared
  runner.

**What re-opens it.** A confirm run passing a failure a later run pins to a member it held unreached; a promoted candidate the background run
finds red on a unit integration landed as unconfirmed; the shadow day disagreeing with the queue on a candidate's outcome; a repository whose
integration needs steps a plugin contributes (the pipeline mark, rejected here); the owner's word.

**What this decision's checks do not cover.** It is proposed and a document: the documentation gates prove its routing, links and shape, not its
claims. The design's §1 was read at `7ebeba1ba` and the in-flight branches at their tips of 2026-10-28; the queue's records were read, never
written; the steward's accounts of the day are the brief's. Nothing was run but those reads and the document checks. How many recorded failures a
confirm run would pass, how long the whole admission waits beside driven sessions, and whether a member can make an out-of-reach unit flaky
without its failure recurring are unmeasured, row b's to measure first.
