## D316 — The person's stop of a session awaiting its run's step is their stop of that run (2026-11-18)

**Decision (SESSIONCLOSE1).** A session awaiting its run's step (`awaiting-step`) runs no process, so the person's stop of it, at
*Stop…* in the window or `daoris session stop <id>` (one implementation), is their stop of the run its record's wait names
(`StepWaits.From`, as `RunWaiters` finds it): every step of that run a stop reaches (one live, or the one a failure holds) is stopped
through the run's node door, as their own `daoris workflow stop <run> <node>` is (`RunNodeWords.WordAsync`), at the settle door as the
channel's speaker, which a stop makes the owner role's for the person's channels (D312). The next pass applies it, the run ends
stopped, and its end ends the session (`RunSessionEnds`). The answer names the run and the steps whose stop is kept, or says why none
was (a run not bound here, one that ended, a step another holder holds against an agent's channel, none standing). Every state the
page counts active has a person's stop that ends or moves it, which a census holds.

**Why.** A session's run sat at a plugin `release` step its holder, the person in the role `operations`, never settled; the work had
landed and merged, and the session waited for days with a *Stop…* that answered nothing stopped, since a stop of a record nothing
runs ended only a start or a working one. A session is the work's passage through its workflow (D293): only the workflow's end ends it.

**Rejected:** moving the record alone to `stopped`, which leaves the run waiting on its step with no session to show it, its quest
taken and its step still on *What needs you*; stopping only the step the record's note names (the note names the step the session's
own work came from, not the one the run waits on now); a stop of the session's own that bypasses the settle door (an agent's channel
would end work another holds).
