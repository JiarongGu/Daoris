## D234 — Daoris's browser plays H.264, AAC and HEVC from its own engine (2026-11-04)

**Decision.** Daoris's browser runs on the kit's Chromium engine, from CEF's standard build, which leaves out H.264, AAC and HEVC.
Daoris measured this when it chose the engine (`docs/archive/2026-09-28-chromium-embedding-evidence.md`, item 8). So most MP4 video, which
Chrome and Edge play, shows nothing in Daoris's browser. It is fixed in the engine: the kit ships an engine with these codecs, and
Daoris moves its pin onto it. The request went to the kit as a quest on 2026-11-04. The patent licensing that carrying the codecs
involves was put to the owner, who chose this route. How the kit carries them is the kit's call.

**Rejected.**
- Offering Edge for each video that fails on its codec. The browser would stay unable to play ordinary video, and every such page
  would become a hand-off.
- Making the person's Edge Daoris's default browser where it is installed. Daoris's own browser exists so that the profile, the
  favorites and the extension refusals are Daoris's (D99, BRW12). The person's Edge stays the option it is today.
- A note in Settings → Browser and nothing more. It names the gap and leaves it.

**Until the kit ships it,** the person's Edge (Settings → Browser) is the way to play such video.
