## D223 — A workflow is drawn and composed as a flowchart of Daoris's own components, and Ask Daoris composes against the open draft (2026-11-02)

**Decision (FLOWCHART2, the owner's direction of 2026-11-02; co-designed under D191, accepted by the steward 2026-11-02).** Per
`docs/2026-11-02-flowchart-design.md`. One component set — a node per step kind, a decision's outcomes as ports, a component per
way on, terminals that tell the configured end from *No further step* and from a recorded *Finished* — draws the place's chart, the
editor, a run and a proposal's diff, on `design/Flow`'s measured layout, always top to bottom, positions never saved. A person
composes on the chart: *＋* on a way, a searchable step picker, drag from a port or a keyboard pick mode, one inspector, undo; the
kind of each wire follows from the way and where its target stands, never chosen. Ask Daoris is told the open draft and proposes
against its fingerprint through `workflow_propose`'s new `draft` door; its proposal is drawn on the chart as a diff the person takes
whole or change by change, and nothing is saved until *Save*.

**Why.** Setting work up is mostly a flowchart, and the owner wants it designed as one, with the help that workflow builders' copilots
give. Both sealed proposals reached the same shape independently; the patterns people know from flow editors are taken where a
governed record of at most 24 steps needs them, and the free canvas's are refused (the design's §5).

**Rejected.** Adopting React Flow (a second renderer, a transformed viewport and stored coordinates over a layout D186 measured; it
is reopened only on a measured need, §6). Free placement, a canvas zoom, a minimap and *Tidy*. An assistant that saves, chooses or
runs a workflow. A separate reads round before building: both makers' reads run beside the first rows and are settled on main.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (FLOWCHART2d, built): Ask Daoris is told the open draft.** The editor registers its draft (`help/openDraft.ts`); its fingerprint is the exact graph's digest, key order aside (`workflow/draftFingerprint.ts`, `g1-` and 64-bit FNV-1a), the key the editor's judgement takes too (§8.7). `SESSION_INPUT`'s `draft` carries the graph, the place, the source and save target, the base, route, pipeline, selected step and open problems, sent with the person's words only where the fingerprint moved since the conversation was told; past 48 KiB it is refused whole in its sentence, never cut. The host adds the compact palette (`WorkflowPalette.Compact`), handed again only when its digest moves; where the person is says a draft is open, ahead of what the 1200-character bound could cut. The record keeps it as told, `chat.told-draft` with the draft's JSON last, which `WorkflowDraftTold.Told` reads back by fingerprint. Proof: `WorkflowDraftToldTests`, `openDraft.test.ts`, `where.test.ts`, `AskDaoris.test.tsx`, the room's goldens.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (FLOWCHART2e, built): the `draft` door saves nothing.** `workflow_propose`'s `draft` is the workflow kind's fourth door, judged before any save or use: a whole graph of either pipeline against the draft the proposing conversation's record was told under that fingerprint (one never told is refused), a base, `choose`, a route or a name beside it refused, each save refusal on the card to put right (`Problems`), its changes `WorkflowGraphDiff`'s (steps by id, ways by structure then id with a moved id shown, every way field, the entries and the end, each change's `needs`). The candidate rides on the card's `Proposed`, never `Graph`, so `Saves` stays false. Its Apply writes no workflow and no choice: from the card it opens the place's Workflow and stands; the editor's press naming every change taken or left settles it, the file keeping `taken` and `left`. Proof: `HelpWorkflowProposalsTests`, `WorkflowGraphDiffTests`, `HelpDraftDoorTests` through the bridge, the tool's description test, `HelpCoverageTests`' draft door.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (FLOWCHART2a, built): the chart's components.** `design/FlowCanvas` is `Flow` split into the layout, ink and keys with `renderNode`, `renderLabel` and `selected` (`Flow` is the canvas with its generic box, its other callers unchanged); an edge may name a `port`, each port a path of its own, and a `data` edge is read by no layout and drawn only while a flow given `selected` has an end selected, its lanes kept free at the right either way. `workflow/chart/` holds `StepNode` per variant, `PortRow` (a port is a foot line made visible, the foot omitting it), `Terminal` (◎ and a run's *Finished* only; the configured end stays a mark on its step), `HoldsGroup` and `EdgeChip` (a `when` has none, a ⚠ problem chip in the danger ink); `graphFlow` hands them each step's variant, ports and data wires, an outcome routed `back` is a routed port with no ◎, and the place's chart and the run view draw through `FlowChart`. The measured check gained each port path's start inside its port's bottom edge. The icons for a decision, a plugin step, the checks, the line and your review are new; no new words.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (FLOWCHART2b, built): the editor composes on its chart.** `workflow/edit/` is the page, `EditCanvas`, `Inspector` with `WaysOnTable`, `StepPicker` and `SaveReview`, `GapInsert` beside the components. `graphDraft` gives a way's kind from its row and its target (an ancestor through `after`, `trigger`, `data` or `when` is taken back to; one that would close a cycle, or a second way back on one result, is never offered), and its insert, connect, retarget, move, duplicate, remove-and-join, carry, clipboard and history are proved over every shipped graph against a mirror of `NodeWires`' refusals. `FlowCanvas` gains `renderInsert` (placed by `placeInserts`, which takes an empty chip as no room) and `pick`; Edit's undo, redo, copy and paste reach a region marked `data-edit-history` (`menus/editing.ts`). The empty draft's presets and saved workflows replace *Start from*; the id is made from the name; the editor registers its open draft (FLOWCHART2d).

**Note, 2026-11-02 (FLOWCHART2c, built): the driver's judgement is on the chart, each answer of one draft.** The bridge keys a judgement by `fingerprintOf` the graph it was asked of (`judgedOf`, `judgeFailed`; the newest request alone is heard); the editor reads it only while the draft it holds is that one, so an edit invalidates it before the debounce, a failed request leaves it invalid (*could not be judged*), and undoing to the judged draft takes it back (SETUPUX1s's first clause). `edit/refusalMarks.ts` places each refusal at the way it names if that is drawn (a ⚠ *To put right* chip, the sentence in the source's foot and description), else at the step (declined's border, the sentence in danger ink and in its description); a refusal inside the holds opens them and keeps them open. The head's `ToPutRight` lists each with a door, and *Ask why* and *Fix it* through `help/askFill.ts`, a door the window registers where it holds Ask Daoris; without one the page offers neither.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (FLOWCHART2f, built): a proposal is drawn on the chart and taken change by change.** Each draft told is kept by its fingerprint with the conversations told it (`help/openDraft.ts`), and the editor's bridge reads those conversations' `draft`-door cards for its place (`useDraftProposals`); the proposal's base is the graph told, so a window that no longer holds it offers nothing to take. `edit/proposalTake.ts` takes a change with its closure, the driver's `needs` and what the page ties to it (a removed step to the end it held, a `land`'s method to the tail steps added or removed and the ways reaching them, a group's ways to each other), atomically against each change's precondition (the step, the way or the end as the base held it), refusing at the change whose base the draft no longer holds or where the result would leave a way to a missing step; proved over every pair of shipped graphs against a mirror of `WorkflowGraphDiff`. The union draws open changes on the draft (a step *new*, *to remove* dashed, *changed*; a way *new* or *to remove* in its chip and cause, one that keeps its ends drawn once *changed*); `ProposalBar` names the agent and models, `ChangeList` words each change with *Take*, *Leave* and, stale, *Ask again* through `askFill`. Each take and leave is one history step with the proposal's record; once every change is named, `HELP_APPLY{taken, left}` goes once, *Not now* is `HELP_DISMISS`, and the card in Ask Daoris says its changes with *Open in the editor*. Proof: `proposalTake.test.ts`, `WorkflowEditor.proposal.test.tsx`, `ProposalCard.test.tsx`, `help.test.ts`, `openDraft.test.ts`, the measured `proposal/*` stories.
