## D223 — A workflow is drawn and composed as a flowchart of Daoris's own components, and Ask Daoris composes against the open draft (2026-11-02)

**Decision (FLOWCHART2, the owner's direction of 2026-11-02; co-designed under D191, accepted by the steward 2026-11-02).** Per
`docs/2026-11-02-flowchart-design.md`. One component set — a node per step kind, a decision's outcomes as ports, a component per
way on, terminals that tell the configured end from *No further step* and from a recorded *Finished* — draws the place's chart, the
editor, a run and a proposal's diff, on `design/Flow`'s measured layout, always top to bottom, positions never saved. A person
composes on the chart: *＋* on a way, a searchable step picker, drag from a port or a keyboard pick mode, one inspector, undo; the
kind of each wire follows from the way and where its target stands, never chosen. Ask Daoris is told the open draft and proposes
against its fingerprint through `workflow_propose`'s new `draft` door; its proposal is drawn on the chart as a diff the person takes
whole or change by change, and nothing is saved until *Save*.

**Why.** Setting work up is mostly a flowchart, and the owner wants it designed as one, with the help that workflow builders' copilots
give. Both sealed proposals reached the same shape independently; the patterns people know from flow editors are taken where a
governed record of at most 24 steps needs them, and the free canvas's are refused (the design's §5).

**Rejected.** Adopting React Flow (a second renderer, a transformed viewport and stored coordinates over a layout D186 measured; it
is reopened only on a measured need, §6). Free placement, a canvas zoom, a minimap and *Tidy*. An assistant that saves, chooses or
runs a workflow. A separate reads round before building: both makers' reads run beside the first rows and are settled on main.
