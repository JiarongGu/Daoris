## D246 — A run's words are two named ports, one per transport: a native door's lines on `DoorInput`, a protocol door's requests on `ProtocolWords` (2026-11-06)

**Decision (AGENTPROVIDER1m-ii).** What `DoorInput` carries for a protocol door is nothing: a run's words reach its door through the port
its transport has, and `AgentRun` names both.

1. **A native door's lines are `DoorInput`'s** (D243): a line is written to the process, acknowledged once the harness names the conversation
   it went to, and replayed to the next process where that one never named it.
2. **A protocol door's words are `ProtocolWords`'**: its prompt, a word said into a running turn and the steer are each a request the
   session answers, acknowledged by that answer (the turn's stop reason, which the turn awaits), and retried on the same session within the
   turn (the transient episode), never on a next process.
3. **Each door's other port refuses or is absent.** A protocol door's `DoorInput` refuses a line, a held word and a control line before
   anything is written, naming the ability (`a line written to a protocol door's process`); a native door has no `ProtocolWords`.
   `AgentRun` keeps no word operation of its own: `SendAsync`, a second way to say a line on the input, and `SteerAsync`, which no door
   admitted, are gone.

**Rejected.**
- **One input stream for both doors, a protocol word acknowledged by its request's answer.** The queue's one rule, that a word written and
  not acknowledged is replayed to the next process, is wrong on the protocol door: a prompt whose word the session took and whose request
  then failed is recovered as that turn's failure (`RecoveredAsync`), never said again, and its retry is the same session's, so the stream
  would either replay a word the conversation took or hold a second rule per door inside it. The answer the turn awaits has no place in a
  line's delivery, and the steer, the next-step word and the transient episode are no lines at all, so they would stay on a port of their
  own beside the stream anyway: one name over two contracts, which D228 counts as a second way to do one job.
