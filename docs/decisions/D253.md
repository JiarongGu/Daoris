## D253 — A session's access is a tier of one reach: at full access it acts, below it asks, nothing is refused by construction; Ask Daoris acts as the person across every workspace (2026-11-10)

**Decision.** Full access (D183) was meant to let Ask Daoris act on this machine itself, Daoris's own commands among it. The person
key (D156's PERSONDOOR1g, 2026-11-08) then made every session's shell a keyless agent, so a full-access conversation's `daoris` verbs
were refused *only the person can…* or *this terminal is a session's*, or sent to the window as one card per press. A day's work in the
install showed the cost: nine separate pause cards for one *stop these*, a repository's retire, a plugin's replacement and a git identity
refused outright, the Accept unreachable from any terminal, and a search across workspaces refused, so the person relayed a request by
hand between two workspaces' rooms. Ask Daoris is the person's front desk; a desk that needs a click for each act it was trusted with
blocks the work it exists to do.

1. **Full access is the person's delegation to Ask Daoris.** Each full-access start is handed a delegate key, drawn at that start, kept
   by Daoris as its hash in memory against the conversation, stripped from every other start, as the holder key is (D208). A person's
   press a `daoris` verb sends over the pipe with the key's proof over its connection's nonce is the person's press already confirmed:
   no card, run at once, attributed to Ask Daoris and its conversation (the machine log, the conversation's console, the window's line of
   what it pressed). The key ends with the conversation's process and with any change of its access; restricted mode is handed none.
2. **What the person can press, Ask Daoris in full access can press.** Every verb that refuses a session's terminal reads the delegate
   first. A service door that is the person's (a retire, a set-up step, an ask's workflow and review choice, a quest's done, accept and
   delete) is reached through the running Daoris, which makes the call with the person key. The Accept of a run's review has a terminal
   verb. A plugin's replacement and a git identity go through their routes. The routes' own judges still stand: the floor that keeps
   work from being lost (tracked paths, a force over what Daoris did not make) and the person's own deny rules.
3. **One conversation, every workspace within reach.** A conversation still belongs to the workspace it started in: its room, its first
   lines and its history are that workspace's (D158's ASKSCOPE1 points 1-3, 6). Its tools read another workspace when it names one and
   every workspace when it asks `all`, in both modes; a proposal may name a repository of another workspace; in full access its acts
   reach any workspace as its terminal already does.
4. **A block is a defect.** Where Ask Daoris cannot finish what it was asked, it says what refused it and the one press that would let
   it; a refusal that is neither the person's rule nor the floor is filed and fixed, not worked around.
5. **Tiers: full access acts, restricted asks, nothing is refused by construction.** The modes are tiers of one reach, and they differ
   only in who says yes. Full access: Ask Daoris's own yes (point 1). Restricted: the person's. A restricted start holds a delegate key
   too, and a person's press it makes is put to the person on a card naming Ask Daoris and the act, and runs on their yes. A tool call
   outside its allow-list is held for the person (allow once, or refuse), as a driven session's is under asking (D184's AUTONOMY1b3a), on
   either door, never refused at once. Configured access is the tiers between: each grant moves a family of acts from asked to allowed,
   in the shape of a terminal's standing confirmation (D201). The proposals and the asked press converge to one way to ask the person
   (D228), which ASKFULL1g designs.
6. **Every session Daoris starts holds the same tiers.** A driven session and a conversation are handed a delegate key of their own at
   each start, as Ask Daoris is, and their repository's access setup is their tier: unrestricted acts with no card; asking and the judged
   default put each Daoris press, and each tool call their harness would refuse, to the person, or to the supervisor that decides
   `allow` at the session's place where one is staffed (D233), on either door, never refused at once. A hold's bound still ends one
   nobody answered, said as such. What differs is whose act it is: Ask Daoris acts on the person's words, so its presses are the
   person's; a working session's are its own, attributed to it, and make nothing the owner's (the steward's acts are the shape, D209).
   A session still asks another repository's work of it through a quest; a delegate press acts on Daoris's records, never on another
   repository's files.

*Rejected:*
- **Handing Ask Daoris the person key itself.** It would reach every host door with no attribution, could not end with the mode, and the
  key is the window's alone (PERSONDOOR1g).
- **One Apply for several proposals.** Still a click for each task the person already asked for.
- **Ask Daoris as the steward.** The steward holds the acts a role grants, from a terminal of read tools; full access is the person's
  whole reach, chosen by the person.
- **Restricted mode refusing what it lacks** (D89, D52's refusal of Ask Daoris's asks, D184's exception for its modes). The person had
  no way to say yes, so a task that needed one more act could not be done in that mode at all.
- **Keeping the workspace fence on reads** (ASKSCOPE1 point 5). It made the person carry requests between rooms by hand; the room's
  focus keeps a conversation on its own workspace without refusing the rest.

**Amends** D183 point 1 (*a push or a key stays the person's* in full access: the delegate key is Daoris's to hand, the person's own
key never leaves the window), D156 (a press proved by the delegate is confirmed without a card), D158's ASKSCOPE1 points 4-5 (the
tools' refusal of another workspace goes; the room's projection stays its focus), and the roles area's rule 55 (in full access Ask
Daoris presses a run's Accept itself), D89 and D184's AUTONOMY1b3a (Ask Daoris's restricted asks are held for the person, not
refused), and the agents area's rule 23 (a permission request reaching the driver is held on every door and mode, not refused).
**Held by:** ASKFULL1a-h.
