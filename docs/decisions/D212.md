## D212 — The foundation: who acts is a participant read from the records, its kind by one rule; where a line's work goes next is one pure decision both loops call, its account by one rule and every move one durable record that owns its delivery; what is said is one closed vocabulary of events, minted once at the commit or at the judgement that owns the fact, every telling a projection of it; reached in rows, the first the account rule (2026-10-25)

**Decision (FOUNDATION1, proposed 2026-10-25).** **Status: proposed**, round 1 under D191: the synthesis of three sealed
proposals (Claude's, Codex's, and an independent Opus reader's in Fable's place under D191's amendment of 2026-10-23), each
written from one brief and one survey. It changes a foundation (the records' data model, a protocol between processes,
concurrency, and decided rules other designs rest on), and some of its rows grant or refuse authority or delete records, so by
D191's note of 2026-10-24 it is not accepted on one maker's read: Codex reads it, an independent Opus reader reads it as a
foundation, and the steward accepts only after both. The contract is `docs/2026-10-25-foundation-design.md`; its §1 compares the
proposals question by question and says what each choice rejects, and its §0.2 corrects the brief, the survey and the proposals
against the code on main at `92ac2bd7e`.

The owner directed on 2026-10-25 that Daoris, grown through many foundational designs each with a mechanism of its own, be given
a structure that names the Fabric, the actor roles and the event system, designed under D191 and never built as a big-bang
rewrite. Read at `92ac2bd7e` (the survey, `docs/2026-10-25-foundation-survey.md`, read at `0e195ee61`): thirteen kinds of
participant, each with its own rule, admission, choice and record; fourteen paths in two loops each choosing an agent, an
account, a conversation and a context its own way; fifteen channels each with its own kinds, ids, order and readers; and 51 of the
273 fix-log entries of 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-25 from two of them answering one question differently.

1. **A participant is a read, never a store** (design §2.1). One function builds it from the stores that hold each kind, in the
   driver library, and its twin in the service answers the connector; its fields are its id (the record's, unchanged on every wire,
   or the identity Daoris already issues a brainstorm voice, a holder or a native child), its kind, whom it serves, its line, D205's
   actor as provenance, its runtime binding, its visibility and its authority. It is D173 ADR-B's `Participant` and
   `RuntimeBinding`. No binding table is added until a subject needs an issued identity.
2. **Its kind is read from the marks by one rule, never stored** (§2.2), as D203 point 1 has it: `teammate`, `reviewer`, `partner`,
   `helper`, `intake`, `ask-daoris`, `chat`, `build`, and `voice`, `holder` and `child` for the subjects with no record. `actor`
   stays D205's provenance, held equal to the kind by a census. The page reads the kind pushed, judged in .NET; it never travels.
   The cap's *the driver's own session* is a property of the kind.
3. **Admission is four layers, a table each, keyed by the kind** (§2.3): may it start (the driver), may it call (the connector),
   may it act (the pipe), is it contained (the launcher, unchanged), sharing one classification and answering closed refusal codes;
   a session Daoris names that the connector cannot place is served reads and refused every mutation. A kind's policy is one shape
   read from today's keys unchanged, common fields and a typed requirement per kind. The person sees a partner drawn as a partner,
   Ask Daoris refused `helper_ask`, and a reviewer's and a voice's mutations refused at the server on every door.
4. **Every start goes through one start plan** (§2.4) whose answer is a settled selection carried to the open, counted once; one
   walk, one set of pass codes, one re-ask; a continuation takes its record's agent and asked choice. A role holder and a meeting are
   the two starts the census names outside it.
5. **A role is held by a participant and grants nothing by its actor** (§2.5): its authority is the appointment's, proven per act;
   the two role doors become one act form and one proof.
6. **Where a line's next words or work go is one pure decision**, `Course.Decide` (§3.1), which the look calls for a driven line and
   the chat runner for a conversation, each executing it as today. Its answers are resume, move (when, where, how), wait, hold and
   never, each with the facts it read, which the executor checks before it acts. The transient retry stays inside the run.
7. **The account rule is stated once** (§3.2): a resume asks for its own account; where that account cannot carry the words and an
   account the line may take is ready, the line goes on now in a new part there, for driven lines, conversations, Ask Daoris and a
   closed quest's session alike (LIMITSWITCH1's decision, LIMITSWITCH2 inside it); otherwise it waits for the earliest reset of
   exactly the accounts it may take, an account that cannot run adding none. An account the person picked and the record keeps waits
   for its own; an agent pick walks its agent's accounts; a record with no kept account pick is an agent pick. A new part takes one
   settled selection, the briefed move included.
8. **Every move writes one durable move record** (§3.3), `<home>/sessions/<old record>.move.json`, owning its delivery and recovery
   through its phases, written and taken up by the executor of its kind alone: a conversation's by the shell's chat runner, a
   driven line's by the look of the loop that holds the home's lease. The destination is opened under the move's key, so a lost
   answer never opens a second. Today's boxes become its adapters; SWITCHCTX1's carry is its `how: carry` and its two carry phases.
9. **Words hold across a move by construction** (§3.4): a word keeps its whole identity wherever it is held; the line's head is
   judged inside the say door's one exclusive step; a move reads the old part's words after the new record's open and moves each by
   id; every word is kept on `said` before anything holds it; an attempt is acknowledged before the wire and a receipt kept after
   the take, so a word once attempted is never sent again and an uncertain one is said; one predicate says which words a new part
   carries.
10. **D210's four kinds are the decision's failure input** for every door and kind of run (§3.5).
11. **An event is one envelope with two minters and three orders** (§4.1): a record event minted by trigger in the statement that
    writes the record, into a journal in the service's database that never travels, so every writer mints it; a machine event by
    the hub of the process that runs the work, at the judgement that owns the fact, the lease's loop alone telling plugins and
    outside readers. A store's position, a hub's run and sequence, and a slice's epoch and sequence order three things and are never
    one. Records stay the truth: nothing is replayed into state.
12. **One closed vocabulary**, `EventCodes` beside `NoteCodes` and extending EVENTHOOK1's grammar (§4.2): each code with its facts,
    privacy class and rendering family, subject-specific where facts differ; an end one event with its note's reason and one class,
    today's four sets of *ended* four declared filters of it. A sentence is composed per reader and language, never parsed back.
13. **Every channel that tells that something happened is a projection** (§4.3): pushed slices stay state, command and held
    answers stay answers, the event file, the lineage and the channel stay stores; the notices have one owner to which every
    teller submits; news is told once by a durable said-set, never replayed (§4.5).
14. **The names** (§6): *Fabric*, *participant*, *move* and *event* are code and prose only, with no Chinese until one reaches the
    page; D203's helper, partner and reviewer are a participant's *kind*; the appointed role is 职务 on the page and in the glossary,
    retiring the 角色 it shares with *actor*; the line of work stays the session (会话) and `Entry` in code.
15. **Reached in rows** (§10), FOUNDATION1a to 1v, each its own branch behind today's tests: the account rule first, then a word's
    identity, then the participant's kind. Rows q, r, t and v grant or refuse authority or delete, and are built only after both
    makers' reads.

**Why.** The defects the survey counts come where two mechanisms answer one question; a question answered once, by a pure function
both loops call, cannot be answered twice. Every proposal reached a read over today's stores, because a new store would be a
second truth (D173's first guard), and one decision with many executors, because the disagreement is in the deciding, not the
doing. The kind is read because the marks are what every record of this machine carries and what admission keys on, and `actor`
is provenance. The move record is a file its one executor writes, as the boxes it generalizes are. A trigger mints for every
writer, an older connector included, as the revisions already do, and a diff of snapshots misses what happens between two of
them. The account rule is first because one function removes the class of LIMITSWITCH1, LIMITRESUME1b, LIMITRESUME1c and
LIMITGOON1 and builds what the owner decided.

**Rejected** (design §1.1, §9): a stored kind, the kind read from `actor`; binding tables before a subject needs one; a flat rule
shape that drops typed requirements; one admission judge across processes; the brainstorm's `off` honoured inside an extraction;
one loop for both kinds of line; every pick waiting, or every pick moving; the service owning move records; recovery of a
conversation's move by the lease holder; a move record for a same-conversation resume; a receiver judged outside the say door's
step; minting by diffing snapshots or in the driver's client; changes in memory only; a new event frame to the page; an
event-sourced database; one writer of the notices in the lease's loop; Chinese for words never shown, and 任命职责.
