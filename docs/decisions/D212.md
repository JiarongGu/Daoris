a structure that names the Fabric, the actor roles and the event system, designed under D191 and never built as a big-bang

273 fix-log entries of 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-25 from two of them answering one question differently.

1. **A participant is a read, never a store** (design §2.1). One function builds it from the stores that hold each kind of subject,
   in the driver library, and its twin in the service answers the connector; its fields are its id (the record's, unchanged on every
   wire, or the identity Daoris already issues a brainstorm voice, a holder or a native child), its work, its role, whom it serves, its
   line, its runtime binding, its visibility and its authority. It is D173 ADR-B's `Participant` and `RuntimeBinding`. No binding table
   is added until a subject needs an issued identity.
2. **Its work is a capability read from the marks by one rule, never stored** (§2.2), as D203 point 1 has it: `review`, `partner`,
   `help`, `intake`, `ask-daoris`, `chat`, `build` and `brainstorm`, with another machine's, a holder and a child placed with no work
   here; `design` is admitted as `build`. `actor` names the configured role the start walked, D205's provenance, held to the work by a
   census. The page reads the work pushed as `work`, an additive field beside the wire's `kind` (driven or chat), judged in .NET, with
   an older host's fallback; it never travels. The cap's *the driver's own session* is a property of the work.
3. **Admission is four layers, a table each, keyed by capability** (§2.3): may it start (the driver), may it call (the connector), may
   it act (the pipe), how it is contained (the launcher, unchanged: a piece fails closed, an ordinary start runs as today where the
   launcher cannot contain it), sharing one classification and answering closed refusal codes; a session Daoris names that the
   connector cannot place is served reads and refused every mutation, a brainstorm voice placed first through its box and served its
   result tool. A work's policy is one shape read from today's keys unchanged, common fields and a typed requirement per work. The
   person sees a partner drawn as a partner, Ask Daoris refused `helper_ask`, and a reviewer's and a voice's calls beyond their handed
   rules refused at the server on every door.
4. **Every start goes through one start plan** (§2.4), which finds the role that takes its work in its place and whose answer is a
   settled selection carried to the open, its reservation counted at the selection and a probe uncounted; one walk, one set of pass
   codes, one re-ask; a continuation takes its record's role and asked choice. A role holder and a meeting are the two starts the census
   names outside it.
5. **An actor and an appointed role are one configured record, the role** (§2.5, §2.7): an actor a role always on that takes work, the
   supervisor and the steward roles filled by appointment that hold acts. A holder's authority is the appointment's, proven per act; the
   two role doors become one act form and one proof that checks the act against the appointed role's authority.
6. **Where a line's next words or work go is one pure decision**, `Course.Decide` (§3.1), which the look calls for a driven line and
   the chat runner for a conversation, each executing it as today. Its answers are resume, move (when, where, how), start (a new line
   with no `from`, for start-from), wait, hold and never, each with the facts it read, the words' state among them, which the executor
   checks before it acts; its facts include the answers-alone hold and the boundary the line stands at; a move standing makes its old
   part's own go-on never; each automatic hand-over passes the gate its door has today. The transient retry stays inside the run.
7. **The account rule is stated once** (§3.2): a resume asks for its own account; where that account cools and an account the line may
   take is ready, the line goes on now in a new part there, for a driven record, a repository conversation and a closed quest's session
   (LIMITSWITCH1's decision, LIMITSWITCH2 inside it); Ask Daoris waits for its own outside a limit, and a conversation whose own account
   is refused, signed out or gone keeps today's door, each an open question for the owner (design §8). Otherwise it waits for the
   earliest reset of exactly the accounts it may take, an account that cannot run adding none, and where none of them has a reset it holds
   naming the repair. An account the person picked and the record keeps waits for its own, the person's hand-over press included; an
   agent pick walks its agent's accounts; a record with no kept account pick is an agent pick; the other doors' named accounts keep today's
   reading. A new part takes one settled selection, the briefed move and both limit moves included.
8. **Every move writes one durable move record** (§3.3), `<home>/sessions/<old record>.move.json`, owning its delivery and recovery
   through its phases (from `old-ended` for a move from an ended record), written under a fenced claim and taken up first at its come-up
   by the executor of its kind alone: a conversation's by the shell's chat runner, a driven line's by the look of the loop that holds the
   home's lease. The destination is opened atomically under the move's key in the service, so a lost answer never opens a second; against
   an older host a lost open is held. Today's boxes become its adapters, one recovery authority at each phase; SWITCHCTX1's carry is its
   `how: carry` and its two carry phases.
9. **Words hold across a move by construction** (§3.4): a word keeps its whole identity wherever it is held; the line's head is judged
   inside the say door's one exclusive step by one service function across kinds; a move reads the old part's words after the new
   record's open and moves each, under its producer key with a transfer id, in one transaction, ahead of later words; every word is kept
   on `said` before anything holds it, once the course holds the moving record's old part; an attempt is acknowledged word by word before
   the wire, each attempted word kept with one disposition (taken, refused at a limit and carried once, uncertain), and a receipt kept after
   the take, so a word once attempted is never sent again by itself and an uncertain one is said; one predicate says which words a new part
   carries.
10. **D210's four kinds are the decision's failure input** for every door and kind of run (§3.5).
11. **An event is one envelope with two minters and three orders** (§4.1): a record event a raw transition journalled by trigger in the
    statement that writes the record, guarded by what changed, never by a mirror, never raising, into a bounded journal in the service's
    database that never travels and wakes its readers by a family of its own, coded in .NET at projection; a machine event minted by the
    hub of the process that runs the work, at the judgement that owns the fact, the lease's loop alone telling plugins and outside
    readers; every process reading the journal from its come-up. A store's position, a hub's run and sequence, and a slice's epoch and
    sequence order three things and are never one. Records stay the truth: nothing is replayed into state, and what a trim loses is said.
12. **One closed vocabulary**, `EventCodes` beside `NoteCodes` and extending EVENTHOOK1's grammar (§4.2): each code with its facts,
    privacy class and rendering family, subject-specific where facts differ; an end one event with its note's reason and one class,
    today's four sets of *ended* four declared filters of it. A sentence is composed per reader and language, never parsed back.
13. **Every channel that tells that something happened is a projection** (§4.3): pushed slices stay state, command and held answers stay
    answers, the event file, the lineage and the channel stay stores; the notices have one owner to which every teller submits, each with
    its own cursor; news is told at most once by a durable said-set claimed before the showing, never replayed (§4.5).
14. **The names** (§6): *Fabric*, *participant*, *capability*, *move* and *event* are code and prose only, with no Chinese until one reaches
    the page; a participant's capability is its *work*, D203's helper, partner and reviewer among them; the role is 角色, one concept of
    which D205's actor and D208's appointed role are cases, so round 1's 职务 is withdrawn; the line of work stays the session (会话) and
    `Entry` in code.
15. **Reached in rows** (§10), FOUNDATION1a-i to 1y-ii, each its own branch behind today's tests: the account rule first, split in three,
    then a word's identity, then the participant's work; attempts and receipts before the move record, the keep for a moving record after
    the course reads move records; the company's rows after the start plan. Rows q, r, t, v and y-ii grant or refuse authority or delete,
    and are built only after both makers' reads.
16. **Capabilities in code, roles in configuration** (§2.7; the owner's direction of 2026-10-25, the brief §8). The code keeps one closed
    vocabulary of capabilities: the work a participant can take, the acts an appointed role may make, the doors each may use and the
    containment each runs under, with each capability's admission. A role is a configured record in `driver.json`'s `roles`, judged by one
    judge for every door: its name in English and Chinese, the work it takes, its staffing (D205's ordered choices), its authority (acts, and
    doors only narrowing its work's), its scope (the machine, a workspace or a repository) and how it is filled (always on, or appointed). D205's
    eight actors and the supervisor and the steward are the default company, a seed read where `roles` names nothing, so an upgrade writes and
    changes nothing. The person adds, changes or removes a role, or Ask Daoris proposes and the person's press applies; a role gaining authority
    needs the person's own press in the window; an owner's act stays the owner's whatever a role says, and appointing stays the owner's press
    in the window alone. MODELROLE1l's Design actor becomes a configured *Designer* role taking `design`, the only code its capability and the
    quest's mark.

**Why.** The defects the survey counts come where two mechanisms answer one question; a question answered once, by a pure function
both loops call, cannot be answered twice. Every proposal reached a read over today's stores, because a new store would be a
second truth (D173's first guard), and one decision with many executors, because the disagreement is in the deciding, not the
doing. The work is read because the marks are what every record of this machine carries and what admission keys on, and `actor`
is provenance. A company's positions are set up, not compiled, so what the code fixes is what each capability may do, and a role
composes capabilities it can narrow and never extend. The move record is a file its one executor writes under a fenced claim, as the
boxes it generalizes are, with the one transactional need, a destination never opened twice, met in the service. A trigger journals
for every writer, an older connector included, as the revisions already do, and a diff of snapshots misses what happens between two
of them. The account rule is first because one function removes the class of LIMITSWITCH1, LIMITRESUME1b, LIMITRESUME1c and LIMITGOON1
and builds what the owner decided.

tables keyed by a fixed list of kinds; hard-coded actors and roles, a role widening its work's doors, and a role holding an owner's act; the
brainstorm's `off` honoured inside an extraction; one loop for both kinds of line; start-from as a move; every pick waiting, or every pick
moving; a manufactured reset; the service owning move records' persistence; recovery of a conversation's move by the lease holder; an older
host's lost open searched for by `from` and moment; a move record for a same-conversation resume; a receiver judged outside the say door's
step; a moved word under the move's key alone; minting by diffing snapshots, in the driver's client or in SQL; changes in memory only; a new
event frame to the page; an event-sourced database; one writer of the notices in the lease's loop; news told exactly once; Chinese for words
never shown; 任命职责 and 职务.
