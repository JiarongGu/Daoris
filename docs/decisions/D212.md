1. **A participant is a read, never a store** (design §2.1). One function builds it from the stores that hold each kind of subject,
   in the driver library, and its twin in the service answers the connector; its fields are its id (the record's, unchanged on every

   line, its runtime binding, its visibility and its authority. It is D173 ADR-B's `Participant` and `RuntimeBinding`. Its binding, today
   `<s>.harness.json`, moves into the desktop store as its one home (point 17); no table of issued identities is added until a subject needs one.
2. **Its work is a capability read from the marks by one rule, never stored** (§2.2), as D203 point 1 has it: `review`, `partner`,
   `help`, `intake`, `ask-daoris`, `chat`, `build` and `brainstorm`, with another machine's, a holder and a child placed with no work
   here; `design` is admitted as `build`. `actor` names the seeded always-on role of the start's work, so every build's continuation reads an
   id its run knows; the role a start took and its revision are kept on its binding, held to the work by a census. The page reads the work
   pushed as `work`, an additive field beside the wire's `kind` (driven or chat), judged in .NET, with an older host's fallback; it never
   travels. The cap's *the driver's own session* is a property of the work.
3. **Admission is four layers, a table each, keyed by capability** (§2.3): may it start (the driver), may it call (the connector), may

4. **Every start goes through one start plan** (§2.4), which finds the role that takes its work in its place and whose answer is a

5. **An actor and an appointed role are one configured concept, the role, in two exclusive shapes** (§2.5, §2.7): a working role takes work

   appointment and widens none, an increase waiting for the next *Appoint*. The two role doors become one act form and one proof.
6. **Where a line's next words or work go is one pure decision**, `Course.Decide` (§3.1), which the look calls for a driven line and

7. **The account rule is stated once** (§3.2): a resume asks for its own account; where that account cools and an account the line may

8. **Every move keeps one durable move record** (§3.3), a row of the desktop store, owning its delivery and recovery through its phases

**Why.** The defects the survey counts come where two mechanisms answer one question; a question answered once, by a pure function
both loops call, cannot be answered twice. Every proposal reached a read over today's stores, because a new store would be a
second truth (D173's first guard), and one decision with many executors, because the disagreement is in the deciding, not the
doing. The work is read because the marks are what every record of this machine carries and what admission keys on, and `actor`
is provenance. The positions of D193's company are set up, not compiled, so what the code fixes is what each capability may do, and a
role composes capabilities and never extends them; an appointment's grant is the owner's, so no edit of configuration can widen it. The
move record is a store row its one executor writes under its role's claim, with a mark before each effect, since a guarded file write fences
no spawn or prompt; the destination never opened twice is the service's, which moves the words in the same step, and an old box beside the
record would be a second recovery authority. One desktop store gives the move, its words and their receipts one transaction, and gives the
Fabric's records, the role catalogue and the settings one place to be read, set and managed, while the service keeps its own. A trigger
journals for every writer, an older connector included, as the revisions already do, with the snapshot its coding needs, since a diff of
snapshots or a later lookup misses what happens between two of them. The driver's folders form one cycle, so a map with one direction, held
by a census and reached a subdomain at a time, is the one that can land in steps. The account rule is first because one function removes
the class of LIMITSWITCH1, LIMITRESUME1b, LIMITRESUME1c and LIMITGOON1 and builds what the owner decided.
