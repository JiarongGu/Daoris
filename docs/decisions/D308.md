## D308 — A consultation records its mode and whether it was independent (2026-11-16)

**Decision.** Each consultation (a brainstorm, a fresh design, a second opinion, a helper's answer) records its mode (`independent`:
from one neutral brief, no sight of another answer; `review`: reads a candidate and its evidence), each giver's maker and account, a
digest of what it read, whether it saw another answer, and the decider's one line on whether it changed the decision. Independence is
recorded, never assumed: another family where one is ready, its own session, no sight of the other's answer; whichever did not hold
is said.

**Why.** Two designs from one maker were counted as two views, and an outside briefing that had read the decision it judged was
offered as a third vote.

**Rejected:** a second agent for every judgment (doctrine rule 3's two reviews before landing, and brainstorms for foundations, stay
as they are).
