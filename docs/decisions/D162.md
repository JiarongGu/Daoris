## D162 — A conversation starts from a prompt in a repository, and a record's head holds its buttons still (2026-10-12)

**Decision (the owner's direction, designed with Codex).** The owner, 2026-10-12: *"we can design the user entry (UI/UX)
more like how ChatGPT (Codex) does it, to add and select a repo to start a chat … currently I can only Ask Daoris and let
that decide"*, and *"the title section for quest needs to be updated (some titles just way too long, and also the buttons'
location) … the session title section too … please co-work with Codex for design decisions"*. Codex reviewed both
read-only; the design, its sketches and its rows are `docs/2026-10-12-entry-and-heads-design.md`.

1. **A conversation starts from its first words, in a repository the person picks.** Sessions' *New conversation* is a
   prompt with three chips (repository, tree, agent and account) and *Start*; it opens the conversation on those words.
   The repository picker adds or imports a repository without losing the draft. Ask Daoris stays the general front door
   (D161 ENTRY1): its go may fill this composer, and the composer hands a draft that is a task to Ask Daoris.
2. **`START_CHAT` carries the opening words and a request id**, and the same id never starts twice; the tree choice means
   what it says.
3. **A head's title is bounded and its buttons hold still.** Two lines for a quest's and an ask's, one for a session's; the
   whole request is one press away in the body, and a session no longer repeats it. The head lays out by the main area's
   width: a fixed acts column beside the title above 640 px, an acts row under the facts at or below it; primary,
   secondary, then ⋯.
4. **An ask heads as a quest does and carries a short title** (at most 40 characters, the asker's words).

**Rejected:** Ask/Code modes on the composer; a kind or branch picker in it; keeping Ask Daoris as the only door that takes
words (D161's rejection of a second entry point, now reversed for this one start); a first-clause rule for short titles on
the page; an expanding head. The design's §3 says why.

**Amends:** D161 point 3 and its *Rejected: a second entry point*; D152 §7 and the UX7 design's §5 (the title sections);
D55's *Start session* drawer, which the composer replaces.

## Note, 2026-10-12: HEAD2a

`PageHead` (`work/ViewMain.tsx`) lays out by the main area's container (`@[40rem]/main`, 640 px): above it a grid of the
title's flexible column and the acts' own, the acts at the title's first line; at or below it the acts take a right-aligned
row under the facts. `acts`, then `fold` (shown only above the line; its caller says it again in the ⋯), then `more`.
The quest's, session's and ask's heads use it in HEAD2b-d.

**Note, 2026-10-12 (NEW1a, the driver's half).** `START_CHAT` takes `text`, `files`, `requestId` and a three-way `ownTree`
(absent: the driver's choice, the repository's standing opt-in; true: a tree of its own; false: the checkout, whatever that
opt-in). With words it opens the conversation and hands it them as its first message (`ChatRunner.StartWithAsync`): files over
a message's limit go as the words alone, no preface, and `sent` means the door took them. A conversation that opened without
taking them answers `sent: false` with its `sessionId`, and the driver keeps nothing. A retry with the same request id shares
the running start's result, and after it finished is answered from `<home>/starts/<id>.json` for 24 hours whatever its words or
repository; a start that opened nothing is not kept. An id is letters, digits and dashes, at most 64, else refused in a sentence.
Without `text` the route is the old empty start, answering `{sessionId, message}`.
## Note, 2026-10-12: HEAD2d

The ask's head (`asks/AskPage.tsx`) is the quest's: its state pill leads (*paused* after it), the title is the request's first
line on two lines, one facts line (`asks/askFacts.ts`: its workspace, its age, and the live fact, *waiting on you* where a
go-ahead is unanswered, else the quests it became) and no id line. *Resume* is the one act drawn; close, pause, abandon,
delete, the clear and *Copy ask ID* are its ⋯, one list with the right-click (CTX1), so the ⋯ is always drawn. A first
line past 90 characters (`LONG_TITLE`) offers *View full request* (`work.head.viewRequest`), which moves the focus to the
body's first block, then the whole request as written; the id is a row of the page's details. The ask has no short title
until HEAD2e, and no receiver field, so its facts name no receiver.
## Note, 2026-10-12: HEAD2b

The quest's head: the state's pill alone leads; *Held* and *Unreviewed* are the first words of the facts line (their sentences
stay in their cards); the facts are where it goes, its age and one live fact, and the requester joins the id, lanes and
exact times in *Details*. The primary holds still in `acts`, *Decline…* is the fold's and is said again in the ⋯ below
640 px. The ⋯'s menu is a portal outside the area's container query, so the page reads the area's width when the menu opens.
*View full request* (`work.head.viewRequest`, as the ask's) sits on the facts line for a title over 80 characters and moves
the focus to the request block.
## Note, 2026-10-12: HEAD2c

A session's page header (`work/SessionPageHead.tsx`) is `PageHead`'s now, inside the sticky box that also holds what asks
under it (the stop's ask, the delete's): the title one line (`clamp={1}`), the state's pill leading, the loud act and *Stop…*
in the acts column and never in `fold`. The ⋯ sits inside the same named group (*Session actions*) rather than in `more`: the
two share one row either way, and the group is how the page and its tests find the buttons.

`WholeTitle` is gone. The request as written is a *Request* (请求) row of the record's *Details* (`SessionHead.tsx`): the
quest's whole title with *Open quest #id* where the frame can open it (`onOpenQuest`, AttendedSession's `onQuest`); a
conversation with no quest puts its whole opening there when it is past 60 characters, the width the header's one line may
cut. The facts of a session (`headFacts.ts`) are its repository, its age and the one fact its state makes matter; the account
leaves them (it is *Details*' row already), and a held session's row line, which names a cooling account, stays. A running chat
says *started 2m ago* where a driven session says how long it has run.
