## D339 — A session's panel keeps a rail of its state beside the timeline, and its changes open as the panel's focus (2026-11-20)

**Decision (UISE18, UISE19, the window redesign `docs/2026-11-19-window-design.md` §10.3; amending D320 §9 and its rejection of a
right rail).**

1. At 68rem or more of main width the session panel draws a **facts rail** beside the timeline: *Changes* (files with ±, the
   commits on the branch, the pull request, checks, *Review the changes ▸*), *With* (one line per relation, a jump to its first row
   and a door), *Models, tokens and cost* (per model that ran, "asked X, ran Y" where they differ) and *Details ▸*. The rail holds
   state, never events: helpers, opinions, hand-offs and the channel stay rows of the timeline (D320 point 1).
2. Below 68rem the rail's sections live in the *Details* sheet, and the head's third line carries the changes as one fact.
3. A file, *Review the changes* or the dock's *Accept* opens the panel's **Changes focus** (the review frame, the diff, the patch,
   the handbacks and the gates) with a breadcrumb and Esc, as a child opens (D320 point 4). The side bar's Outputs view goes.

**Why.** The purpose names what changed in the repository as part of a session's one place; D320 left it in the side bar and the
models that ran in *Details*. D320 rejected a right rail for events, and its note left the width cost unmeasured; the rail appears
only where the timeline keeps 72ch beside it, which UISE22 measures. Both fresh designs put changes and cost beside the story.

**Rejected.**

- *Changes kept as a side-bar view* (D320 §9): a session's story read in two regions, and the side bar is Ask Daoris's (D334).
- *A rail of events* (helpers and opinions in the rail): two places for one exchange; D320's reason stands.
- *Tabs for the story, steps, changes and cost at every width*: the story hidden while the changes are read.
