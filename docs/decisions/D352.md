## D352 — An unchecked done waits on its step's role, the `asker`, cast like any role; it is never compared with the ask's words instead (2026-11-20)

**Decision.** D255 holds a run's `quest.holds` where its ask's quest carries no requirement, and named the person as the one who passes
it. That wait stood on the `acceptor`, a role no cast may give an agent (a review's Accept is a press alone), so an agent that filed the
ask through the person's terminal or held the place's line was refused as not its holder, and the window's only door was the person's. In
actor mode who may answer a step is the actor configuration's, never the kind's. The rule:

1. **The step's own role.** The unchecked done's wait (`pass`) is on its `quest.holds` step's role, as an Accept, an approval, a task and a
   go-ahead are: a step naming a role (the owner, a supervisor's role, a catalogue role) is answered by that role.
2. **`asker` where it names none.** A new Daoris role, `asker`: who reads a done against what was asked. Its holder is the attempt's tenure
   as the place casts it (D312): the person until a cast names another, since every ask is filed through the person's doors; the steward
   or a supervisor's agent where the place casts it to them. It is not among the roles no agent may hold.
3. **The holder answers; the line stands in.** The holder passes it with words through the node door (`workflow skip`, the window's press
   on its wait card and its *What needs you* row), an agent through its own channel as itself; a supervisor's held decision stands in
   where the person holds it (`SettleDoor`). Where the step names `owner`, it waits on the owner and the window offers the press.
4. **Its words name the role.** The reason kept on the step says the role answers it, never *you*.

*Rejected:* comparing the done with the ask's own words through the requirement path, so no hold is needed. D255 rejected it and its reason
stands: the ask quoted as a requirement with a check nobody wrote is answered `met` by the agent that did the work, so the step would pass
on the worker's own word; a long ask is past a requirement's bound. *Rejected too:* handing the pass to the run's working session as a
task's Done is handed (`AgentSettles.Handed`, D318): the session that did the work would check its own done. *Not taken now:* reading the
asker from the ask record itself (the record keeps no agent asker, only a keyed door's name) and a supervisor kind of its own for holds
(its cast of `asker` gives the same answer).
