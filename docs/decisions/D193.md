## D193 — Daoris is framed as a development company run by agents: the person owns it, Ask Daoris is its front desk, repositories are its departments, the fabric is how each works, message levels keep its rhythm, the CLI and the connector are its network, knowledge is its growing know-how, and machines are its offices (2026-10-21)

**Decision (the owner's direction).** On 2026-10-21 the owner set the picture the project is described and planned by:
Daoris runs a development company whose staff are agents and whose product is software. Each part of the company has a
home Daoris already has, named in `docs/2026-10-21-company-concept.md`:

1. **The owner** is the person: direction, targets, acceptance, accounts and money (D37, D191 point 3).
2. **The front desk** is Ask Daoris, where every request arrives in plain words and becomes an ask (D110, D183, D65).
3. **Departments** are repositories, each owning its domain and its work; one department never edits another's (D45).
4. **Staff** are sessions on accounts, the cap their number at work at once (D130, D189).
5. **Work orders** between departments are asks and quests (D45's core loop, D65).
6. **The handbook** is the doctrine every repository receives from the canon, and each agent's harness settings (D11, D183).
7. **How a department works** is the fabric: workflows, graphs and pipelines, a start node routing work into a pipeline
   (D157, D173 and its note of 2026-10-20, D190, FABRIC-PIPELINE1).
8. **Quality assurance** is verification of its own, the declared gates and reviews, never run inside a worker's session
   (D115, D154, D180).
9. **The rhythm** is the message levels: work runs undisturbed between natural points, as a sprint does; only what would
   make work in flight wrong reaches it sooner, and only the person stops work; outcomes are reviewed when the work lands
   (D188).
10. **The network** is the CLI, the connector's tools and the service that carries records between them, the way a company
    works through its internet and its chat (D45, D50, D182).
11. **Know-how** is the knowledge each repository keeps and the canon promotes across repositories, growing with the work
    (D9, D21, D129, D151).
12. **Offices** are machines and workspaces, which exchange permitted records through a remote so know-how and work travel
    and a machine's private state never does (D45 part 3, D47).

**What it changes.** The roadmap is ordered by these parts (`ROADMAP.md`), so each priority says which part of the
company it makes work. A new feature is placed by the part it serves, and a gap is read as a part of the company that does
not yet work. No mechanism changes by this decision: each part keeps its own decisions.

**Why.** The project had grown as features named by mechanism (drivers, slices, routes), and its order was hard to read
from them. The company picture names what each mechanism is for in terms anyone can check against how a working company
behaves: a department interrupting another mid-task, quality checks run at every worker's desk, or know-how kept in one
office are failures in a company and are the same failures in Daoris. It also keeps the boundaries the project already
holds: departments own their work, a request travels and an edit does not, and the owner is asked only what is theirs.

**Rejected.** A hierarchy of agents directing agents (a department never commands another; it sends a work order, and the
receiver decides how: `repository-owns-its-work`); a model judging outcomes, routes or landings (D24, D54, D157); the
picture as a mechanism of its own (it orders and explains; each part's contract stays in its decision).

**Relates to.** D45 (the direction: Daoris drives the work) is unchanged and now read through this picture.
