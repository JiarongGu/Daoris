## D321 — An ask keeps the person's requirements, and every quest it becomes carries them (2026-11-18)

**Decision (ASKREQS1, amending D277's `--require` on a new ask).**

1. A new ask takes the person's requirements (`--require "<their words>" "<the check>"`, the door's `requirements`) with or without a
   receiver. They are kept on the ask, judged by the quest's own shape rule, each quote kept as a `quoted` word for the quote judge, and every
   quest the ask becomes carries them: the one published at once to `--to`, and each its intake or `ask publish` publishes, an intake's own
   requirements added beside them, the same pair once.
2. Before Ask Daoris makes an ask it proposes one to three requirements from the person's own words in its conversation, each their words
   and the check an agent's done must show, and makes the ask with the ones the person said yes to, as they edited them; the ask's card shows
   each requirement and the through words it carries, so the person's yes on the card is a yes to them.
3. A quest an ask becomes that carries none is answered "Published with no requirement: its run's holds step will wait on the person's
   word", to the person's press, Ask Daoris's and an intake's alike.

**Why.** On the work workspace three asks published with no requirement held every run at `quest.holds` until the person passed it by hand,
while the one that carried a requirement passed on its own. Ask Daoris makes asks at a workspace, where the intake settles the receiver, and a
requirement was refused there for want of `--to`, so its asks could carry none.

**Rejected:** refusing a requirement without `--to`, so that Ask Daoris names the receiver whenever it carries one (the receiver is the
intake's to settle, a team plan's parts included, and a guessed receiver publishes on a guess); handing the requirements to the intake as
words only, for it to copy (an agent may drop them, and the person's yes was to them as given); a requirement's check written by Ask Daoris
alone with no proposal (the check is what the person's done is measured by, so it is theirs to accept).
