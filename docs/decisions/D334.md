## D334 — No movable views: the side bar is Ask Daoris's, the panel the terminal's, and a closed list hides (2026-11-20)

**Decision (UISH7, UISH13, the window redesign `docs/2026-11-19-window-design.md` §3.1; superseding D118's movable views and
desktop-app rule 25's closed strip).**

1. The frame has fixed regions: title strip, away line, rail, list pane, main area, side bar, panel, status strip. The **side bar**
   holds Ask Daoris and nothing else; it stands beside the main area while the main area keeps its 560 px floor, and over it
   otherwise. The **panel** holds the owner's terminal and nothing else; a session's raw output opens from its own ⋯.
2. View placements, the Views menu and view dragging go. A column beside a page (a session's facts rail, an ask's side column) is
   drawn inside its place's main area.
3. A closed list hides. Below the main area's floor the main area replaces the list, with ← back keeping the list's scroll and
   choice; the 56 px strip of marks goes.
4. Every list has one head (name, count, filter, group-by, +, ⋯) and one set of states.

**Why.** D320 already moves a session's views into its panel, and the redesign moves Outputs there too; once the per-session views
leave, one view per region needs no moving, and a moved view is one more place to look for a thing. A strip of marks without names
identifies nothing; the waits pill and the breadcrumb's ← carry what it did, and all three fresh designs draw the narrow swap.

**Rejected.**

- *Movable views kept for the few that remain* (D118): two ways to reach Ask Daoris and the terminal.
- *The closed strip kept with each group's most urgent mark* (the sessions plan): marks without names; the one number already
  says that something waits.
