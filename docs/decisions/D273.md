## D273 — The person reviews a run's work as one immutable record of every kind, its Accept a receipt the effects after it are fed, and a session is pending, working, needs you, done or failed (2026-11-11)

**Decision (REVIEWUX1, accepted 2026-11-11).** Settled after Codex's independent read (D191), each of its findings checked against the
code. The contract is `docs/2026-11-11-review-for-every-kind-design.md`. The owner's rules hold throughout: visibility is the key; a
security improvement never blocks development; a person who wants more control uses restricted mode or a workflow node.

The review was built for a session's branch to land. Work in place, in a folder, in a copy and in a system had no Accept in the window,
and a branchless review was withheld `no-subject`, so the terminal's Accept could not reach it either; the run's unit carries no
executors, so `apply` found no plan and `return` reported nothing to bring back; and a graph without a landing could end only at its
review, so neither effect could follow one.

1. **The subject is a review record.** Each attempt of a review reads the run's work into one immutable record beside the run's: its kind
   (`branch`, `checkout`, `folder`, `copy`, `plan`), the artifact's reference (a tip and the uncommitted paths' hashes; a checkout's turn
   log; a folder's retained baseline and newest manifest; a copy against its base; a plan's record and number with its preconditions),
   a revision over the reference and the handbacks' ids, every part's handback with its checks, and what could not be read. A moved
   revision is a new attempt and a new record. Attribution is said as far as it is known (*made while its turn ran*, *changed outside its
   turns*), never claimed.
2. **The Accept is a receipt** naming the record, its revision and its author. One press, `WORKFLOW_NODE accept`, for every kind, the
   terminal its twin; the revision and attempt are checked when the word is kept, when the pass applies it after reading the work afresh,
   and when the effect begins. `LAND_SESSION_TREE`'s press and `trees land`'s go. The early Accept is kept by record; the unchecked done is
   passed in the same edit as the Accept that named it; a carried Accept stands for its landing's repair alone; a conversation's press is
   its submission and then the keep.
3. **Completion is nothing left to move.** A run is over when no node can move and nothing is pending; its end keeps its outcome. The
   effects (`land`, `return`, `apply`) come after the review where one exists, every way to them passing it, and are fed its accepted
   record by reference; a graph's end may be any node no effect lies past; a graph without a review lets its effects take the work as it
   stands, the standing authority judged first, as a landing does today.
4. **Partial effects are kept and said:** a return's files brought back before its choices, each choice bound to the versions shown; a
   stale plan's applied operations, the replan reviewed as its own record. Every outcome settles, an apply with no plan included.
5. **Five states.** A session and its run are *needs you*, *failed*, *working*, *pending* or *done*, read in that order, mapped from
   today's record states, shown words, groups, verdicts and waits as the design's §4.2 says; the list, the head and Notifications say
   them and the finer reason inside. There is no landing task as a person's state; *To review* as a group goes; *Archived* is a mark.
6. **One review surface, opened by run,** from any of its sessions, its run view, Notifications and the terminal, after its trees are
   gone; previews and *viewed* marks pinned to the revision; a newer revision announced, never swapped in; the decision and its presses in
   reach. *Send back…* names the part; *Stop…* ends what has not begun and says what already happened.
7. **Failure is a state, not a wait.** A failure nothing goes on from is *failed*, its recovery the doors that exist (an attempt's *Try
   again*, a held attempt's words, the planner's *Try again*), said absent where none does. *What needs you* holds one row per run with
   every wait on it; *Done and failed* holds each outcome once by run, node and attempt, across a restart.
8. **The supervisor decides as today, on every kind,** on the record's facts, admitted by the run's chain, its decision bound to the record
   and `stale` when it moves. A place that wants the person alone before a write composes a `go-ahead`.
9. **Nothing is consented to after the fact.** The migration binds no old unscoped word to a revision: it is kept *legacy* and honoured
   only where its pinned graph has nothing after the review; no pinned graph is edited and no department's choice changed.

*Rejected:*
- **A synthetic branch for non-git work:** a second meaning of a branch for every reader of one.
- **A subject on the review alone:** what is carried out would still not be what was accepted.
- **A change set recomputed by each reader on `claimed`'s wire** (round 1): readers disagree, and a corrected answer at the same content is
  no new review.
- **The review per session, or opened through the waiting session:** one decision in parts, and no door to a finished run's review.
- **Two press doors, or a third.**
- **An Accept in the head:** it accepts a revision the head never drew.
- **A skip of the review:** it reads as accepted; a workflow without a review is the way.
- **The revision checked against the stored binding alone:** a stale page would accept work the runtime had not yet seen move.
- **One effect allowed to end a landless graph** (`ICarriesOut`): the step after it is unsavable again.
- **A run ended once its end node settles:** a step after the end not yet started is cut off.
- **A failed step as a wait in *What needs you*,** or listed only where the node table offers a press: the first asks nothing the person
  must answer, the second lists nothing, since a failed node is offered no act.
- **A system's plan the person's alone by the kit, or a grant per kind:** a new block on development, and a second way to say a
  `go-ahead`.
- **An undo of work in place or partly carried out:** Daoris has no revert of the person's folder, checkout or system.
- **An old unscoped Accept bound to the present folder or the newest plan:** it would assert what the person never saw.
- **A new preset chosen for existing system departments, or a pinned run's graph edited:** a choice changed silently, and a version's
  immutability broken.

**Amends** D264 (its note's end rule, by a dated note), D255 (the hold passed by the Accept that named it), D256 (the early Accept kept by
record) and D233 (the supervisor's decision bound to the record). **Held by:** REVIEWUX1a-j.
