## D296 — A planned ask's part and delivery are kinds a place maps, and the ask's review is answered by the place's landing supervisor (2026-11-16)

**Decision (TEAMLOOP1).** Three rules for which workflow a planned ask's work follows and who answers its one review (area
`workflows-review-landing.md`, rules 39a and 39b; area `roles-and-supervision.md`, rule 49):

1. **Daoris gives a planned ask's work two kinds of its own, `team-part` and `ask-delivery`, which a place maps as it maps any
   kind** (`daoris workflow kind`, `daoris workflow use --kind`). A part's run (its quest the one the ask's plan gave the part) takes
   `team-part` where the ask makes no choice of its own and the place maps it; the ask's delivery run takes `ask-delivery` where the
   place maps it. A place that maps neither binds as before: a part by its ordinary choice, a delivery by the home's saved copy of the
   shipped `ask-delivery`.
2. **The review of an ask's delivery is answered by the place's appointed landing supervisor, as a chain's is.** Its step is kept
   against the newest session of the quests the ask gave that repository, its review's handbacks, so `LandingKind` lists and keeps it.
   The party is the place's, never the step's: a low-risk place appoints a landing supervisor held by an agent and granted
   `landing.accept`; a high-risk place appoints none, and the same review waits for the person.
3. **This repository's own team follows two tracked files**, `tools/workflows/daoris-part.json` (apart, checked, a second opinion,
   integrated onto the ask's branch, released) and `tools/workflows/daoris-ask.json` (parts, the whole checked, the review, merged
   into main): the close review is the part's `opinion`, the wide one the ask's `review`, and no step waits on the owner.

**Rejected:** a workflow named on each `TeamPart` by the lead, since delivery by risk is the place's policy and not one plan's; a
new route key in `driver.json` beside the kinds, a second way to choose a workflow (D228); the delivery binding staying the home's
one saved copy, which would give every workspace on a machine one delivery, the work workspace's included; an `opinion` node for
the wide review, which no session or tree on an ask's delivery run can ask today; and a per-step party option on `review`, which
would let one graph's step outrank the place's risk.

**Left:** parts landing straight on the line with a delivery run that has no branch of its own (teamwork design §7.1) needs the
train's target and the `parts` reading changed, not a workflow version; it is not built.
