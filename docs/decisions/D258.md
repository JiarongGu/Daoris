## D258 — How many sessions work apart in a place is the isolation step's option, and a place's writer holds it for a turn, or in git until its work is committed (2026-11-10)

**Decision.** PLACEWORK1b's design (`docs/2026-11-10-place-work-design.md`) settles two choices D254 left to it:

1. **The count lives on the step.** How many of a place's sessions work apart at once is the `isolate` step's `at-once` option, counted
   over every session of that place working apart whatever workflow version bound it, under the machine's cap. A workflow with no
   `isolate` runs one session at a time in place, so a count above one without isolation cannot be said. A place that wants another
   count saves its own copy of its workflow (design §4.3).
2. **The writer's lease is a turn's, and in a git place also a line's uncommitted work.** A place is held by the one in-place record
   whose turn is running, and in a git place also by the record whose last turn left uncommitted changes of its own, until it commits
   them, the person discards them, or the person lets others work there. The ledger takes and releases it in the writes that move the
   record, and nothing renews it on a clock; the sweep's conclusion of a dead run releases it (design §2.1 to §2.4).

*Rejected:* a place setting for the count beside the workflow, which would say a place's parallelism in two records that can disagree
and accept a count no isolation can honour (D228); a lease held per line until its work is done, as today's tree lock holds the root,
which makes a department wait on every question a session asks the person; a lease renewed by a heartbeat, a second way to say a
session stopped; the lease as a table of the desktop store, a second holder record to keep in step with the ledger.

**Amends** driving rule 12 (one session at a time in the root, a parked one holding it). **Held by:** PLACEWORK1.
