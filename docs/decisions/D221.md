## D221 — Setting work up is one place page, a reviewed set-up, one top-to-bottom drawing and an editor of its own (2026-11-01)

**Decision (SETUPUX1, co-designed under D191; accepted by the steward 2026-11-01).** The Agents place's Setup mode becomes one
experience, per `docs/2026-11-01-setup-ux-design.md`. A place's page is a head and three sections folded to one line each, read from
`PLACES` alone, with the head and the three lines on the first screen at both widths. *Set up…* is a choices page and then a review
page, one draft and one plan, with refusals under the part they are about and a *Keep how it lands* ask that leaves a saved workflow
alone. One drawing serves the place, the editor and a run: always top to bottom, endings drawn as they are, *Finished* only from the
driver's record. The editor is a page of its own, the graph beside one step's inspector, with an outcome table per step. Choosing a
workflow stays `WORKFLOW_USE`, reviewed as a plan of one where the driver announces it; saving and choosing stay two presses. One role
editor serves the place, *All roles* and a step. No workflow, delivery or step shares a name in either language. A not-driven place's
configuration is editable without driving it. The flag `daoris.agents.setup` is lit by default only after the installed window shows
every flow and every old door has a new home.

**Why.** AGENTSPLACE1 and FABRIC2 built the parts slice by slice; read as a whole on the installed code, the page was an unfolded
stack, the editor drew each step twice and could lay its graph sideways, Set up… threw a saved workflow away, one name covered two
mechanisms, and several zh words named other things (the design's §1). Nothing here widens a press: the routes keep what they
authorise, and the rows add read fields, one review token and one narrower plan.

**Rejected.** Every change of what a place follows through `PLACE_APPLY` (one proposal's choice): its receipt is for the multi-effect
set-up, and a plan of one on `WORKFLOW_USE` keeps choosing the narrow press it is. A sideways graph where the ranks fit, and *Sent
back n times* without a driver field.

**Record.** The brief, both sealed proposals, the synthesis and both reads (`docs/2026-11-01-setup-ux-*.md`); the settlements the
design follows are its §7; its rows, SETUPUX1a to 1y, are in TASKS.md.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (SETUPUX1a, built; D221): a delivery and a workflow have names of their own, from the shipped data.** Each default's `delivery` carries `names` and `for`, which the quick set-up's `SetupPresets.Table` reads; its top-level `names` are the workflow's and end in *workflow* (…工作流); `WORKFLOW_LIST` carries `for`, `order`, `setUp` and `delivery`; and `PLACE_PLAN`'s suggestion says `why: {code, said}`. *Merge* and *Integration* became *Reviewed merge workflow* and *Reviewed integration workflow*, and the deliveries with no set-up of their own are *Merge with no review* and *Integrated with no press* (集成入主线，无需按键), because *Automatic merge workflow* contains *merge workflow* and 自动合并 opens 自动合并工作流, and no name may contain another's whole name. The three steps' words (`pull-request`, `integrate`, `handed`) stay for rows n and w. Proof: `ShippedDeliveryWordsTests`, `DriverModuleWorkflowTests`, `PlacePlanTests`.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (SETUPUX1c, built; D221): choosing is read as a plan of one where the list announces it.** `WORKFLOW_LIST` says `use: ["plan"]`; `WORKFLOW_USE {plan: true}` writes nothing and answers the reach, what lowers the person's part, each place whose workflow changes, what the kinds a cleared default leaves mapped keep, and an `expected` token over all of it (and over the chosen version's digest and Current's, so a moved rule moves it). A press carrying the token is refused `moved` where the plan it would now read differs; `moved` joins `stale`, which still answers a `version` that moved, and a press with neither acts as it always did. The route stays the person's (the census) and the terminal's `workflow use` is untouched. Proof: `DriverModuleWorkflowWritesTests`.
