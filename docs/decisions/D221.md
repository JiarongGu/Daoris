## D221 — Setting work up is one place page, a reviewed set-up, one top-to-bottom drawing and an editor of its own (2026-11-01)

**Decision (SETUPUX1, co-designed under D191; accepted by the steward 2026-11-01).** The Agents place's Setup mode becomes one
experience, per `docs/2026-11-01-setup-ux-design.md`. A place's page is a head and three sections folded to one line each, read from
`PLACES` alone, with the head and the three lines on the first screen at both widths. *Set up…* is a choices page and then a review
page, one draft and one plan, with refusals under the part they are about and a *Keep how it lands* ask that leaves a saved workflow
alone. One drawing serves the place, the editor and a run: always top to bottom, endings drawn as they are, *Finished* only from the
driver's record. The editor is a page of its own, the graph beside one step's inspector, with an outcome table per step. Choosing a
workflow stays `WORKFLOW_USE`, reviewed as a plan of one where the driver announces it; saving and choosing stay two presses. One role
editor serves the place, *All roles* and a step. No workflow, delivery or step shares a name in either language. A not-driven place's
configuration is editable without driving it. The flag `daoris.agents.setup` is lit by default only after the installed window shows
every flow and every old door has a new home.

**Why.** AGENTSPLACE1 and FABRIC2 built the parts slice by slice; read as a whole on the installed code, the page was an unfolded
stack, the editor drew each step twice and could lay its graph sideways, Set up… threw a saved workflow away, one name covered two
mechanisms, and several zh words named other things (the design's §1). Nothing here widens a press: the routes keep what they
authorise, and the rows add read fields, one review token and one narrower plan.

**Rejected.** Every change of what a place follows through `PLACE_APPLY` (one proposal's choice): its receipt is for the multi-effect
set-up, and a plan of one on `WORKFLOW_USE` keeps choosing the narrow press it is. A sideways graph where the ranks fit, and *Sent
back n times* without a driver field.

**Record.** The brief, both sealed proposals, the synthesis and both reads (`docs/2026-11-01-setup-ux-*.md`); the settlements the
design follows are its §7; its rows, SETUPUX1a to 1y, are in TASKS.md.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (SETUPUX1f: a plugin's step names itself).** A manifest step may declare `title` and `about` (a title one line of at most 60 characters, an about at most 240; built as `{ en, zh }`, which D222 opens to one text or any languages, I18NOPEN1); `WORKFLOW_PALETTE` carries them, and a step without them reads as it did. They are words for reading, so they sit outside a declaration's equality, a graph's digest and an acceptance. The example plugin's steps say they find a pending release, record it and copy the package into their own registry folder.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (SETUPUX1a, built; D221): a delivery and a workflow have names of their own, and the page words them by id.** The shipped data holds no per-language field (the owner's direction: Daoris is not English and Chinese alone): each default keeps one English `name` and `for`, and its `delivery` an `id`, `name` and `for`, as the terminal's words and the page's fallback. The page names a workflow and a delivery from its catalogue (`locales/<locale>/workflow.shipped.json`, keys `workflow.shipped.<id>.name|for` and `workflow.shipped.delivery.<id>.name|for`) in every shipped locale, and a census (`shippedNames.test.ts`) fails an id with no key in any. `SetupPresets.Table` reads the delivery names; `WORKFLOW_LIST` carries each preset's id, `name`, `for`, `order`, `setUp` and `delivery`, and no `names`; `PLACE_PLAN`'s suggestion says `why: {code, said}`, the page wording the code. A workflow's name ends in *workflow* (…工作流); the deliveries that have no set-up of their own are *Merge with no review* and *Integrated with no press*, because *Automatic merge workflow* would otherwise hold 自动合并 across kinds; the glossary's `distinct` lists them. The three steps' words (`pull-request`, `integrate`, `handed`) stay for rows n and w. Proof: `ShippedDeliveryWordsTests`, `DriverModuleWorkflowTests`, `PlacePlanTests`, `shippedNames.test.ts`.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (SETUPUX1c, built; D221): choosing is read as a plan of one where the list announces it.** `WORKFLOW_LIST` says `use: ["plan"]`; `WORKFLOW_USE {plan: true}` writes nothing and answers the reach, what lowers the person's part, each place whose workflow changes, what the kinds a cleared default leaves mapped keep, and an `expected` token over all of it (and over the chosen version's digest and Current's, so a moved rule moves it). A press carrying the token is refused `moved` where the plan it would now read differs; `moved` joins `stale`, which still answers a `version` that moved, and a press with neither acts as it always did. The route stays the person's (the census) and the terminal's `workflow use` is untouched. Proof: `DriverModuleWorkflowWritesTests`.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (SETUPUX1f: a plugin's step names itself).** A manifest step may declare `title` and `about`, each `{ en, zh }` of non-blank text (a title one line of at most 60 characters, an about at most 240); `WORKFLOW_PALETTE` carries them, and a step without them reads as it did. They are words for reading, so they sit outside a declaration's equality, a graph's digest and an acceptance. The example plugin's steps say they find a pending release, record it and copy the package into their own registry folder.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (SETUPUX1g: the words, before any row writes them).** The glossary holds §2.5's rows: new terms (place, how it lands, preset, current, change, use the workspace's, edit steps, step, way on, decision step, back to, your review, your look, needs attention, gates, work done here, no further step, Daoris makes sure, staffing, plan) and amended ones (setup, version, landing, publish, ends here, adopt, hold, appoint, changes, not yet, Use here, apply). Its `distinct` lists hold the shipped workflows', the deliveries' and the steps' names, and `names:check --strict` fails a name that holds another kind's whole name in either language. A word the design forbids that existing strings still say sits under the term's `avoid.later`: the check reports those strings as rewords, so row w has its list, and fails on none until w moves the word to `avoid`.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (SETUPUX1b, built): the effective graph.** `WORKFLOW_CURRENT` with a `route` also answers `effective`, the graph that route follows (`WorkflowEffectives.Read`): Current through `WorkflowOperations.CurrentSource`, the handler `workflow new --from current:<repository>` writes through, and a workspace's by that handler over `WorkflowCurrent.Derive(config, null, workspace)`; a saved route as its newest version that reads; landed `current` draws none. Read only, and a caller sending no `route` is answered as before.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (SETUPUX1d, built; D221): `PLACES` answers what the place page draws.** A place's detail (`detail: {workspace, repository}`, driven or not) and a workspace's whole member list (`members`) come with `lands` (the preset the followed workflow's `land` matches, a saved version's read as Current's is), each work's role names and scope, the summaries `look`, `helpers` and `answer`, and `adoption` (`none`, `running` while its set-up quest is open or taken, `adopted`); a repository whose adoption runs is listed with the driven, and a workspace counts them as `running`. Proof: `PlaceProjectionTests`, `DriverModulePlacesTests`.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (SETUPUX1e, built): `keep` is an ask, not a delivery.** `PLACE_PLAN`'s preset `keep` (`SetupPresets.Keep`, outside the deliveries' table; the terminal's `--preset keep`) writes no landing and no workflow choice, and plans driving with its tree, the two ticks and adoption as every delivery does, so `PLACE_APPLY` applies a subset of what it wrote and a saved workflow survives a set-up. Proof: `PlacePlanTests` and `PlaceApplyTests`' keep cases.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (SETUPUX1h, built): the place page's shell.** A place's page is a head (pill, facts from `PLACES`, the parent as a door, *Set up…*, *Hold* or *Resume*, and a ⋯ whose drive and stop press is confirmed in place, with a tree per session) and three folds whose lines come from the place's `PLACES` row alone, each value marked *from aurora*, *from this machine* or *default* and a value set here left unmarked (`placeShell.ts`). Workflow is first on a repository and a workspace, Roles on This machine; only a held start opens another fold, Roles at that work. The folds, the row a door asked for and the way back are held above the mounts per place (`usePlaceShell`, in `useAgentsView`), and a folded fold mounts no body, so it asks no route; an imported repository is read through `detail` and edited through the same presses, which never set driving. A door names a section and a row (`placeAt`, carried by `placeOpening`; a role's source mark is one), *Back* shows once, and a choice in the list asks the head for the focus, taken only if the list has let it go. Proof: `placeShell.test.ts`, `PlacePage.test.tsx`, `SetupPlaces.test.tsx`, `PlaceRoles.test.tsx`, and `measured/place.spec.ts` (the head and the three lines end at 328 px at 80 rem and 386 px at 39 rem on a Workflow body the size the design draws; row o's own body must keep to it).

**Note, 2026-11-02 (SETUPUX1n, built): one drawing, `workflow/graphFlow.ts`.** The chart, the editor and a run draw a graph through it: always in sections (*Before it lands*, *After it lands*, else *Steps*), so top to bottom or the rail and never across, on a 14rem track (`WORKFLOW_TRACK`); the configured end flagged *Work done here* (with *Steps after it still run* where steps follow), a drawing-only ◎ *No further step* for each declared outcome nothing follows, *Finished* only where a run's record says it finished, and the declared requests and holds one group, *Daoris makes sure · n*, that `graphFlow` folds itself. A graph's `data` wires are no way on and are not drawn. Proof: `graphFlow.test.ts` over every file in `Defaults/workflows`, and the measured stories `graph/*`.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (SETUPUX1j, built): *Set up…*'s choices page.** *How it lands* offers *Keep how it lands* (preselected where the plan's `why` is `kept` or the place follows a saved workflow), the deliveries `WORKFLOW_LIST` announces with `setUp` (worded by id from `workflow.shipped.delivery.*`, the suggestion leading with its `why` by code and the driver's sentence beneath) and *Not driven* from its own chrome key; `defaultName.*`, `set.line.*` and `PRESETS` are gone. Driving is captioned from the plan's `before` and `after`; each refusal stands whole under its part, and a door for *no reviewers*, *no review environment* or *no line* leaves the set-up with its draft kept, opens the landing rows of the place (a workspace's, of the refusing repository) and returns by *Back*, the plan read again. A workspace's repositories are chosen from `PLACES`' `members` (All, Driven, Not driven, None, a count, a search), and where its repositories suggest differently nothing is preselected and each choice names those it suits. Ask Daoris's `setup_propose` names `keep`. `readPlaces` now passes `detail` and `members` on. Proof: `placePlan.test.ts`, `PlaceSetUp.test.tsx`, `PlaceSetUpPanel.test.tsx`, `setupKeys.test.ts`, the stories `Agents/PlaceSetUp`.
