## D206 — Work cut off by an account's limit resumes by itself: the end is *limited*, never a failure or a strike; another account or choice takes it now where one can; otherwise the line waits, shows its earliest reset, and at it resumes its own conversation on its own account or a briefed one elsewhere, the person's held words going with it, for driven lines and conversations alike (2026-10-23)

**Decision (LIMITRESUME1, accepted 2026-10-24).** **Status: accepted** (the steward, under D191) after round 3, the note at the end. Written as proposed; It extends D205's walk to a driven line at a limit, so an independent Opus
reader reads it as a foundation before it is built (D191). The contract is `docs/2026-10-23-limit-resume-design.md`, read at
`9950b24cb`. The direction (the owner, 2026-10-23, through the quest that asked for this design) is that work cut off by a token
limit must resume by itself. The steward set its shape.

What the install showed (design §1, from the machine log). A driven line ran on three accounts, each part ending at a limit
(`account.limited`), each `failed` with the ledger's `limited` flag and so no strike (`ReadStrikes`,
`Service/ServiceClient.cs:2033-2034`). Each was announced as an *Ended* notice. The next start was then held for two hours, and
at the earliest reset it came back by itself as a **briefed carry-on on the same account** its last part ran on
(`session.moved … from=account C to=account C why=carry-on briefed=true`). The agent's own conversation was still in that
account's home and was not resumed, since a carry-on walks and is briefed (`Loop/Driver.cs:1731-1735`, `:2192-2196`). The page
showed three failures and *waits for an account*, with no time. A conversation with no account left stays on its cooling account,
refused turn by turn (LIMITGOON1's branch), and an ended one's waiting words go on only at a word, a press, the sweep or the
come-up, never at the reset (`Conversations/Chat/ChatGoOn.cs`).

1. **The limited end is the ledger's existing flag, worded as its own end** (design §3.1). A `failed` record with `limit` stays
   `failed` on the wire, with its `account.cooling-*` note. Every reader says *limited* / *额度用尽*, quiet, never red, and
   `AttentionWatch` raises no *Ended* for it. No strike, as today. The pipe door gains its limit read once a sentence is recorded.
   An unwritten cool-off stays a strike, which bounds a spin.
2. **The reset is kept per account, as today** (§3.2): `cooling.json`'s entry, the agent's stated reset plus the margin, else
   60 minutes said as unstated. A line's wait is computed, never stored: the earliest moment any account of any of its choices is
   ready.
3. **At each look a limited line, `Open` or `Taken`, is planned as its resume** (§3.3):
   1. its own account ready and its conversation resumable (D189 point 2) → **a full resume**: the same conversation, handed
      Daoris's word `limit.reset` and then the person's held words, never a brief;
   2. else another account of its choice, or another choice → **a briefed resume**, the held words after the brief;
   3. else **it waits**, held, `held.limit-waits` naming the earliest reset.

   Own first where both are ready. A refused resume falls to ii. No step counts a strike.
4. **The wake is the look** (§3.4). A driven line needs nothing new. For conversations, a `limits` stage runs beside the look and
   asks the chat runner only where a cool-off ended since the last look. There is no timer and no second loop.
5. **Conversations follow the same rule** (§3.5). LIMITGOON1's move and D205's (e) go on now where they can. Otherwise the
   conversation waits, its words kept on `said` and not dispatched. At the wake it goes on in the same conversation on its own
   account, else in a briefed move. A pick waits for its own account.
6. **A driven line at a limit walks its actor's choices** (§3.6), choice-major, as (e) does for a conversation. The owner's
   direction recorded in D205's acceptance note is *a driven line's next start walks its choices*. A pick, a handed agent and an
   actor *as before* wait. Only an end by a limit reaches this; a refusal after the prompt does not.
7. **The page** (§3.7) says *Waiting for a limit to reset: it resumes by itself at {{time}}, on {{account}}* / *正在等待额度重置：
   将于 {{time}} 在 {{account}} 上自动继续。*, with *Go on now with…* (D163's offer, the person's press) and *Stop*. It is not a row
   in *What needs you*.

**Why.** The account says when it is free, and Daoris already keeps it, plans at every look and keeps the person's words. What
was missing is resuming the conversation the account still holds rather than briefing a new one. Words were also lost to pinning,
because the person's words held a line to a cooling account. A new ledger state would break teammates' and older builds' reading
of a shared set, and the flag already travels. The look already wakes within the 2-minute margin.

**Rejected** (design §5): a new state; a timer per reset or a loop for conversations; a briefed carry-on on the same account; a
transcript copied across homes; words pinning a line to its cooling account; a *What needs you* row; a pick moved by itself;
limits counted toward a park.
