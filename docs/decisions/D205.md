## D205 — Actors: every kind of agent Daoris starts is an actor the person gives an ordered list of choices (agent, model, effort, in the tool's own words); a start takes the first choice that can run, walking each choice's accounts by D197's walk before the next, carries the model at the tool's session level, and says the actor, the choice and what the agent reported; nothing spends differently until the person presses (2026-10-23)

**Decision (MODELROLE1).** **Status: proposed**, round 1, under D191: the synthesis of three independent proposals (Claude's,
Codex's, and an independent Opus reader's in Fable's place under D191's amendment of 2026-10-23), written from one brief. It
changes a foundation (D24's and D98's rule, and every start's protocol), so Codex reads it and an independent Opus reader reads it
as a foundation; the steward accepts after both. The contract is `docs/2026-10-23-actors-design.md`; its §1 compares the
proposals question by question and says what each choice rejects, and its §0.2 corrects the brief and the proposals against the
code at `656acf6de`.

The owner directed on 2026-10-23 that, for token use, Daoris chooses the model for each kind of work it starts: every kind of
agent it starts is an actor, configured by the person as a range of agent plus model in order of preference, Ask Daoris helping,
the UI kept simple. Read at `656acf6de`, no start names a model or an effort: `session/new` carries `cwd`, `mcpServers` and
`_meta` alone (`Acp.cs:918-923`), the driven `AcpSession` takes no options (`Driver.cs:3775-3778`), and the pipe door's arguments
name none (`Adapters.cs:2088-2096`), so every session runs its account's own model, and Daoris learns which only after the turn,
in the usage history alone (`TurnModels.cs:9-11`).

1. **Eight actors**, each a kind of start (design §2): *Build* 开发 (`build`), *Intake* 受理, *Second opinion* 第二意见
   (`opinion`), *Helper* 助手, *Partner* 搭档, *Brainstorm* 头脑风暴, *Ask Daoris* 问道衍 (`ask-daoris`) and *Chat* 聊天. A
   continuation keeps its record's actor. *actor* / 角色, *choice* / 选择 and *account default* / 账户默认 enter the glossary.
   Orientation, exact work, a meeting and a model-scoped cool-off arrive with the start, the signal or the reading each needs
   (rows i to k); a workflow node and a set-up take the actors of what they start.
2. **One to four choices per actor**, each `{ agent, model?, effort? }` in `driver.json`'s `actors`, the values the tool's own
   (the SDK's aliases on the pipe door; what a protocol agent last offered, kept in a new home entry `data/agent-offers.json`), or
   a free field. An absent model or effort is *account default*: nothing passed, today's cascade, and the page says so. Judged in
   one place for every door (`ActorChoices.Judge`). Off only for Intake and Ask Daoris.
3. **The carriers.** A protocol door, every agent alike: `session/set_config_option` for `model`, then `thought_level`, after the
   posture and before the first prompt, only with values that answer offered (`Acp.cs:82`, `:516-517`, `:527-539`). The pipe
   door: `--model` and `--effort` (a spawn-file `effortLevel` where `--effort` is absent), not appended where the person's own
   command names them. A named model therefore holds over the account's and a repository's settings and `ANTHROPIC_MODEL`, for
   that start; *account default* leaves the cascade as today. The account's `settings.json`, `ANTHROPIC_MODEL` at spawn, a
   `model` in the spawn's settings file, `_meta.claudeCode.options.model`, `--fallback-model` and the tools' homes are rejected
   as carriers.
4. **The walk is choice by choice, each choice's accounts before the next.** A choice that cannot run here is passed, coded
   (`unavailable`, `not-offered`, `refused`, `no-full-access`, `not-admitted`, `not-listed`), and D197's account walk runs
   unchanged inside each choice; only the choice taken counts under the cap. A model the agent does not offer stops the process
   before any prompt, with no strike, and the walk goes on. An effort not offered runs at account default and is said. No choice
   ready: a driven start holds with one sentence naming the soonest way out, and a conversation's press is refused in that
   sentence; *What needs you* gains no kind.
5. **Nothing moves silently.** A running session never moves to another choice. A limit carries on with the same choice on
   another account of its agent; when every account of that agent cools, D163's hand-over offer names the actor's next choice
   on another agent, under the person's `handOver` rule. A resume re-applies the record's choice. A refusal after a prompt was
   sent is never a fallback. A later choice is said in the opening note, a head chip, the machine log and the terminal loop.
6. **The rules say who may; the choices say with which model.** `opinions.reviewers`, `helpers`, `partners` and the brainstorm
   policy's agents stay where they are; each listed agent takes its model from the actor's choices for it, and where a rule lists
   none the actor's choices are the order. D155's maker passes stand; a model never makes a reviewer independent, and the
   same-agent label names the model. A partner is still named by the ask, and its admission digest, which hashes the posture,
   guard and rule lists only (`PartnerConfiguration.cs:56-72`), is unchanged.
7. **The upgrade changes nothing.** Each actor is read from today's keys (`adapter`, `intakeAdapter`, `helperAdapter`, the rules)
   at account default, shown *as before*. Setting Build, Intake or Ask Daoris writes its first agent through to the old key, so an
   older build starts the same agent. A suggestion, one dated table from actor to Claude Code's aliases and *account default*,
   held to `docs/model-roles.md` by a census that fails both ways, is offered on one card whose words say it can spend more and
   applied only by the person's press, which on the owner's install is D191 point 3's yes. Its first content leads Build with
   `opus` · high, as the owner directed for reasoning work, and leaves Intake, Ask Daoris and Chat unchanged.
8. **It lives in one place**: Settings → AI features → *Actors*, machine-wide, a row per actor showing its first choice and a use
   line, one inline editor open at a time; *Details* on a session's head; `daoris driver actor` in `DriverChoicesCommand`
   (`ACTORS` `Open`, `SET_ACTOR` `Person`). A quest, a node and an ask name no actor and no model.
9. **Ask Daoris proposes with `actor_propose`**, one or several actors, carrying the revision it read, judged by the same judge.
   In both modes the change is the person's press: the card's *Apply* when restricted, and under full access the verb meets the
   window's card, since a `Person` route waits for it whoever calls (`InstancePipe.cs:557-562`) and D201 covers no standing
   choice. No `settings_*` grant covers actors.
10. **The tier that ran is said.** The session record gains four fields of this machine's own (`actor`, `choice`, `asked`,
    `ran`), kept off the wire as `profile` is; *Details* shows asked, acknowledged and ran, never judged against each other, since
    that would need a model list. The usage history's turn line gains `actor`. Cost is tokens per session and per actor over 7
    days, never a price, never a missing reading shown as zero.

**Why.** The owner asked Daoris to choose per kind of work and the person to configure it; the only way both hold under D24 is
that the person's ordered choices are the deployment's choice and Daoris's own choosing is availability alone, said every time.
Every proposal reached choice-major ordering for the same reason: the person's order is about who does the work, and one agent's
accounts are interchangeable (D197). The session-level carriers are the only ones that hold for one start without writing a
tool's file or outranking the person through the environment, and they are the doors the composer already uses. Keeping the rules
as fences keeps a machine-wide list from widening who may write in a repository. Reading the actors from today's keys makes the
upgrade spend nothing new, and the census keeps the suggestion tied to this development's measured reference rather than to a
list Daoris maintains.

**Rejected.** Claude's account-settings carrier on the ACP door, silent move to another model mid-line, mirroring the rules from
the actors, and `suggested.json` without a census; Codex's exact-work and synthesis actors before a signal or a measurement, a
separate `actor-runs/` store, a reported model outside the choice holding dispatch, model and effort in the partner digest, and
full access applying a change without the person's press; a router or *Auto* pool; cheapest-first routing; per-workspace
choices; a notification per fallback; a suggestion applied by itself or followed into accepted choices. Each with its reason in
the design's §1 and the section it names.

**This would amend**, on acceptance and each by a dated note: D98 (Daoris keeps no model list and names no model of its own; the
person chooses per actor; Daoris carries the choice at the session level and chooses only by availability), D24, D57 (narrowly),
D179 and TOOL3, D174 point 4, D155 points 3 and 4, D163 point 3, D65 §1b and D89, D183, D176 point 9, D130 point 4, D195 and D116,
as the design's §12 says. Until then each stands as written. D197, D199, D201 and D203 stand.

**Rows:** MODELROLE1a (each door measured, first) to MODELROLE1h, with i to k filed behind their measurements (design §14).
