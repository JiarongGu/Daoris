## D312 — One door settles a step: its holder's word in its epoch, the owner role's end of the work and answer to a decision, or an observation (2026-11-17)

**Decision (HOLDDOOR1, D304 points 2, 4, 5 and 8).**

1. One door (`SettleDoor.Admit`) judges who may settle a run's step, at every door that settles one: a run step's word
   (`RunNodeWords.Word`: accept, changes, done, approve, reject, decide, skip, stop, send, a file's choice) and a held attempt's
   (`WorkPersonWord.Word`: happened, did-not, stop, again, done outside). It reads the attempt's tenure now (`StepTenureFiles`), the
   speaker and the epoch the speaker read, never the step's kind: what settles a step is its kind's, who may say it is its holder's. A
   word from an ended epoch is refused `ended-epoch`, one from a holder that does not hold the step `not-holder`, each naming who
   holds it now and since when.
2. The speaker is its channel's (`SettleBy`): the person's presses and terminal speak `person`; an agent's channel names itself
   (`agent`, `agent:<name>`), the role's staffed `agent` holding for any agent it runs; an observation (a plugin's, or Daoris's own
   reading of its records) settles whoever made it. A proposal's proposer is the record's words, never who speaks. Two acts are the
   owner role's above every holder: the end of the work (a stop, the owner's direction) and a decision's answer, which confirms or
   overrules its holder's proposal. A supervisor's held decision stands in for a step the person holds, overrulable by them, and never
   for one an agent holds.
3. A step at a destination the owner marked (the approver where the place's production releases are marked, `PlaceCastBook.At`) is
   held by the owner role from its attempt's start (`TenureWhy.Marked`): it offers no *Take*, *Hand to…* or *Give back*, refuses a
   hand-over (`marked`), and takes no word but the owner role's, a supervisor's included.
4. An attempt with no tenure kept (not started, or not cast yet by the pass) refuses nothing: the pass casts it at its next look.

**Why.** D304 point 8 moves the authority to settle a step from the step's kind to its holder; while a kind decided it, a person and an
agent were told apart (D304 point 2). CASTING1 wrote a tenure per attempt and refused an ended epoch, but any channel could still settle
a step in the current epoch whoever held it.

**Rejected:** a stop admitted only from its step's holder (the owner could not end work an agent holds without taking each step
first); the owner role admitted on every word (the tenure would bind no word the person's channels give, which are most of them
today); a word refused where no tenure is kept (a step not cast yet would refuse every word on a guess); the proposer's name as the
speaker (it is free words on the record, and no agent channel speaks a proposal yet).

**Left (HOLDDOOR1's rest):** the `human` kind and the wait switch by kind (`StepWaiting.Read`) deleted. The kind's saved versions
are readable without changing what a saved step means, so D309 point 3's trigger does not hold: a version keeps the digest of its saved
graph (`NamedVersion.Digest`, as `PluginFormerKinds` keeps a renamed plugin step's), so one reader can read a saved `human` step as a
step of a neutral kind under its role (its kind's default `owner` where it names none), the person holding it by the owner's cast, and a
version saved after it closes going back below the build that saved it. A mark set on a place after an attempt's tenure began is read
at the next attempt, not at the word.

**Amended 2026-11-17 (HOLDDOOR1, the one press).** The person's press on a step another holder holds, in the epoch the press read, is
their take of it: the door's `not-holder` for the person's channel becomes a *Take* made as the word is kept (a new epoch, journaled with
the reason `pressed <word>`), and the word settles in that epoch, one act. An agent's word and the line's never take; a press in an ended
epoch is still refused `ended-epoch`; a marked step is already the person's. Rejected: refusing the press until the person takes the step
(two presses where there was one, and D304's trial counts the owner's presses); taking at the door before the word's other checks (a word
refused for its state would leave the step taken with nothing settled).

**Amended 2026-11-17 (HOLDDOOR1, the task kind's floor and the wait).** The neutral kind is `task`. A save reads the kinds it names,
so one naming `human` is refused; a stored version's `human` step reads as a `task` at its saved digest. A save writing a `task` step
keeps its build as a floor of its own (`tasks-since.txt`) beside the roles' (`roles-since.txt`), and a going back below either is
refused: a roles' floor kept first lies below the builds that know no `task`. Each kind says what its active step waits for
(`IWaitsOnRole`), so `StepWaiting.Read` names no kind. Rejected: one floor raised to the newest reason (it would miss the builds between
the two or refuse going back to builds that read every saved version); a save rewriting `human` to `task` (a door that changes what it
was handed). Left: a request's `start: you` and a steps version's `you` still name the person, not the role's holder.

**Amended 2026-11-17 (wave R5, `you` a holder word).** A step whose `start` or `accept` says `you` is first held by the person through its
tenure (`HolderWords.First`, read by `AttemptTenures`), so the settle door admits the person's word and the step waits on its role; a
census holds the runtime (`Workflows/Kinds`, `Workflows/Nodes`, `Roles`) to never comparing against the word. Rejected: a branch in each
kind that waits for the person (the word would mean something per kind). Left: the named steps' reading, gate and diff, which lower and
compare the stage's `start` and the landing's `accept`, are not yet moved onto the holder.

**Amended 2026-11-17 (the roles scope's review, the door's integrity).** What reaches the door is made true at its cause. (1) *The
speaker is the channel's, never the default.* Every route that settles or passes on a step (`WORKFLOW_NODE`, `WORK_HELD`,
`WORKFLOW_HOLD`, their verbs' paths included) names its speaker from the caller's channel (`SettleBy.OfChannel`, read off the pipe's
door): the window, a card the person confirmed and the person's terminal speak `person` (and the owner role for a stop or a decision);
a working session's own press and an appointed role's act speak `agent`, Ask Daoris's `agent:ask-daoris`. An agent's stop is its
holder's word, never the owner role's, so a marked step refuses every speaker but the person. An agent hands on only a step it holds and
never takes one; the owner role is handed to nobody but the person (`owner-role`). (2) *Compare-and-set.* Every write of a tenure (a take,
a hand-over, a give-back, a take on a press) names the epoch it read and is refused `ended-epoch` where another write ended it first,
compared under the tenures' lock; a settling word is judged and kept in one hold of that lock (the record's lock taken inside it), its
take made only once every check of its state admits the word. (3) *Fail closed.* Tenures that do not read take only the person's word
and refuse every hold act (`tenure-unread`, a line in the driver's log); a unit's file drops only ended attempts' tenures past its cap; the
pass casts standing attempts before its rounds as well as after. Point 2 is kept: the owner role's stop and decision answer are judged
before the epoch, so a stop from a stale row ends the work. One rule casts every first epoch and is *Give back*'s target
(`AttemptTenures.FirstHolder`). Rejected: a speaker named in the payload (any caller writes it); an agent's press admitted as the
person's where it is unrestricted (the owner's production approval would be any session's); refusing every word where the tenures do
not read (the person, the top of every line, could not settle their own marked step); keeping the newest tenures by file order (an
early, long-waiting approver's would go first). Left: a working session speaks as the unnamed `agent`, since a start keeps no agent's
name, so a step cast to `agent:<name>` refuses it; a whole review's stop (`ReviewStops`) still reads no speaker.

**Amended 2026-11-17 (roles review, waits by their own role and the agent holder).** A wait is answered by its own role
(`StepWaiting.HolderOf`): the step's own role by its attempt's tenure, any other role a wait is on (a vacancy's line, a plugin's actor
such as `credentials-keeper`, an attempt held for `operations` on a step of another role) by the place's cast of that role, never by the
step's holder; What needs you, the run view and Sessions' placing read it alike. No agent's channel speaks a press yet, so an agent
settles a step only by an observation (a check, a landing, a go-ahead read on its record, a plugin's act read from its outcome) or by its
session's work (the work, the second opinion), never one its own role's press settles (an Accept, a task's Done, an approval or a
decision, `AgentSettles`): a place's cast refuses an agent for the role such a kind takes where it names none, a hand-over refuses one for
such a step, each with that reason, and a wait an agent holds that only a press settles stays the person's row with *Take*. A landing
supervisor is listed only the steps the door admits its word on, and its decision names the epoch it read at its release. Rejected:
refusing an agent at every role (the work, the second opinion and the plugins' roles are agents' to hold); dropping a stranded wait until
an agent channel exists (it would wait with no settler and no deadline climbs it); reading a vacancy's or an actor's holder off the step's
tenure (the step's holder is not the line's, nor a plugin's actor's).

**Note 2026-11-18 (wave R7).** The cast moved to `Workflows/Cast`, since it reads the workflows' steps and the plugins' acts; the
settings' command learns whether a `cast` word changes the home from the answer the cast installs, and names none of it (D231).
