## D230 — A repository owns its context and reconciles with the canon through its own session's model; Daoris manages only the sessions it runs, each scoped to the session (2026-11-03)

**Decision (CONTEXTONE1, the owner's direction and answers of 2026-11-03; designed under D191; round 2, proposed for its reads).** It
follows `docs/2026-11-03-context-one-design.md`, with no second route (D228).

1. **The repository owns its context.** Its rules, skills, knowledge and setup are versioned files that work without Daoris, for any
   developer and any agent. *Doctrine works without a service* stands, and so do D13 and D19: drift is the disk against the lock.
2. **A canon update can be seen, and the canon is reconciled, not imposed.**
   - `daoris status` reports what a sync would update or add, whatever the stamps say.
   - The lock's canon digest orders the canons, and a newer lock is never rolled back.
   - A reconcile is a case of the set-up press. Its brief is composed offline by `daoris reconcile` and worked by a model in the
     repository's own session.
   - That session adopts each item, keeps it (recorded with its reason and the canon digest in `daoris.json`), or proposes it
     upstream (a quest's words to the canon's repository). It commits in its own line.
3. **Daoris manages only the sessions it runs, each scoped to the session.**
   - The driver composes one standing block, handed at the start and at a move, never on a resume. It replaces exactly the routing
     line, the standing answer's preface and the instruction's "where things are" pieces.
   - The whole first prompt is bounded by the native door's single argument.
   - The tools are loaded at the start.
   - The tree's skills and the per-session plugin copy are the skills. Auto-memory is off for the session.
   - No account home, vendor set or person's tool home is written.
4. **The standing answer stays the person's machine choices.** Its lines that state a repository's own rule move into that repository
   through its reconcile (amending D135 §3 for those lines only).
5. **Older shapes are converted by ONEWAY1's rows,** not here.

**Rejected:**
- doctrine served by Daoris alone;
- a sync that overwrites a repository's differences;
- a reconcile beside the set-up press;
- a C# copy of D19's table;
- writing into account homes;
- a block handed again on every resume.

**Note, 2026-11-04 (CONTEXTONE1d2, the owner's ask): the tools at the start are the session's own.** Point 3's tools load whole at
the start, so a session is listed only what it may call. The kind is the work of the record the session's `DAORIS_SESSION_ID` names,
which the connector already read to refuse calls; its one table, the call layer's (`CallTable`), now also filters `tools/list`. A
session working in a repository is served the core set (18 tools: knowledge, memory, quests, its line, the registry, a helper's ask,
the channel, handbacks) and its own kind's: a quest's session its go-ahead, question, hand-over, rule proposal, opinion answer and a
set-up step's two; a conversation the first four; a helper its answer; an intake none. Ask Daoris keeps every tool. Measured in
`CoreSetTests`: 28,341 characters of definitions for the core set, 39,229 at most for a repository kind, 102,191 for all 58. Rejected:
a second list passed on the spawn (the driver's kind would be a second table beside the one that refuses calls).
