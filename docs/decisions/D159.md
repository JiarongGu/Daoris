## D159 — Shared startup instructions, current guides and structural documentation checks (2026-10-10)

**Decision (DOCSYS1).** The owner requested a review and update of the entire documentation system
after many sessions. `docs/2026-10-10-documentation-review.md` records coverage, discrepancies and
verification. D45 remains the product direction; D122 and D127 govern document roles and reading cost.

The local brief belongs in `AGENTS.md`, outside the generated doctrine region. `CLAUDE.md` keeps only
its generated import. Detailed contributor procedures belong in `docs/development.md`; the former
Claude guide is preserved in the archive. This is a local placement correction, not completion of
D117's broader layout/rooms migration or a new canonical rule.

The roadmap gives forward sequence and growth criteria. Open acceptance criteria stay in TASKS;
historical outcomes stay in the archive. The previous roadmap is preserved as a dated snapshot.
The document router is condensed into subject tables, with its previous full text archived and a
declared 2,500-word ceiling. Every document keeps a route; details remain in their original records.
Contracts keep their original baseline and evidence, with a standing pointer to later decision notes.
Current guides describe the implemented surface and identify unmeasured boundaries.

`tools/doc-system.mjs`, called by `npm run verify`, inventories Markdown and checks router coverage,
stale inline-code table paths and component guide freshness declarations. The devkit's existing
date-based docs gate covers all component guides. Neither check establishes semantic accuracy;
behavioral prose still needs a source/test review. Existing budget and shape reports remain advisory.

**Rejected:** deleting or compacting historical records (D127: lookup cost is per entry and evidence
would be lost); rewriting design-time observations as current measurements; a second task list in
the roadmap; keeping project instructions only in Claude's file; claiming link or commit-date checks
prove prose; and moving the target to `.agents` incidentally during a documentation audit (D117's
migration has its own dependencies and proofs).

**What the checks do not cover.** This change does not exercise installed windows, vendor account
flows or live adoption. Structural checks do not evaluate historical paragraphs or every source
line. The audit records the exact code areas read, gate results and remaining owner-only proofs.
