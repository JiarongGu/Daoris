## D163 — A task may go on with another agent, and a session may ask other agents to help, by the person or by the agent within the person's rule (2026-10-12)

**Decision (the owner's direction).** The owner, 2026-10-12, after a ticket's Codex conversation ran its account's
allowance low and was carried to Claude by hand: *"I don't see a way to mix the use of both agents (like codex to claude) so
currently when usage is almost full I'm kind of stuck on that task, also it should be able to run subagents to use other
agents to support the task and we need a clear view to show those behaviours (and should be decided by the agent itself or
triggered by user)"*. The design, its facts and its rows are `docs/2026-10-12-mixed-agents-design.md`.

1. **A hand-over is a carry-on with its agent named.** A session goes on in a new session on another agent, in the same
   tree, handed Daoris's record of it (plan, last words, what is uncommitted, the request); a quest stays taken and its chain
   unchanged. The machine's agent setting is not changed. A running session finishes its turn first, or the person stops it.
2. **A helper is a read-only session on another agent that answers one question** the working session or the person asked,
   on the reviewer's machinery (D155), its answer returning as the asker's next turn marked as the helper's. A helper cannot
   ask for a helper.
3. **The person or the agent may start either; the person's rule says what an agent's ask does.** Per repository or
   workspace, `handOver` and `helpers` are `ask` (default: the person's yes on the session's page), `on` (taken within the
   limits), or `off`. Daoris offers a hand-over when the session's account is near its limit by its own word, or cut by one.
4. **The view says who worked.** Records link a session to the one it continues (`from`) and a helper to the session it
   serves (`serves`); heads, coded timeline notes, the rail and the cost per row show both, in both languages.

**Rejected:** changing the machine's agent for one task; carrying the transcript or a model-written summary; automatic
hand-overs by default; writing helpers in a first version; agents conversing with no person between (D155 stands).

**Amends:** COWORK1's hold (*owner's direction required*) for hand-overs and helpers; extends TOOL6d (another account of the
same agent, D130 pt 11) across agents; XAGENT1g4d's grouping now covers helpers.

**Note, 2026-10-12 (MIXAGENT1a-i, a conversation handed over).** What the first leg settled, conversations only (a quest's
session is MIXAGENT1a-ii, and says so when asked).

1. **The ask is a box, `<home>/sessions/<id>.hand-over.json`** (agent, account, why `press|near|limit|agent`, asked-by
   `person|daoris|agent`, when), written as `<id>.new-session.json` is, listed in the home's inventory so a delete and a clear
   take it, with its name in the kept-names twin (`hand-over`). `HandOver.AskAsync` judges and writes it; each refusal has a
   code and a sentence: not found, a teammate's, an intake, Ask Daoris's, not a conversation, an unknown agent, an account that
   is not that agent's, the agent and account it already runs on, one already waiting, its tree gone, and (the runner's own) one
   another Daoris process runs, which this process cannot end.
2. **The shell's chat runner takes it** (`ChatRunner.HandOverAsync`, conversations living there and not in the driver's tick):
   at once where the conversation is not talking, else at its turn's end (the queue's change to idle), else as the shell comes
   up (`TakeUpAsync`). The terminal verb reaches the runner as `start-from` does, through the host's chat door, so the new
   conversation is that terminal's own. The box is judged again as it is taken and is gone however the take came out, so a
   refused start is not tried at every come-up.
3. **The old ends before the new starts** (a tree is held by one session at a time, D51): its process is stopped by the
   runner's own stop, its record says it ended, then the new conversation starts in the same tree (`StartAsync` gains a `tree`
   parameter, refused where it is gone) on the named agent and account. The old record's last line is `chat.handed-over`
   (agent, session); the new one's first is `chat.taken-over` (agent, session, `why` of the new `Handed` family of reasons),
   facts only, never an account's name or a path. The first prompt is a `ChatMessage` with a `Briefing` and no person's words
   (`HandOverBriefing.Compose`, a pure function: the request as first written, its last plan and words, bounded as a parked
   session's are, and what is uncommitted), recorded as that one note in place of a person's message and of the browser's
   line; where the conversation was handed the browser, its line follows the briefing in the prompt.
4. **Left for 1a-ii and 1b:** a quest's session; the page's press and the roster's offer when an account is near its limit
   (which write the same box with `daoris` as asker); the handed-over conversation shown with its `from` link.

**Note, 2026-10-12 (MIXAGENT1b-i, the press).** The page's press is `SESSION_HAND_OVER` (`id`, `agent`, optional `account`, `why`
defaulting to `press`; the asker is always the person on this door), which answers `{ sessionId, kept, why, message }` as a
refusal's answer and never throws. *Go on with another agent…* is one act with two names by state (*Stop and hand over…* while
it works), offered only on a conversation of this machine's own (not an intake, Ask Daoris's, nor a quest's session), and it
folds into the head's ⋯, never a second danger button beside *Stop…*. It asks once under the head with the agent·account chip
(the roster's agents but the conversation's own; the chosen agent's accounts in the scope's order). While it works the page asks
the hand-over first, which is kept and waits for the turn's end, and then stops the turn (`CANCEL_TURN`), so the turn's end
takes it: no flag on the route. An answer with a conversation attends it; a kept answer with none says in the ask that it goes
on with the named agent when its turn ends; `unbriefed` is said as an answer, since the new conversation opened. The
conversation's `chat.handed-over` line carries a press to the new one. Left for 1b-ii: Daoris's own offer when an account is near
or cut (`near`, `limit`, `daoris` as asker).

**Note, 2026-10-12 (MIXAGENT1b-ii, the offer).** Daoris's offer is one line under the head of a conversation this machine runs
(the same ones the press is offered on), in the place of an ask when none is open: *This conversation's account is near its limit* or
*cut until {time}*, with *Go on with {agent} ({account})*, *Choose…* and *Not now*. It is read from facts the frame already holds, no
driver read: the conversation's own account is cut (`coolingFor`; the tool's own sign-in can be cut), or near its limit by the
account's own word (the scope's `near`, workspace's own scope else the machine's, and only where its agent `speaks`; an own
sign-in is never near), and the target is the roster's first other present agent, in the roster's order, whose scope's `next` is not
`waits` (the account the walk would take, or the agent's own sign-in); none ready, no offer. It is the page's own line, not
Overview's band: `useAccountsKnown` reads the roster, the accounts and the waits for every session, where the frame holds the
first two for the one it attends. *Go on with…* is the person's press and is the only way it is taken (the `ask` rule: no
offer takes itself); *Choose…* opens MIXAGENT1b-i's ask with that agent and account chosen and the reason kept; *Not now* puts it
down for the frame's run, not stored. `SESSION_HAND_OVER` takes `why` `near` or `limit` and honours `askedBy` `daoris` only with
them (`DriverModule.HandOverAskedBy`; a press, or any other reason, is the person's whatever the payload says), and `useHandOver`
sends both for the offer. The new conversation's `from` link is MIXAGENT1a-ii's, which needs the record's field.

**Note, 2026-10-12 (MIXAGENT1a-ii, a quest's session handed over).** A driven session works its quest in one turn, so a quest's
