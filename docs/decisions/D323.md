## D323 — No build reads a record an earlier build wrote; what a purge keeps is read or refused (2026-11-19)

**Decision (NOCOMPAT1).** A change to what a store keeps changes its one creating statement, its writer, its reader and its tests
together, and leaves nothing that reads the old shape: no migration runs at a start, no importer of an older file, no column loop,
no reader of a former spelling, no census allowance for one. A store an older build opens after a purge is not supported. Two
things stay. What a purge keeps (a home's configuration: saved workflows and their versions, hand-written files, a repository's
own lock, manifest and hooks) is read in its older shape until no instance spells it, each reader naming that condition, or is
refused with a sentence saying what to do; never crashed on, never ignored. And a home that was never purged and is older than
this build's shape is refused when the service opens it, naming the file and what to do (`OlderStoreException`). The rule is
`.claude/knowledge/compatible-builds.md`; area `install-home-update.md`, rule 51.

**Why.** The ask that filed NOCOMPAT1 reserved this number, read here as: a home's history is purged (CLEANSTART1, D322) and every
repository keeps its own records, so nothing an earlier build wrote is left in front of a later one. D232's one migration per
release, its salvage copies and the readers that stayed beside it were carried for homes that no longer need them.

**Rejected:** keeping D232's migration and its readers for an older store (every release paid for a shape no supported home
holds); dropping the readers of configuration with the history (a purge keeps configuration, so a saved workflow's `human` step,
a lock from before its digest or a hand-written `coreBudgetBytes` is still in front of this build, and without its reader a
workflow stops reading or a command crashes); converting an unpurged store in place (the conversion is the migration this
rejects; refusing names the purge).

**Left:** NOCOMPAT2, the readers NOCOMPAT1 did not reach (the home table's old-place column in all three copies, the salvage
kind, the store's imports, the accounts' folding and the rest it lists), and `HumanFormerKind`, which goes once no saved version
on the install spells `human`.
