## D309 — Today's workflow engine is evolved in place to the final design, in one scope (2026-11-16)

**Decision (a fresh-design pass on the engine: two designs from a purpose brief alone, a prior-art survey, and a comparison against
the code).**

1. Route A: today's engine evolves in place to the target of D304 to D308: a step done outside settles on its holder's word and stands
   in-flight acts down; one wait reader; release targets by route with acts re-checked at their effect; every step names its role;
   the release as observed steps; acts keyed by their effect; a holder record per attempt; the place casts every role and one door
   settles a step; held decisions survive a restart (the rows DONEBYHOLDER1 to DURABLEWINDOW1).
2. It is built toward the final design in one scope, not trial by trial: builders take whole waves and hand back once; merges inside
   the scope run no per-merge gates; the full set of checks and two agent reviews (wide and close) run once at the scope's end.
3. A second engine starts only on evidence: two of three trial releases needing a hand repair traced to the lifecycle or the
   attempt-keyed journal; an outside act made twice because of the per-attempt key; HOLDDOOR1 or EFFECTKEY1 impossible without
   changing what a saved version's step means; three successive engine-touching rows each with two or more HIGH findings of the
   unreached-reader class with FRAMESWEEP1's census in place; or a second domain needing units and edges that cannot be built in one L.
4. Names: the concept is a **role** (角色) on the window and in code (a step's field is `role`); D304's "actor" reads "role". A role's
   current filler is its **holder** (担任者); how it acts is **via** (经由); a step not needed this run reads *Not needed this run*
   (本次不需要); stop reads *End this work* (终止这项工作); the reporting line's levels are named by the supervisor holding them.
5. The work workspace's mark covers its production releases only; other production writes (a configuration version beside the released
   one) follow the place's risk. The forced-restart rehearsal of a release step runs on the plugin's stand-in first; a real release is
   tried during development.

**Why.** Of 44 target concepts today's engine holds 7 whole and 17 in part, lacks 10 and contradicts 10; a second engine would still
need the accept, second-opinion and landing paths made holder-blind, or ported (about 125,000 lines); and a two-engine period was paid
for once already (D190 to D227), its older readers still in the driver.

**Rejected:** a second engine now (about 65 to 75 builder-days against about 50, two vocabularies on one window); building on a
runtime (Temporal needs a server and a database, Orleans' value is clustering); "actor" as the window's word (no fitting Chinese word;
`actor` already names a staffing choice in code); a trial before each foundation row.
