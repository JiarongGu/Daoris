## D324 — A sign-in only the person can make is a question with its site, settled by their sign-in in Daoris's browser (2026-11-19)

**Decision (SIGNINWAIT1).** A driven session whose work in Daoris's browser meets a sign-in it cannot make (an identity expired, a page
asking for a password) asks it through `question_ask` with `site`, the page: the question says which identity and why, is marked
`sign-in`, and offers no answers of the agent's. Its park is a question wait like any other, named *Sign in*, and the dock adds
*Open the sign-in…* (the site in Daoris's browser) and *Signed in* (the person's answer, sent as those words). The look reads the
running browser's pages and their addresses, never bringing it up; a page that went through a sign-in address and came back to the
site's host is the sign-in done, and Daoris's word under one key settles the park for the next look to take up (area `plugins.md`,
rule 34).

**Why.** On 2026-11-18 a session restoring production configuration through Daoris's browser found the production identity expired,
stopped and said so only in its words: nothing read *waiting on you: sign in*, and the owner's sign-in in Daoris's browser did not
resume it, so the steward told it by hand.

**Rejected:** `go_ahead_ask`'s `sign-in` kind (a go-ahead is the approver's yes for an act, held on an ask, so a conversation or a
line on no ask has none, and a yes is not the sign-in the work waits on); a tool of its own (a second way to ask the person what only
they can do, beside the question it already is); a change in the site's cookies as the sign-in (opening the sign-in page sets cookies
before anyone signs in, so the wait would settle at *Open the sign-in…*); a page on the site alone (the address the person opened
stands there before the site sends it to its sign-in).

**Left:** a conversation's sign-in need (its person reads its words as they come, and `question_ask` refuses it); a site whose sign-in
returns to another host than the one the agent named settles only by *Signed in*.
