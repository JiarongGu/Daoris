## D364 — A product of several repositories is a family: each consumer pins one landed commit per producer, a landing fans out one pin-move quest per edge, and a break holds the pin (2026-11-26)

**Decision (FAMILY1, under D290 and D349; the owner's direction: a complete game will need several repositories whose
agents work as a chain, developed together over a long time and improved as they go).** Brainstorm `gamefamily-1126`
asked Fable 5.2 and DeepSeek with an outside suite of apps as prior art (a shared-code repository every app pins at one
merged revision, a pin move as its own change); both answered one model and differed on four points, chosen below.

1. **The family is declared, the graph derived.** Each repository keeps its edges in its own `daoris.json` `uses`. A
   family file, workspace data, names the members, a layer order and optional denied pairs; no family file, no family.
   One check, which needs no service, refuses a cycle (printing its path), a non-member, an edge to an equal or higher
   layer and a pin with no `uses`; a `uses` with no pin is warned, not refused, so an edge may be declared before its
   first pin. No producer keeps a list of its consumers.
2. **A pin is one full commit per (consumer, producer)**, kept in the consumer's own `daoris.family.lock` (not in
   `daoris.json`: a pin move is then checkable by path), valid only on the producer's line; a branch, a tag or "latest"
   is refused, and a second pin of one producer in one consumer is refused. Two consumers may pin different commits.
3. **What a pin becomes is the workspace's.** Each repository declares a `materialize` command that turns its pins into
   what its build reads (a checkout of the producer at the pin, a package restored, a generated reference); its gates
   build against the pinned producer, never the sibling checkout as it happens to stand.
4. **A landing fans out by edge, automatically.** A producer's landing opens, or retargets to the new commit, the one
   pin-move quest of each consumer edge; nothing is written into the consumer. The consumer's own session runs its gates
   at the new commit and lands the pin with only the adaptation the move forces, never a feature. On red the pin holds
   and the line stays green: a hold-back record with its age, a break quest to the producer with the failing case, and
   the person told only past the workspace's lag bound. Whether a green pin move lands unasked is the risk policy's, as
   every landing is (D354); the fan-out itself needs no press.
5. **A feature across repositories stays D290's.** Its parts build at once against the contract and land under the
   joint receipt; the consumer part's landing carries its pin move to the producer part's landed commit. Code moved into
   a shared repository is three changes: the shared one gains the code, its tests and attribution; each consumer moves
   its pin; each deletes its copy with its tests unchanged.
6. **Framework and configuration** (D349): Daoris supplies the formats, the check, pin validity, the fan-out, the
   pin-move change kind and the lag report; a workspace declares members, layers, each repository's `materialize`
   command and the gates a pin move runs, and per edge its cadence and lag bound. The rules agents follow (never pin a
   branch, a pin move is its own change, an extraction is three changes) ship as a canon pack.
7. **Environments** (D361): an environment's version is its owner's line commit; a lease matches the user's pin of that
   owner, or the user gets its own instance. Built after SHAREDENV1 and the pin.

**Rejected.** A pin per landing quest (a busy producer floods its consumers: one open quest per edge, retargeted);
pins in `daoris.json` (a pin move would not be a path of its own); refusing a `uses` with no pin (it blocks declaring an
edge first); a pin move that holds only the pin (an incompatible API cannot be adapted against the old pin); from the
prior art: pins moved by hand, a fixed taxonomy of repository roles with "no app depends on an app" as law (it forbids
game to engine), a CI-actions repository as a member, a copied rules file and a second layer table (the canon sync and
the structure gates already do both), and one family-wide revision by default (a workspace may still choose it).

**First slice.** In `examples/engine` and `examples/game`, through `npm run rehearse:family`: a family file with two
members and one edge; game pins one landed engine commit; the check green on game to engine and red on a planted
cycle; game's gate builds at the pinned commit; an engine landing opens game's pin-move quest; a breaking engine
landing keeps the old pin and files the break quest. Rows `FAMILY1a` to `FAMILY1e`.
