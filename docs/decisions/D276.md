## D276 — Any agent opens a department or a room when the work needs one: its press ends before content, the work follows as quests, and a person-only switch can hold it to asked or off (2026-11-11)

**Decision (GROWDEPT1).** Repositories and rooms are the company's departments (D193), the structure co-working runs on, and until now
only the person made them; a request needing a part of its own was done inside one Ask Daoris turn. The contract is
`docs/2026-11-11-grow-department-design.md`.

1. **Which place.** The department that already owns the work, then a room in it where the work needs conventions of its own, and only
   then a new department; the person's own words go ahead of that order. Any agent asks it of the registry and the records, and says
   in its turn or handback which places it looked at.
2. **Who.** Any agent: Ask Daoris, a lead or a driven session, through one press, its tier deciding as for every press.
3. **The press's hand.** `daoris repository create` makes the folder, an empty history, the registration, the drive and the quests, and
   stops before any content; `place <repository> --room <path>` is a part of the one set-up door and has no hand of its own, its whole a
   quest to that repository. Unasked in full access, on a card in restricted mode; not a grantable family of acts.
4. **The switch.** `growth` per workspace and repository: `mode` (the default, as the access mode says), `asked` (a card even in full
   access) or `off` (refused even in full access). Set by the person alone, never by an agent in any mode.
5. **The work follows.** The adoption and the work go to the new place's own session as one quest chain; nothing of the department's
   text is written by the agent that opened it.

**Rejected.** The turn doing set-up and adoption itself (today's way, around the structure); the press writing `daoris.json`, the brief
or the first files; an act family that would let restricted mode open departments unasked; opening as Ask Daoris's act alone; the
switch as an agent's setting or as the access mode alone; a stored workspace folder beside the registry's roots; a room verb of its own
beside `place`; the intake creating departments.

**Supersedes nothing.** It adds the agents' act; the person's own `repository add`, `import` and `place --adoption` stay as they are.
