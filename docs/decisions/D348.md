## D348 — Words held for a record's own account are read again at each look, and go on elsewhere past half an hour (2026-11-20)

**Decision (HELDWORDS1, on how the *cannot go on* marks are read for the reason `account`).** A record's words that could
not go on because its own account could not carry them stay marked by their ids (`<session>.cannot.json`, why `account`), but the
look reads that mark again each time (`UnableLook`, `HeldAccountWords`) against the account as the resume judges it without a probe
(`HarnessRoster.OwnHold`: present, in the set, not kept, cooling or refused, its last reading not signed out; an unknown sign-in is
usable, as a start treats it):

1. **Usable again**, five minutes or more after the mark, the planner goes on with the words: they resume the record's own
   conversation, on its own account. A try the resume refuses again marks them anew, so a disagreement costs a note per five
   minutes, never one per look.
2. **Unusable past half an hour** from the mark, the words a new session is handed (the person's, Daoris's that state a fact, a
   helper's answer) go on in a new session on a ready account, in the record's tree: a closed quest's session through the shell's
   runner, as an account that cools while another is ready already does; an open or taken quest's record already carries them on
   at once. Another agent's findings alone are never handed to a new session; they went to the person when they were held, and
   stay there.
3. **The person's choice**: a session awaiting its step whose words are all so marked is no longer reached by them, so *Go on in a
   new session* (`daoris session hand-over <id>`, the hand-over press naming no agent) takes it; a choice made after the mark lifts
   it at the next look.

The dock says each lift as `held  session …` with why.

**Why.** On the install three authors awaiting their step were handed another agent's findings while an update hid their account;
the resume judged the account missing, the words were marked, and the mark held them for good: the account came back and nothing
looked again, and the only door refused them as words that reach the session. An account is a fact that moves by itself (a sign-in
made again, a folder back, a cool-off ended), unlike a closed quest or another client holding the conversation.

**Rejected.**

- *Clear an `account` mark at every look and let the go-on judge again*: every look would resume, refuse and write the same
  *cannot go on* note into the session while the account stays gone.
- *Wait for the own account however long it takes, saying so*: a signed-out account waits for a person who may not be there; the
  owner's runs stood still for over an hour on exactly that.
- *Carry another agent's findings into the new session*: they would reach it as the person's words, which makes claims
  requirements; the person already holds them.
