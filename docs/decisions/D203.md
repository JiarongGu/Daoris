## D203 — A session's co-workers are its team: each a record of its own serving the session's line in a role, shown in the session's head, its conversation and its own thread like a session; co-working takes named structures (a team, sessions alongside, a relay, several reviewers, mixed makers), each made of links the records already carry and chosen by the door that starts it; and a session waiting on its own co-worker waits on it, not on the person (2026-10-23)

**Decision (COWORKUX1, proposed 2026-10-23).** **Status: proposed**, round 1, under D191: the synthesis of three independent
proposals (Claude's, Codex's through Daoris's helper, Fable's) written from one brief, with the owner's direction on structures
that reached the steward after the brief. No reader has read it; Codex's read and Fable's foundation read are the steward's to
ask, since it changes how a driven turn's end is read and what a reviewer's record carries. The contract is
`docs/2026-10-23-coworker-design.md`; its §1 compares the proposals question by question and says what each choice rejects.

The owner found the helper (助手) shown as a side answer, the partner (D199) and the reviewer (D155) separate notions, and none of
them reading as a colleague beside the session; and then directed that co-working takes more than one structure, chosen by the
task, each first-class, chosen by the person, the asking agent or a workflow, shown and driven like sessions, with its rules.
On the install, a driven session that asked a Codex helper and ended its turn so the helper could start was read as waiting on
the person, and the helper's answer could not reach it (HELPERWAIT1). Read at `0a4a14d9a`, a reviewer's record carries no
`serves`, so the server's role filter refuses it nothing (`OpinionDesk.cs:128-131`, `RoleFilter.cs:108-118`).

1. **A co-worker is a record of this machine's serving another session's line in one role**: `serves` names a part of the line;
   the role is read, never stored, as reviewer (`opinion`), partner (`serves` with `piece`) or helper (`serves` alone). It has
   its own entry and one turn, and never writes the lead's tree. A part, another repository's session, a meeting's participant,
   a native subagent, Ask Daoris, an intake and a brainstorm's pass are not co-workers.
2. **A session's team is its line and every record serving any part of it**, and follows the line through a hand-over. A
   record may name the one it follows (`after`), and a record with those after it is one thread: one row, one card with its
   follow-ups, the colleague the person asked again. A member is a record; no key by agent groups records.
3. **The structures are named and first-class, and each is made of what the records carry**: serving (`serves`), continuing
   (`from`) and the ask's channel. **Team** (lead and co-workers): the lead alone writes its tree. **Alongside** (lead and lead):
   two leads of two lines on one ask, each its own tree, the person's one *Accept* their meeting point; never two writers in one
   tree. **Relay**: parts in sequence along `from`, one at a time in one tree. **Several reviewers**: N opinions of one candidate,
   one gate, agreement establishing nothing. **Mixed makers**: any of these with members of several makers, each marked. Each
   keeps the rules design §3.2 lists (D51, D155, D163, D167, D188, D189, D199). No structure is stored, and no model chooses one.
4. **The door that starts a structure is its choice.** The person chooses by the door they press, gathered in one dialog,
   *Bring in another agent…*; the lead by its tools under the person's rules (`helpers`, `partners`, `handOver`); a workflow by
   the opinion's occasions, and later by a declared node (D157, D190). Ask Daoris proposes the person's doors and applies none.
5. **The lead waits on its own co-worker, not on the person.** A driven lead that ends its turn cleanly with its quest taken and
   an open helper ask of its own, and no open question to the person, ends `completed` with `ended.awaits-helper`, held by the
   planner from the ask's box (no park, no carry-on, no strike, no running slot), and its helper's answer reopens it as one go-on
   under D188 point 4. A partner holds it only where the lead itself asked the piece in that turn. A co-worker that ends with no
   answer moves the lead `completed → awaiting-person` with its own note. A lead parked on the person keeps its park and its
   helper's answer is kept on its record at once, going on with the person's answer. This is HELPERWAIT1's mechanism; the row
   keeps its id.
6. **The reviewer's record gains `serves`**, naming the session whose work it reads, this machine's own as `opinion` is, so the
   server's role filter binds it as it binds a helper. Every reader that took `serves` to mean helper reads `opinion` first.
7. **The page draws the team one way.** The head's *Helpers* becomes *Co-workers* (同事): the lead, then each member with its
   maker's mark, role, state and door, the pending asks keeping their presses. Each exchange is one card in the lead's
   conversation, placed at the ask, its body read from the box, its run, delivery and taking-in said apart, never folded as
   *The target Daoris composed*. A member's conversation opens in the same surface as a thread with *Back to lead*. The rail nests
   members under the lead before *Ended* is truncated, says each one's role, and folds ended ones behind a count. *What needs
   you* gains no kind.
8. **Talking to a member is asking it again**: a new one-turn record on a fresh copy, its `after` naming the one it follows;
   nothing is resumed. The person's follow-up to a helper answers the person, and reaches the lead only by *Hand to the lead*,
   as the helper's claim; a follow-up the lead asks returns to the lead. A partner's follow-up is a new piece; a reviewer's is
   D155's recheck. The person's ask is their word for D188's answers-alone count.
9. **The names.** *co-worker* / **同事**, not 协作者: 协作者 is a person with rights in a repository on Chinese git hosts, 协作
   already titles *Who it worked with*, and it spends three of a status's five units. *team* / 团队 is the structure's name in
   prose; the window titles the section *Co-workers*, since *team* already names the people sharing a workspace's remote. *lead*
   主导, *helper* 助手, *partner* 搭档, *reviewer* 阅读者, *piece* 分项 are terms; the structures are *alongside* 并行, *relay*
   接力, *several reviewers* 多位阅读者 (*panel* is the window's region, 面板) and *mixed makers* 多厂商; *subagent* narrows to
   an agent inside a session's own process with no record of its own.
10. **Both doors.** The terminal lists `serves`, `piece` and `role` in `sessions --json`, prints the team with `sessions team`,
    keeps the role verbs (`sessions helper` gaining `--after` and `--to-me`) and hands a person-only answer with `sessions hand`.
11. **The rows** are the design's §12, filed when this is accepted. The first shows today's helper as a co-worker in the
    session's team and changes no stored shape; HELPERWAIT1 is built beside it, and the first's installed proof waits for both.

**Why.** The records already make a team: a helper and a partner are records serving their lead, and a reviewer needs one field
to join them and the server's filter, which it lacks today. What was missing is one reading of them and one drawing. The owner's
structures need no new mechanism, because each is a shape of `serves`, `from` and the channel; naming them is what lets the
person and the rules see who writes the tree and who starts a turn in each. The lead's state comes before any live drawing of
its team, since a session read as waiting on the person while it waits on its own co-worker makes every live state beside it
wrong.

**Rejected.** A member keyed by agent across records (Claude's), since it groups unrelated asks and needs a rule for when a
member ends; the reviewer joined only through its opinion (Claude's, Codex's), which leaves it outside the server's filter; the
lead kept `working` while it waits (Claude's), which the session watch and the come-up would each need an exception for; the
lead kept parked with a cause every reader must learn (Codex's), which leaves every reader of a park wrong until it does; a
reader's rule placing an unanswered lead with the person (Fable's first choice), the same leak; a co-worker's answer to the
person's follow-up always sent to the lead (Fable's), which makes the person's every question a go-on of the lead; *reader* /
读 for the helper on the page (Claude's), beside 阅读者; 协作者 (Codex's); *Team* as the window's title (Codex's, Fable's); a
stored structure, a coordinator agent, two leads of one tree, depth past one, and members talking to each other.

**Amends, once accepted:** D163 point 2 for the person's follow-ups, and point 4's view; D155 point 11's record, and point 10's
cap when several reviewers are built; D199 §6.6's names; D167's SESSONE2b note when CHANPART1 is built; D116's glossary. D51,
D137, D177, D188, D189 and D193 stand.
