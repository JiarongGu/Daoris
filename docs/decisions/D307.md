## D307 — An act is checked again when it takes effect (2026-11-16)

**Decision.** An act with an outside effect re-reads, at the moment it acts, the facts its approval or choice named (the target, the
build, the source branch, the version, the scope); one that no longer matches is refused (`target-changed`) and goes back to the role
that chose it. A destination's mark is judged per act's destination; a place's risk level never changes mid-run.

**Why.** On 2026-11-16 a release went to the wrong release definition: the target was chosen without reading that the work merged
into a branch whose automatic release is another definition, and the plugin started a production stage with nothing checking the
choice again.

**Rejected:** per-step risk settings to catch a change of risk (the place sets risk once); trusting the choice made at setup time.
