## D209 — The steward is a role the owner appoints once in the window: its holder makes development's management acts on the owner's behalf with no card, each recorded as the steward's and overruled by the owner, on D208's appointment with one holder per run, D201's presses its acts (2026-10-24)

**Decision (STEWARDROLE1), proposed 2026-10-24.** **Status: proposed**, round 1, written by Claude Opus at `d580caeb4`, for Codex's read and
an independent Opus reader's foundation read under D191 (its amendment of 2026-10-23: it changes the pipe's census and its judge's
admission, and amends D201 and D208, which other designs rest on). The steward settles it after both reads. The contract is
`docs/2026-10-24-steward-role-design.md`.

The direction (the quest that asked for this design, 2026-10-24): the steward is a role the owner appoints, so development's management
needs no click. Every management act the steward makes for the owner from the terminal waits for the window's Confirm today (a repository
driven or given trees, a setup, a session's words, a go-on after an outage, a hand-over, a finish), and the owner pressed many such cards
on 2026-10-24. These sit under D208's role logic: appointed once in the window, acting on the owner's behalf by its own decision, each act
recorded as the steward's and never as the owner's words, revocable at once; never a production or live go-ahead, a sign-in, an access
widening, a push or publication, spending, or a deletion of the owner's data.

What was read (design §0). Each of those acts is a `Person` route in the pipe's census (`PipeCensus.cs:203-257`), so a terminal's press
waits for the window's card and, confirmed, is the owner's: a confirmed `sessions say` is kept with no author (`Sessions.cs:398`), a
set-up's ask is published as the person's and later quoted to sessions as *the person's own words … verbatim* though Daoris composed it
(`SetupPress.cs:185-194`, `AskWords.cs:143-144`), a hand-over is `HandOverBy.Person`. D208's door is not built: no `SET_APPOINTMENT`, no
`Role` class, no holder key. D201's standing confirmation is built and not offered; it was asked for the steward's stops and finishes, and
its containment is on the install with its look not yet complete on every door. The steward's working session merges and runs the gates,
so it runs other branches' code.

1. **The steward** (管家, design §1, §8). A second role on D208's appointment, scoped to this Daoris: every repository its registry names,
   one registered later included. Appointed once on Settings → This Daoris → *Roles* by the owner's own press; off by default; lasting
   until Daoris restarts, then dormant until *Appoint again*.
2. **One appointment model** (§2). D208's record, judge, mutation step, key, proof, image check and dedicated holder, amended so **one
   holder per run holds every role appointed to it**: the first *Appoint…* of a run starts the holder's terminal with the key, a later one
   adds its role to that holder with its own generation; the variable is `DAORIS_ROLE_KEY`; *Appoint again* names every role found.
   **D201's four presses (`stop`, `finish`, `hold`, `resume`) and TERMTRUST1b's `done` become the steward's acts**, in D201's forms and
   conditions; the standing confirmation is not offered as a grant of its own, its code serving the role; every role's activation waits
   for D201 part (i)'s case 4 seen on every door, rather than for D201's offering.
3. **Its acts** (§3.1), each one route in one exact form: drive and stop driving a registered repository; give it trees (never take them
   away); hold, and resume its own hold; stop a session; finish one waiting with nothing running (never decline); hand a quest's session
   over to another configured agent with no account named, and answer an agent's own ask to; say words to a quest's session; set a
   repository up with Daoris's fixed nine-verb rule, only where the owner ticked it at the appointment; close a quest as done (after
   TERMTRUST1b). Each is the steward's by its own decision, with no card. **Everything else is never a role's act** (§3.2), refused before
   any handler: go-aheads, sign-ins and accounts, every widening, landings and every push, publication or acceptance, spending (the cap,
   the actors, an opinion, an account named), deletions, and what only the owner's word lets go on.
4. **Its door** (§3.3-§3.4): one route, `ROLE_ACT { role, act, generation, form, proof }`, class `Role`, judged in order by the caller look,
   the proof, the appointment, the act's table and form, and its conditions; admitted once under the judge's lock; run through the act's own
   handler inside `PipeDoor.EnterRole`. The verbs are `daoris-driver steward <act>`, over the pipe only; every press verb refuses
   `role-terminal` where the key is set, so the holder never presses as the owner.
5. **Its words** (§4): kept as `From: steward`, only through an admitted act whose id the driver's memory recorded, any other claim
   quarantined; handed in a paragraph Daoris composes that grants nothing and puts the owner's words first; only to a quest's own session,
   never on an open question or go-ahead, never `now`, no files; outside COWORK1e's count; taken back by the owner while waiting. A set-up's
   ask carries `by: steward` and is never quoted as the person's.
6. **The record, the notice and the overrule** (§5): the note part `role.steward` on what it acted on, `HandOverBy.Steward`, a setting's
   *Set by the steward* from the run's memory; `role.act`, `role.refused`, `role.appointment` in the machine log; one line per role in
   *Notifications* opening to the run's acts, each with the owner's reverse press, no notice per act. Revoked at once; reversed by the
   owner's ordinary press; and an owner's press on a route and target makes it theirs for the run (`owner-set`). No overrule window.
7. **The holder and the working session** (§6): the holder is D208's dedicated session, the steward's management desk, running only read
   tools and the install's own `daoris-driver` in its roles' verbs and the reads an open route answers, never a verb that runs git itself
   or an agent; driving a repository is not dispatching. The steward's working session, which builds, tests and merges, **holds no key and
   no role**: its presses ask as today, and its management needs reach the holder through the records, then the desk
   (`STEWARD_REQUEST`, open, read by the holder as claims).

**Why.** A card is worth a press only where the owner decides something (D37, D193 point 1). Each act here either only narrows, is the
development's own run on accounts and a cap the owner set, or is undone by one press of theirs; the one widening, the set-up's rule, is
the owner's own tick. Recording the act as the steward's, not as the owner's confirmed press, keeps the owner's words theirs and makes
the overrule real; D208's key is what lets the record name the role, which an unkeyed terminal outside every agent job cannot, since it
is the owner's own terminal as much as the steward's. One holder per run keeps one dedicated session and one press after a restart for
both roles. Keeping the key out of the working session keeps every process that runs a branch's code from acting as the steward.

**Rejected** (design §11): the working session holding the key, a signer in it, or stripping the key before each build; D201's standing
confirmation as the steward's door; two doors for D201's presses; a route per act; a card per act; an overrule window on every act; a
notice per act; the steward's words as the owner's, bare, or counted in COWORK1e's count; `trees off`, `decline`, *Try again*, pause and
resume, a quiet session's carry-on; naming an account on a hand-over; a holder per role; 总管. Not taken now, the owner's to choose later:
keeping the owner's overrules past a restart; an unkeyed terminal's standing grant for the owner's own presses.
