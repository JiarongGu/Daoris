The direction (the quest that asked for this design, 2026-10-24): the steward is a role the owner appoints, so development's management
needs no click. Every management act the steward makes for the owner from the terminal waits for the window's Confirm today (a repository
driven or given trees, a setup, a session's words, a go-on after an outage, a hand-over, a finish), and the owner pressed many such cards
on 2026-10-24. These sit under D208's role logic: appointed once in the window, acting on the owner's behalf by its own decision, each act
recorded as the steward's and never as the owner's words, revocable at once; never a production or live go-ahead, a sign-in, an access
widening, a push or publication, spending, or a deletion of the owner's data.

What was read (design §0). Each of those acts is a `Person` route in the pipe's census, so a terminal's press waits for the window's card
and, confirmed, is the owner's, and its record says so: a stop's and a finish's own notes read *the person stopped/finished*, a hand-over's
reason *the person asked for it*, words to a running session are kept on the ask as the person's verbatim words, and a set-up's ask is
quoted to sessions as the person's though Daoris composed it. SUPERVISOR1a-ii's door is built dark on main: `appointments` in D201's
record, a key per appointment in `DAORIS_SUPERVISOR_KEY`, its hash in memory alone, the holder's terminal a tab of Daoris's own terminal
panel in `work/rooms/roles/supervisor/`, `PipeClass.Role` and `PipeDoor.Role`, `SUPERVISOR_WAITING` told by event, `AppointmentsOffered`
false and waiting on D201's offering, the one flag the supervisor's door reads. The holder's start line loads the user's own settings,
hooks and plugins. A hand-over, an agent's approved ask and a terminal's live stop take effect after their handler returns, the start
rereading the handed file from disk; Ask Daoris's applied proposals and other owner doors act on the same repositories and quests as the
`SET_*` routes. The programs the holder's key is handed to are files any process of the account can change: the tab's shell is the
home's tools file's `pwsh` else PATH's, the harness the `claude` that shell's PATH resolves, an npm install of the account's, and the
tab's environment Daoris's own inherited one; Daoris also keeps a managed, pinned `claude-code` the ACP door starts by its path. Trees are
opt-in, so a repository never driven is by default a checkout one. D201's standing confirmation is built and not offered; its containment's look is not yet complete on every door. The
driver reads no provider error today; AGENTERRORS1 (D210, proposed) ends a session whose provider kept failing `ended.transient`, held
until the person's words or *Try again*. The steward's working session merges and runs the gates, so it runs other branches' code.

1. **The steward** (管家, design §1, §8). A second role on D208's appointment, scoped to this Daoris (`scope: machine`): every repository its
   registry names, one registered later included. Appointed on Settings → This Daoris → *Roles* by the owner's own press; off by default;
   lasting until Daoris restarts, then dormant until *Appoint again*, which re-appoints one role at a time from the window's ask, never from
   the file, its set-up tick always shown off.
2. **One appointment model** (§2), amending SUPERVISOR1a-ii's door as built: a holder is apart from its appointment, so a later *Appoint…*
   either adds its role to the live holder's tab or gives it a terminal of its own, the owner choosing knowingly; a role revoked this run
   goes only to a new holder; a holder ends with its last role or its tab, the judge told on every path a tab ends by; the variable is
   `DAORIS_ROLE_KEY`, and the entry records that a process started in a role's terminal; a shared holder's folder is
   `work/rooms/roles/holder/`; the start line, stated whole, allows every role's verbs and the open reads by exact prefix, the judge being
   the authority, and `roles brief` gives the holder its brief, every role verb's answer naming the holder's roles and the brief's
   revision. An appointment carries `scope`, the `acts` its ask named and the owner's `setup` tick. **D201's four presses (`stop`,
   `finish`, `hold`, `resume`) become the steward's acts**; the standing confirmation is not offered as a grant of its own. **Each role is
   offered by a flag of its own** (`SupervisorOffered`, `StewardOffered`): the steward's activation waits for D201 part (i)'s case 4 seen
   on every door and never turns on the supervisor's; SUPERVISOR1a-iii's waits for SUPERVISOR1b and the same look, in place of D201's
   offering.
3. **Its acts** (§3.1), each one route in one exact form on a named path, writing a record of its own: drive a registered repository whose
   landing does not push **and whose sessions open trees of their own** (`no-tree` otherwise, so Daoris's sessions never work in the
   owner's checkout by the steward's act), and stop driving any registered repository; give it trees where its landing does not push (never take them
   away); hold, and resume its own hold; on a quest's own session, stop it (parked, or live in a process this instance runs: `running-elsewhere` otherwise), finish it where it waits with nothing running and nothing said
   to it (never a helper's or a workflow node's, never decline), neither where it would make a pushed landing due; try again after its own
   stop where that releases the stop and forgives no strike; hand it over to an agent in the machine's effective `build` order (within a repository's
   hand-over fence where one exists) with no account named, and answer an agent's ask to, binding the exact ask and the agent the holder saw; one fixed go-on after an error
   AGENTERRORS1 read, once per quest per run; set a repository up with Daoris's fixed nine-verb rule where the owner ticked it, held again against the plan
   applied. **Everything else is never a role's act** (§3.2), refused before any handler: go-aheads, sign-ins and accounts, every
   widening, landings and every push or publication, **closing a quest as done and every acceptance of what is delivered**, spending,
   deletions, what only the owner's word lets go on, and free words.
4. **Its door** (§3.3-§3.4): one route, `ROLE_ACT { role, act, generation, form, proof }`, class `Role`, judged in order by the caller look,
   the proof, the appointment, the act's table, the appointment's `acts` and the form, and its conditions; admitted once under the judge's
   lock with an act's id Daoris draws and registered in the run's memory; then, under its target's gate, checked again in one step (not
   cancelled by a revoke, the holder and generation live, the target not the owner's) before it runs inside `PipeDoor.EnterRole`. **An act
   whose effect lands later is a pending operation** (a hand-over, a yes to an agent's ask, *Try again*, the go-on): its final consumer
   (the look, the turn's end, **the look's `GoAsync` for a session parked on the owner, its box `askedBy: steward` with the operation's
   id**, the start, the planner's start) takes the quest's gate and the operation from memory, checks it again and
   acts with its fields and as the steward, never from a file; a revoke or the owner's act cancels it. The live stop runs in the instance
   with its note, never through the request folder. The verbs are `daoris-driver steward <act>`, over the pipe only; in a role's terminal,
   by the marker its entry recorded, every press verb and every alone path refuses `role-terminal`.
5. **Its go-on** (§4): one fixed go-on, **AGENTERRORS1's release of a quest held after its agent's provider kept failing**: admitted only
   where the quest's last record ended `ended.transient` and its quest is held for it (`not-outage`), nothing waiting on it (no question,
   go-ahead or word of any author), and the quest not the owner's; **one per quest per run** (`go-on-spent`: a quest whose

   with its act's id, so a restart withdraws only the steward's and keeps the owner's. **It takes no briefed fallback and no walk**: a
   resume that meets the account, the conversation or the choice gone is not started, the release withdrawn and the record held. A pending
   operation consumed by the planner's start, its record check D210's end key in the start's own step, it is withdrawn by a revoke, the
   owner's act or a failed check; a failure after the start is admitted restores the record to `ended.transient` and its hold, every word
   kept. D210's matching amendment, with its reason, is the design's §4.3a, a note D210's next round takes. Free words
   are not decided here: they are their own row (STEWARDROLE1h) with their own read, as D208 gave SUPERVISOR1f.

7. **The holder and the working session** (§6): the holder is the dedicated session in its tab, running only read tools and the install's
   own `daoris-driver` in its roles' verbs and the reads an open route answers; driving a repository is not dispatching. **Daoris starts
   the holder's harness itself as the tab's process**: its managed, pinned `claude-code` by its absolute path and hash, an argument
   vector with no shell between, and an environment Daoris composes (no inherited loader variable, the updater off), refused
   `holder-harness` with no pin and never PATH's; wherever a role's tab is still a shell, the system's Windows PowerShell, never the tools
   file's or PATH's. **Its harness runs on a configuration Daoris writes** (its `settings.json` byte for byte and no instruction file,
   other settings file, plugin, command, agent, skill or hook folder, the harness's own state left alone, the user's own configuration
   never read; the owner signs in once in the tab), **established at each act by what the harness actually started with**: its image,
   its argument vector and configuration directory read from the process, the owned files checked, refused `holder-config` otherwise.
   That the harness honours that configuration, and reads no instruction file above the holder's folder, is an open limit proved at
   activation with canaries. **Not closed**: the programs the key is handed to are files of the account's, as built entirely and after
   1a-i at the pinned binary's folder and what its Bash tool resolves, so a process of the account that rewrites them, or reaches into the
   holder, can act as the steward. The steward's working session **holds no key and no role**: its presses ask as today, and its
   management needs reach the holder through the desk (`STEWARD_REQUEST`, open, bounded per caller, keyed by the caller's agent job or
   long-lived ancestor, and in all, read as claims, admitting nothing), which lands before activation.

**Why.** A card is worth a press only where the owner decides something (D37, D193 point 1). Each act here either only narrows, is the
development's own run on accounts and a cap the owner set, or is undone by one press of theirs that exists, and none may publish: where a
repository's landing pushes, driving it, giving it trees, and a stop or finish that would make its landing due stay the owner's. The one
widening, the set-up's rule, is the owner's own tick, and accepting what is delivered stays theirs. Recording the act with records of its own, not as the owner's
confirmed press with a suffix, keeps the owner's words theirs and makes the overrule real; D208's key is what lets the record name the
role, which an unkeyed terminal outside every agent job cannot. Keying the overrule by the thing acted on, marking it at every owner door,
and ordering it by a gate that every deferred effect's consumer also takes, is what makes it stick against another door, a race and a
later effect. Keeping the key out of the working session keeps every process that runs a branch's code from holding it, and the user's
configuration out of the holder keeps a planted hook from running beside it; neither keeps such a process from choosing the programs the
key is handed to, which is why Daoris starts its own pinned harness with an environment it composes, and why what remains (the account's
own files and reach) is said, not claimed closed. Refusing a drive with no session trees keeps Daoris's sessions out of the owner's
checkout, where a done delivers with no acceptance. One go-on per quest per run, with no briefed fallback and no walk, keeps D210's hold
the bound before the owner: an outage never loops without them.
