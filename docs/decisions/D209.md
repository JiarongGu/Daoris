## D209 — The steward is a role the owner appoints once in the window: its holder makes development's management acts on the owner's behalf with no card, each recorded as the steward's and overruled by the owner, on D208's appointment with a holder shared or its own, D201's presses its acts (2026-10-24)

**Decision (STEWARDROLE1), proposed 2026-10-24.** **Status: proposed**, round 2. Round 1 was written by Claude Opus at `d580caeb4` and read
at `48f9b3cff` by Codex (another round) and the independent Opus foundation reader (accept after named changes, 20 findings, 7 blocking),

The direction (the quest that asked for this design, 2026-10-24): the steward is a role the owner appoints, so development's management
needs no click. Every management act the steward makes for the owner from the terminal waits for the window's Confirm today (a repository
driven or given trees, a setup, a session's words, a go-on after an outage, a hand-over, a finish), and the owner pressed many such cards
on 2026-10-24. These sit under D208's role logic: appointed once in the window, acting on the owner's behalf by its own decision, each act
recorded as the steward's and never as the owner's words, revocable at once; never a production or live go-ahead, a sign-in, an access
widening, a push or publication, spending, or a deletion of the owner's data.

What was read (design §0). Each of those acts is a `Person` route in the pipe's census, so a terminal's press waits for the window's card
and, confirmed, is the owner's, and its record says so: a stop's and a finish's own notes read *the person stopped/finished*, a hand-over's
reason *the person asked for it*, words to a running session are kept on the ask as the person's verbatim words, and a set-up's ask is
quoted to sessions as the person's though Daoris composed it. SUPERVISOR1a-ii's door is built dark on main: `appointments` in D201's
record, a key per appointment in `DAORIS_SUPERVISOR_KEY`, its hash in memory alone, the holder's terminal a tab of Daoris's own terminal
panel in `work/rooms/roles/supervisor/`, `PipeClass.Role` and `PipeDoor.Role`, `SUPERVISOR_WAITING` told by event, `AppointmentsOffered`
false and waiting on D201's offering. D201's standing confirmation is built and not offered; its containment's look is not yet complete on
every door. The steward's working session merges and runs the gates, so it runs other branches' code.

1. **The steward** (管家, design §1, §8). A second role on D208's appointment, scoped to this Daoris (`scope: machine`): every repository its
   registry names, one registered later included. Appointed on Settings → This Daoris → *Roles* by the owner's own press; off by default;
   lasting until Daoris restarts, then dormant until *Appoint again*, which re-appoints one role at a time from the window's ask, never from
   the file, its set-up tick always shown off.
2. **One appointment model** (§2), amending SUPERVISOR1a-ii's door as built: a holder is apart from its appointment, so a later *Appoint…*
   either adds its role to the live holder's tab or gives it a terminal of its own, the owner choosing knowingly; a role revoked this run
   goes only to a new holder; a holder ends with its last role or its tab's shell; the variable is `DAORIS_ROLE_KEY`; a shared holder's
   folder is `work/rooms/roles/holder/`; the start line allows every role's verbs, the judge being the authority, and `roles brief` gives the
   holder its brief. An appointment carries `scope`, the `acts` its ask named and the owner's `setup` tick. **D201's four presses (`stop`,
   `finish`, `hold`, `resume`) become the steward's acts**; the standing confirmation is not offered as a grant of its own; every role's
   activation waits for D201 part (i)'s case 4 seen on every door, not for D201's offering.
3. **Its acts** (§3.1), each one route in one exact form on a named path, writing a record of its own: drive and stop driving a registered
   repository whose landing does not push; give it trees (never take them away); hold, and resume its own hold; on a quest's own session,
   stop it, finish it where it waits with nothing running and nothing said to it (never a helper's or a workflow node's, never decline),
   try again after its own stop, hand it over to another configured agent with no account named and answer an agent's ask to, binding the
   exact ask; one fixed go-on; set a repository up with Daoris's fixed nine-verb rule where the owner ticked it, held again against the plan
   applied. **Everything else is never a role's act** (§3.2), refused before any handler: go-aheads, sign-ins and accounts, every
   widening, landings and every push or publication, **closing a quest as done and every acceptance of what is delivered**, spending,
   deletions, what only the owner's word lets go on, and free words.
4. **Its door** (§3.3-§3.4): one route, `ROLE_ACT { role, act, generation, form, proof }`, class `Role`, judged in order by the caller look,
   the proof, the appointment, the act's table, the appointment's `acts` and the form, and its conditions; admitted once under the judge's
   lock with an act's id Daoris draws, then run under its target's gate inside `PipeDoor.EnterRole`. The verbs are `daoris-driver steward
   <act>`, over the pipe only; where the key is set, every press verb and every alone path refuses `role-terminal`.
5. **Its go-on** (§4): one fixed go-on whose text is Daoris's own, kept as `From: steward` only by the service's conditional step to a quest's
   ended session waiting on nobody but the owner (no question, go-ahead, word of any author or answers-alone hold), never to a running
   session nor onto the ask, nothing held in the process; attributed and composed from memory, any other claim quarantined; withdrawn
   before its start by a late wait, a revoke or the owner's press; neutral to the answers-alone count, its hold and the go-on marks. Free
   words are not decided here: they are their own row (STEWARDROLE1h) with their own read, as D208 gave SUPERVISOR1f.
6. **The record, the notice and the overrule** (§5): the steward's own move notes and hand-over reasons (`ended.stopped-steward`,
   `stopped.checkpoint-stopped-steward`, `stopped.checkpoint-finished-steward`, why `steward`), every reader updated by code; *Set by the
   steward*; live marks only from this run's memory, the hand-over's files consumed only on an exact match with it; `role.*` in the machine
   log; one line per role in *Notifications* with the owner's real reverse presses, no notice per act; a steward's stop shown in *What needs
   you*. Revoked at once, the run's waiting go-ons and untaken boxes swept. **An owner's press on a repository or a quest makes it theirs
   for the run, whatever the route** (`owner-set`), its successor sessions included, ordered against an admitted act by a gate per target.
   No overrule window.
7. **The holder and the working session** (§6): the holder is the dedicated session in its tab, running only read tools and the install's
   own `daoris-driver` in its roles' verbs and the reads an open route answers; driving a repository is not dispatching. The steward's
   working session **holds no key and no role**: its presses ask as today, and its management needs reach the holder through the desk
   (`STEWARD_REQUEST`, open, bounded, read as claims, admitting nothing), which lands before activation.

**Why.** A card is worth a press only where the owner decides something (D37, D193 point 1). Each act here either only narrows, is the
development's own run on accounts and a cap the owner set, or is undone by one press of theirs that exists; the one widening, the set-up's
rule, is the owner's own tick, and accepting what is delivered stays theirs. Recording the act with records of its own, not as the owner's
confirmed press with a suffix, keeps the owner's words theirs and makes the overrule real; D208's key is what lets the record name the
role, which an unkeyed terminal outside every agent job cannot. Keying the overrule by the thing acted on, and ordering it by a gate, is
what makes it stick against another route and a race. Keeping the key out of the working session keeps every process that runs a
branch's code from acting as the steward.

**Rejected** (design §11): the working session holding the key, a signer in it, or stripping the key before each build; D201's standing
confirmation as the steward's door; two doors for D201's presses; a route per act; a card per act; an overrule window; a notice per act;
the steward's mark as a suffix after the person's note; `owner-set` by route and target; closing a quest as done; free words in D209
through `SESSION_INPUT`; the steward's words counted in COWORK1e's count; `trees off`, `decline`, *Try again* on a stop not its own, pause
and resume, a quiet session's carry-on; naming an account on a hand-over; one holder for every role always; *Appoint again* for every role
found in one ask; a relay file before the desk; 总管. Not taken now, the owner's to choose later: keeping the owner's overrules past a restart;
an unkeyed terminal's standing grant for the owner's own presses.
