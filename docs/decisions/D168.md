## D168 — What waits on the person in a session is answered where they read: at the foot as at the head (2026-10-15)

**Decision (the owner's direction).** The owner, 2026-10-15: *"currently in the session chat if there is an approval needed
from the user it always displays at the top of the chat, but I'm usually looking at the last message; this kind needs a better
design so we don't lose both access methods (currently missing the end access, so I have to scroll back to the top), and also
this is not really referencing where the wait is for"*. A live or parked session opens at its tail and follows it (D76, SESS2
H1), while every wait is drawn in its record's head, a record's length above. The same trap was fixed once, for a park's answer
only (FIX-LOG 2026-09-29: the box moved to the foot, the card says the box carries it on).

1. **The head keeps every wait, and a strip at the foot names each open one with the same press.** The strip (`WaitStrip`)
   stands above the foot's box (the composer or the session's box), outside the conversation's scroll as Ask Daoris's note row
   is (`help/AskPanel.tsx`), so it never covers the words. One line per wait with its press; with several, the strip folds to
   one line naming them; *To the top* scrolls the record's head into view. It is absent when nothing waits, and on a
   teammate's record, which has no foot and no press here (D47 §4).
2. **The waits, and where each stands.** The head's are unchanged (SESS2 §"What the top section should be"; D162):

   | Wait | The head | The foot |
   |---|---|---|
   | A park's question | its card, with *Finish…* and *Decline…* | a line naming it; the box below is its answer's one door, so no second one (FIX-LOG 2026-09-29); without a box, the line says its moves are at the top |
   | An intake's park | its card, *Answer ask #…* | the same press |
   | Go-aheads (KNOWUSE1a2) | every one while parked | the open ones, the same list (`GoAheadList`) |
   | Work to land (LAND4) | *Accept…* and its ask (`Lands`) | the same molecule, where it offers a press of the person's: *Accept…*, the rebase words (LANDSYNC1w), or a gate in the person's hue |
   | Second opinion, review (XAGENT1g, REVIEWENV1g) | in `Lands`, before *Accept…* | there too, by the gate's own presses (`opinionTone`, `reviewTone` open) |
   | A discard the line holds (SQUASHTIDY1b) | *Discard branch…* | the same molecule (`Discards`, `HeldDiscard`) |
   | A hand-over offer (MIXAGENT1b-ii) | the pinned page head | none: that head is on screen wherever the person reads |
   | A rule proposal (PROPOSEHERE1b) | not yet built | a line by this rule once it is |

   Not waits: a workflow's hold (it waits on a workflow), a failed attempt's branch offered for discard (a tidy-up nothing
   holds on), an answered park. Which wait stands is one pure rule (`work/waits.ts`), read by the strip and, for the work,
   by `Lands` itself, so the head and the foot cannot disagree on it.
3. **Each answer has one owner (D56).** The frame holds every press and its pending, and hands both places the same object:
   the same `onGoAhead`, `onLand`, the gates' acts, `resolving`, the acts' `busy`, and the landing's or discard's own. A press
   in either place disables the other's until it answers, and the record read again settles both. An ask that confirms
   (*Accept…*, *Discard branch…*) is open in one place at a time: the frame holds where (`WorkAsk`), opening it in one puts
   the other down, and a branch name typed in the one put down goes with it (LANDNAME1b). A refusal is said where the press
   was made (UXFIX2). The gates' own word-taking asks and a go-ahead's words stay their places' own; their presses share the
   pending.
4. **Each wait is anchored to the event in the conversation that raised it, with a way between the three** (the head, the
   strip, the event): WAITFOOT1c. The joins the frame can make today:

   | Wait | Raising event | Join |
   |---|---|---|
   | A park's question | the last agent message before the park | `lastWords(turns)` (`SessionConversation.tsx`), its block's key |
   | Work to land | the turn that ended with it | the last turn's key (`conversation.ts`, `toTurns`) |
   | A second opinion handed to it | a `user` event, `origin: 'target'`, `opinion` | its held block's key, once handed |
   | A go-ahead | its `go_ahead_ask` tool call (`Service.Mcp/GoAheadTools.cs`) | none by id: `GoAhead.asked[]` is `{session, quest, at, why}` (`data/api.ts`); join on the call's input (act, why) within the session, else `at` near the block's time |
   | A rule proposal | its `permission_propose` tool call | unknown: the call id against the proposal's id |
   | A hand-over offer, a review's gate | none | no anchor |

   The way: `SessionConversation`'s private `show(key)` and its `[data-block]` scroll become a prop the frame presses, as
   `onTail` is; the raising block carries a marker with *To the wait*, and the strip's line *In the conversation*. Unknowns
   for WAITFOOT1c to read on a real record: whether a park's `note` is its last message's text; how far a go-ahead's `at` lies
   from its call's event; the proposal's call id. A go-ahead's call id named on the service's record is its own row
   (service lane), not the page's guess.

**Rejected:** the waits at the foot alone (an ended session opens at its head, which says what it needs, SESS2 H1, D162); the
head's waits pinned (the page head is already pinned, and a pinned card covers the words); hiding the strip while the head's
waits are on screen (an ask open at the foot would vanish under the person's scroll); a strip of its own presses (copies drift,
so it draws the head's molecules, `Lands` lifted out of `SessionHead` to be one); a second answer box for a park.

**Amends:** FIX-LOG 2026-09-29's door at the foot, now every wait's; SESS2's top section, which keeps what the session needs
while the foot repeats its presses; D56's one owner, which is the frame's callback and its pending, drawn in two places, with
an ask open in one.
