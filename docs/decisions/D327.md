## D327 — A staged build's drain holds new work for a bound at most, then defers to the first moment nothing runs (2026-11-19)

**Decision (DRAINSTALL1, amending D139's drain).** A staged build said *when idle* drains as before: the loop starts nothing new, what
runs ends or parks, and the build installs once no driven session runs and no turn or plugin step is in flight. The drain now holds new
work for a bound at most: the machine's `drain` setting (`drainMinutes` in `driver.json`, 10 minutes by default, 1 to 120, at
Settings → Agents and sessions, by `daoris settings drain <minutes>` or `SET_DRAIN`). `InstallUpdater` reads the bound at each look of a
drain, beside the work, so the bound lives where the drain is decided. Past it the update's state is `deferred`: its starts and the pipe's
presses are held no longer, the build's mode stays *when idle*, and the look installs it at the first moment it reads the work idle,
checking again under the presses' hold as a drain does; *Update now* and *Not now* still hold, and a newer build drains anew from its own
start. The deferral is said once in the machine log (`update.deferred`), and the window says that the build waits, on what and since when,
with *Update now* and *Not now*; while it drains, the banner says until when new work waits at the latest.

**Why.** On 2026-11-19 a build staged at 00:29 UTC drained behind one builder session's long turn, and an ask the owner published
meanwhile stayed *Proposed* for over half an hour, its intake never started, until the steward said *not now*. A drain that waits for
every session to end holds the person's new work as long as the longest turn, which has no bound.

**Rejected.**

- *Past the bound, install at the next moment nothing is mid-turn*, cutting driven sessions between turns as *Update now* does: a long
  turn is exactly what held the drain, so the person's work would still wait on it, and a driven session cut between turns is carried on
  at the next start at a cost no idle install pays.
- *Past the bound, write the person's *Not now**: the word is the person's, holds for that build until their next word, and keeps the
  launcher from swapping at the next start; a deferral the person did not say would read as theirs and keep the build from ever
  installing by itself.
- *Drain again after a deferral, in cycles*: each cycle would hold new work for another bound behind the same long turn.
