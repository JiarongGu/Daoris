## D229 — A line's behaviors are counted as facts in its own context, which the session sees; the supervisor acts on them, held by an appointed agent or by rules the person sets and Daoris reads with no model; today's caps become those rules, converted on the person's press (2026-11-03)

**Decision (CONTEXTDRIVE1, the owner's direction of 2026-11-03; designed under D191, proposed for its reads).** Per
`docs/2026-11-03-context-counters-design.md`, within the structure and with no second route (D228).

1. **The counts.** What a line does is counted by one pure read, `LineCounts.Of`, each count from the one record that already
   carries it (the desktop store, the service's records, the event files, the turn lines), keyed by the line as `role_moves` is, so
   a hand-over and a restart keep it. No total is stored. A count is a session's, a line's, a quest's or a role's, and reads
   *exact*, *at least* or *unknown*, with why.
2. **The session sees its course**: in its brief, a go-on's appendix, its start and `line_read`, in Daoris's words, as facts
   composed from codes and numbers, never an instruction and never another agent's text.
3. **A rule** is *when this count reaches n here, do this*, the action one of the doors that exist (wait for the person, ask before
   the next move, wait for a reset, move to another choice, try again, ask the supervisor, none here). It is acted between turns
   alone, inside D212's one course decision, the planner's start and a run's start, never on an unknown count, never ending a
   running or quiet session, and never answering a question.
4. **A rule is a holder of the supervisor role** (D208), kept in the desktop store and set through the role door (`SET_ROLE`,
   `SET_ROLES`, the terminal's role verb, `role_propose` applied by the person's press). It grants nothing, so it is set as a role's
   policy is, while an agent holder is still appointed only by the owner's press in the window. Rules are inherited by place,
   the most specific place holding a rule on a count replacing the rest, with no layering.
5. **An agent and a rule.** The rule is read first and its act stands; the agent's answer is refused where it would make a go-on a
   rule holds; the agent judges a count only where a rule refers it, through the supervisor's third act, *course*, kept and
   overruled as its answers are. Its reading carries the counts, the rules in force and the line's course, and its reasons may cite
   a count it was handed.
6. **Today's caps become rules**, seeded on every home at today's numbers (answers alone 2 in a row, the home's failed sessions,
   five transient tries) with each role policy's moves and `most`; an existing home converts through a `rules` part of ONEWAY1's
   conversion door, and each old reader is deleted with its register entry once the part's census ends. Machine load (the cap,
   sessions and helpers at once), a shared host's protocol (a part's channel posts) and Daoris's own nudges stay as they are.

**Why.** A line's drive was steered by numbers each counted and acted on in its own place, so the person could not see them
together nor set a hard stop where they wanted one, and an appointed agent had no one place to read what a line had done. Counting
from the records that already carry each behavior keeps every count durable without a second store; making each cap a rule the
person sees keeps every home as it runs today while no number stays fixed in the product (D225's note of 2026-11-03); and holding
the rules as the supervisor's, read inside the one course decision, gives the agent supervisor and the person's hard limits one
place, one judge and one order.

**Rejected.** A table of counts beside their records. A new feed to the session. Rules inside each role's policy record. A rule
set through *Appoint* alone. A model judging every go-on. A rule ending a running or quiet session, or watching a role's day. An
unknown count read as zero or as the bound. A notification for a rule's act. The cap, the channel's bounds and Daoris's
carry-ons as rules.

**Amends.** D188 point 4 (the answers-alone bound, which D225's note and the brief call point 5) and D58's strikes: each number
becomes a seeded rule, and `SET_STRIKES` with `driver.json`'s key retires. D208: points 1-2 gain the `rule` holder, set through the
role door; point 4 gains the act *course* and the defer bound `course`. D210 point 3: the hold past the episode's tries becomes a
rule; the schedule stays. D225's note of 2026-11-03: its supervisors are this decision's; COMMS1g keeps the count durably and builds
no rule. D226: its moves on a condition and `most` become rules naming the role, the policy keeping its rule, weights, shares,
budgets and works.
