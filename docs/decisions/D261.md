## D261 — The press is the one way to ask the person: a proposal kind becomes the card's typed view of its route, and the proposal tools go (2026-11-11)

**Decision.** Ask Daoris asked the person for one change in two ways: a `<kind>_propose` tool, whose file a kind's class judged and
the panel's card applied through the screen's door, and the `daoris` verb, whose proved press waited on the window's Confirm card
(D253 point 5). The press is the one way. Ask Daoris presses verbs in every tier, and the tiers differ only in who says yes: in full
access its own, for a family the person granted the person's standing yes, otherwise the person's on the card. What a proposal had
that the card lacked moves onto the press as the route's view: a unit per route judges the press at the ask by the route's own code,
answers a refusal to the terminal at once so the person never sees it, and otherwise gives the card a typed view (its facts, worded by
the page's catalogue, and the command the press is) above every value the press carries. A press of Ask Daoris's that no line waits on
is said in its conversation once it settles. Each kind is removed whole (its tool, writer, judge, door, card and room line) in the row
that brings its route's view, and the proposal box goes with the last; `go`, which asks no yes and changes nothing, leaves the box for
a link of its own. Configured access's housekeeping and chosen-settings tools become families of acts, so a grant has one shape.
The design and its rows are `docs/2026-11-11-one-way-to-ask-design.md`.

**Rejected.**
- **The proposal as the one way** (a restricted delegate press turned into a proposal card). Kinds exist only where one is wanted, so
  any other verb would need a generic proposal, the Confirm card under another name; a working session's and a terminal's presses
  keep the Confirm card; full access would press while restricted proposed, so the modes would differ in more than who says yes; the
  pipe would spell every route again as a kind's file; and proposals have no grant, so configured access would need a second one.
- **Both doors onto one card** (a `*_propose` tool writing a press). Two doors to one act are two ways (D228), and a tool spelling
  every verb again tracks the command tree by hand.
- **The view in place of the values.** The card still shows every value a press carries (D156): the view says what it means, the
  values are what runs.

**Amends** D253 point 5 (the convergence it left to ASKFULL1g), D170 and D110 (a proposal kind per change: a route's view instead),
and the roles area's rules 56 and 60.

**Note, 2026-11-11 (ASKFULL1g, act families as configured access's shape).** Configured access keeps two grants beside the browser's
plugin approval: the checkouts turned off, and `acts`, a list of families of acts (`pause`, `clean-up`, `discard`, `settings`). A
family names the driver's routes it covers (`ActFamilies`); a proved press of one runs with no card where its family is granted, read at
each press, so a revoke puts the next press on a card and no conversation restarts. The `housekeeping_*` and `settings_*` connector
tools, their request files, judges and the `housekeeping` and `settings` grants left whole: the clean-up and a discard are the routes
`SWEEP` and `DISCARD_SESSION_BRANCH` with a view each, the five settings the routes `SET_NOTIFY`, `SET_LANGUAGE`, `SET_CAP`,
`SET_COOLOFF` and `SET_QUIET`, and a record an older build kept is converted by the home's migration (`ConfiguredAccessStep`). The
grants page draws one row, *Acts without asking*, a box per family with what it covers under it. Rejected: a grant per verb (the
command tree spelled again by hand, a list that grows with every verb), and the older inspection and recovery-ref operations as grants
of their own (a look is a read and asks none).

**Note, 2026-11-11 (ASKFULL1g-ix, the box is gone).** `go` left the box for a link of its own: Ask Daoris writes
`[words](daoris:go?view=…)` in its answer, its fields the places the room lists, and the window opens it on the person's press
through the starters' door; a link no place answers is its words alone. With it went the last of the box whole: the service's
`go_propose` and its writer and file, the driver's proposals, kinds, the go judge, the help topic's watch, the modules'
`HELP_PROPOSALS`, `HELP_APPLY` and `HELP_DISMISS`, the screen's doors, the owner marks' by-plan seam, the page's card and its words.
A home's proposals folder is removed once by the home's migration (`help-proposals`), each file kept in the salvage folder. The
room's *What you may do* names the verbs alone, and its doors table says where Ask Daoris never asks on the card. Rejected: keeping
the go card as a view on the Confirm card (a go asks no yes, so a card would ask a press for nothing), and a verb that moves the window
(no terminal has a window to move).

**Note, 2026-11-12 (D284, the access model).** No press waits on a card: a press runs as its caller's in both modes, and restricted
mode's asking is the harness's prompt on the command. A route's view stays as the words its press is said in, and its judgement at the
press stays as the route's own rules; the act families of the ASKFULL1g note go.
