## D245 — A chain's completion past its pull request is fabric steps: a monitor and a tidy, from a prebuilt set of actions (2026-11-06)

**Decision (FABRIC-SQUASHCHAIN1; the owner's choice, 2026-11-06).** What happens to work after its pull request is opened is not judged
by the keeper and the press each in their own way. It is the place's workflow, made of steps, as the pull request itself is (D241 point 4).

1. **A full completion is steps.** A place that completes work through a pull request composes `land` (branch), `push`, `pull-request`,
   then a **monitor** step that watches the request until it is merged or closed, then a **tidy** step that ends the chain's parts on the
   line and removes their branches, keeping each part's commits on a recovery ref as the undo.
2. **The monitor is its own step.** It reads the request's state through the plugin that opened it (`work/state`) and reaches when the
   request is merged or closed. A squash merge is read as merged: the parts it carries are on the line by their content.
3. **One judgement.** The keeper and the press read the tidy step's record for a part that a squashing chain carried. Neither judges that
   part on its own any more, so the part is not judged again at every pass.
4. **A prebuilt set of actions.** Daoris ships the steps a workflow composes for this as one set (`land`, `push`, `pull-request`,
   `monitor`, `tidy`), each a node of its own with its proof. A place's workflow uses the ones it needs, and *Set up…* offers the set
   where a place completes through a pull request.

**Rejected.**
- **The press accepting a squashing landing ended by its own proof.** It settles the disagreement but keeps the judgement in two places,
  and the person cannot see or change it in the workflow.
- **The keeper no longer ending that part.** The part would stay a candidate until someone landed or discarded it by hand.
- **Retiring the row.** A place that squashes on its platform still needs its chain's parts tidied.

**Amends:** D173's ENDAGREE1 note (the squash chain's earlier part, left to the owner) and D241 point 4 (the steps after `pull-request`).
