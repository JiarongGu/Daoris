## D367 — A reviewer's knowledge is chosen by code from the repository's catalogue, and knowledge joins Daoris when its work lands, by git (2026-11-27)

**Decision (KNOWLAND1, under D349, D366 and D323; the owner's direction: a reviewer takes the knowledge it needs from
Daoris's own base and reviews with the summary code builds; new or changed knowledge joins only once its work has fully
landed, with merge and revert like git's, since knowledge will be synced and work rolled back; "fully landed" differs by
repository: a remote merge, a local landing, a release; finding the right book in the library must not be a plain word
search).** Brainstorm `knowland-1127` asked Fable 5.2 and DeepSeek; both answered one model and differed on four points,
chosen below. Its own packet showed the problem: picked by words, it carried the OpenAI troubleshooting skill and the
pack-adding skill to a question about landing and memory.

1. **The reviewer's brief is composed by code:** D366's run summary, the repository's always-loaded rules, then the
   documents its catalogue selects for the change's reach, read at a commit, bounded, with a receipt of what is in, why,
   and what the bound cut. The catalogue is a library's: the change's paths name its areas, rooms and lanes; each
   knowledge entry's `applies_when` and declared reach (`reaches:` globs) say when it applies; decisions are found by the
   areas they cite and by citation. Word or meaning search never chooses a rule; its hits are shown apart, as leads.
2. **Recall is the gate:** past reviews are replayed at their recorded commits, labelled with the rules that applied;
   the composer must deliver every one, and a missed rule fails the census.
3. **Knowledge that lands is a tracked file** at a declared knowledge path, reachable from the landing ref. A session
   stages knowledge as commits on its own branch, seen only by that work and its reviewer until it lands; merge, revert
   and an abandoned branch are git's. `memory_propose` routes a repository's own fact to such a file; memory keeps what no
   repository carries (the person, the machine, a cross-repository fact).
4. **Fully landed is a kind each repository declares** (a local landing, a remote merge of a pull request, a release, a
   family's pin moved), read from the end of the workflow it follows unless it declares a later one. The knowledge index
   reads the landing ref, not the working disk, so knowledge applies when its work lands with no person's press, and a
   revert withdraws it with git's own revert. A knowledge change's record (path, blob, parent, source work, landing) is
   derived from git; sync is git plus an index rebuilt from landing refs.
5. **Knowledge with no repository** is staged as a typed change bound to its run and applied at that run's landed fact;
   an append-only ledger with inverse entries and three-way merges is built only if what stays in memory proves to need
   revert and sync.
6. **Lyntai** is asked, by quest to its owner with measures, for store features only (versioned writes with a parent and
   opaque provenance, history, tombstones); Daoris keeps all that knows work, landing, scope and the brief.
7. **Framework and configuration** (D349): Daoris supplies the composer, the receipt, the landing observers, the derived
   records and the replay harness; a repository declares its landing kind, catalogue rows, brief ceiling and cases.

**Rejected.** A model or a word search choosing the rules a reviewer gets (it cannot be replayed exactly, and the
packet above shows what it picks); a second copy of tracked documents in a store; applying knowledge at a press or at a
push; a clock picking a winner between two changes; one definition of landed for every repository; a one-time triage
that moves the 13 waiting memory proposals into files (memory is the person's; their Keep or Drop stands until routing
is built); Lyntai applying knowledge on a landing (it would have to know work).

**First slice.** In this repository: the composed brief with its receipt, its catalogue rows seeded from the
verification rules HEAVYBATCH1 adds and the entries' `applies_when`, and a replay of 10 to 20 past reviews as its gate.
Then the routing of repository facts out of memory and the index on the landing ref; then landing kinds beyond a local
landing. Rows `KNOWLAND1a` to `KNOWLAND1d`.
