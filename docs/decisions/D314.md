## D314 — A newer landing's heavy tier takes over the older one's unrun classes; one heavy tier holds the tree at a time (2026-11-18)

**Decision (HEAVYSTACK1).** One heavy tier holds the heavy tree at a time (`local/heavy-stack.json`). A landing's tier that finds an
older one running waits and asks it to halt (its halt file). The older tier starts no new group of classes; the group in flight it
finishes where the newer commit changes nothing those classes read, since its passes still count on the newer commit, and ends (by its
own process) where it does. It then hands the newer tier its range and its unrun classes: the newer tier runs once from the oldest base,
the union of both, replaying from the store what the older one passed. The older run's record says `superseded` and to whom it handed;
the stage reads `superseded`, and any state but green, as no pass. A wait on the tree or the machine's admission past ten minutes is
said; a tier gives up waiting for the tree after three hours.

**Why.** Each landing started a tier whose git half runs half an hour or more; five to seven landings a day kept the machine's three
heavy places full, holding every merge and preflight in admission and leaving a gate's "run again alone" never alone (2026-11-17 and 18).

**Rejected:** cancelling the older tier outright (its finished and in-flight passes would be lost, though most still count); always
ending the group in flight (wastes passes the newer commit leaves standing); always finishing it (a group the newer commit changes
would pass on code no longer there).
