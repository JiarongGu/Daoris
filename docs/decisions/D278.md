## D278 — What a change reads stays the size it was on day one: a present search, finished designs filed, each area its size, history kept in files and looked up by identifier (2026-11-12)

**Decision (DOCDIET1).** A long-lived repository developed worse than a fresh start because what a change reads and what a search
returns grew with its age. Measured on this repository against a fresh copy holding only its present state, the cost was in the
read path and in what a search returns, not in the size of the history. The contract and the measurements are
`docs/2026-11-12-long-lived-repository-design.md`.

1. **The read path keeps its size.** The brief, the area document, the backlog row and the index have a size; `daoris check`
   reports one that has grown past it, a folder's ceiling measuring each document in it. It reports and never fails (D54).
2. **A present question is searched in the present.** How something works now is searched in the brief, the area documents, the
   index, the code and the knowledge; history (decisions, archive, fix log, changelog, finished designs) is looked up by its
   identifier, or searched when the question is about history. A fresh copy found the present rule first for 10 of 12 questions,
   this repository for 1; without its designs and records, 8.
3. **A finished design is filed.** A design no open row and no area document cites moves to the archive folder and its router, with
   every citation rewritten in the commit that moves it.
4. **Each area says the rules true now**, history in its decision; this repository's areas are declared at 4,500 words.
5. **History stays in files, whole, looked up by identifier.** The archive and the fix log stay one file each.

**Rejected.** The archive and fix log split by month: no hit and no returned byte changed, a month here is 600 entries and up to
2.2 MB, and the move rewrites every citation and writer. History moved into Lyntai's memory: the top hit for 8 of 36 questions
against 28 from files, identifiers lost, four times the bytes per answer; asked of Lyntai instead (design §7) and measured again
when it answers. History summarised or deleted: builders read under 1 KB of archive and 0.2 KB of fix log each, by identifier, so
there was no cost to recover. A size that fails the check (D54). A history folder for every record: it moves hundreds of citations
for what the present search gives without a move.
