## D332 — The window has six places: Overview, Asks, Sessions, Workspaces, Company and Settings (2026-11-20)

**Decision (UISH2, the window redesign `docs/2026-11-19-window-design.md` §4.1; the owner's direction of 2026-11-19 and the
steward's; superseding D150 §2's nine places, amending D67's Map place and the place D30 gave convergence).**

1. The rail holds six places, Settings at its foot: **Overview** (总览, Ctrl+1), **Asks** (需求, Ctrl+2), **Sessions** (会话,
   Ctrl+3), **Workspaces** (工作区, Ctrl+4), **Company** (公司, Ctrl+5) and **Settings** (设置, Ctrl+,). A seventh needs a decision.
2. **Quests** leave as a place: an ask's quests are rows of its page, a quest no ask holds is a group of the Asks list, and a
   quest's page sits under Asks.
3. **Workspaces** holds one tree of workspaces and their repositories. **The Map** is a workspace's Wiring tab (every workspace's
   on the Workspaces root); **Knowledge** is a tab on each repository's page, *Said in more than one repository* is the workspace's
   Knowledge tab, and search is the palette's.
4. **Company** holds Accounts (by agent), Roles (working, appointed, *What stays yours*), Connectors and Plugins: machine-wide,
   since one set of accounts serves every workspace (D197). Agents › Setup's tree goes: a workspace's and a repository's setup is
   its own Setup tab (D150's "a setting lives on the thing it is about" kept and applied harder).
5. **Overview keeps its name and 总览.** Its job changes to what waits on the owner, what is moving and what happened since they
   left; the glossary's *means* is rewritten.
6. Places wear marks, never numbers; a browser shows Overview, Asks, Workspaces and Settings, with Sessions and Company absent.

**Why.** The owner's questions cross today's places: "can my company work now, and who decides for me" is answered across five
(Agents, Setup's machine page, the roles view, Settings › Driver, a workspace's page), and the same repositories are listed in two
trees. Both fresh designs cut the rail to five or six; Quests, Map, Knowledge, Repositories and Plugins are views of a workspace's
or an ask's things, not separate jobs. The owner placed the Map and Knowledge in Workspaces (2026-11-19).

**Rejected.**

- *Nine places* (D150): three of them are second ways to read another place's objects.
- *Knowledge as a seventh place* (the brainstorm's g1): knowledge is what a repository knows about itself, read where the
  repository is and found from the palette.
- *Both the Map and Knowledge kept on the rail*: the owner's answer.
- *Renaming the home "Desk"* (工作台): on the glossary's avoid list; "Home" is already the data folder's word. *概览* for it: the
  glossary's word is 总览, and one name holds one job.
- *Accounts apart from roles and connectors* (the Opus design): the product scopes roles and connectors at three levels, so one
  table with a place filter plus each place's Setup tab reads better than three copies.
