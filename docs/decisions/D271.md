## D271 — A terminal's presses have a tier of their own: ask by default, act once the person confirms it, run on the delegate's path as the person's (2026-11-11)

**Decision.** Since D261 the press is the one way to ask the person, and every person's press a terminal sends that no Daoris session
owns waits on the window's Confirm card (D156). The owner develops Daoris with an agent of their own driving the installed copy from a
terminal, so each clean-up act it makes (a quest's done, a session's archive, a tree's land) costs the owner a click. D253 gave Ask
Daoris and every session Daoris starts a tier, full access acting and restricted asking; a terminal no session owns had none, and
D201's standing confirmation for it was folded into the steward's acts (D209, STEWARDROLE1d).

1. **The terminal's tier is a machine setting**, `driver.json`'s `terminal`: `ask` or `act`, read at each press. Under `act`, a person's
   press that proves no session's key, from a process the pipe places outside every agent Daoris runs (`StandingCaller`), is the
   person's press already confirmed: it runs at once with no card on the delegate's confirmed-press path (`PipeDelegate.Pressed`),
   logged `terminal.pressed`, kept in the run's pressed list marked a terminal's and listed in *Notifications*, its answer said to the
   terminal. A process inside an agent's job, or one the pipe cannot place, is asked on the card as before: a session's process that
   left its key off is still a session's. A session's own terminal, holding a delegate or holder key, keeps its own tier, and the
   steward's acts and Ask Daoris's presses are unchanged.
2. **The default is `ask`.** The repository's rule is that development tools default permissive and restrictions are opt-in; the
   Confirm card exists because any program on the machine reaches the pipe, and it is the one thing that tells the person's press from
   another program's (D156). The permissive rule is about what a developing agent may do; this setting is about who counts as the
   person. `act` by default would open every person's door to every program of every install, most of which no agent develops, to save
   the one install that wants it a single confirmed press.
3. **`act` is granted only on a card.** `daoris settings terminal ask|act` is itself a press (`SET_TERMINAL`), so a terminal sets `act`
   only through the person's Confirm, its card saying what it opens; the window's Settings row is the person's own press. Acting alone,
   with no Daoris running, the verb writes `ask` and refuses `act`, since no card could be shown: the alone path is the writer a terminal
   reaches (D201's reason for keeping its grant out of `driver.json`), and it grants nothing.
4. **The floor stands.** A press the card could not show whole, or a field the pipe never carries, is refused before anything runs,
   and the route's own judges still answer: a refusal reaches the terminal at once under both tiers.

*Rejected:*
- **`act` by default**, the development-permissive rule applied: it makes the card opt-in on every machine, and a person who never
  develops Daoris would never know a program could act as them.
- **`act` only on a development build or install**: two behaviours of one setting by build (D228), and the install the owner drives
  is a released one.
- **A grant of the terminal's own beside the machine settings** (D201's `data/standing-confirmation.json` behind a window-only route,
  which STEWARDROLE1d deleted): a second store and door for one setting; the setting's one writer that is no press refuses `act`.
- **Families of acts for the terminal**, in configured access's shape: the developing agent's clean-up spans every family, and a list
  kept beside Ask Daoris's would be a second grant.
- **A path of its own for a terminal's covered press**: the delegate's confirmed-press path is reused, its log, record and line (D228).
- **Counting a process the pipe cannot place as outside**: an agent running uncontained, or an older Daoris beside this one, would
  then press as the person.

**Amends** D156 point 4 (a terminal's press is confirmed by the tier where it is `act`), D201's rejection of covering every person's
press by one word (the tier is that word, granted on a card and read against the caller's place), the desktop-app area's rules 14
and 15, and the roles area's rule 58 (the pressed list holds a terminal's presses beside Ask Daoris's).
