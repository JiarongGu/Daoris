## D213 — One provider contract every agent implements: a provider is an agent with one or more doors, one registration of required parts and small ports, each fact known with its source and where it applies; Daoris selects and the provider translates, through a run each door admits by its abilities; two shared transports, each door handing its dialect; one vocabulary of readings in Events, minting nothing; every record's strings kept; reached in rows after FOUNDATION1's Agents move, the census, a conformance suite and a third stand-in the proof, OPENROUTER1 the first new provider (2026-10-26)

**Decision (AGENTPROVIDER1, proposed 2026-10-26, round 1 under D191).** **Status: proposed; both makers' reads owed.** The owner directed
on 2026-10-26 that Daoris support more agents than the two makers it runs, so that what an agent is to Daoris becomes one provider contract
every agent implements, every place outside a provider that tells one agent or door from another moving behind it, designed under D191 and
built after FOUNDATION1's move of the Agents subdomain (D212, row mod-ix), never as a big-bang rewrite. The survey
(`docs/2026-10-26-agent-provider-survey.md`, read at `f58f1be61`) counts 350 sites in 234 files, 160 of them accidents of where the code was
written. Two sealed proposals were written from the brief and the survey alone, Codex's and the independent Opus reader's; round 1 of the
design, `docs/2026-10-26-agent-provider-design.md`, is their synthesis by Claude Opus 5.5 from main at `0f4d2a8a7`, answering each of the
brief's twenty questions (its §1), choosing each difference with its reason and the case the other gets wrong (§1.1), and correcting the brief,
the survey and the proposals against the code (§0.2). It changes a foundation (the seam every agent's process is started, spoken to and read
through, and decided rules other designs rest on), so by D191's note of 2026-10-24 no part of it is accepted on one maker's read. Nothing is
built.

1. **A provider is an agent with one or more doors** (design §2.2). Today's adapter ids are its doors' ids: `claude-code` with `claude-code`
   and `claude-code-acp`, `codex` with `codex-acp`, `dsh` with `dsh`, `stub` with `stub` and `acp-stub`; a plugin's harness a door of the
   provider its `accountOf` names, or a provider of its own. The `codex` holder folds into Codex's provider. A provider has at least one door;
   a door's id may equal its own provider's and never another's; an unknown id, or a provider's id where a door is asked, is D23's error naming
   what exists. A door's id is opaque outside the providers: carried, stored, compared and shown, never read for behaviour. The ids are typed.
2. **One registration of a sealed record of required parts and small ports** (§2.3). The provider holds its identity, program, accounts, word
   tables, usage question, account settings, children's vocabulary, rule language, skill layout, conversation home and reach; the door its
   transport, program, postures, spawn, dialect, abilities and its wire's tables. Each fact sits in one place, the agent's on the provider and
   the door's on the door, so no reader asks *own, else owner*, and a door never inherits another's wire codes, turn report or program. Every
   part is stated, `Absent` a statement read as today's behaviour for an agent that says nothing (D53). Each optional fact is a `Known<T>` with
   its source (declared, negotiated, measured), its evidence and where it applies (the door, the agent's and adapter's versions, the posture,
   the configuration's digest); a negotiated value describes only the opening that answered it, and a measurement admits only its effective
   configuration. COAGENT1e's sheet is a pure read of the contract.
3. **Daoris selects; the provider translates** (§2.5). A start passes six stages, each its owner's: Roles' settled selection (D212 §2.4), the
   door's process recipe and carriers, Platform's start and containment with Daoris's own variables applied after the recipe, the transport's
   open or resume, Roles' re-ask of a refused choice, and words through Course's dispatcher. An executor holds the door's `AgentRun` (open or
   resume, apply a choice, send, steer, stop a turn, stop a task, close), each operation admitted by the door's abilities and refused
   `unsupported` before anything is written. A provider never walks accounts, chooses another model, starts another participant or decides
   whether a line goes on.
4. **Two shared, agent-neutral transports, each door handing its dialect** (§2.6): the protocol transport (the Agent Client Protocol's spec
   frames) and the native transport (a process per run, read line by line, its resume retried once where the run exits before naming its
   conversation). Every read of one agent's extension leaves the shared code for that agent's dialect; neither transport names an agent, and a
   transport of another kind is a provider's only by a design of its own.
5. **One vocabulary of readings, in Events, written to one sink per run; the provider mints nothing** (§3). `WireReading` extends
   `SessionEvent` with a turn's end, a conversation named, a model reported or refused, windows, a failure, a transient try, a child seen,
   speaking and ended, a background task, a woken turn, a held request, a refused call, an unread frame and an end, each with its run's
   sequence, its correlations, its provenance and whether a resume replayed it. FOUNDATION1's hubs and triggers mint from what the sink keeps
   (D212 §4.1, §4.2): a stopped, refused or failed turn is no answer, a child closed by its parent's end is closed by inference with its outcome
   unknown, and what a provider does not say stays unknown. Nothing a provider says leaves the machine but as an event's ids and codes.
6. **Accounts, models, the session's life, connectors, permissions, process and errors** (§4). An account belongs to its provider, a key handed
   only to its own provider's spawn; the owner is resolved once. A door's offers come in two scopes, a session's and an account default's, and
   a model is kept as asked, carried, acknowledged and reported apart; D205's judge and walk stay Roles'. The provider says how to start,
   resume, take words, stop and close; Course says whether, and a carry across doors or versions needs a recipe the provider declares
   compatible. Connectors reach every door through one plan, the session's identity in every entry Daoris writes, a start that needs an
   exclusion its door cannot honour refused. Postures are each door's own words, a full or asking start with none mapped refused; Claude Code's
   rule grammar stays the product's (D72), Claude Code's provider's own, and the lineage hook Claude Code's. Daoris's variables and the composed
   instruction are Daoris's, no `DAORIS_` variable in a recipe; containment stays the launcher's (D201). One `AgentErrors.Read`, the door's table
   before the provider's, every failure one reading on both transports.
7. **A plugin's door is made by Agents from a declaration that has no field for what a manifest may never claim** (§4.8): rules, words,
   usage, children, lineage, confinement, steering, queueing, resume, a dialect, a conversation home, `StandIn`, `DeclaredBy` (COAGENT1 §3.4).
8. **Every record keeps its strings** (§5). One table names the kind of id each field holds, and the registry resolves each by it; an id a
   build lacks reads as an unavailable door or provider keeping its id, and a start on it refuses. No field is added to any record or wire, and
   no binding-details table until a reader needs one.
9. **The page, the service and the tools read the driver's answer** (§6): the roster's answer is the sheet; the service's call keys are one
   file per provider, a twin by one fixture; the twins that stay are named.
10. **A move changes nothing; each disagreement is a correction of its own** (§7), its old and new cases paired and its note dated: no machine
    door, Build's candidates' eligibility read by the loop, the window, the terminal and set-up alike; a resume's own conversation judged on
    the record's door; the floor the provider's; the efforts in two scopes; the Claude Code seam on Claude Code's doors alone; other providers'
    credentials stripped from a spawn, measured first; usage and the leading agent counted by agent. D70's rule that a native agent cannot drive
    an unadopted repository stands, once; D179's token weights stay its interim; `ProfileMustExist` is retired with today's behaviour kept;
    `WeekFixed` is the provider's, read by its one consumer.
11. **The names** (§8): *provider*, *door*, *transport*, *dialect*, *run* and *reading* in code and prose; the window keeps *agent* (智能体)
    and *way in* (接入方式), the product names as written; *pipe* names only the terminal's named pipe in new code; the glossary gains nothing.
12. **The proof** (§9, §10): a census, failing on any agent's id, provider's type, variable, file, wire key or door's test outside the
    providers, its vocabulary read from the registrations, every site today a named exception with its removing row, the permanent ones with
    their decisions; a conformance suite every provider passes, with a pending table of what each cannot pass yet; and a third stand-in,
    `alt-stub`, native and protocol, its vocabulary no real agent's, added by its folder and one registration line, the diff its proof.
13. **Reached in rows** (§11), AGENTPROVIDER1a to 1t with the corrections 1s-i to 1s-vii, each its own branch after mod-ix, on files no open
    branch holds, every gate green and the brief's suites whole: the contract and its census (a), the plumbing (b), the stand-in (c), the owner
    once (d), then part by part (accounts, program, abilities, spawn, the protocol dialects and each door its own, the native transport, the
    readings, the run, models, permissions, connectors, plugins, the page), each correction after the move that makes it one place, the old
    seam's end and the records held last (t). Rows h, j, o, p, q and s-vi move or narrow what an agent is handed or may do, and are built only
    after both makers' reads. **OPENROUTER1** is the first new provider, its own design written on the contract once the owner, accounts,
    program, spawn, readings and models have moved: its executor named (Claude Code's program, by the row's reading), its key its own, its reach
    the repositories opened to it, its gateway no proof of another maker.
14. **Beside FOUNDATION1** (§2.7, §2.8): the census families that name Agents from below (*Install*, *Events*, *Knowledge*, *Service*,
    *Instance*) are cut by FOUNDATION1's rows before mod-ix, and this design adopts the shape they leave; the *Agents names …* edges in the
    files the contract replaces are cut by this design's rows, once, D212 taking a note in row a; its Part B rows a-ii, a-iii, j, w-s, w and x
    read the contract as the design's §2.8 says.

**Why.** The survey's tangles are one agent's facts and one door's tests living outside any provider and decided more than once: Claude Code's
extensions read inline in the shared protocol door, Claude Code's formats taken as the product's, the agent rebuilt from a door's id in four
copies with four rules, the door decided by tests of `Wire` in the callers, and resume and failure written once per door and per kind of run.
A provider that holds its agent's facts in one place removes the copies; a record of required parts makes a provider state what it cannot do
instead of inheriting an answer by omission, which is how a plugin's door was given `Resumes` today; a typed run removes the callers' branches
on which capture they hold; two transports shared by every door keep the next agent's process handling from being copied again; and readings
in Events keep the stream's vocabulary below everything that reads it, as FOUNDATION1's map requires. A move that changes nothing, with each
correction on its own, keeps every regression found on the install revertible on its own. A stand-in added by its folder alone is the
contract's meter: anything it cannot do is a gap the census and the pending table name.

**Rejected** (design §1.1, §14): one interface of defaults, or of facets with defaults (an answer by omission); a provider per agent-and-door
pair (every account fact twice, the owner rebuilt); a provider with no door (the holder kept under a new name); negotiating everything at the
open, and a negotiation admitting a later run; the native transport as one provider's own, and the shared transports inside the providers'
boundary; a provider's own event channel or minting; the readings declared in Agents; a protocol turn's end read as a part's; an `agent` field
beside `adapter`; renaming door ids; a binding-details table now; a correction inside a move; today's stand-ins given a vocabulary of their own;
a lineage hook every provider must take; a manifest declaring a dialect, words or abilities; an assembly per provider now; *provider* on the
window; a universal rule translator; a provider choosing another account at its limit; a token calibration or a fixed week in the driver now;
the lower census families cut a second time.

**What this decision's checks do not cover.** It is proposed and a document: the documentation gates prove its routing and shape, not its
claims, which round 1 read at `0f4d2a8a7` for the corrections its §0.2 lists and otherwise takes from the survey and the proposals; both makers'
reads check them next. Its notes on D23, D53, D57, D70, D130, D172, D182, D205 and D212 are written by the rows that make each change, never
before.
