## D228 — One way to do each thing: a structure that supersedes another retires it, so no job has two paths, and a transitional path is kept only with the condition that ends it and the row that removes it (2026-11-02)

**Decision (the owner's direction of 2026-11-02).** Daoris does each thing one way, on its latest structure. When a design
supersedes an older way of doing a job, the older way is removed — its code, its doors (routes, verbs, tools, page surfaces), its
words and its defaults — in the same chunk that brings the new one, so the system never behaves differently by which path a piece of
work took and nobody has to know which of two ways is current. Where an older record must still be read (an in-flight run, an older
build's file, a home set up long ago), the reading is a transitional path: it is kept only with the condition that ends it, checked
and said where the person can see it, and the row that deletes it once the condition holds; old records convert to the new shape on
the person's press (D227 point 3), never silently. A design is accepted only with its retirement rows; a review counts a second way to
do one job as a defect.

**Why.** The owner found the old workflow logic still showing beside the new (D227's inventory) and set the rule for every area:
two ways to do one thing confuse the person and the agents, and the system behaves differently by path. FOUNDATION1 was opened on the
same finding (defects come where parallel mechanisms disagree); this makes retirement part of every design rather than a later
clean-up.

**Applied first** to the workflow logic (WORKFLOWCLEAN1, D227), then by a sweep of every area for jobs that still have two ways
(ONEWAY1).

**The latest structure, named (the owner's confirmation, 2026-11-02).** The one way every area converges on is the agent
structure: who acts is a participant holding a role, a role a configured record of agents and models chosen by its policy (D212,
D205, D226); work moves by graph workflows a place follows, composed and drawn on one canvas (D157, D190, D223, D224, D227); agents
co-work and talk through one exchange model over the records that carry it (D203, D225); and the appointed roles act on the owner's
behalf within their grants (D208, D209), the company the owner runs (D193). Automation is the default within the policy the person
sets: what a policy, a role's grant or a workflow allows runs without a press, and the person's press stays for what this brief and
the decisions reserve to them (credentials and sign-ins, a push, a publication or a history rewrite, spend or authority beyond the
policy they set, an irreversible act). Where the design of an area chooses between asking the person and acting by such a policy,
it acts by the policy and says so.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (the owner's choices on ONEWAY1's census, `docs/2026-11-02-oneway-census.md`).** An agent's two doors
(Claude Code's native and protocol doors) are two providers of one contract, not two ways: both stay, refactored onto the provider
contract (D213) so every act behaves the same through either and a provider added later joins the same way — AGENTPROVIDER1's
executor and adapter rows are taken back from the parked list for it. Old records in an older shape (six-character quest ids, notes
from before codes, home files at their pre-move places) are converted where they are still in use and cleared where they are only
finished history, by a plan the person presses; no older-shape reader is kept as history. Parked rows that remove a second way or
build the agent structure are taken back; the parked list is triaged against this direction.

**Rejected.** Keeping an older way *as before* or *for now* with no end; a flag that leaves the old surface the default after the new
one is built; a migration that rewrites the person's records without their press.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (ONEWAY1's design, `docs/2026-11-02-oneway-design.md`, proposed for both makers' reads).** It settles what this
decision left open. Every area's old records convert through one door, WORKFLOWCLEAN1's widened into parts, never a press per area. A
record that something open reads is converted; finished history is cleared by the retention pass. Every kept
read is one register entry with its condition and deleting row, shown on This machine's page and held by a gate. *For an older build*
ends at the home's floor. No part writes at a start or at an unrelated edit.
