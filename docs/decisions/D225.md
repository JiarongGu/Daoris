## D225 — Communication is a read over the records that already carry it: say, request and hand over, each request with what it expects back and its delivery apart from its outcome; who said a word is read from its marks; every answer reaches the line's head through one door; parts of one entry may address each other (2026-11-02)

**Decision (COMMS1, the owner's direction of 2026-11-02; co-designed under D191, accepted by the steward 2026-11-02).** Per
`docs/2026-11-02-comms-design.md`. Every store stays: words wait on a line's `said` (D188), work between repositories is a quest
(D32), and helpers, pieces, opinions, questions, go-aheads, hand-overs and the channel keep their own records. What moves between
participants is one of three acts, *say*, *request* or *hand over*, and a request names what it expects back (an answer, work, a
piece, an opinion, a decision, a successor). One read, `Exchanges.Of`, composes a line's requests from those records, keeping what
Daoris observed of the delivery apart from the protocol's own outcome, saying a source it could not read and a case whose door is not
available; the waits, *At work*, the head, the brief and `sessions at-work` read it. Who said a word is read by one rule from the marks
it carries and the door it came through (`Senders.Of`, three twins, one fixture), never from a supplied field or its text. A helper's,
a partner's and an opinion's answer go through one door to the line's head, taken by one table, an answered park taking each beside
the person's answer; new opinions write `serves`. Which ask a line is on is its entry's origin. A choice's press names its question,
and a stale press is refused. The count of a line going on by answers alone is kept durably, one serialized admission per line. A
hand-over's brief says what the line waits on. A message is drawn whole once, at its home; *What needs you* counts each obligation
once. In a later wave a line on no ask gains a channel and a part may address a sibling part, kept where no older build reads it.

**Why.** The owner wants communication and co-working made solid. Both sealed proposals found the mechanisms disagreeing in the
code on who said a word, where an answer goes (an opinion's findings went to the record named and so missed the part that continues
the line), when a parked asker takes an answer (an answered park took a helper's and refused a partner's and an opinion's), how *once*
is kept (five receipt mechanisms) and which ask a line is on; and found two jobs with no way: parts of one entry cannot hear each
other, and a line on no ask has no channel. Both kept every store and reached one read; the steward settled where they differed
(design §2).

**Rejected.** One inbox table for every kind, and a stored sender. Answers through the channel. A new message tool family beside
the channel's. Immutable question revisions in a new record: the question file's per-question id gives the correlation. `partner_ask`
in this design, before the partner's confinement is measured (COAGENT1d keeps it). Messages in place of quests, agents addressing
lines, *now* for anyone but the person, urgency judged by a model. A chat waiting on a quest.

**Note, 2026-11-02 (Opus's read, settled in the design's §13): two authority changes said, and D188's reset kept.** The read found
the rows narrower than the disagreements they settle, and each row now carries its part. Two changes widen who may act, and are
decided here: the record allowed to answer an opinion is the one holding its findings, so when a hand-over moves the word, the
part continuing the line answers it; and a go-ahead the person approved on an ask covers the same act from every session the
ask's entry holds, a quest a sibling of the ask published included. The durable answers-alone count keeps D188's reset (the
person's words and Daoris's own fact words reset it) and seeds each line from today's count, so nothing holds on upgrade. Codex's
read is owed: its run stopped at the workspace's spend cap.

**Note, 2026-11-03: the answers-alone count is a fact, not a limit.** The owner settled design §11: a quest's result a line waited
on counts, and no fixed number holds a line. A hard cap stops good work as readily as runaway work, and judging which is the
supervisor's job. So the line's appointed supervisor reads the count (or *unknown*) and decides whether the line goes on, holds for
the person, or is redirected, saying why. With none appointed, the count is shown and drive, cap and strikes still bound the line.
This amends D188 point 5's bound (design §5.3).
