## D341 — An ask becomes quests without a press: the supervisor splits and starts it, and the split is shown as decided without the owner (2026-11-20)

**Decision (UIWF2, UIWF6, UIWF7, the window redesign `docs/2026-11-19-window-design.md` §11; the owner's direction of
2026-11-19; reversing the ask page's rule that a proposal is a person's to accept).**

1. A sent ask goes to the supervisor (the intake), which splits it into quests for the right repositories and publishes and
   starts them. The New ask sheet then shows the words becoming quests: the supervisor session with the model that ran, the parts
   and why, the quests as published, the requirements it drafted (editable). *Stop it* is the ask's abandon.
2. The ask's page shows the split, the kind of work and the plan's choices under *Decided without you*, each with *Reverse…* where
   a door exists. The publish section (*Publish to <repository>*), the kind proposal's *Apply* and the owner's *Take* on a quest
   go; the attention kind `proposal` is the agents'.
3. Where no planner can take an ask, one card asks *Where should this go?* with the declarations' matches; that is a direction
   question and the owner's.

**Why.** The owner answers and never dispatches: publishing each part by hand, applying an intake's proposal and taking quests are
the manager's work the doctrine keeps off the owner, and a press on every ask holds the company while the owner is away.

**Rejected.**

- *A Start press on every ask* (the brainstorm's g1): the owner's answer; it holds every ask on the owner.
- *The owner publishing each part*: dispatch is the supervisor's.
