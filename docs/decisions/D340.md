## D340 — Every owner act is defined once and any actor can be given it; an act that cannot be taken back is one held press, any other a 5-second Undo (2026-11-20)

**Decision (UISH24, UISH14, UISH15, UIRP17, UIRP29, the window redesign `docs/2026-11-19-window-design.md` §6; the owner's
direction of 2026-11-19; extending D325 point 1 and APPROVEACT1; amending D284 §5's home of the rules list and the platform's
rule that every act which ends or removes asks once in place).**

1. **An act is defined once** in the driver's modules (`OwnerActs`): its id; its verb and its effect sentence (catalogue keys);
   whether it can be undone; what it needs (a role, a sign-in, a place's declaration, a budget); the role that holds it at each
   place (the place's cast; the owner by default for the closed list); and its doors: the window's route, the `daoris` verb, and
   the tool a holding agent is handed. The first acts: answer a question, give or refuse a go-ahead, approve or hold a release,
   sign in an account or a remote, raise a budget or hold work at it, delete the owner's data, confirm it was you, reverse a
   decision made without the owner, keep or drop a workflow change to the owner's part, accept a check or landing the owner
   keeps. A census fails a press that performs an owner act with no definition, and a definition with no door.
2. **The window is the human actor's door.** A wait card's presses and a held press take an act and read their label,
   consequence and form from its definition. An act that **cannot be taken back** is one held press (800 ms, its fill drawn), its
   consequence written on the control (*Ends*, *Keeps*, *Loses* where they apply), with no second question. An act that **can** is
   sent after a 5-second grace with *Undo* (nothing sent before it ends; closing the window sends at once). An act another actor
   holds reads "held by <role> (<maker · model>) · Overrule".
3. **An agent actor can be given an act.** On Company › Roles › *What stays yours* the owner gives an act to an appointed role at
   a place and takes it back; that role's holder performs the act through its tool, journaled and settled by its receipt as D325
   point 1 settles a role's act, the way APPROVEACT1's `approve-release` already works. An act that needs a credential or the
   owner's leave can be given only where the holder has it.
4. *What stays yours* is the one list of what the owner keeps: the closed list, each act and its holder, what agents may do
   unasked on this machine, and the rules switched on and every *always* given, with *Take back* (D284 §5's list, at its new
   home).

**Why.** The owner: an irreversible act is one held press with its consequence on the control, and every owner act is an act any
actor can be given; the UI is the human's skill over the acts, never the only way to do them. D325 and APPROVEACT1 already made
a release approval the approver role's act wherever the role is cast; leaving every other owner act as a window press would keep
the owner the only actor for them, against "owner, not manager". Asking once for a reversible act is noise the fresh designs
both drop; a single gesture stays true everywhere.

**Rejected.**

- *A press, then a confirmation dialog*: the owner's answer; a second question for every act.
- *Acts only a window press can perform*: the owner's answer; the window would be the only door.
- *Undo for irreversible acts*: a release or a deletion cannot be called back after the grace.
- *The rules list as its own Settings domain* (*Limits you set*): a second list of what the agents may do, apart from the roles
  that do it.
