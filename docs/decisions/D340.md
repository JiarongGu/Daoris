## D340 — Every owner act is defined once and any actor can be given it; an act that cannot be taken back is one held press, any other a 5-second Undo (2026-11-20)

**Decision (UISH24, UISH14, UISH15, UIRP17, UIRP29, the window redesign `docs/2026-11-19-window-design.md` §6; the owner's
direction of 2026-11-19; extending D325 point 1 and APPROVEACT1; amending D284 point 6's home of the rules list (the access design
§5), D152 §3 with desktop-app rule 29 for keys, and the platform's rule that every act which ends or removes asks once in place).**

1. **An act is defined once** (`OwnerActs`, in the driver's modules), built over the doors that exist: D325's role act handed to
   its holder as a tool and settled by its receipt, APPROVEACT1's `approve-release`, the window's routes and the `daoris` verbs.
   Each carries its id; its verb and its effect sentence (catalogue keys); whether it can be undone; what it needs (a role, a
   sign-in, a place's declaration, a budget); the role that holds it at each place (the place's cast; the owner by default for the
   closed list); and its doors. FOUNDATION1's one Act contract, when it is built, adopts these definitions as its first kinds
   rather than adding a second registry. The first acts: answer a question, give or refuse a go-ahead, approve or hold a release,
   sign in an account or a remote, raise a budget or hold work at it, delete the owner's data, rewrite history, confirm it was you,
   reverse a decision made without the owner, keep or drop a workflow change to the owner's part, accept a check or landing the
   owner keeps. A census fails a press that performs an owner act with no definition, and a definition with no door.
2. **Person-only acts.** *Confirm it was you* (D156's door) is the person's alone and can never be given: its point is that Daoris
   cannot tell the person from an agent there. The census marks it so, and a request to give it is refused.
3. **The window is the human actor's door.** A wait card's presses, its keys and a held press take an act and read their label,
   consequence and form from its definition. An act that **cannot be taken back** is one held press (800 ms, its fill drawn; a
   key held as long), its consequence written on the control (*Ends*, *Keeps*, *Loses* where they apply), with no second question.
   An act that **can** is sent after a 5-second grace with *Undo* (nothing sent before it ends; closing the window sends at once).
   An act another actor holds reads "held by <role> (<maker · model>) · Overrule".
4. **An agent actor can be given an act.** On Company › Roles › *What stays yours* the owner gives an act to an appointed role at
   a place and takes it back; a wait card's *Not mine* writes the same record under the same rules. That role's holder performs the
   act through its tool, journaled and settled by its receipt as D325 point 1 settles a role's act. The act is handed only to the
   session holding the role's step where the act belongs to a step (ACTORFLOW1's open item, which UIRP29 builds on). An act that
   needs a credential or the owner's leave can be given only where the holder has it.
5. *What stays yours* is the one list of what the owner keeps: the closed list, each act and its holder, what agents may do
   unasked on this machine, and the rules switched on and every *always* given, with *Take back* (D284 point 6's list, at its new
   home).

**Why.** The owner: an irreversible act is one held press with its consequence on the control, and every owner act is an act any
actor can be given; the UI is the human's skill over the acts, never the only way to do them. D325 and APPROVEACT1 already made
a release approval the approver role's act wherever the role is cast; leaving every other owner act as a window press would keep
the owner the only actor for them, against "owner, not manager". Asking once for a reversible act is noise the fresh designs
both drop; a single gesture, held for what cannot be undone, stays true for buttons and keys alike.

**Rejected.**

- *A press, then a confirmation dialog*: the owner's answer; a second question for every act.
- *Acts only a window press can perform*: the owner's answer; the window would be the only door.
- *Undo for irreversible acts*: a release or a deletion cannot be called back after the grace.
- *Giving Confirm it was you to an agent*: it would confirm nothing.
- *A registry of the window's own beside FOUNDATION1's Act contract*: two ways to define an act; the contract adopts these.
- *Two doors for handing an act over* (*Not mine* with its own setting beside *Give this act to…*): one record, one rule set.
- *The rules list as its own Settings domain* (*Limits you set*): a second list of what the agents may do, apart from the roles
  that do it.
