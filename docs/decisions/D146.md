## D146 — A done may carry captured proof: the session captures screenshots and API answers, Daoris keeps them on the machine, a requirement may require them, and whether one shows the right state is the person's look (2026-10-04)

**Decision (EVID2).** The owner, looking at a ticket's work that Daoris recorded as done: *"there is no proof it's
completed if the server is stopped (for local development) it should take some screenshot for its completion state
since there are tasks not really related to code, it's configuration based"*. Much of that work was configuration made
through a running environment's API: records created, and a report configured. Its proof is the environment's state
and a page's look, not a diff. The environment is local and may be stopped later. D144 (EVID1) designed evidence Daoris
reads itself, a path in a commit and later a gate, and left a screenshot's presence to path evidence (its §7). The
contract is `docs/2026-10-04-landing-and-proof-design.md`, Part II.

1. **The session captures, and Daoris keeps.** At a done, a session captures screenshots through the browser server it
   is handed (Playwright MCP from a browser plugin, D78 and D99), and saves the answers of the API reads it relied on.
   Both go into `${proof}`, one folder per session under the home (`<home>/proof/<session>/captured`). The driver makes
   the folder at spawn and expands `${proof}` when it hands a session its servers, as it expands `${browser}`. The
   browser plugins in `examples/plugins/` pass it as Playwright's `--output-dir`. The rules handed at spawn let the
   session write there and nowhere else outside its tree (D72). Nothing goes into the repository.
2. **The done names its proof.** `quest_respond`'s done gains `proof`: at most ten items, each a file in `${proof}`, its
   kind (`screenshot` or `answer`), what it is of (the page address or the request line) and what it shows, in the
   session's words, at most 300 characters, and optionally the requirement it serves. The exchange judges the shape at
   every door. *Capture proof* on a session's or a quest's page asks for it on demand. Its fixed words reopen the
   session in its tree (D137), ask it to capture each requirement's proof and change nothing, and say this works only
   while the environment runs.
3. **A requirement may require captured proof.** D144's evidence gains two kinds, words a session and a person read:
   `{ "screenshot": "<page> showing <state>" }` and `{ "answer": "<the read it relied on>" }`, each at most 300
   characters and counted in D144's five. Whoever writes the requirement writes them, never the session they will judge
   (D144 §1). The intake asks for one on configuration work, where the person's words name a state outside the
   repository, and asks for a screenshot only where the room says sessions here are handed a browser.
4. **A done without it is held as D144 holds one.** A met answer on such a requirement closes done and held, *evidence
   unread*. When the session ends, the driver reads its proof and posts the verdict through D144's door as an
   `Evidenced` operation. An item is found when the done names at least one kept capture of its kind for that
   requirement. The codes are `kept`, `not-captured`, `missing`, `unreadable`, `too-large`, `wrong-kind` and
   `no-browser`. Missing keeps the hold. The person's yes accepts the done as it stands, and *Check again* reads again
   after an on-demand capture.
5. **Presence and readability are facts. What a capture shows is the person's judgement** (D54). Daoris checks that a
   capture is present, its kind by its leading bytes, that it reads, and its size. It never judges whether a screenshot
   shows the right state. It shows the image beside the session's words for the person to judge. No model reads a
   screenshot (D24), and capture needs a browser, not a model.
6. **Daoris keeps it, bounded and redacted.** At the session's end the driver copies each named file into
   `<home>/proof/<session>/kept/<sha256>.<ext>`, with a manifest beside it, and never changes it. A screenshot is PNG or
   JPEG of at most 5 MB and 8,000 pixels a side. An answer is UTF-8 of at most 256 KiB. At most ten per done, and 50 MB
   per session. An answer keeps no header lines, and its credential-shaped values and machine paths are replaced by
   `[redacted]` and counted. A screenshot's pixels cannot be read without a model, so the instruction forbids a page
   that shows a credential or personal data beyond the check, and asks for the element rather than the window. Proof
   stays until the person removes it.
7. **It is shown on this machine, and never crosses it.** The session's page and its review show a *Proof* section,
   and the quest's page shows each requirement's captures. The bytes come through the modules' bridge, as a preview's
   do. `daoris-driver proof <session|quest> [--save <dir>]` lists and copies them out, and `quest check` and `trace`
   print codes, hashes and sizes. Across machines (D47) the `Evidenced` operation carries per item its kind,
   requirement, code, hash, size, dimensions, words and which machine keeps it. It never carries the bytes, the address
   or a path. No host serves a proof byte over HTTP.

**Why.** D144 made a met answer stand on a fact where one can be read. Configuration work leaves no file to read, and
the state it leaves can vanish with the environment. A capture taken while the environment runs is the only proof that
survives it. The session already drives a browser for that work, so capture costs a tool call, not a new runner.
Keeping it under the home, as transcripts are kept (D76), keeps an environment's look off the repository and off the
team's remote. Reusing D144's hold, door and yes adds no state and no new act for the person.

**Rejected.**
- **Daoris taking the screenshot itself over the browser's port.** It would make the capture Daoris's act, but a
  session cannot ask the shell for one today, and the person's look is the judgement either way. It is a later row, if a
  real case shows a session's own capture is not enough.
- **Proof committed to the repository.** It would publish an environment's state with the code.
- **Proof synced to the remote.** A teammate would receive images of this machine's environment (D47 §4).
- **A model reading the screenshot to gate the done.** That is a judgement (D54) that needs a model (D24).
- **Refusing an answer that holds a credential-shaped value.** It would hold dones on a pattern's false match.
  Redaction keeps the answer and says what it removed.
- **Daoris's own record of the tool call as the answer.** A session names the file it saved more reliably than a tool
  call's id, and a call's output in the events is bounded and transcript-class.
- **A landing plugin attaching proof to its pull request.** That is an outward act that needs its own say-so, so a later
  row.
- **Putting a done with no commits in *To review* for its proof.** *To review* is for work to land, and the person's
  attention comes from D144's hold where a requirement needed the proof.

**This amends** D144: two kinds of evidence, read at the same moment and posted through the same door, and its §7 row
for a screenshot gives way to captured proof. D78 §3.5: `${proof}` is expanded beside `${browser}`. D72: one folder
outside the tree that a session may write. D133 §4 as D144 amended it: a met answer also holds for its captures. D47 §4
and D76 stand: proof is machine-local material, as a transcript is.

**What the checks do not cover.** Documents only, and nothing is built. EVID1, which this builds on, is not built
either. No session was asked to capture proof. Whether Playwright MCP 0.0.82 writes its screenshots to `--output-dir`
under the names a session gives was read from how the plugins already pass the flag, not measured. The redaction's
patterns are a design, and no answer from the work repository's environment was read against them. `verify` checks
that this entry names what it rejected, and none of its words.
