## D330 — The window's colours and type are one role set: blue acts, amber is the owner's alone, one scale for Latin and Han (2026-11-20)

**Decision (UIDS1, the window redesign `docs/2026-11-19-window-design.md` §7, amending D41's bronze accent and D56's 13 px body
and denser scale).**

1. **Roles, not colours.** Components name roles; only `tokens.css` holds a value, light block then dark block then the forced
   blocks. The accent turns from bronze to blue (`#2F5BD3` light, `#5F86F2` dark): buttons, links, focus, selection, and the
   moving mark (`live`, a second name on the same hue). Amber (`you`, `you-ink`, `you-soft`, `on-you`) marks only what waits on
   the owner: ◆, the waits pill, a wait card's edge and field, its *yes*. Red (`fail`) marks only a failure; declined and stopped
   are neutral. There is no neutral state colour: waiting, queued, ended and stale are told by their mark's shape in `ink-soft`
   or `ink-faint`. The warm paper of D41 stays in the neutrals.
2. **Contrast held by the test.** Every text pair at least 4.5:1 on every surface in both themes (`ink-faint` raised from 3:1),
   every mark, focus ring and control edge at least 3:1, the soft fields exact tokens; `tokens.test.ts` holds the whole table.
3. **One type scale for Latin and Han**: caption 12, small 13 (rows), body 14, heading 16, display 20, mono 13; line heights
   that `:lang(zh)` raises; no step under 12 px; weights 400 and 600, no italics; no tracking on Han; Han punctuation full width
   through a font alias; spacing between Han and Latin at render time; mono only for code, paths and hashes, never a sentence.
4. **One density.** Rows 32 px (44 for two lines, 28 in tables) on a 4 px grid; the owner's size control is zoom.
5. Named sizes, containers, window breakpoints (720, 1024, 1280), layers, shadows and motion durations become tokens; the live
   mark breathes three times on a change of state and is still (FREEZE1 kept).

**Why.** The owner's amber and the interactive bronze are one hue family (1.55:1 apart in dark), so "waits on you" cannot be seen
at a glance; the faint ink carries text in 978 places at 3.38:1; an 11 px step and sentences set in mono put Han below a readable
size. All three fresh designs take a single interactive accent that is not amber; two of three take a 14 px body, and Han in
Microsoft YaHei UI at 13 px is the reason.

**Rejected.**

- *Keeping bronze and moving the owner's hue elsewhere*: every fresh design reserves amber for the owner, and bronze would stay
  one hue family with it.
- *A 13 px body*: legible in Latin, cramped in Han; rows stay 13, so density holds.
- *Two densities or a density switch*: zoom already does the job, and a second density doubles every baseline and every state.
- *An endless breathing mark*: measured at 601 frames in 5 s and a quarter of a GPU core.
