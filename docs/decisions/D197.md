## D197 — An agent's accounts signed in for Daoris are one set that serves every workspace, in one order with one set of use settings; the machine's list and the workspaces' lists go, and the person's own sign-in stays theirs, outside it (2026-10-21)

**Decision (POOL1, proposed 2026-10-21).** **Status: proposed** (the steward accepts it under D191). On 2026-10-21 the owner,
adding an account on an agent's page, met the add-account dialog's third step, *Let it run work in*: a box for a workspace's own
list of three accounts and a box for *This machine's list*, worded "used by games; its starts run on account-4 instead of
Daoris's accounts, in turn". The owner found the step confusing and the very idea of a machine's list a sign that the model was
wrong, and read the sentence as Daoris moving the machine onto another account. The owner decided one set: every account signed
in for Daoris serves every workspace, with nothing about lists left for the person to reason about; and the machine's own account,
the person's own sign-in, stays the machine's, which Daoris never changes and adding an account never changes. The contract is
`docs/2026-10-21-account-pool-design.md`.

Why it confused (design §0). Each agent's accounts are read per scope, the machine's or one workspace's, each with its own list and
settings (D130 points 3 and 4; `HarnessSettings.ResolveScope`); a scope naming nobody takes every account of Daoris's (D175's
OWNSPACE1a note); and ticking *This machine's list* while the machine names nobody makes a list of that one account
(`WithJoined`, `HarnessSettings.cs:998-1005`), moving every workspace on it from all of Daoris's accounts to one: a narrowing the
dialog offered beside the others as if it were sharing (`joinChoices`, `agents.ts:524-560`; `choiceDetail`,
`AddAccount.tsx:70-79`).

1. **One set per agent, Daoris's accounts** (design §2.1). Every account of the agent that Daoris holds, the folders under the
   home's account homes, serves every workspace on the machine. There is no machine's list and no workspace's list; a start of
   any workspace reads the same accounts. A signed-out account stays in the set and is passed until signed in again; only
   *Remove…* takes one out. The person's pick at a start stays the one exception (D130 point 4).
2. **One order and one set of use settings per agent** (§2.2, §2.3). The order holds every account, a new one last; its first is
   where starts begin under *One by one, in order*, and it breaks the last tie under *Make the most of them*. There is no default
   beside it, and every reader of the machine's default reads the order's first. The settings, `use`, `keep`, `switch` and
   `curve`, are one entry for the agent; clearing the order clears none of them.
3. **The person's own sign-in is the machine's, outside the set** (§3). Daoris never signs it in or out, never starts work in it
   (D175), and no door writes it: `agent trust` with no account named takes the order's first account, never the tool's own
   trust file as it does today. Adding an account never touches it. A conversation begun there goes on there. Its row stays last
   and read-only and says this.
4. **Adding an account asks nothing about where it runs** (§4.1). The dialog's *Let it run work in* step goes: at a sign-in's end
   it says *{{account}} joined Daoris's accounts and now serves every workspace* (*{{account}} 已加入 Daoris 的账户，现在为所有工作区运行工作*)
   and asks only the name.
5. **The page shows one set** (§4.2-§4.4). *Runs for*, *Use in a workspace…*, *Add to / Remove from … list*, *Use by default…* and
   the *Workspaces* fold go; *How accounts are used* edits the one order and settings; the start dialog lists the set; the wait
   that asked *Let account-1 run work…* goes, since no account is outside. *This machine's list* (本机的列表) and *this machine's
   accounts* leave every screen and sentence, and the glossary's new term *Daoris's accounts* (Daoris 的账户) holds them out.
6. **The doors follow** (§5). `daoris agent profile order` and `use` act on the set and refuse `--workspace`; `profile default`,
   `profile join` and `login`'s `--join` and `--join-machine` are refused, saying what to do instead. `ACCOUNT_USE` loses `inherit`
   and `workspace`, `HARNESS_ACTION` its `profile-join`, `profile-default` and `joins`. Ask Daoris's `use` and `order` act on the
   set, its `default` door is retired, and a proposal stored before with a workspace or for `default` is refused at its Apply.
7. **The store folds once, and says what changed** (§6). At the driver's come-up, under the file's keep lock, `harnesses.json`
   folds per agent: the order is the union (the machine's default and list, then each workspace's by name, then every other
   account), nothing signed in dropped; each setting is the machine's where it set one, else the one value every workspace that set
   it agreed on, else the default; the defaults and the workspace sections go. What it changed is kept beside it and shown on the
   agent's page and in `agent list` until the person presses *Got it*. A file an older build writes again folds again.
8. **What stays machine-wide** (§7): the cap (D130 point 10), each account's readings and cool-offs, the machine's default agent,
   a workspace's pinned versions, and the person's own sign-in.
9. **The consequence, stated** (§8). A workspace that had its own list now runs on every account of Daoris's; a scope that named
   one account alone now runs on all, that one first; an account no list held now runs. D130's aim E, *keep work where it belongs*,
   has no setting now: a person who needs a workspace on one account picks it at the start, or keeps the others out of Daoris.
10. **The rows** (§11), all after PUSH1f2e: POOL1a, one set in the driver with the fold; POOL1b, the doors; POOL1c, the page;
    POOL1d, the install's fold and dialog seen.

**Why.** The lists answered a question the person should not have to hold: which scope a start reads, which accounts that scope
excludes, and what joining it moves. One set is the rule a person already assumes, and the one D175 had already made true wherever
nothing was named (*Daoris's accounts, in turn*); only a list, once made, broke it. The order and one set of
settings keep every choice D130 §16 gave the person except where work may run, which is the choice the owner gave up. The person's
own sign-in is the one account Daoris must not move, since the person works in it at their own terminal (D125's founding
observation was the reverse, the person's sign-in moving Daoris), so it stays outside by construction and the dialog no longer
names *this machine* at all. Folding rather than asking keeps every start running across the change, and writing the fold in the
shape an older build reads as the machine's list keeps a rollback starting the same accounts.

**Rejected** (design §9): a workspace's own list as an opt-in narrowing; the machine's list under another name; a per-account switch
in or out of the set; a default beside the order; a fold at every read, never written; asking the person before the fold; the
fold's account as a notice in D196's record now; per-scope settings dropped silently, or merged to the most cautious; `trust`
granted in every account where none is named, in this change.

**Amends,** once accepted (design §10): D125 point 4, point 8 and its ACCT1 note; D130 points 3, 4, 5, 11 and 12, the goal's point
7, its TOOL4g, TOOL4l-a and TOOL4l-b notes, and the account-use design §2 rules 1-3, §3 and §9; D152 points 4 and 5, its UX7b note's
step 3, and the UX7 design §4.2, §4.4 and §4.5; D175's OWNSPACE1a settlement 2, OWNSPACE1b settlement 5 and OWNSPACE1d's join
sentence. Each carries a dated note pointing here. Unchanged: the cool-off and a limit not a strike (D125), the walk (D130 §16.3,
ACCTWALK3), what is left by the agent's own word, the cap, D165's curve, and D175 points 1 to 3.

**Held by** (once built): the fold's table and its idempotence, an older build's reading of a folded file, the sign-in spawn's
configuration home under the home, `held.no-account` unchanged, each door's refusal and the kinds test both ways, the room's
goldens, `HelpCoverageTests`, the page's tests and stories in en and zh, `i18n:check` and `names:check -- --strict`, and the
parent's look at the install (POOL1d).

**What the checks do not cover.** Written from the code at `dbac74517`; nothing was run and the window was not looked at. Open to
the steward (design §12): whether the owner's *machine's own account* could mean the machine scope's default rather than the
person's own sign-in (this design folds the default into the order as its first); and whether `trust` with no account named
should grant every account rather than the order's first.
