## D188 — A message to a working agent never stops it: its urgency, ordinary, urgent or now, says where it reaches, only a stop stops a turn, and every path follows one delivery rule with a durable queue and four receipts (2026-10-20)

**Decision (COWORK1, proposed).** **Status: proposed**, pending the owner's review. No row builds before the owner's yes.

The owner decided the rule on 2026-10-20: a message to an agent never forces it to stop, and whether and how it interrupts
depends on the message's urgency. Read at `b5ff8dfb9`, the paths disagree, and none carries a level:
- the person's *Send now* stops the turn on a door that holds words for its turn's end (D136 point 4);
- another session's channel claim enters the next step, while a helper's answer and an opinion's findings never enter a
  step in flight and reopen the ended record;
- Daoris's own words are kept as the person's, and reach a running session only once it parks or ends;
- a claim with no next part is lost unsaid;
- a conversation's queue is lost at a restart (MSG1d2);
- the record tells *said* from *taken*, and nothing more.

The contract is `docs/2026-10-20-message-urgency-design.md`.

1. **Three levels, and no level stops a turn.**
   - **Ordinary** waits for a natural point, never entering a turn in flight: the turn's end, the space between turns, the
     next part, or the agent's own read.
   - **Urgent** reaches the agent at its next step, where its door takes words then, and cuts nothing.
   - **Now** cuts the step in flight where the agent steers, and the turn goes on with the words.
   - A turn or a session is stopped only by a stop: the person's *Stop*, *Stop turn* or the keeping stop, or Daoris's own
     timeouts, pauses, limits and pressed hand-overs, each as its decision has it. No agent stops another's turn. Urgency
     is when a message goes, not its order.
2. **Who may send each.**
   - **The person**: all three, *now* for their own words only. Their default is urgent, D136's reach.
   - **Daoris**: ordinary, and urgent for a fact that makes the work in flight wrong (a source reopened under a step, a
     step gone on with).
   - **A solicited answer**: ordinary, or urgent where its ask asked for it. An opinion's findings and a quest's outcome
     are always ordinary.
   - **An unsolicited claim**: ordinary, or urgent within a bound (three of a part's ten posts).
   - **Nobody but the person** sends *now*.
3. **Until STEER3, an urgent word on a door that takes words only at its turn's end waits for that end, said so.**
   - It stops nothing.
   - *Send now* there holds the words, and the line beside them offers the keeping stop (`CANCEL_TURN` with `keep`) as
     the person's own press.
   - An agent's urgent claim there waits for the entry's next part, unless the person passes it on.
   - Codex's `turn/steer` and Claude Code's stream-json input are used for a level only once STEER3 has measured them.
4. **Agents still do not talk each other into turns.**
   - An unsolicited message never starts, reopens or extends a run.
   - A solicited answer may extend or reopen its asker's run once per ask, since the asker asked under the person's rule
     and the answerer cannot ask back.
   - A line of work goes on by answers alone at most twice in a row. The third answer waits for the person.
5. **One delivery rule for every path.** A message is kept in its path's store with its level, sender and id before its
   sender is told. It reaches the agent at the earliest point its level allows on the session's door. Where it cannot go
   sooner, it waits as ordinary and says why. At a natural point, it goes into the next prompt where its sender may make a
   turn happen, and otherwise waits for a turn that comes anyway. The design's §5 tabulates it over the person, Daoris,
   the channel, a helper, an opinion, a meeting and a quest. A meeting's barrier and a quest's doors are unchanged.
6. **One durable queue per session, and each path keeps its store.**
   - The record's `said` takes words for a running record too: the person's, Daoris's (marked `from: daoris`) and
     solicited answers. The in-memory queues become its views, filled again at the shell's come-up.
   - A word leaves `said` by id once it is on the wire.
   - The channel's mark moves when a part takes a message, not when it holds one.
   - A claim never taken expires, visibly, when its ask has no quest open, and the person may pass it on.
7. **Four receipts, each where it is observed.** For a word, all four are on the session's record. For a channel message,
   *posted* is on its row, and the rest are on the part's record and the entry's mark.
   - **Held** is queued.
   - **Sent** is acknowledged: it left Daoris on the wire.
   - **Taken** is delivered: the agent took it into its conversation.
   - **Answered** is acted upon: the turn that took it ended with an answer, or a correlated reply came. Answered never
     means obeyed.

**Why.**
- A stop that a message causes ends work the person did not mean to end. A turn-end door has no way to hear words sooner,
  so the honest answer is to say when they will go and leave the stop to the person's own press.
- The sender knows how much a message matters, and the door knows how soon it can be heard. Today the second decides
  alone, so a claim nobody asked for interrupts while an answer the agent asked for waits.
- One rule with a level puts both inputs in one place. ADR-D asked for durable, deduplicated messages with four states told
  apart. Each path already has a store, so the queue to a session is the one record that every path but the channel and
  the meeting already reaches.

**Rejected.**
- **A level that stops a turn.**
- ***now* for an agent or for Daoris.** A step the agent chose can always wait one step for their words.
- **Codex's `turn/steer`, or the native door's stream-json, as a level's door before STEER3 measures it.**
- **An unsolicited message that makes a turn happen.** D155 and D167 stand.
- **Urgency judged by a model.**
- **A priority queue that reorders waiting words.**
- **A clock that decays urgency.** A message that finds no running turn waits as ordinary already.
- **One new store for every message.** That would be two authoritative stores for each concept, against D173's guard.
- **The level on the travelling session record.**

**What changed.**
- **Today, restated per door and per kind** (design §0.2). The native driven run and the native conversation are apart: the
  conversation's stream-json has an interrupt, so *Send now* stops its turn today, and the run has none. A text-only
  conversation sends every word at once and keeps the person's words in no record, a helper's answer included. A word
  reaches the next step only once the first prompt is on the wire and nothing waits before it. `ReopenTelling` returns on a
  running record before it asks the say door, and on a park its words are the park's answer. An agent's own `channel_read`
  reaches a claim with no new part, and a start's packet is bounded. A meeting's real-agent turn is refused today. Two
  queues, not all of them, are lost at a restart.
- **Points 1 to 3: a level is a request.** Each door's outcome per level is tabulated (design §3.0). On a turn-end door,
  urgent and now get what ordinary gets, and an urgent claim there gets no earlier delivery at all. A text-only conversation
  honours no level. The keeping stop is offered only where the door has an interrupt that keeps the session, so never on the
  native driven run.
- **Point 4: the turn-end race is decided by weakening the rule to what Daoris does.** No door offers a queueing prompt that
  refuses an idle session, so the rule now reads that Daoris never prompts a claim at a turn's end. The window is narrowed to
  the harness's answer still in the pipe, the rest is named, and STEER3 measures whether a door can close it (design §2.3).
  The answers-alone bound is counted from the line's records, through both paths that go on and across hand-overs, and is
  judged by the session's one dispatcher.
- **Point 6: delivery by identity.** A word is kept once by its producer's key. One dispatcher drains `said` in its order. An
  attempt is marked before the wire, and a restart promises at most once without the person, with an uncertain outcome shown,
  since the wire's write and the receipt after it cannot be one step. The channel delivers by per-entry acknowledgments by
  message id, written by every hand that moved the mark, and the mark is kept for display. One part of an entry claims a
  message. An ordinary claim rides a prompt the run sends anyway. Expiry checks that no part of the entry still runs
  (design §4.2, §6).
- **Point 7: a receipt is shown only where the door observed it.** *Taken* is written on the door's word, where today it can
  be written before the wire. The channel adds *read* and *partly handed*, and a restart adds *uncertain* (design §7).
- **Stored shapes** (design §9): the channel's `urgent` is said on every new message, an explicit false included, since
  absent must keep meaning *from before*. A word's author is its own field, never read from its transport, since the person's
  own channel message arrives with `channel` set.
- **Rows** (design §14): COWORK1b first carries the producer key, COWORK1c follows it, the channel's mark trap moves to
  COWORK1d, and each row names its most likely mistake as its trap.

**What did not change, and why.**
- The three levels, who may send each, *now* for the person alone and no level stopping a turn: the read found them holding
  once read as requests.
- Steering needs both declarations, and the three-of-ten bound fits in the insert's own statement: the read confirmed both.
- The bound stays a part's, not an entry's. The ten-post cap it sits in is a part's, and counted over an entry's life it would
  leave a later part with none (design §12).
- No automatic resend after a restart. No harness drops a repeated prompt by its id, so a replay could make the agent act
  twice.
- `said` already gives a word's destination, order and level, being one record's ordered list; what it lacked was a
  dispatcher and an attempt.
- An urgent answer taken inside a running turn is not counted toward the answers-alone bound. No run goes on past its own
  turn, and D163's two helpers at work and the run's own minutes bound an asker asking again.
- `channel_read` still acknowledges what it returns before its answer reaches the agent. It is the agent's own call, so a
  lost answer is a failed call it makes again with `since`.

**What the checks do not cover.** Still documents only. The facts were read, not run, and the race's window and a door's
confirmation of *taken* are not measured.
