## D306 — An act spends only what its grants cover, and lapses with its subject (2026-11-16)

**Decision.** A role's or the steward's act is admitted only where every grant on its chain covers both its name and what it spends
(money, a sign-in, a destination): a delegate never holds more than its delegator. Each act is pinned to one subject and lapses with
it. Only the owner widens what agents may grant; an agent never grants agents acts. A refused act says which grant was missing.

**Why.** On 2026-11-16 review found a reviewer vacancy's `recast` able to name the prepaid paid agent without the prepaid grant, and
the vacancy's newest act read by every later walk without end; and an agent could write the roles that grant agents acts.

**Rejected:** admission by the act's name alone (today's per-act check); a risk score in place of the grant chain; expiring acts by
time alone (a vacancy signed out or refused has no reset time).
