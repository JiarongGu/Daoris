## D214 — A repository keeps a number of session trees and hands a free one from one line of work to the next: between lines a tree waits at a parking name no reader keys, where it is switched to the next line's branch and checked, and only then is it renamed into that line's path, so a path and a branch still name one line, every record keeps its meaning and an agent's conversation is found where it was; a tree is taken only when nothing in it is work no ref holds, until the owner agrees to work kept aside, and only what reads its own path crosses a move; the count is the trees kept, a start past it opening a tree as today; reached in rows after FOUNDATION1's Workplace move (2026-10-26)

**Decision (TREEPOOL1, proposed 2026-10-26, round 1 under D191; round 2 the same day).** **Status: proposed; round 2 answers both makers'
reads of round 1, and both makers' reads of round 2 are owed before any of it is accepted.** The owner directed on 2026-10-25 that each
repository keep a pool of session trees: a session takes a free tree and holds it, locked, while it works; the tree is first brought to the
line, its untracked work cleared and its ignored dependency and build folders kept; the session works on a branch of its own; at its end the
tree goes back to the pool with the work on that branch, or with no branch where it was done with no output; the pool is a managed number of
slots, at most one per session the repository may run at once, each made lazily; and trees are removed only when that number is lowered or the
repository leaves, never by age. Today a tree is opened once for one line of work and never reused (D51 rule 2), its folder's name is its
branch's, its path is the line's identity to some forty readers, and its end is a move aside that KEEPSIZE1b deletes an hour after its record,
or fourteen days later otherwise; the price is the setup a fresh tree pays (`npm ci` 170 to 221 s, .NET's `bin/` and `obj/` built again) and the
bytes moved-aside trees hold (about 205 MB each). The survey (`docs/2026-10-26-tree-pool-survey.md`, read at `6b999d6b1`) and the brief
(`docs/2026-10-26-tree-pool-brief.md`) set nineteen questions; two sealed proposals answered them, Codex's and the independent Opus reader's;
round 1 of the design, `docs/2026-10-26-tree-pool-design.md`, was their synthesis by Claude Opus 5.5 from main at `f8fbb1bb7`, and round 2,
from main at `f92e95415`, answers both makers' reads of round 1 (the design's §22). **Its central choice is neither proposal's**: both kept a
slot at a stable path of its own and moved a line into it, and the code shows such a path either reaches a shipped build as the line's own tree
while it holds another line's work, or needs a second session family in the service to hide it; so the slot moves to the line's path instead,
and both makers' reads of round 1 keep that choice. It changes decided rules other designs rest on and it switches and removes trees, so by
D191's note of 2026-10-24 no part of it is accepted on one maker's read, and its ⚖ rows are built only after both makers' reads find nothing
blocking in them. Nothing is built, and nothing is built before FOUNDATION1's row mod-x (D212 §14.6), which has not landed.

1. **A slot is a kept tree at its line's path, or parked** (design §2.1, §2.2). A repository keeps a number of linked worktrees of its
   checkout, each known by `(workspace, repository, number)` and by git's registration name, which a move keeps. A slot's folder sits at a
   line's path, `<home>/work/trees/<workspace>/<repository>/s-<8 hex>`, today's layout, only while that line holds it or may come back to it,
   and only on that line's branch; between lines it waits at its parking name, `k-<n>`, which no record, branch or reader keys. A finished
   line's slot is parked at its finish, so its path is empty as after today's tidy. A slot's first making is today's open, said in today's
   words. A repository with submodules is never pooled, since git will not move a tree holding one *(O1-F2, C1-2.7, O1-F9, O1-F15)*.
2. **A path and a branch name one line, always** (§3.1, §3.2, §3.3). No line's path ever holds another line's work, nor shows, even for a
   moment, another line's branch or a detached HEAD, so `BranchOfTree`, TREEFOLLOW1's learning, the ledger's lock key, the auto-lander's and
   the entry landing's grouping, the trace's *same tree*, the work record's sessions by path and the page's folder-named branch all keep their
   meaning. Only the sites that meet a gone tree are re-keyed, so that a free slot is brought back where today they refuse or remake a bare
   tree: TREEGONE1, words going on, a conversation's go-on and moves, a landing, the review of an unlanded line, *Show it again*, the tidy's
   set-aside, the press, the session lock, the opened moment and the Disk space card. `TreeScratch`'s list for removals stays byte for byte.
   Nothing is cut. A census holds the list *(C1-2.1, O1-F2, O1-F12, C1-4.2)*.
3. **The claim is a desktop-store row, Workplace's, by FOUNDATION1 §13's rules, and every rename is under the repository's exclusive hold**
   (§2.3, §2.4). `kept_trees`, `tree_claims`, `tree_visits`, `line_ends` and `line_records`, every column required. One claim in flight per slot
   and per destination (a unique index over its `to`), taken in one `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction, with its whole intent written before its first
   effect: the commit a new branch grows at, the HEAD the judgement read, the paths it leaves, parks at and goes to, the record it opens. A
   start's claim reserves the slot from its choice until its record opens. Every kind (take, return, finish, end, retire) has its phases, each
   effect a mark in §13.4's sense; git's steps run in a job of the claim's own; a claim passes on only from a dead process run by `HomeLease`'s
   test, and the takeover reads both folders, git's registration and git's own lock files before it acts, repairing a registration a rename
   outran and never leaving a slot at a line's path unchecked. Every rename holds the repository's `TreeLock` alone, which every build's start
   and tidy honour. Whether a slot is held is read live from the ledger, the in-use set, the claims and the visits, never stored. git's lock is
   TREELOCK1's while a session holds the slot, `daoris kept tree` while it is free, and the pool's own reason naming the claim's run through a
   claim; OPENCUT1's opening lock only at a slot's first making; an unlocked slot is locked again. A conversation's slot is locked too
   *(C1-2.2, O1-F1, O1-F5, O1-F6, O1-F10, C1-3.1)*.
4. **A line's agent finds its conversation where it was** (§4). Claude Code files a conversation and keeps its trust by the working directory's
   path; a line coming back runs at its own path, on its own branch, whichever slot is brought there, so D189 point 2's *same agent, account and
   tree* holds and its resume-first rule stands.
5. **Brought to the line, and only when safe** (§5). A start takes, in order, the returning line's own slot at its path on its branch, a parked
   slot, a free slot whose line may return and that is judged clean, a new slot below the count, or else a tree as today. A slot is taken for
   another line only where nothing holds it, git lists it, its links and repositories are judged whole (a link carried only where npm made it,
   inside a package folder and leading into the slot itself; a repository holding the slot unless it is a finished line's ignored scratch), no
   git operation is half done, nothing is uncommitted or untracked and every ignored path lies under the one list (a finished line's other
   scratch carried, never cleared), and its HEAD is where its line let it go and a ref holds it; judged again inside the claim under the hold.
   It is then renamed to its parking name and detached there; what does not cross a move is dropped; it is switched by a non-forcing
   `git switch --no-overwrite-ignore` to its new branch or its returning line's; **it is checked at the park** (HEAD the target, nothing
   changed, nothing untracked, every ignored path carried); it is renamed into a destination that must not exist, git listing it there exactly;
   its npm links are made again into that path; **it is checked again there**; and only then does its record open. A failed check returns it to
   its park, detached and `stuck`, and opens nothing. The first build resets and cleans nothing *(C1-1.2, C1-2.4, O1-F4, C1-2.1, O1-F1)*.
6. **Two ends a person sees** (§6). *Done with output*: commits on the branch, ready to land, the slot free but for its holders. *Done without
   output*: no commit and nothing uncommitted, the slot free on its empty branch until a take moves it, the branch then the tidy's, grown again
   under its own name only if its record goes on. *Done, with work it did not commit*: the slot stays the line's until the work is committed,
   discarded or kept aside. The run's end, and idempotently the look, run the end, recording a HEAD moved on the line's own branch as the line's
   work and anything else as held for the person. Daoris commits nothing on a branch *(O1-F10)*.
7. **What holds a slot** (§7): a claim, a terminal's visit admitted in the store before its shell starts, a running or parked record, words
   waiting for as long as they wait, a start due this look, a helper wait, a served review, a standing move (today's box until Part B's
   `moves` lands), a due auto-landing, and work no ref holds; a D79 wait, an ended conversation, a clean kept lost take and a done of either kind
   hold none. When every kept slot is held, a start opens a tree of its own as today: nothing waits *(C1-2.5, O1-F11)*.
8. **The count** (§8) is `driver.json`'s `keptTrees`, per repository, 1 to 16, beside `trees`; absent, `min(cap + 1, 16)` read live,
   `min(max(cap, laneCap) + 1, 16)` once DEV6 builds lanes, so a lowered cap lowers an absent count and retires as a lowered count does, said
   where the cap is set; set by a verb, the Projects row and `SET_TREES`, none of which lands before returns and retirement have. The cap,
   set-ups' `atOnce` and CAPCOUNT1 are unchanged; a conversation takes a slot and counts under no cap. A tree past the count with no commit stays
   until the person's press, as today. What the count means is the owner's question (point 17) *(C1-3.2, O1-F15, C1-4.1)*.
9. **Lazy creation, lowering, leaving** (§9). A slot is made the first time a start finds none free below the count. A lowered count retires the
   slots above it, highest first, each by a claim of its own: a free one has the slim's own manifest written, is moved aside whole by the
   set-aside's move, its packages going at the slim's hour and the rest waiting D195's days as `unmarked`; a held one goes at its let-go; one
   holding work no ref holds stays; a repository leaving retires every slot; the person's press on a slot is today's press, refusing work no ref
   holds; never by age *(C1-1.3, O1-F8, C1-2.4)*.
10. **The finished record at the line's finish** (§10). The look's tidy takes a pooled line's branch where it takes today's, and runs
    KEEPSIZE1b's steps with *park* for *move aside*: judge, hold the tip, park the slot, delete the branch at the commit judged, compose the
    record into `line_records` and post it on today's route with the line's path, so its sessions are exactly the line's. What needs the tree
    (its read moment, its scratch) is captured in `line_ends` at every let-go and at every take that moves a slot away, so a line whose slot was
    taken long before it became removable is recorded whole; the composed record keeps its first identity and moment until the service
    acknowledges it. `Left` names scratch carried in the parked slot; `Whole` is false. A no-output line posts none. D195's kinds gain
    `kept-trees`, rule `count`; retired slots are `set-aside`; KEEPSIZE1c to 1f stand *(C1-2.3, O1-F7)*.
11. **Dependencies kept, and what crosses a move** (§11). One list of build and dependency folders, TreeScratch's thirteen and `wwwroot`, its
    uses its named subsets. Only what reads and writes its own path is carried across a move: `node_modules` (its top caches dropped, its
    workspace links made again), `bin`, `dist`, `build`, `out`, `coverage`, `wwwroot`, `__pycache__`; `.venv`, `obj`, `target`, `.angular`,
    `.next` and `.gradle` are dropped at a move and made again, each to be carried only by a dated note on row b's proof that it reads and
    writes nothing at its old path, which may hold another line's tree. Per npm project, fresh by npm's own staleness rule (the agent told to
    skip `npm ci`), stale and matching the checkout (replaced by TREEDEPS1's copy), or stale (the agent told to run `npm ci`); build outputs kept
    and said to be no proof of a build. TREEGONE1's remake, where no slot is free, copies TREEDEPS1's *(O1-F3, C1-1.4, C1-2.8)*.
12. **Git and Windows** (§12): a branch checked out only at its line's path; the four lock reasons and the pool's; `daoris/` on every branch;
    a destination that must not exist, since `git worktree move` nests into one that does; a registration repaired where a rename outran it;
    git's processes in the claim's job; a refused rename `busy` and a refused switch `stuck`, each measured; long paths as today; junctions moved
    as entries and npm's made again, never followed *(O1-F1, O1-F5, C1-2.2)*.
13. **Records and older builds** (§14). No record changes shape or meaning; a build that does not know the pool reads every path as today,
    refuses a free or claimed slot's lock in its tidy, let-go and press, never reads a claim as an opening, leaves a parked slot alone, goes on in
    a line's slot only where it is that line's on that line's branch, meets a finished line's empty path as after today's tidy, and remakes a
    taken line's tree by TREEGONE1 on its branch; the store's new tables and `keptTrees` are invisible to it. What is left is said, item by
    item: its remake racing a return, which git refuses once; its start racing a take of its line's free slot between its two holds, bounded by
    the slot's holders and failing rather than running elsewhere, which a pinned build from before the pool proves from row d *(C1-1.1, O1-F14,
    C1-4.3)*.
14. **The words** (§15), en and zh, new codes with no path or account, the old ones unchanged: `started.tree-kept` (its files those of what it
    was based on) and its clauses, `started.tree-kept-dropped`, `started.tree-back`, `tree.returned`, `started.tree-past-kept`, `ended.output`
    (已完成，有产出), `ended.no-output` (已完成，无产出), `ended.uncommitted`, `tree.kept-held`, `tree.kept-stuck`, `tree.kept-unclean`,
    `tree.visit-refused`, `review.show-again-moved`; the glossary's *kept tree* (保留的工作树) *(O1-F15)*.
15. **The install's trees** (§16) are not adopted, converted or removed by this work; they drain by today's rules, and a legacy line whose tree
    is gone may have a slot brought to its path.
16. **Reached in rows** (§17), TREEPOOL1a to 1j, each its own branch after mod-x: the slots and the census (a); the take, return, finish, end
    and retirement over git, unwired, with every boundary stopped and what crosses a move proved and measured (b), whose note both makers read
    before row d; the sites that meet a gone tree (c); the pool wired, read only through a test's seam, with the pinned pre-pool build's
    rehearsal (d); lines coming back (e); lowering, leaving and, last, the count's doors (f); the install's repository on, measured from a
    start's admission to its first turn, with what permits the default's flip (g); the default flipped (h); work kept aside, the index and the
    working tree both (i); and finished scratch cleared (j). Rows b to j are ⚖ and built only after both makers' reads find nothing blocking in
    them *(C1-4.1, C1-4.3, C1-4.4, C1-2.6)*.
17. **The owner's** (§18, D191 point 3): what the count means; the pool on for the install's repository; Daoris keeping a let-go line's
    uncommitted work aside off its branch and clearing it from the tree; a finished line's ignored scratch cleared when its tree is taken; and
    one bounded real resume on the owner's account. Each is asked saying what it is, what a yes does and where to look.

**Why.** The disk and the setup are spent on making and discarding whole working copies, so a working copy is kept and handed on. What must
not be handed on is identity: twenty-odd readers take a folder for its branch and a path for a line, a shipped build goes on in a record's path
by whatever folder stands there, TREEFOLLOW1 learns whatever branch a line's folder shows, and Claude Code finds a conversation by its
directory. Keeping the slot at the line's path, parking it between lines and placing it only once it is switched and checked, keeps every one of
those facts true with no reader rewritten, no service family added and no resume traded for a brief, at the cost of two renames per take under
the repository's exclusive hold and of the build folders that name their own path, which are made again rather than carried and which a row
measures before anything is switched on. A slot is taken only when nothing in it is outside a ref and opens a record only when it reads clean
where it will run, so the first build resets nothing and loses nothing; keeping uncommitted work aside, which Daoris committing off-branch would
be, is the owner's to allow. KEEPSIZE1b's protocol, the set-aside's move, the slim's manifest, TREEGONE1's remake and TREEDEPS1's gate are
reused where they already prove what the pool needs, so the pool is a layer over today's trees rather than a second lifecycle.

**Rejected** (design §1.1, §19): a slot at a stable path of its own, and with it a second session family in the service for pooled lines; a
durable lease per occupation; git's lock alone, a lease file or a lease in the service; a permanent pool lock reason; `reset --hard` and
`clean -fd` before the owner's yes, `clean -fdx`, a forced switch, `worktree add --force`; loose files moved to a kept area; Daoris committing
leftovers on the line's branch; the count in `daoris.json`, the registry or a store table of its own; the cap at opt-in as a stored default; a
start waiting for a tree (put to the owner); new tables, refs or a route for a finished line's record; removal by age; `git worktree remove` of a
retiring slot; a branch named for a slot; a junction per line; copying an agent's conversation between folders; a new part handed the brief
whenever a line's slot was taken; copying dependencies at every take; a stale `node_modules` kept untouched; adopting the install's idle trees;
and, in round 2, a slot moved to a line's path before its switch; a destination keyed by the slot alone; OPENCUT1's lock through a take or a
return; a record opened without reading the slot after its switch; a stuck slot retired whatever it holds; every listed folder carried across a
move; a keep of the working files alone; a takeover by phase alone; a terminal's hold known to the shell alone; the count's doors before
returns and retirement; and a finished record's inputs left with no table.
