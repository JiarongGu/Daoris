## D358 — A consumer is declared on three layers, the bus, the dispatch and the stream's operators, so no consumer writes its own ordering, bounds, folding or acknowledgement rule (2026-11-22)

**Decision (BUSFLOW; the owner's direction of 2026-11-22, its choices the steward's in that direction; refining D356 points 6 and 7,
whose queue and reducers stand).**

1. **Why.** The first consumers, the opinion's gate (BUS2d) and the run's progression (BUS2e1), each wrote by hand what every consumer
   needs: rounds and folding, the names a folded move carried, a semaphore per repository and one across them, a window crediting a
   begun node, a bound on a pass. Their reviews found their defects there, the same classes each time: a lock order, an acknowledgement
   before the job moved, a name lost in a fold, all of an open set judged at once, one failure costing more than its entry. Each new
   consumer would write them again. The rules the driving area states (five, 2026-11-22) belong in one layer that keeps them.

   The shape is the common one, a log, its subjects and their subscribers (a topic's partitions and a consumer group's workers, with
   a stream's operators between): Daoris writes the few pieces it needs on it rather than taking a framework (below).

2. **The bus** stays as it is: the durable log, the cursor, the acknowledgement in the cursor's transaction, the park and its backoff,
   the epoch's fence (D356 points 1, 5, 6). It knows nothing of what a consumer does.

3. **The dispatch** says who gets an entry and how work is shared, by declaration, never by a consumer's own locks: the key an entry is
   worked under (its subject, or a key the consumer derives: a run, a repository, an owed opinion), the order within a key (in order,
   always), the bounds (at most so many keys at once, at most so many within a group such as a repository), and the mode (one lane per
   key, a shared work queue, or every subscriber).

4. **The stream's operators** are the shared steps a consumer composes: coalesce by key (the entries waiting on one key become one piece
   of work, with a declared merge of what they named), batch by time (with the frames' pace), deduplicate by effect key, a time window, and
   a join with a kept view (the state a judgment reads). A consumer declares its key, its merge and its handler; the handler receives one
   key's merged work and returns what came of it.

5. **The outcome decides the acknowledgement, in the layer.** A handler returns moved, not moved (with why: not ready, not governed, a
   bound passed) or failed. Moved ends the entries' obligations; not moved acknowledges them as handled and leaves them armed for the
   stall view; failed parks that key's work alone and retries it with backoff, never its lane, another key or another fact. No consumer
   chooses `Ended` itself.

6. **Order.** The layer is built with the opinion's gate and the run's progression moved onto it in the same change, each shorter than
   today and its tests unchanged in what they prove; then each job BUS2e moves off a look is written on it; a consumer that needs what
   the layer lacks extends the layer, not itself.

**Rejected.** A framework from outside (a stream library or a broker): D356 point 6 keeps delivery in the lease holder's process on
.NET channels, and the operators needed are few. Leaving each consumer its own handling: the reviews showed the cost.
