## 1. Blocking findings

1. **Row a can lock an engine without proving it can carry the editor.** React 19, ports, custom routes and pointer capture are explicitly non-blocking, although the shipped chart depends on custom nodes, outcome ports, insertion controls and pick mode. A candidate could pass viewport measurements while failing essential editor integration. The choice rule also has no explicit stop when both candidates fail; row a allows “its failure recorded.” Make editor compatibility mandatory and require a viable winner before building further. Evidence: `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:292`, `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:309`, `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:382`; `src/Daoris.Web/src/workflow/chart/FlowChart.tsx:75`, `src/Daoris.Web/src/workflow/chart/PortRow.tsx:43`.

   **The comparison also needs a fair interpretation.** The blocking bundle ceilings differ—12 KB versus 150 KiB—so an 18 KB extension could fail where a larger adoption passes. Keep the extension preference explicit, but distinguish candidate-specific targets from common product requirements. Measure equivalent, fully adapted scenes, including startup and measurement costs; do not score library defaults against the extension’s existing Daoris integration. Evidence: `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:306`, `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:310`; `docs/archive/2026-11-02-canvas-proposal-codex.md:267`.

2. **“Every box unchanged on a run’s state change” contradicts the folding being retained.** The design keeps `flowFolds.ts` whole while requiring status refreshes to leave every box unchanged. Existing large-flow folds deliberately follow current, settled and reached states; their membership can change as a run advances. Refusals also deliberately open the holds group. Preserve these behaviours as explicit scene changes, with anchor preservation; test geometry stability only while the projected scene and fold membership remain fixed. Otherwise the proof either cannot pass or removes existing behaviour. Evidence: `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:329`, `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:410`; `src/Daoris.Web/src/design/flowFolds.ts:19`, `src/Daoris.Web/src/design/FlowCanvas.tsx:261`, `src/Daoris.Web/src/workflow/edit/WorkflowEditor.tsx:433`.

3. **The bands do not yet represent the actual appointment model.** The design puts appointed roles among each band’s roles, dashed while dormant, and removes appointment edges. Today there is one node per appointed-role kind, potentially connected to several places; dormancy belongs to each appointment edge. Offered roles also exist without an appointment. One shared node cannot occupy several bands or have different dormancy at each place. Specify per-place appointment occurrences, their mapping to the shared selection/detail identity, and where offered-but-unappointed roles appear. Preserve every appointment and its words in the list. Evidence: `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:187`, `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:189`; `src/Daoris.Web/src/agents/roles/roleStructure.ts:563`, `src/Daoris.Web/src/agents/roles/roleStructure.ts:574`, `src/Daoris.Web/src/agents/roles/RoleDiagram.tsx:66`.

   Workspace folding also needs a new projection: today `diagramShape` assigns workspace membership only to **place nodes**. Keeping that adapter unchanged will not fold the proposed bands’ roles with their places. Evidence: `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:334`; `src/Daoris.Web/src/agents/roles/diagram.ts:27`, `src/Daoris.Web/src/design/groupFolds.ts:33`.

4. **The proposed map walk does not explain how every line remains reachable.** Right reaches the *first* outgoing line and then its destination; `order` is node reading order, and `walk.out/into` each return one ID. There is no stated traversal of remaining incident lines, parallel relationship kinds or undirected knowledge links. Today those relationships have individually focusable buttons. Removing their Tab stops needs a complete replacement, not just the first-edge walk. Define edge stops, cycling/order and return behaviour, including asks and knowledge links, before the shared navigation API is fixed. Evidence: `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:60`, `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:104`, `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:408`; `src/Daoris.Web/src/map/LayeredMap.tsx:347`, `src/Daoris.Web/src/map/LayeredMap.tsx:368`; `src/Daoris.Web/src/design/FlowCanvas.tsx:417`.

5. **The blanket “300 px run stays its rail” statement is wrong about built pages.** Release and review runs currently draw at 300 px; the unrouted-outcome fixture needs the rail there. The design’s general instruction to retain today’s placement below 36rem is sound, but its specific sidebar statement contradicts it. Keep compact drawings where they fit at 100%, and the existing feature rail otherwise. Evidence: `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:148`, `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:333`; `src/Daoris.Web/measured/flow.spec.ts:60`, `src/Daoris.Web/measured/flow.spec.ts:63`, `src/Daoris.Web/measured/flow.spec.ts:67`, `src/Daoris.Web/measured/flow.spec.ts:71`; `src/Daoris.Web/src/workflow/WorkflowChart.tsx:433`.

## 2. Non-blocking findings

- **The measurement inventory omits important obstacles.** `worldBoxes` names nodes, ports, labels and inserts. Existing routing also requires waypoint boxes, and label placement avoids section dividers. Include both explicitly in row a’s shared geometry contract. Evidence: `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:38`; `src/Daoris.Web/src/design/FlowCanvas.tsx:1005`, `src/Daoris.Web/src/design/FlowCanvas.tsx:1018`; `src/Daoris.Web/src/design/flowInk.ts:72`.

- **An 80% floor does not establish usable controls.** The current insert is `size-5`, and ports use small text and padding. Transforming them makes their targets smaller; keeping the toolbar unscaled does not protect controls inside the world. Measure effective targets and define enlarged hit areas or an explicit readable-scale editing transition. Also reconcile “only positioned worlds have tiers” with the later promise that unreadably small canvas nodes become chips. Evidence: `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:47`, `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:113`, `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:365`; `src/Daoris.Web/src/workflow/chart/GapInsert.tsx:22`, `src/Daoris.Web/src/workflow/chart/PortRow.tsx:52`.

- **Clarify the camera convention before implementing `viewMath`.** `View.x/y` are described as the world point at the top left, while the transform equation uses screen translation. Those are different quantities: translation is `−k × cameraOrigin`. The existing map uses the former convention. Evidence: `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:37`, `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:52`; `src/Daoris.Web/src/map/LayeredMap.tsx:162`, `src/Daoris.Web/src/map/LayeredMap.tsx:229`.

- **Bring a small installed-host check forward to the engine choice.** The design correctly says browser tests cannot prove host wheel, focus, DPI or accessibility behaviour, but the comprehensive installed row comes last. Check the candidate-sensitive wheel/focus/reader seams before locking the engine; retain the final integrated journeys in row g. Evidence: `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:283`, `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:311`, `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:388`.

## 3. Proposal material worth retaining

- **Startup cost and lazy loading.** Codex asked for parse/init time and lazy-loading measurements alongside bundle bytes. The synthesis retains byte limits but drops these explicit costs. Keep them in row a’s report. Evidence: `docs/archive/2026-11-02-canvas-proposal-codex.md:272`; `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:306`.

- **Explicit interaction precedence.** Keep Codex’s scrollable-node exception, Escape ordering and release-outside cancellation. These make the new ground gestures coexist with embedded controls and existing pick mode. Evidence: `docs/archive/2026-11-02-canvas-proposal-codex.md:113`, `docs/archive/2026-11-02-canvas-proposal-codex.md:136`, `docs/archive/2026-11-02-canvas-proposal-codex.md:165`; `src/Daoris.Web/src/design/FlowCanvas.tsx:378`, `src/Daoris.Web/src/design/FlowCanvas.tsx:386`.

- **A selectable search-results list.** Codex proposed named results, not only sequential matching. Keep that alongside the synthesis’s better decision to leave focus in the find field after Enter. Choosing among thousands of matches should not require repeated stepping. Evidence: `docs/archive/2026-11-02-canvas-proposal-codex.md:242`; `docs/2026-11-02-canvas-design.md:249`.

First rows should carry: **a—complete and fair engine gates, preserved narrow readings and explicit geometry contracts; b—state/fold exceptions and complete interaction rules; c—appointment identities and band-aware folding; define full edge navigation before fixing the shared API.**
